# F01 provider evidence

Read the bounded evidence by owner: [FFVB](evidence/ffvb.md) and [LNV/DataProject](evidence/lnv.md). Earlier [F02 provider evidence](../002-sporting-data/provider-evidence.md) retains its dated observations with the corrected interpretation of shared club numbers.

The exploration covers 2026/2027, 2025/2026, 2024/2025 and 2023/2024 where available, with older witnesses explicitly separated. An unavailable or unlabeled context is reported as such, not replaced with current data. Sampled IDs are evidence values, never production constants. Exact supplier labels are retained only where needed to understand the example.

## Evidence classes

- Observed: public page/menu/form/CSV examined, with URL and method in the provider record.
- Historical documentation: dated provider manual or rules, useful interpretation evidence but not a guarantee of unchanged current behavior.
- Synthetic: deliberately reduced fixture or proposed negative case, not a claim about a real match. In particular, add `3/P` to regression scenarios without labelling it a directly observed sample unless its source is established.
- **NOT EXECUTED**: full provider/context coverage qualification, parser regression execution, code generation, real consumers, PostgreSQL/OpenSearch transactions and native checks. Their criteria are in [quickstart](quickstart.md).

No raw-page archive, personal-contact history, authenticated provider mutation or source-repository consultation forms part of this research. Request counts measure exploratory method only; redirect exchanges and separate web search/open operations are not a controlled traffic experiment.
