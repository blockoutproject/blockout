## Evidence limitations and rejected expansion

The samples establish supported navigation shapes, not an undocumented public API promise or exhaustive coverage. No light universal LNV set feed was established. Do not reverse-engineer a complex browser protocol to avoid necessary detail reads. Historical seasonal labels and all professional phases require actual scope qualification. Missing club correspondence stays unresolved rather than being guessed from a name.

Observed byte sizes/timings in earlier F02 evidence remain exploratory single-read observations, not targets. No benchmark, load/capacity program or monthly availability metric is added. All proposed initial runtime settings remain subject to qualification without changing accepted functional cadence silently.

Provider-format incompatibility blocks its affected scope and returns to explicit owner decision where product behavior would change; it does not justify a hidden fallback source, fuzzy match or custom recovery system.

## Terminal closure and independent controls

Need: prevent accidental irreversible operator action without retaining an exceptional recollection workflow. Decision: exact-label confirmation checked with the current revision; CLOSED is terminal. Alternative: a simple confirm button would be easier but insufficiently deliberate for the owner. Consequence: a mistaken closure has no supported recovery in V2; initial reconstruction must finish or its gaps be accepted first. Validation: V03/V09/V12/V13, including cycle-start races and uncertain responses. Competition controls belong to a season/provider; the simpler global pause was rejected by the owner because it interrupts unrelated seasons. Provider concurrency limits remain shared without a global pause command.

## F03 reference and chronology qualification

[F03 research](../007-sporting-consultation/research.md#d06--source-references-and-direct-pdf) records a focused follow-up of seven FFVB reads. The source emits an information-sheet POST descriptor and a direct FDME URL; acquisition carries these references as secondary facts, without retrieving PDFs. Professional links must actually be published and attributable; F07's exclusion of automated LNV TV discovery remains unchanged. Canonical season ordering uses qualified published startYear, never match dates or lexical labels. The [model](data-model.md#f03-consultation-additions) defines candidate-to-season ownership.
