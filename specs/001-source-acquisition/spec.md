# Functional specification: source acquisition and observation reliability

**Feature branch**: `feature/257-source-acquisition`

**Created**: 2026-09-15

**Status**: Accepted

**Requested scope**: Scope approved by the product owner for [#257](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout-legacy/issues/257): specify acquisition of FFVB/LNV competitions, club sheets, geocoding, reliable observations and operational commands; retain FFVB discovery and CSV exports, with explicit V2 changes and without technical implementation.

<a id="user-scenarios--testing-mandatory"></a>

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-09

- Q: Do championship pools across phases or rounds require a competition-progression model? → A: No. Covered pools remain independent ordinary pools; one team can participate in several pools with the same classification. Team identity uses club, name, season, division, gender and format. Keep F02 name comparison, including significant dots and hyphens, rather than copying V1 normalization. French Cups remain excluded; no qualification calculation, progression link or automatic team/follow transfer is introduced.

### Session 2026-10-07

- Q: Does launching a season or discovering its pools require mapping first? → A: No. Qualified sources can launch and discover pools independently of mapping. Mapping remains in its separate administration journey; collection screens show neither classification readiness nor a mapping shortcut. FR-050 and A40 express this source-launch boundary.
- Q: What is acquired before mapping? → A: Only pool and native pack discovery. Calendars and results are acquired after effective mapping, subject to the other eligibility rules; no pending-calendar store is introduced.

- Closure is definitive with exact typed season confirmation; FR-048 is withdrawn and replaced by FR-055. A35/A44 now verify closure rather than post-closure recollection. Initial reconstruction occurs before closure. Competition pause/resume is independent per season and provider; club controls remain seasonless.
  — approved KISS and F01 planning revisions, 2026-10-06

- Q: What is the simple acquisition policy? → A: LNV pages alone supply professional matches and standings; FFVB supplies other covered competitions. One qualified current empty calendar hides its pool and dependent visibility, while still-listed references remain collected. Manual pack/pool exclusions and consecutive-failure counters are removed. The first failed competition cycle opens an incident; closed-season history remains protected.

- Q: How are youth pools, seasonal identities and LNV preparation handled? → A: Youth qualifiers remain ordinary manually classified pools without progression modelling. Clubs persist, teams/pools do not cross seasons. LNV uses assisted discovery with supported pre-launch URL completion and explicit season confirmation when needed; launched sources lock permanently in ordinary administration. A38–A45 and FR-050–FR-054 define the approved amendment.

## User scenarios and validation _(mandatory)_

The main actor is an operator with explicit collection permissions. Visitors and signed-in users benefit from reliable sporting data but cannot control collection. Live-stream moderator status grants no collection permission. The sporting-data domain consumes observations; its decisions belong to F02 and do not constitute a second end-user role.

The scenario identifiers below are local acceptance references, not implementation-task identifiers. Independent validations describe acceptance evidence to obtain; they do not claim that executable V2 exists.

<a id="user-story-1---collect-only-eligible-official-competitions-priority-p1"></a>

### User story 1 — Collect only eligible official competitions (Priority: P1)

As an operator, I want current official coverage to determine what is collected so obsolete references or assumed classifications do not drive acquisition.

**Priority rationale**: Source eligibility determines the reliability and scope of all subsequently obtained sporting data.

**Independent validation**: Supply representative catalogs and operational settings, then compare the collection-eligible set and exclusion reasons without requiring sporting integration.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a supported readable catalog and an active season, **when** a classified pool within coverage is discovered, **then** its calendar becomes eligible; each of the five excluded league codes remains outside scope. (FR-001, FR-004, FR-008)
2. **A02** — **Given** a previously discovered pool, **when** a new usable catalog no longer lists it, **then** collection through its old reference stops immediately, without waiting for further absence cycles or deleting business data. **When** it reappears, **then** current eligibility rules apply again. (FR-005, FR-019)
3. **A03** — **Given** pool references already successfully discovered for the same season, **when** their catalog is unreadable, partial or replaced by an error page, **then** collection through those references may continue if other eligibility conditions are met while discovery is retried; discovery failure remains visible and proves no absence. Without prior successful discovery, no reference is guessed. (FR-004, FR-006, FR-018)
4. **A04** — **Given** successful discovery, **when** an intermediate five-minute collection becomes due, **then** its same-season references may be reused under current eligibility, including after discovery failure. **When** a later usable catalog confirms absence, **then** the absent source reference ceases to be eligible, even if its old URL responds. (FR-005, FR-006, FR-031)
5. **A05** — **Given** a newly detected season, **when** no operator has activated it, **then** it is visible for preparation but not periodically collected. **When** it is activated while another season remains active, **then** both may be collected. **When** one is closed, **then** its periodic collection stops without deleting history. (FR-007, FR-019)
6. **A06** — **Given** a pack awaiting classification, **when** its pools also have no effective classification, **then** discovery still records the native pack and its pool references, but calendars and results are not acquired and no sporting creation is requested. **When** a pool receives a complete individual classification, **then** only that eligible pool may be collected. Removing that classification without a replacement supplied by the pack stops collection from the next cycle, even if F02 retains already published classification and visibility; a refused restoration does not restart collection. Deactivating a division suspends dependent collection from the next cycle; reactivation reassesses eligibility without lifting independent visibility restrictions. (FR-008, FR-009, FR-034)
7. **A07** — **Given** a hidden but still catalog-listed pool with active season/division and effective classification, **when** collection runs, **then** its calendar remains eligible. No manual pack/pool exclusion control exists; selected season/provider pauses and fixed coverage boundaries still apply. (FR-008–FR-010, FR-034)

<a id="user-story-2---keep-unreliable-input-from-becoming-sporting-truth-priority-p1"></a>

### User story 2 — Keep unreliable input from becoming sporting truth (Priority: P1)

As a Blockout data consumer, I want collection failures and ambiguous source data to remain distinct from actual sporting changes.

**Priority rationale**: A successful download is insufficient to establish reliable results or disappearance decisions.

**Independent validation**: Present complete, empty, malformed and contradictory observations, then examine their acceptance, reasons and functional transmission independently of storage or display.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A08** — **Given** a 100-match calendar with one unusable match, with usable overall structure and context, **when** it is acquired, **then** the other 99 matches remain usable, the affected match is isolated with reasons and the observation is incomplete for absence-based reconciliation. If overall structure or context is unreadable, the calendar is instead rejected; independent pools continue to be processed. (FR-011, FR-012, FR-018)
2. **A09** — **Given** a source calendar and its context, **when** two rows contradict each other for the same source reference, **then** the match is isolated without choosing a version and other usable matches are retained; this incomplete observation cannot justify absence-based withdrawal. Identical repetition, reuse in another season and disagreement between providers are distinct cases. (FR-013, FR-019)
3. **A10** — **Given** an otherwise usable record, **when** a secondary field such as a club website is malformed, **then** that field is flagged and isolated without rejecting the whole record or requesting erasure of its previous value. (FR-014)
4. **A11** — **Given** a match whose result is unpublished, whose start time is unknown or whose future participant is recognized as still to qualify, **when** it is acquired, **then** normally absent values remain unknown and context for the future slot is retained without inventing teams or dates or rejecting other identifiable matches. Special sporting notations are retained for F02 interpretation without conversion to zero. (FR-015, FR-016)
5. **A12** — **Given** a valid calendar, **when** its official standings are unreadable overall or cannot be attached to their context, **then** those standings are independently rejected. A usable table retains official ordering and labels even if some statistics are absent or invalid; no zero is invented. Absence of a match to a Blockout team belongs to F02 association, not acquisition invalidity. (FR-017, FR-018)
6. **A13** — **Given** a calendar attributable to the correct pool and season, **when** empty observations are obtained, **then** they are distinguished from failures and handled according to the following cases; F02 owns sporting consequences. (FR-011, FR-019, FR-020, FR-047, FR-055)

- **Qualified current empty**: One complete, correctly contextualized and successfully integrated empty observation hides the pool, matches and participations without erasing identities. Its catalog reference remains traversed.
- **Closed history**: New observations for a closed season are refused; only its already running cycle may finish once. Retained history and independent restrictions remain.
- **False empty**: HTTP 200, header-only content without qualified context, all rejected rows, errors and partial integration authorize no absence-based hiding.
- **Reappearance**: A valid attributable return reuses known identities without a second confirmation; F02 preserves other hiding reasons.
- **Normal observation**: Valid empty results produce no collection-failure incident.

7. **A14** — **Given** an observation requested for a season, **when** supplied content clearly belongs to another season, **then** it is rejected and context inconsistency is reported immediately; current-season content cannot fill a historical gap. (FR-012, FR-040, FR-047)
8. **A15** — **Given** validated acquisition, **when** some subsequent sporting integrations fail, **then** operators can distinguish successful acquisition, successful updates and remaining failures; the observation is not presented as fully integrated. (FR-021, FR-022, FR-043)

### User story 3 — Obtain all covered professional phases (Priority: P1)

As a user following professional volleyball, I want acquisition to cover every official phase of the three supported championships beyond regular seasons alone.

**Priority rationale**: The product owner identifies incomplete professional coverage and late V1 enrichment as significant limitations.

**Independent validation**: Use official entry pages and representative regular-season, playoff and final-phase references with changing season identifiers, then examine discovered coverage and observations attributed separately to their sources.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A16** — **Given** the three official professional-championship entry points, **when** seasonal references or published phase links change, **then** discovery follows official entry points and phases without treating previously observed codes as permanent or adding unrelated competitions. (FR-002, FR-003)
2. **A17** — **Given** a published LNV professional phase, **when** its grouped calendar and match pages are collected, **then** page facts retain their context and match detail cadence. Missing coverage remains unqualified; neither XML nor FFVB completes these matches. FFVB remains the source for other covered competitions. (FR-002, FR-003, FR-018, FR-021)
3. **A18** — **Given** a new unsupported official-page structure, **when** discovery cannot interpret it, **then** an explicit diagnostic and structural incident require collector maintenance; no reference is guessed; supported pre-launch URL completion remains possible under FR-003, but cannot repair an unsupported parser. (FR-003, FR-040)

<a id="user-story-4---enrich-covered-clubs-without-repeated-useless-requests-priority-p1"></a>

### User story 4 — Enrich covered clubs without repeated useless requests (Priority: P1)

As a user viewing a club or map, I want club information and coordinate observations refreshed without equating provider failure with disappearance.

**Priority rationale**: Club details support viewing, while an unresolved address must not cause identical geocoding requests indefinitely.

**Independent validation**: Supply covered-club references, changed addresses and geocoding results; examine requested work and observations without deciding final coordinate publication.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A19** — **Given** a newly encountered covered club, **when** its sheet is first collected then refreshed, **then** the first attempt follows discovery and subsequent attempts respect a 24-hour cadence; unrelated clubs in the directory are not added. Failure of another club’s page neither deactivates this club nor discards its result. (FR-024, FR-025)
2. **A20** — **Given** a new need for coordinates or an effective-locality change passed by F02 after correction, intentional absence or return to the source, **when** collection is enabled and the provider available, **then** a geocoding attempt occurs within one hour and passes the result and address context to F02. (FR-026, FR-029)
3. **A21** — **Given** a correct geocoding response without a usable candidate or with an ambiguous result, **when** the address remains unchanged, **then** ordinary collection does not query it again. **When** the locality context defined by F02 changes or the operator reruns the geocoding family, **then** a new attempt becomes possible. Existing valid results are not recalculated by this rerun. (FR-027, FR-028)
4. **A22** — **Given** a technical club-sheet or geocoding failure, **when** no operator pause prevents work, **then** it remains distinct from a valid empty or ambiguous response, is retried before 24 hours and triggers an incident if the outage persists for 24 hours. (FR-029, FR-037, FR-042)

<a id="user-story-5---control-collection-and-cadence-predictably-priority-p1"></a>

### User story 5 — Control collection and cadence predictably (Priority: P1)

As an operator, I want to pause and rerun collection without duplicate executions, hidden fallback configuration or accumulated missed work to catch up.

**Priority rationale**: Predictable commands limit provider traffic and allow safe maintenance.

**Independent validation**: Observe scheduled times and operational-command outcomes in a simulated calendar with fast windows, overruns, configuration changes and refused commands.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A23** — **Given** an eligible pool, **when** no reliable match start places it in a fast window, **then** competition collection follows 30-minute scheduled intervals. From one hour before to four hours after a reliable match start, it follows five-minute scheduled intervals regardless of weekday. Absence of a result after the window does not extend it; an unknown match start creates no window. (FR-030, FR-031)
2. **A24** — **Given** a five-minute collection target, **when** a normal execution overruns, **then** missed ticks are skipped without normal overlap or catch-up. **When** the collector crashes, **then** accepted integration survives and a new eligible scheduled cycle may collect again. Exceptional bounded overlapping downloads during failure never duplicate business effects or overwrite newer data; interruption remains visible. (FR-035)
3. **A25** — **Given** a season/provider competition cycle in progress, **when** an authorized operator pauses that scope, **then** its current cycle finishes and its next does not start; the same provider in another season, the other provider and club controls remain eligible according to their own settings. Resuming does not replay missed scheduled runs. Activating or deactivating F12 access maintenance triggers neither collection pause nor resumption. (FR-032, FR-033, FR-035)
4. **A26** — **Given** a cycle in progress, **when** classification, division or season configuration changes, **then** the current cycle uses its starting configuration and the next uses the new one. If reliable configuration cannot be obtained at the next start, no collection starts with remembered settings. (FR-034)
5. **A27** — **Given** an authorized manual rerun for a season/provider or seasonless club control, **when** it is already collecting, **then** the state of its existing execution is returned without duplicate work. Otherwise, rerunning respects eligibility and does not implicitly lift that scope’s pause. A paused scope without an existing cycle rejects relaunch until explicitly resumed. (FR-036)
6. **A28** — **Given** an ordinary user or live-stream moderator without collection permission, **when** they request a pause, resumption, rerun, season command or collection-setting change, **then** the command is refused. Permission assignment belongs to F12. (FR-038)
7. **A37** — **Given** an authorized mobile operator, **when** the dashboard is consulted or refreshed, **then** its season/provider controls and two independent club controls distinguish activation, execution, last attempt, last success and failure or partial results without fabricated recovery. Season controls affect competition families only; technical diagnostics are expandable when available, and source preparation is available before launch and definitive closure requires exact typed confirmation and closed seasons are read-only; exceptional post-launch source repair remains technical. Permission denial, stale decisions and uncertain outcomes remain explicit. (FR-007, FR-023–FR-029, FR-032–FR-039, FR-055)

<a id="user-story-6---understand-incidents-and-actual-recovery-priority-p2"></a>

### User story 6 — Understand incidents and actual recovery (Priority: P2)

As an operator, I want grouped, actionable reports that clearly distinguish failure, voluntary pause and actual recovery.

**Priority rationale**: Repetitive or misleading notifications make actual acquisition failures harder to identify.

**Independent validation**: Submit bounded observation sequences to functional incident rules and compare expected incident states and notifications without choosing a monitoring product.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A29** — **Given** the same collection scope, **when** observations follow one another, **then** counting and incident opening follow the cases below. Every sequence starts without an open incident unless stated otherwise. (FR-020, FR-039, FR-041, FR-043, FR-044)

- **First failure**: The first failed competition cycle opens one incident; repeated attempts and later failed cycles update it.
- **Recovery**: A usable observation demonstrating recovery resolves it. A later new failure opens a new incident.
- **Unexecuted cycle**: A pause or missing attempt establishes neither failure nor recovery.

2. **A30** — **Given** an open technical incident, **when** its family is paused, its division deactivated or another pool processed successfully, **then** no recovery is announced. **When** a new usable observation of the affected scope demonstrates the end of the problem, including a valid empty calendar if that observation establishes resolution of the technical failure, **then** recovery is announced once; it remains independent of the threshold permitting F02 withdrawals. (FR-043, FR-044)
3. **A31** — **Given** several newly detected seasons or unclassified entries, **when** they require operator action, **then** reports are grouped instead of sent for each item; neither awaiting classification nor a voluntary pause is presented as a provider outage. (FR-044, FR-045)
4. **A32** — **Given** an incident remaining open over several cycles, **when** the problem persists, **then** it remains visible without periodic reminder notifications. Structural or context anomalies are reported immediately, on the first failed cycle. (FR-040, FR-044)
5. **A33** — **Given** failed acquisition with diagnostic metadata, **when** an operator views it, **then** source, scope, observation time, outcome, available counts and reason are understandable without automatic raw-document archives, personal contact details or secret identifiers in diagnostics. (FR-021, FR-023)

<a id="user-story-7---reconstruct-obtainable-history-through-a-bounded-operation-priority-p2"></a>

### User story 7 — Reconstruct obtainable history through a bounded operation (Priority: P2)

As an operator preparing V2, I want one controlled initial reconstruction faithful to available sources, followed by definitive season closure without an archive-management product.

**Priority rationale**: Historical reconstruction is useful, but source-data unavailability must not become a false coverage promise.

**Independent validation**: Prepare references covering three completed seasons and the current season, including absent or wrong-season sources, then examine the acquisition report and periodic-collection eligibility.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A34** — **Given** prepared and verified historical references, including for a completed sporting season still PREPARING in Blockout whose pools are absent from the current catalog, **when** initial reconstruction runs, **then** those references are eligible without season activation or discovery in the current catalog, provided classification, division activation, coverage and permissions allow the operation; it targets three completed seasons plus the current season, applies normal validations and distinguishes obtained, rejected and unavailable coverage; partial source availability is not presented as complete reconstruction. Absence of a pool from the current catalog removes no already acquired historical data. (FR-046, FR-047, FR-049)
2. **A35** — **Given** an authorized operator reviewing a season, **when** closure is requested, **then** the modal states the definitive consequence and requires the exact displayed season label, such as `2026/2027`. Blank, different, stale or unauthorized requests have no effect. After acceptance, no new collection, relaunch, reactivation or source edit is possible for that season; already running work may finish once under its existing cycle. Other seasons and clubs keep their controls and data. (FR-007, FR-032–FR-038, FR-055)

3. **A36** — **Given** an absent historical LNV source and an available current-season feed, **when** reconstruction is attempted, **then** the current feed is not used as a substitute; the gap is reported for the transition decision. An inaccessible, unreadable or wrong-season archive retains previously accepted data and never constitutes empty-calendar confirmation. (FR-012, FR-047, FR-049)

<a id="edge-cases"></a>

### Edge cases

- A URL still responding after removal from the catalog does not authorize continued current collection (A02). Failure to read the catalog does not prove removal and allows reuse of references already successfully discovered for the same season under current eligibility (A03).
- Simultaneously active seasons retain distinct observation contexts even if provider codes repeat (A05, A09, A14).
- An individually classified pool may remain eligible under an unclassified pack. Public hiding does not stop discovery or eligible calendar collection; no manual pack/pool exclusion exists (A06–A07).
- A normally absent value and a malformed value do not have the same meaning; neither authorizes an invented replacement (A10–A12).
- An always-empty phase and a formerly populated calendar becoming empty are not incidents; full withdrawal of previously visible data follows the single qualified observation rule in A13; closed seasons retain history and reject new collection under A35.
- A normal manual family rerun cannot bypass pause, coverage, classification or permission requirements (A27–A28). Controlled historical acquisition has its own season and reference eligibility rules while retaining classification, coverage and permission requirements (A34–A35).
- A valid observation may resolve a network outage while establishing another data-quality incident; recovery concerns only the problem that has actually ended (A30).
- Initial reconstruction uses ordinary provider work limits before closure; it does not alter another season’s pause state (A34–A35).

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional requirements

All requirements below express V2 behavior approved by the product owner. V1 evidence is separately identified in [Evidence and decision traceability](#evidence-and-decision-traceability).

<a id="coverage-and-discovery"></a>

#### Coverage and discovery

- **FR-001**: Acquisition MUST discover covered FFVB national, regional and departmental championship pools through official seasonal entries, national selector options/frames and organizer menus/links/forms. Youth qualifying pools are ordinary pools classified manually, for example M18 REG or M18 DEP, with format and gender; no qualifying entity, progression link or qualification calculation is required. Exclude French Cups, unrelated competitions and organizer codes `LIGU`, `LIGY`, `LIMART`, `LIMY`, `LIRE`. Use calendar CSV exports and contextual HTML complements; directories alone do not prove catalog completeness.
- **FR-002**: Acquisition MUST cover the published phases of all three professional championships through official LNV pages: grouped calendars, match details and standings. Do not supplement these matches or standings from LNV XML or FFVB. Ordinary covered competitions retain FFVB CSV plus complementary pages. Unproven phase coverage remains explicitly unqualified; no sampled identifier becomes permanent configuration.
- **FR-003**: Acquisition MUST derive seasonal and phase references from published official entries rather than increment years or sampled IDs. LNV assisted discovery proposes the three championship references; before launch an operator may supply or correct official URLs in supported formats. Verify accessibility, structure and scope, and require explicit operator season confirmation when machine-readable seasonal evidence is absent. Persist accepted season/source bindings; a changed provider ID alone proves no new season. Unsupported structures require technical collector maintenance, not a generic URL/scraping editor. FR-050–FR-052 govern preparation and locking.
- **FR-004**: A usable discovery observation MUST establish that the expected catalog structure and affected scope were read and interpreted without missing parts. An HTTP success status is insufficient. Error pages, unrecognized structures and partial reads MUST NOT establish confirmed absence. A catalog recognized as actually empty must be distinguishable from an unreadable catalog.
- **FR-005**: During periodic collection, omission from a complete usable catalog for the affected season and scope MUST stop calls through that reference immediately. A single round page is not the whole seasonal catalog. Rollover of a current LNV entry MUST NOT remove the championship already bound to an older active season; inspect its own qualified catalog. Failure or partial discovery proves no absence. Reappearance triggers normal eligibility assessment; F02 owns visibility and identities.
- **FR-006**: Failed or unusable discovery alone MUST NOT invalidate references from prior successful discovery for the same season. Those references MAY serve otherwise-eligible intermediate and subsequent collections while discovery continues every 30 minutes when enabled. Without prior successful discovery, acquisition MUST NOT guess references. A later usable catalog confirming absence ends reuse under FR-005. Prior references MUST NOT bypass pauses, deactivated divisions, closed seasons or unavailable reliable configuration. Discovery failures remain visible even when calendar requests succeed; no new validity duration or substitution with another season is required.
- **FR-007**: New seasons MUST be detected automatically but activated and closed only by an authorized operator. Several seasons MAY be active simultaneously. Only active seasons participate in normal periodic collection. Season closure follows FR-055 and affects that season’s FFVB and LNV scopes only, never club sheets, geocoding or other seasons. Activating the next season does not close its predecessor or impose an arbitrary simultaneous-season limit.
- **FR-008**: During normal current collection, calendars MUST be downloaded and sporting creations requested only if permitted by a discovered source reference authorized under FR-005–FR-006, an active season, effective classification sufficient for acquisition, an enabled division and covered scope. Controlled historical acquisition follows FR-047. Before effective mapping, acquisition MUST be limited to discovery of pools and native packs, without collecting their calendars or results. Effective classification MUST identify division, format and gender; unclassified packs may record a need but MUST NOT create assumed sporting resources or a reserve of pending calendars. Already published classification retained by F02 MUST NOT replace missing current acquisition settings. Restored settings must be accepted under F02 correction rules before authorizing collection. F02 defines division and classification semantics.
- **FR-009**: Native FFVB packs MUST remain acquisition groups with pack classification and pool-level classification overrides. A fully individually classified pool MAY be collected even if its pack is unclassified. [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md) defines complete overrides, division activation and correction semantics.
- **FR-010**: Withdrawn: manual pack and pool exclusions and their precedence are removed. Fixed coverage limits, effective classification, active seasons/divisions and independently scoped pauses remain. Public visibility of a pool does not govern its discovery or otherwise eligible collection.

<a id="reliability-and-observation-handoff"></a>

#### Reliability and observation handoff

- **FR-011**: Acquisition MUST distinguish complete nonempty, validly empty, partial, invalid and unavailable observations. A partial observation means only part of the requested match scope was reliably obtained; it MUST identify discarded matches or missing parts instead of claiming completeness. Usable matches from a partial calendar remain available for integration, but partial evidence MUST NOT authorize absence-based withdrawal. Isolated invalidity of a secondary field does not necessarily invalidate established match presence.
- **FR-012**: An overall unreadable calendar structure or incorrect overall season or pool context MUST lead to rejection of the affected calendar while retaining previously accepted data. A locally unusable match or participant reference MUST instead isolate that match, provide the reason and preserve other usable matches. A calendar with an unresolved part of its match scope is incomplete for absence-based reconciliation; rejected rows MUST NOT implicitly be treated as absent sporting resources.
- **FR-013**: Contradictory rows for the same native reference within the same source and calendar context MUST isolate that match without arbitrary version selection, preserving other usable matches and reporting incomplete evidence for absence-based reconciliation. Identical repetition is not a contradiction. This rule defines neither universal match identity, code reuse across seasons nor provider priorities.
- **FR-014**: A malformed secondary field MUST be isolated and reported when the rest of the observation is usable. Its invalidity MUST NOT be represented as an explicitly empty value or a request to erase previously accepted data. Missing and invalid fields MUST remain distinct during handoff.
- **FR-015**: Normally absent values, including unpublished results or unconfirmed dates or times, MUST remain unknown. Acquisition MUST NOT invent statistics, scores, dates, times or coordinates. Recognized slots for future qualifiers or byes MUST retain source and phase context without being mistaken for malformed data or actual participants; they MUST NOT cause rejection of other identifiable matches. F02 defines when a match has sufficient identity to be viewable.
- **FR-016**: Sporting notations and special source values MUST be retained as evidence for F02 interpretation instead of automatically converted into ordinary results. Structural validity MUST NOT be confused with a business decision on forfeits, postponements, exceptional sets or sentinel values in official standings. Acquisition MUST NOT calculate replacement official standings.
- **FR-017**: Official standings that are unreadable overall or impossible to attach to their context MUST be rejected independently of the calendar. In an otherwise usable table, invalid or absent statistics MUST remain distinct from known values and MUST NOT become invented zeros. Official ordering, indicated positions, labels and special values MUST remain available. Inability to link a valid source row to a Blockout team belongs to F02 and does not justify discarding the acquired official table.
- **FR-018**: A failure MUST be isolated to the affected dependent scope. Pools, club sheets and independent FFVB/LNV calendar, standings and enrichment observations MUST remain separately usable. A failure MUST NOT make independent successes appear as failures, nor independent successes as evidence of recovery of the failing scope.
- **FR-019**: Rejected, partial, unavailable or stopped acquisition MUST NOT establish sporting absence. Repeated HTTP errors for a catalog-listed pool authorize no withdrawal. Send qualified catalog absence separately from a complete calendar observation. F02 decides visibility only after successful integration; discovery failure is not confirmed catalog absence.
- **FR-020**: One complete, valid, wholly empty calendar attributable to the correct current season and pool or phase, successfully integrated, MUST hide the pool, its matches and participations under F02 without erasing identities. No second confirmation or scheduled-only counter is required. A manual current collection follows the same qualification. All rejected rows, error pages, partial downloads and wrong context are not true empty observations. A still-referenced hidden pool remains eligible for collection. Closed seasons accept no new collection; already running cycles may finish once under FR-055. Wholly empty initial historical reconstruction preserves retained history.
- **FR-021**: Functional observation handoff MUST contain the source and its reference, expected season, competition, pack, pool and phase context where available, acquisition time, provider publication time if supplied, obtained scope, outcome and completeness, native identifiers, relevant observed values, classification context and actionable exclusion or rejection reasons. Any unknown context MUST be explicit; context MUST NOT be invented. Relevant values include participant references and names, dates and times, results and sets, venues and referees; official standings; professional phase and live-stream references; club details and geocoding results. This is an information requirement, not a requirement for a public API or raw-data exposure.
- **FR-022**: Operators MUST distinguish validated acquisition from subsequent sporting integration, including successful match updates while other integrations remain failed or unconfirmed. Neither acquisition success nor partial integration success may be presented as complete synchronization of application data. F02 defines integration consistency and absence-reconciliation conditions; F13 defines shared operational visibility.
- **FR-023**: Diagnostics MUST expose source, scope, time, outcome, available counts and failure reasons without personal contact details, secret identifiers or automatic raw CSV or HTML archives. Useful normalized observations are distinct from raw-document archives. Diagnostic retention and delivery mechanisms belong to F11/F13; this specification chooses neither duration nor monitoring architecture.

<a id="clubs-and-geocoding"></a>

#### Clubs and geocoding

- **FR-024**: Club-sheet acquisition MUST target clubs encountered in covered competitions, with a first attempt on discovery then collection every 24 hours. It MUST NOT independently browse the entire FFVB club directory. Club sheets describe the currently available club information independently of sporting seasons; geocoding likewise follows the current effective locality, not a selected historical season.
- **FR-025**: Failure to read a club sheet MUST remain distinct from club disappearance and MUST NOT trigger deactivation or erasure of prior information still authorized under F11. Available observations of name, address, municipality, postal code, contact and website MUST preserve distinctions between unknown and invalid fields; F11 governs authorized collection and retention.
- **FR-026**: A new geocoding need or one tied to a locality change passed by F02 MUST receive a collection attempt within one hour when collection is enabled and the provider available. F02 defines municipal and postal context and coordinate changes; a street-only change does not constitute a new municipal-geocoding need; acquisition MUST NOT invent coordinates. The passed context is the effective F02 locality after manual corrections, intentional absences and restrictions; a source change that does not change this locality creates no new need.
- **FR-027**: A correct response without a usable geocoding candidate or with ambiguous candidates MUST be distinguishable from a technical failure. Ordinary collection MUST NOT repeatedly query that same unchanged unresolved address. A change in geocoding locality context, defined by F02, makes it eligible again.
- **FR-028**: An explicit manual rerun of the geocoding family MUST retry still-unresolved addresses, including unchanged addresses previously receiving an empty or ambiguous response, while respecting normal permissions and pauses. It MUST NOT recalculate valid results merely because the family was rerun.
- **FR-029**: Geocoding MUST pass address context and a success, no-candidate, ambiguity or technical-failure outcome to F02. A failed request MUST NOT become a definitive no-result observation or an instruction to erase accepted coordinates. A technical failure follows FR-037/FR-042, not FR-027 suppression of retries for an unchanged address.

<a id="cadence-and-controls"></a>

#### Cadence and controls

- **FR-030**: Eligible calendars and standings MUST follow 30-minute scheduling, or five minutes when their pool/phase has a reliable match start within H−1 to H+4. Each LNV match detail MUST independently follow five minutes only inside its own reliable window and 30 minutes otherwise, including after a final result. Unknown times create no fast window; missing results do not extend it. Corrected reliable times affect subsequent scheduling. A field not reread MUST NOT receive a new observation time; incompatible older sets must not survive a changed result as valid detail.
- **FR-031**: Discovery MUST run every 30 minutes when enabled, even if no pool is currently eligible. This cadence and those in FR-030 define scheduled times, not guarantees of provider publication or display freshness in the application. Overruns, pauses and configuration unavailability follow FR-033–FR-035; unavailable sources remain visible under incident rules.
- **FR-032**: FFVB and LNV competition controls MUST be independent for each season. Club sheets and geocoding each retain an independent seasonless control. Pausing one season/provider does not pause another season, its other provider or either club control. These are functional controls, not separate services. Shared provider access limits still apply. F12 access maintenance changes neither pause nor resumption state.
- **FR-033**: Pausing MUST allow the current cycle of the selected control scope to finish and prevent its next cycle. A scope without a running cycle becomes paused immediately. Resumption restores eligibility without replaying missed ticks. Other scopes keep their own activation and pause states.
- **FR-034**: A cycle MUST use reliable configuration selected at its start. Classification, division-activation and season changes MUST apply to acquisition from the next cycle. Without reliable configuration at startup, collection MUST NOT use remembered settings. Previously discovered references under FR-006 do not authorize this use of old configuration. Completing acquisition already in progress does not authorize F02 integration to undo current visibility or presentation rules.
- **FR-035**: Normal scheduling MUST prevent concurrent collection of the same target, skip missed ticks after an overrun and resume at the next eligible scheduled opportunity without catch-up. After a collector crash, preserve accepted integration and use a new ordinary collection; fine-grained resumption, custom crash supervision and automatic repair of a persistent bug are not required. Exceptional bounded overlapping reads may occur during failure but must respect supplier limits and cannot justify stale or duplicate business data. Monitoring exposes the interruption for manual correction. Eligible collection before closure can recover only data still supplied; it never recreates user corrections or irreplaceable logos.
- **FR-036**: A normal manual rerun MUST target the selected season/provider for competitions, or the selected seasonless club/geocoding control. Individual pool or club rerun commands are not required. An existing execution is returned without duplicate work; otherwise paused or pausing scopes reject relaunch until explicitly resumed. Eligibility and permissions remain required. Closed seasons reject all new collection requests under FR-055.
- **FR-037**: Provider reads MUST share bounded active and pending work and configurable conservative access limits per provider. Reuse connections and common downloads; use gzip or HTTP validators only where qualified. Allow at most two additional attempts for transient read failures, honoring provider limits and Retry-After; do not retry parsing errors or automatically replay an entire integration pipeline. Exhaustion produces explicit failure, never fabricated success or emptiness.
- **FR-038**: Pausing, resuming, rerunning, season commands and acquisition-setting changes MUST require explicit operational permissions. Ordinary users and live-stream moderators without these permissions MUST be refused these actions. F12 defines assignment and shared authorization rules; F02 defines administrative semantics for classification mappings.

<a id="incidents-and-history"></a>

#### Incidents and history

- **FR-039**: Operators MUST be able to distinguish: never collected, paused, in progress, successfully observed, degraded or failed acquisition, failed or unconfirmed integration. These distinctions are mandatory without imposing a state machine or screen layout. The last observation and its reasons MUST remain understandable even while work is paused. The mobile collection dashboard MUST separate scheduling activation, confirmed execution and observed outcome. It exposes pause, resume and relaunch for the selected season/provider or seasonless club control, last attempt, last success or its absence, and available partial-result counts and affected scope. Expandable diagnostics may expose a filtered technical trace when available, subject to FR-023; missing diagnostics or measurements MUST remain explicit. Refreshing the dashboard MUST only read state, not trigger collection. Exceptional post-launch source repair, sporting data correction and a complete execution-history explorer are outside this dashboard. Supported pre-launch source preparation follows FR-050–FR-052; definitive closure follows FR-055. Competition controls are scoped to the selected season and provider. Classification is managed separately in F02 mapping administration, not displayed as a season/source readiness state or linked from collection screens.
- **FR-040**: A structural acquisition anomaly or season/context inconsistency MUST immediately open or update an actionable incident for the affected scope, without a consecutive-failure counter.
- **FR-041**: The first failed competition-collection cycle for an affected scope MUST open an incident. Repeated failures update the same incident rather than generating repeated openings; HTTP attempts within one cycle are not separate incidents. A confirmed manual-cycle failure follows the same policy. Unexecuted work is not an observed failure or recovery. Valid empty calendars are not technical failures. Recovery follows FR-043.
- **FR-042**: A technical club-sheet or geocoding failure MUST be retried before 24 hours and trigger an incident if the technical outage persists for 24 hours. This duration is measured from the first failed observation, not the usual daily refresh deadline. An operator pause prevents attempts without inventing recovery; no provider outage is inferred merely because work was intentionally not attempted. Valid empty or ambiguous geocoding responses are not outages.
- **FR-043**: Recovery MUST require a new usable observation of the affected scope demonstrating that the reported problem has ended. A pause, closure, success in another scope or successful acquisition while an integration failure remains unresolved MUST NOT announce recovery of that incident. A successful manual observation MAY establish actual recovery; it does not artificially create missing scheduled failures.
- **FR-044**: Each incident MUST remain visible and produce an opening notification and an actual-recovery notification, without periodic reminders. Repeated failures MUST NOT create duplicate logical opening transitions. Exceptional transport duplicates after a lost response follow the accepted F13 delivery limits; no intentional repeat or reminder is allowed. Different problems remain distinct; the end of a network outage does not hide a newly observed content anomaly. Delivery and grouping mechanisms belong to F13.
- **FR-045**: Newly detected seasons and classification needs MUST be grouped into actionable operator reports rather than one notification per item. A voluntary pause and ordinary waiting for classification MUST NOT be described as provider outages.
- **FR-046**: Initial V2 reconstruction MUST target the current season and three completed seasons, according to available source data, through a controlled operation using prepared and verified historical references. It MUST NOT require a generic product that automatically discovers all archives.
- **FR-047**: Initial historical reconstruction MUST use prepared and verified references while the historical season is PREPARING, before definitive closure. Neither periodic activation nor presence in today’s catalog is required. Classification, enabled divisions, coverage, permission, reliable configuration and source validation still apply. Use ordinary adapters and F02 integration, with one explicitly requested passage and a report; retry only an identified failure before closure. No separate reconstruction engine, automatic archive crawler or pause of other seasons is required. Unavailable, unreadable, wrong-season or wholly empty historical input preserves retained data; no current feed may fill a historical gap. Complete nonempty authoritative input may reconcile individual facts before closure. F14 owns transition authorization and F07 suppresses initial-import announcements.
- **FR-048**: **Withdrawn on 2026-10-07.** The former mobile closed-season recollection capability and its global family pause/resume procedure are removed. This identifier is retained for traceability only; FR-055 defines terminal closure.
- **FR-049**: Reconstruction MUST report requested coverage, obtained observations, rejected data and unavailable sources by season and source scope. Sampling a few available archives MUST NOT be presented as exhaustive reconstruction or evidence of migration readiness. F14 defines resulting launch, launch-refusal and recovery criteria; F02 defines reintegration consequences.

- **FR-050**: Season preparation MUST show discovered sources, missing or unqualified source coverage and seasonal evidence. Source launch and pool discovery MUST be independent of pool mapping; a qualified covered source can launch even when all its pools are unmapped. Collection screens MUST NOT expose classification readiness or navigation to the mapping journey. Pool mapping remains a separate F02 responsibility; operator-selected divisions are independent of organizer codes and classifications MUST NOT automatically carry over by source name or code. Launch locks qualified sources without creating or changing mappings and makes no claim that all providers have published a complete season. Source launch is distinct from pool-calendar acquisition eligibility under FR-008.
- **FR-051**: Launch MUST lock the affected season/source bindings. Pause and closure MUST NOT unlock them. Initial reconstruction locks its validated references before its first passage; closing without launch grants no editing right. Exceptional later repair is a controlled technical intervention; no administrative reassignment, reset or generic scraping editor is required. Discovery and qualification of additional child pools/phases beneath the bound sources continue normally.
- **FR-052**: LNV discovery MUST retain a proposed reference separately from its accepted season/championship binding. A provider ID is not a year. Missing historical references may be supplied in supported official formats before launch; a reachable URL alone is not seasonal evidence. Old active bindings survive current-entry rollover. Explicit human confirmation supplies only missing season attribution, never proof of parser compatibility or complete phase coverage.
- **FR-053**: Reused FFVB pool codes for distinct round groups MUST produce separate contextual pools with their own participants, calendar and standings, using published organizer/season/grouping/round/pool context. Ordinary matchdays MUST NOT create pools. Partition a shared full CSV only after demonstrating the mapping for that source format; `Jo` and match-code prefixes are not universal pool or round identifiers. Unattributable scope remains unqualified and cannot justify absence reconciliation.
- **FR-054**: Acquisition MUST preserve FFVB club references as strings including leading zeros. Shared club numbers for distinct named teams are normal; `equipe=` and seasonal LNV TeamID are not global club/team identities. Follow F02 for immutable club identity and seasonal teams/pools, with no inferred interseason continuity, promotion or follow transfer.
- **FR-055**: Closing a season MUST be definitive and require an authorized operator to type its exact displayed label, checked before acceptance. The confirmation states that all FFVB/LNV collection for this season stops, history remains and there is no reopening or recollection. Check the current revision atomically; refuse incorrect, stale or unauthorized requests without effects. Acceptance prevents any new cycle, source validation or queued work from starting; only a cycle already RUNNING at acceptance may complete its selected work once, subject to existing order and visibility guards. No new operation may use a retained reference to bypass closure. Reads remain available, source locks and data are retained, and other seasons or club controls remain unchanged. After an uncertain response, read current state; never repeat automatically. No reopening UI, command, technical recovery workflow or bypass is introduced.

<a id="key-entities-include-if-feature-involves-data"></a>

### Key entities _(include if the feature involves data)_

- **Source reference**: Provider-specific location and native identifiers with season and competition context; neither a selected business primary key nor a permanent URL.
- **Discovery scope / catalog**: An official grouping whose successful interpretation establishes discoverable child references. Its boundaries determine which discovery failed and where confirmed absence can be established; references already successfully discovered for the same season remain subject to current eligibility.
- **Source pack / pool classification**: A native grouping of pools and effective classification by division, format and gender used for acquisition eligibility. It does not replace the durable Pool or Division concepts defined by F02.
- **Observation**: Evidence obtained for a source and instant, with scope, completeness, relevant values and reasons. It is distinct from accepted business state and downstream publication.
- **Collection family / cycle**: An independently controlled family of acquisition work and a bounded set of work selected at cycle start. No process, message queue or deployment mode is imposed.
- **Geocoding need**: A request concerning locality context, passed by F02, and its result; the acquisition domain does not own durable Club coordinates.
- **Acquisition incident**: A bounded problem requiring operator attention, with evidence establishing its opening and recovery. This concept does not impose a separate incident-management product.

### Measurable outcomes

- **SC-001**: In A01–A07, eligible current references or authorized last-known references are included in calendar acquisition, while out-of-coverage or unclassified references and those attached to a deactivated division or inactive season are omitted from calendar acquisition, with zero acquisition-based deletion requests. This pool-level rule does not prevent qualified source launch or discovery under FR-050/A40. Discovery failure never creates a reference or falsely confirms absence.
- **SC-002**: A08–A15 reject wrong context and overall unusable observations, isolate local anomalies and preserve 99 accepted matches when one fails, without absence reconciliation. One wholly empty qualified current observation hides its pool, matches and participations; false empty observations and refused closed-season requests do not. (FR-011–FR-020)
- **SC-003**: A16–A18 demonstrate discovery of the three professional championships and supplied phase examples, including when seasonal references change; no sampled fixed identifier is assumed to remain valid in another season. Actual future-phase availability remains a provider dependency.
- **SC-004**: When collection is enabled, the provider available, configuration reliable and execution does not overrun, A19–A24 respect the 30-minute discovery and baseline cadence, five-minute fast cadence, daily club refresh and one-hour deadline for a geocoding attempt. Deviations due to failures or overruns are distinguished from successful timely acquisition.
- **SC-005**: In A25–A28, every pause blocks the next cycle, every unauthorized command is refused and repeated requests produce zero duplicate normal executions and zero accumulation of missed cycles; FR-035 exceptional overlaps produce zero duplicate business effects or stale writes.
- **SC-006**: A29–A32 open a grouped incident on the first failed competition cycle and announce recovery only from positive evidence. Repetition, pauses and unrelated successes produce no false recovery. Club/geocoding failures retain their 24-hour policy; valid empty calendars create no technical incident. (FR-039–FR-045)
- **SC-007**: A21–A22 distinguish the four geocoding outcomes and produce zero repeated automatic requests for unchanged addresses that already received an empty or ambiguous result, while retaining the option of an explicit family rerun.
- **SC-008**: A33–A36 distinguish obtained, rejected and unavailable initial reconstruction coverage, without raw-document archives or fabricated completeness. A35 and A44 reject incorrect/stale/unauthorized confirmation without effects, preserve existing history after closure and reject every new cycle for the closed season. Already running work may finish once; unrelated season/provider and club controls remain unchanged. A36 preserves accepted data when an archive is inaccessible, unreadable or belongs to another season.
- **SC-009**: A37 exposes zero season settings for club sheets or geocoding, zero collection executions caused by refresh, zero normal relaunches started while paused, and zero success claims from absent observations or unconfirmed integration. Technical traces contain no secrets, personal data or raw provider documents.

<a id="assumptions"></a>

## Assumptions

- The current application remains the functional starting reference, not the implementation architecture to reproduce. Accepted scope intentionally changes V1 behaviors described below; speculative social-network, fantasy-game, player-roster or additional-provider features are excluded.
- Official FFVB/LNV sources remain external dependencies and may change or omit data. A recognized catalog may be empty; recognizing its HTML structure does not prove the provider published everything it intended. Provenance and bounded outcomes make these limits explicit.
- Time-window examples use reliable start instants supplied by F02. Source time interpretation and user-facing time-zone/day semantics belong to F02/F03; no new midnight or default-time convention is chosen here.
- Cadence denotes scheduled times, not a measured delay after an execution ends. Pauses require separate explicit resumption; a manual rerun does not bypass eligibility. Normal duplicate prevention and operator control follow FR-035, including its explicitly accepted exceptional-overlap rule.
- The authorized reset concerns business-data reconstruction only. [identity continuity requirements](../005-accounts-identity/spec.md#v1-evidence-and-limitations), [Pro continuity evidence](../006-pro-subscriptions/spec.md#v1-evidence-and-limitations) and [transition requirements](../014-v1-v2-transition/spec.md) continues to constrain F14: this specification authorizes no deletion of Auth0 accounts or valid paid access rights.
- Completing this document constitutes neither acceptance of the entire functional corpus, evidence of provider or software acceptance tests, nor authorization to start technical planning. [#247](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout-legacy/issues/247) remains the complete-corpus acceptance milestone.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-05

- Q: Does an interrupted collector require fine-grained recovery or a four-hour acquired-data loss guarantee? → A: No. Fix persistent errors using monitoring and manual intervention, then let ordinary collection refresh what suppliers still publish. Preserve accepted data and ordinary source limits; do not build custom crash supervision or download resumption.

- Q: What happens after a collector crashes? → A: Preserve accepted integration and collect again at the next eligible scheduled opportunity; do not require detailed automatic resumption of the interrupted download. Temporary freshness degradation is acceptable.
- Q: Must exceptional failures prevent every overlapping download? → A: Prevent normal duplicates, but accept bounded exceptional overlap while preserving supplier limits and prohibiting duplicate business effects or stale writes. Stop new calls when lost authority is detected.

### Session 2026-09-29

- Q: How are seasons operated? → A: A current season with an overlapping transition, explicitly activated and closed; competition seasons never scope club sheets or geocoding. (FR-007, FR-024–FR-026)
- Q: Who maintains changing source links? → A: Supported official structures are discovered automatically; unrecognized structures require technical collector maintenance, with supported pre-launch source preparation but no generic editor or post-launch reassignment. (FR-003, FR-050–FR-052)
- Q: What belongs in the mobile dashboard? → A: Compact family controls and latest outcomes, with expandable filtered diagnostics; repairs remain on PC. (FR-023, FR-039)
- Q: What can be relaunched? → A: One season/provider for competitions, or one seasonless club/geocoding control, without duplicate cycles; normal relaunch requires explicit resume when paused. (FR-028, FR-036)
- Q: Can a closed season be collected again? → A: No. Closure is definitive after exact typed confirmation. Initial historical reconstruction happens before closure; no reopening command or recovery workflow is part of V2. (FR-047, withdrawn FR-048, FR-055)

<a id="session-2026-09-16"></a>

### Session 2026-09-16

These are clarifications accepted by the product owner and incorporated into requirements, not unanswered questions or a second execution process.

- Q: Does catalog absence require repeated confirmations? → A: No; discoverable presence determines current collection. A usable catalog omitting a pool stops its collection immediately without deleting business data. (FR-005, FR-019)
- Q: Does discovery failure allow reuse of previous references? → A: Yes, references already successfully discovered for the same season remain usable under current eligibility during retries; confirmed absence always stops collection from that source. (FR-005–FR-006)
- Q: Can a hidden pool still be collected? → A: Yes, when its source reference and ordinary acquisition settings remain eligible. Manual pack/pool exclusions are withdrawn. (FR-008–FR-010)
- Q: What is the rejection boundary for unreliable input? → A: Reject overall unusable calendar structure or context, isolate local matches including contradictory rows for one reference, and retain usable secondary fields. Incomplete calendars never justify absence-based withdrawals. (FR-011–FR-014, FR-019)
- Q: What level of historical tooling is needed? → A: Initial current-plus-three reconstruction using prepared references, ordinary adapters and an actual coverage report, before historical seasons are definitively closed. (FR-046–FR-049, FR-055)
- Q: How can geocoding and rerunning remain simple? → A: Attempt new needs within one hour; rerun only by family, including unchanged unresolved addresses without recalculating valid results. (FR-026–FR-028, FR-036)
- Q: What happens after an overrun? → A: Finish, skip missed scheduled runs and resume at the next scheduled opportunity without immediate catch-up. Exceptional failure overlap and crash recovery follow the 2026-10-05 clarification. (FR-035)
- Q: Should persistent incidents send reminders? → A: Notify only on opening and actual recovery; retries are not separate scheduled cycles and a pause is not recovery. (FR-041–FR-044)
