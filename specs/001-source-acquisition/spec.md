# Feature Specification: Source acquisition and observation reliability

**Feature Branch**: `develop`

**Created**: 2026-09-15

**Status**: Accepted

**Input**: Scope approved by the product owner: specify acquisition of FFVB/LNV competitions, club sheets, geocoding, reliable observations and operational commands; retain FFVB discovery and CSV exports, with explicit V2 changes and without technical implementation.

<a id="user-scenarios--testing-mandatory"></a>

## User Scenarios & Testing

Design input: [source-operation approval](plan.md#design-acceptance), the approved prototype, and [administration designs](https://www.figma.com/design/rKu4xc8eJsx0f0Vu4E6U03/?node-id=1170-3654). The approval covers its recorded source/season and independent-pool scope. The scoped prototype and Figma approvals are linked in the [plan](plan.md#design-acceptance); native qualification remains separate.

The main actor is an operator with explicit collection permissions. Visitors and signed-in users benefit from reliable sporting data but cannot control collection. Live-stream moderator status grants no collection permission. The sporting-data domain consumes observations; its decisions belong to F02 and do not constitute a second end-user role.

The scenario identifiers below are local acceptance references, not implementation-task identifiers. Independent validations describe acceptance evidence to obtain; they do not claim that executable V2 exists.

<a id="user-story-1---collect-only-eligible-official-competitions-priority-p1"></a>

### User Story 1 — Collect only eligible official competitions (Priority: P1)

As an operator, I want current official coverage to determine what is collected so obsolete references or assumed classifications do not drive acquisition.

**Why this priority**: Source eligibility determines the reliability and scope of all subsequently obtained sporting data.

**Independent Test**: Supply representative catalogs and operational settings, then compare the collection-eligible set and exclusion reasons without requiring sporting integration.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a supported readable catalog and an active season, **when** a classified pool within coverage is discovered, **then** its calendar becomes eligible; each of the five excluded league codes remains outside scope. (FR-001, FR-004, FR-008)
2. **A02** — **Given** a previously discovered pool, **when** a complete authoritative catalog for the same season and scope no longer lists it, **then** collection through its old reference stops without waiting for another cycle or deleting business data. **When** authoritative evidence lists it again, **then** its identity and mapping are reused, and current eligibility and independent visibility rules apply. (FR-005, FR-019; F02 FR-037, FR-042)
3. **A03** — **Given** a complete catalog previously accepted for a source and season, **when** its next read is incomplete or unavailable, **then** its known entries, classifications and visibility are preserved. Sources shows the latest outcome. The detail shows its read context, available factual reason and previous complete read, and explains that the earlier catalog is still shown. Active, otherwise eligible sporting collection may continue through retained references. Without a previously qualified read, no child reference is guessed. (FR-004, FR-006, FR-018, FR-050)
4. **A04** — **Given** successful discovery, **when** an intermediate five-minute collection becomes due, **then** its same-season references may be reused under current eligibility, including after discovery failure. **When** a later usable catalog confirms absence, **then** the absent source reference ceases to be eligible, even if its old URL responds. (FR-005, FR-006, FR-031)
5. **A05** — **Given** a newly detected season, **when** no operator has activated it, **then** the operator can consult the discovered Sources while periodic calendar and standings collection remains ineligible. **When** the operator confirms activation from the selected season, **then** acknowledgement produces one shared green notice and ordinary sporting collection may start for effectively eligible pools, including while another season remains active. **When** the season is closed, **then** its periodic acquisition stops without deleting history. (FR-007, FR-008, FR-031, FR-050–FR-051, FR-055)
6. **A06** — **Given** a pack awaiting classification, **when** its pools also have no effective classification, **then** discovery still records the native pack and its pool references, but its calendars and results are not imported and no sporting creation is requested. A shared official page or export may be read for discovery or another eligible pool; out-of-scope rows are discarded without retaining a pending calendar. **When** a pool receives a complete individual classification, **then** only that eligible pool may be collected. Removing that classification without a replacement supplied by the pack stops collection from the next cycle, even if F02 retains already published classification and visibility; a refused restoration does not restart collection. Deactivating a division suspends dependent collection from the next cycle; reactivation reassesses eligibility without lifting independent visibility restrictions. (FR-008, FR-009, FR-034)
7. **A07** — **Given** a hidden but still catalog-listed pool with active season/division and effective classification, **when** collection runs, **then** its calendar remains eligible. No manual pack/pool exclusion control exists; selected season/provider pauses and fixed coverage boundaries still apply. (FR-008–FR-010, FR-034)

<a id="user-story-2---keep-unreliable-input-from-becoming-sporting-truth-priority-p1"></a>

### User Story 2 — Keep unreliable input from becoming sporting truth (Priority: P1)

As a Blockout data consumer, I want collection failures and ambiguous source data to remain distinct from actual sporting changes.

**Why this priority**: A successful download is insufficient to establish reliable results or disappearance decisions.

**Independent Test**: Present complete, empty, malformed and contradictory observations, then examine their acceptance, reasons and functional transmission independently of storage or display.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A08** — **Given** a 100-match calendar with one unusable match, with usable overall structure and context, **when** it is acquired, **then** the other 99 matches remain usable, the affected match is isolated with reasons and the observation is incomplete for absence-based reconciliation. If overall structure or context is unreadable, the calendar is instead rejected; independent pools continue to be processed. (FR-011, FR-012, FR-018)
2. **A09** — **Given** a source calendar and its context, **when** two rows contradict each other for the same source reference, **then** the match is isolated without choosing a version and other usable matches are retained; this incomplete observation cannot justify absence-based withdrawal. Identical repetition, reuse in another season and disagreement between providers are distinct cases. (FR-013, FR-019)
3. **A10** — **Given** an otherwise usable record, **when** a secondary field such as a club website is malformed, **then** that field is flagged and isolated without rejecting the whole record or requesting erasure of its previous value. (FR-014) An unavailable or invalid LNV logo likewise leaves usable match acquisition/integration independent; retrieval occurs once per distinct team during the ordinary calendar passage. (FR-018, FR-030)
4. **A11** — **Given** a match whose result is unpublished, whose start time is unknown or whose future participant is recognized as still to qualify, **when** it is acquired, **then** normally absent values remain unknown and context for the future slot is retained without inventing teams or dates or rejecting other identifiable matches. Special sporting notations are retained for F02 interpretation without conversion to zero. A published FFVB information sheet can be observed before any result; a published FFVB scoresheet is attributed to its exact match independently of the observed result. (FR-001, FR-015, FR-016)
5. **A12** — **Given** a valid calendar, **when** its official standings are acquired, **then** a usable FFVB or LNV table retains every row in published sequence, including when no rank is printed or an individual statistic is absent or invalid. A printed rank remains a separate source value. A table that is unreadable overall or cannot be attached to its context is independently rejected. A missing Blockout team association belongs to F02 and does not invalidate acquisition. (FR-017, FR-018)
6. **A13** — **Given** a calendar attributable to the correct pool and season, **when** empty observations are obtained, **then** they are distinguished from failures and handled according to the following cases; F02 owns sporting consequences. (FR-011, FR-019, FR-020, FR-047, FR-055)

- **Qualified current empty**: One complete, correctly contextualized and successfully integrated empty observation hides the pool, matches and participations without erasing identities. Its catalog reference remains traversed.
- **Closed history**: New observations for a closed season are refused; only its already running cycle may finish once. Retained history and independent restrictions remain.
- **False empty**: HTTP 200, header-only content without qualified context, all rejected rows, errors and partial integration authorize no absence-based hiding.
- **Reappearance**: A valid attributable return reuses known identities without a second confirmation; F02 preserves other hiding reasons.
- **Normal observation**: Valid empty results produce no collection-failure incident.

7. **A14** — **Given** an observation requested for a season, **when** supplied content clearly belongs to another season, **then** it is rejected and context inconsistency is reported immediately; current-season content cannot fill a historical gap. (FR-012, FR-040, FR-047)
8. **A15** — **Given** validated acquisition, **when** some subsequent sporting integrations fail, **then** operators can distinguish successful acquisition, successful updates and remaining failures; the observation is not presented as fully integrated. (FR-021, FR-022, FR-043)

<a id="user-story-3---obtain-all-covered-professional-phases-priority-p1"></a>

### User Story 3 — Obtain all covered professional phases (Priority: P1)

As a user following professional volleyball, I want acquisition to cover every official phase of the three supported championships beyond regular seasons alone.

**Why this priority**: The product owner identifies incomplete professional coverage and late V1 enrichment as significant limitations.

**Independent Test**: Use official entry pages and representative regular-season, playoff and final-phase references with changing season identifiers, then examine discovered coverage and observations attributed separately to their sources.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A16** — **Given** the three official professional-championship entry points, **when** the season is still preparing and its published pages establish the correct championship, season and a supported phase structure, **then** its phases appear in the catalog. When a later complete read attests a new phase for that scope, it appears automatically. A reused or changed provider identifier alone establishes neither a phase nor a season. (FR-002–FR-005, FR-050, FR-052)
2. **A17** — **Given** a published LNV professional phase, **when** its grouped calendar and match pages are collected, **then** page facts retain their context and match detail cadence. Missing coverage remains unqualified; neither XML nor FFVB supplies their sporting facts. Qualified document references follow the narrow exception in FR-002. FFVB remains the source for other covered competitions. (FR-002, FR-003, FR-018, FR-021)
3. **A18** — **Given** an unsupported official-page structure or another championship or season at an expected reference, **when** discovery reads it, **then** it reports the factual problem for the affected source. No phase, absence or interseason association can be established from that observation. Unsupported structures require collector maintenance. (FR-003–FR-006, FR-040, FR-052)

<a id="user-story-4---enrich-covered-clubs-without-repeated-useless-requests-priority-p1"></a>

### User Story 4 — Enrich covered clubs without repeated useless requests (Priority: P2)

As a user viewing a club or map, I want club information and coordinate observations refreshed without equating provider failure with disappearance.

**Why this priority**: Club enrichment follows reliable competition collection. It remains in V2; missing secondary details do not prevent sporting consultation.

**Independent Test**: Supply covered-club references, changed addresses and geocoding results; examine requested work and observations without deciding final coordinate publication.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A19** — **Given** a newly encountered covered club, **when** its sheet is first collected then refreshed, **then** the first attempt follows discovery and subsequent attempts respect a 24-hour cadence; unrelated clubs in the directory are not added. Failure of another club’s page neither deactivates this club nor discards its result. (FR-024, FR-025)
2. **A20** — **Given** a new need for coordinates or an effective address, municipality or postal-code change passed by F02 after correction, intentional absence, restriction or return to the source, **when** collection is enabled and the provider available, **then** a geocoding attempt occurs within one hour using the current permitted context. It first seeks the full address when present. An absent address or a qualified empty or ambiguous address response leads to municipality lookup; a technical failure does not. Without a permitted municipality, no provider request occurs. The final result and unchanged context revision pass to F02. (FR-026, FR-029)
3. **A21** — **Given** the full-address and municipality fallback rules have produced a located point or a final qualified empty or ambiguous result, **when** the effective context remains unchanged, **then** ordinary collection does not query it again. **When** F02 changes that context, a new attempt becomes possible. An operator rerun retries final unresolved contexts only; it does not recalculate a valid address or municipality-fallback point. (FR-027, FR-028)
4. **A22** — **Given** a technical club-sheet or geocoding failure, **when** no operator pause prevents work, **then** it remains distinct from a valid empty or ambiguous response, is retried at the next ordinary eligible passage of its collection family and is actionable from its first failed observation. (FR-029, FR-037, FR-042)

<a id="user-story-5---control-collection-and-cadence-predictably-priority-p1"></a>

### User Story 5 — Control collection and cadence predictably (Priority: P1)

As an operator, I want to pause and rerun collection without duplicate executions, hidden fallback configuration or accumulated missed work to catch up.

**Why this priority**: Predictable commands limit provider traffic and allow safe maintenance.

**Independent Test**: Observe scheduled times and operational-command outcomes in a simulated calendar with fast windows, overruns, configuration changes and refused commands.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A23** — **Given** an eligible pool, **when** no reliable match start places it in a fast window, **then** competition collection follows 30-minute scheduled intervals. From one hour before to four hours after a reliable match start, it follows five-minute scheduled intervals regardless of weekday. Absence of a result after the window does not extend it; an unknown match start creates no window. (FR-030, FR-031)
2. **A24** — **Given** a five-minute collection target, **when** a normal execution overruns, **then** missed ticks are skipped without normal overlap or catch-up. **When** the collector crashes, **then** accepted integration survives and a new eligible scheduled cycle may collect again. Exceptional bounded overlapping downloads during failure never duplicate business effects or overwrite newer data; interruption remains visible. (FR-035)
3. **A25** — **Given** a season/provider competition cycle in progress, **when** an authorized operator directly requests pause for that scope, **then** its current cycle finishes and its next does not start, without an additional confirmation prompt or an opening-revision prerequisite, even if collector admission advanced current control state; the same provider in another season, the other provider and club controls remain eligible according to their own settings. An acknowledged pause for the selected season/provider reports “Pause demandée. Le cycle en cours se termine.” while its admitted cycle drains, or “Collecte mise en pause.” when no cycle is running. Resuming acts directly without replaying missed scheduled runs, and the acknowledged resumption reports “Collecte réactivée.” Activating or deactivating F12 access maintenance triggers neither collection pause nor resumption. (FR-032, FR-033, FR-035)
4. **A26** — **Given** a cycle in progress, **when** classification, division or season configuration changes, **then** the current cycle uses its starting configuration and the next uses the new one. If reliable configuration cannot be obtained at the next start, no collection starts with remembered settings. (FR-034)
5. **A27** — **Given** an authorized manual rerun for a season/provider or seasonless club control, **when** it is already collecting and still enabled, **then** the state of its existing execution is returned without duplicate work. Otherwise, one pending request is recorded and distinguished from an actual start; repeated requests return that same pending state. The acknowledged pending season/provider request shows “Relance demandée.” once through [F13’s success feedback](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements). A response returning an existing execution does not show that confirmation. Pausing cancels a request that has not started. Rerunning respects eligibility and does not implicitly lift that scope’s pause. A paused or pausing scope rejects relaunch until explicitly resumed, including while its admitted cycle finishes. A failed or lost mobile response keeps the selected target and ordinary action feedback for a later explicit action, without a verification read or automatic replay. (FR-036)
6. **A28** — **Given** an ordinary user or live-stream moderator without collection permission, **when** they request a pause, resumption, rerun, season command or historical preparation, **then** the command is refused. Permission assignment belongs to F12. A later technical read failure is shown as the current failure and restores neither a refused command nor forbidden data. (FR-038)
7. **A37** — **Given** an authorized operator reviewing FFVB or LNV for a season, **when** they open Suivi, **then** the provider and season identify the selected control, and activation, execution, whole-control result totals, last attempt and last complete success have separate meanings. Changing Bilan criteria or reaching an empty page leaves those totals whole. If only the Suivi result summary fails, authorized commands stay usable and the summary's Retry rereads results only. A later read failure cannot retract an accepted command or restore withdrawn data. If the landing seasons read fails, its common F13 card replaces the whole menu body and retries only seasons under FR-039. Account admission, catalogue consultation and typed closure retain their respective scoped feedback. (FR-007, FR-023–FR-029, FR-032–FR-039, FR-050–FR-051, FR-055)

<a id="user-story-6---understand-incidents-and-actual-recovery-priority-p2"></a>

### User Story 6 — Understand incidents and actual recovery (Priority: P2)

As an operator, I want actionable current observations that clearly distinguish failure, voluntary pause and actual recovery.

**Why this priority**: Missing or misleading observations make actual acquisition failures harder to identify.

**Independent Test**: Submit bounded observations and compare current acquisition outcomes, safe local diagnostics and affected-scope recovery evidence; no external alerting or independent incident-state engine is required.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A29** — **Given** repeated and out-of-order observations, **when** an operator browses Bilan, **then** one latest-first list shows the newest observation per key, with stable ties and its retained useful success. Search and combined Status, Source and Step filters can find a result beyond the unfiltered first page within the authorized control. A failed first or full read uses the scoped F13 card and its Retry. A failed continuation retains accepted rows and position and retries only the failed page. A delayed log keeps its original committed observation. (FR-020, FR-039, FR-041, FR-043, FR-044)

- **First failure**: The first failed, partial or unconfirmed observation produces actionable affected-scope evidence for every collection family; repeated attempts preserve their stable control/scope/stage/phase context.
- **Recovery**: A committed usable observation after a non-successful outcome supplies recovery evidence only for its exact control/scope/stage/phase key. A delayed recovery log describes that observation, not the current state; later failures remain diagnosable in their own events and the current projection.
- **Unexecuted cycle**: A pause or missing attempt establishes neither failure nor recovery.

2. **A30** — **Given** a technical failure followed by a pause, deactivation or another successful scope, **when** current results and dated diagnostic observations are inspected, **then** no actual recovery is inferred without a new usable observation of the affected scope. A valid empty calendar may resolve its technical acquisition failure; acquisition success does not resolve failed integration. Recovery evidence remains independent of F02 withdrawal eligibility. (FR-043, FR-044)
3. **A31** — **Given** a newly discovered source binding or a catalog observation, **when** its operation commits, **then** a dated report identifies provider, season and subject with the safe counts already available from that operation. Repeated source proposals create no new source report. Waiting for classification and voluntary pause remain distinct from provider failure. (FR-023, FR-040–FR-045)
4. **A32** — **Given** a problem persisting across cycles, **when** its current projection and dated diagnostics are inspected, **then** the problem and its last useful success remain identifiable without synchronizing delayed logs with the latest state, a reminder schedule or external notification policy. Structural or context anomalies are actionable on their first failed observation. (FR-040, FR-044)
5. **A33** — **Given** a failed result with an established source, **when** an authorized operator opens Bilan from Sources, **then** its Source filter uses that binding. “Voir la source” opens the same binding with any established pack, pool or phase context. Returning restores the same Bilan criteria and list position. The card shows the available factual reason and displays a lone technical code inline. A machine category alone leaves its cause and timeout unknown. (FR-021, FR-023, FR-039, FR-044)

<a id="user-story-7---reconstruct-obtainable-history-through-a-bounded-operation-priority-p2"></a>

### User Story 7 — Reconstruct obtainable history through a bounded operation (Priority: P2)

As an operator preparing V2, I want one controlled initial reconstruction faithful to available sources, followed by definitive season closure without an archive-management product.

**Why this priority**: Historical reconstruction is useful, but source-data unavailability must not become a false coverage promise.

**Independent Test**: Prepare references covering three completed seasons and the current season, including absent or wrong-season sources, then examine the acquisition report and periodic-collection eligibility.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A34** — **Given** prepared and verified historical references, including for a completed sporting season still PREPARING in Blockout whose pools are absent from the current catalog, **when** initial reconstruction runs, **then** those references are eligible without season activation or discovery in the current catalog, provided classification, division activation, coverage and permissions allow the operation; it targets three completed seasons plus the current season, applies normal validations and distinguishes obtained, rejected and unavailable coverage; partial source availability is not presented as complete reconstruction. Absence of a pool from the current catalog removes no already acquired historical data. (FR-046, FR-047, FR-049)
2. **A35** — **Given** an authorized operator reviewing a season, **when** closure is requested, **then** the closure screen states the definitive consequence and requires the exact displayed season label, such as `2026/2027`. Blank, different or unauthorized requests have no effect. After acceptance, no new collection, relaunch, reactivation or source edit is possible for that season; already running work may finish once under its existing cycle. Other seasons and clubs keep their controls and data. (FR-007, FR-032–FR-038, FR-055)

3. **A36** — **Given** an absent historical LNV source and an available current-season feed, **when** reconstruction is attempted, **then** the current feed is not used as a substitute; the gap is reported for the transition decision. An inaccessible, unreadable or wrong-season archive retains previously accepted data and never constitutes empty-calendar confirmation. If a team logo fails, an explicitly selected eligible parent-pool retry may also update that pool’s authorized sporting facts and other team images; failures outside that pool remain visible. (FR-012, FR-047, FR-049)

<a id="edge-cases"></a>

### Edge Cases

- A URL still responding after removal from the catalog does not authorize continued current collection (A02). Failure to read the catalog does not prove removal and allows reuse of references already successfully discovered for the same season under current eligibility (A03).
- Simultaneously active seasons retain distinct observation contexts even if provider codes repeat (A05, A09, A14).
- An individually classified pool may remain eligible under an unclassified pack. Public hiding does not stop discovery or eligible calendar collection; no manual pack/pool exclusion exists (A06–A07).
- A normally absent value and a malformed value do not have the same meaning; neither authorizes an invented replacement (A10–A12).
- An always-empty phase and a formerly populated calendar becoming empty are not incidents; full withdrawal of previously visible data follows the single qualified observation rule in A13; closed seasons retain history and reject new collection under A35.
- A normal manual family rerun cannot bypass pause, coverage, classification or permission requirements (A27–A28). Controlled historical acquisition has its own season and reference eligibility rules while retaining classification, coverage and permission requirements (A34–A35).
- A valid observation may resolve a network outage while establishing another data-quality incident; recovery concerns only the problem that has actually ended (A30).
- Initial reconstruction uses ordinary provider work limits before closure; it does not alter another season’s pause state (A34–A35).

## Requirements

<a id="functional-requirements"></a>

### Functional Requirements

All requirements below express V2 behavior approved by the product owner. V1 evidence is separately identified in [Evidence and decision traceability](#evidence-and-decision-traceability).

#### Coverage and discovery

- **FR-001**: Acquisition MUST discover covered FFVB national, regional and departmental championship pools through official seasonal entries, national selector options/frames and organizer menus/links/forms. Youth qualifying pools are ordinary pools classified manually, for example M18 REG or M18 DEP, with format and gender; no qualifying entity, progression link or qualification calculation is required. Exclude French Cups, unrelated competitions and organizer codes `LIGU`, `LIGY`, `LIMART`, `LIMY`, `LIRE`. Use calendar CSV exports and contextual HTML complements; directories alone do not prove catalog completeness. Acquire FFVB information-sheet and scoresheet references from the published forms of the same qualified match and season, independently of result acquisition. A missing or unqualified form supplies no document proposal. F03 owns document access and eligibility.
- **FR-002**: Acquisition MUST cover the published phases of all three professional championships through official LNV pages: grouped calendars, match details and standings. Do not supplement sporting identity, presence, participants, schedule, results or standings from LNV XML or FFVB. For professional information sheets only, the applicable LNV XML may supply the FFVB document code through verified team associations scoped to the championship and season and an unambiguous participant/date match. It supplies no sporting fact or replacement identity. Published DataProject scoresheet and statistics-PDF links remain separate document references. Unqualified associations supply no document; current feeds cannot establish historical coverage. Ordinary covered competitions retain FFVB CSV plus complementary pages. Unproven phase coverage remains explicitly unqualified; no sampled identifier becomes permanent configuration. Professional team acquisition MUST NOT require an FFVB club association; F02 owns native LNV identity and the optional club relationship.
- **FR-003**: Acquisition MUST derive seasonal and phase references from published official entries. Reuse the canonical F02 season for the qualified sporting start year, keeping its canonical label and acquisition lifecycle. Verify provider, organizer or championship, supported structure and season attribution before attaching a source or child. A provider ID, successful HTTP response or usable page from a different season supplies no attribution. An unsupported structure requires collector maintenance. Ordinary discovery preserves the accepted season binding at provider rollover. Controlled historical preparation follows FR-047 and FR-052.
- **FR-004**: A complete catalog observation MUST establish that the expected structure and affected season and source scope were read and interpreted in full. Distinguish a qualified empty catalog, an incomplete read and an unavailable or unusable read. Only complete authoritative evidence can reconcile additions, absence and reappearance in its scope under F02. An HTTP success status, an isolated round or an unqualified part does not establish completeness.
- **FR-005**: Omission from a complete usable catalog for the affected season and scope MUST stop ordinary calendar and standings calls through that reference once the observation is accepted. A later authoritative reappearance triggers current eligibility assessment. Current LNV entry rollover MUST preserve a different season's binding; that season's own qualified catalog governs its references. F02 owns identity, mapping and visibility consequences.
- **FR-006**: An incomplete, unavailable or unqualified catalog observation MUST preserve the last accepted entries, mappings and visibility. Retained same-season references MAY serve otherwise eligible sporting reads while discovery continues under FR-031. A complete authoritative catalog confirming absence ends that reuse under FR-005. Without prior qualified evidence, acquisition MUST NOT guess a reference. Current settings, scoped pauses, division activity and terminal closure still govern eligibility. Catalog failure remains visible independently of successful calendar or standings reads.
- **FR-007**: New seasons MUST be detected automatically. Season activation follows FR-051. Only active seasons participate in ordinary periodic sporting collection. Catalog discovery runs before and after activation under FR-031. Several seasons MAY be active simultaneously. Terminal closure follows FR-055 and affects only that season’s FFVB and LNV scopes.
- **FR-008**: During normal current collection, calendars MUST be downloaded and sporting creations requested for integration only if permitted by a discovered source reference authorized under FR-005–FR-006, an active season, effective classification sufficient for acquisition, an enabled division and covered scope. Controlled historical acquisition follows FR-047. Shared official pages or exports MAY be read when needed to discover references or obtain an eligible pool. Read a shared resource once per passage when reusable; discard ineligible sporting rows without importing or archiving them. Before effective mapping, do not request a dedicated calendar for that ineligible pool or retain its results. Effective classification MUST identify division, format and gender; unclassified packs may record a need but MUST NOT create assumed sporting resources or a reserve of pending calendars. Already published classification retained by F02 MUST NOT replace missing current acquisition settings. Restored settings must be accepted under F02 correction rules before authorizing collection. F02 defines division and classification semantics.
- **FR-009**: Native FFVB packs MUST remain acquisition groups with pack classification and pool-level classification overrides. A fully individually classified pool MAY be collected even if its pack is unclassified. [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md) defines complete overrides, division activation and correction semantics.
- **FR-010**: No manual pack or pool exclusion control is required. Fixed coverage limits, effective classification, active seasons/divisions and independently scoped pauses remain. Public visibility of a pool does not govern its discovery or otherwise eligible collection.

#### Reliability and observation handoff

- **FR-011**: Acquisition MUST distinguish complete nonempty, validly empty, partial, invalid and unavailable observations. A partial observation means only part of the requested match scope was reliably obtained; it MUST identify discarded matches or missing parts instead of claiming completeness. Usable matches from a partial calendar remain available for integration, but partial evidence MUST NOT authorize absence-based withdrawal. Isolated invalidity of a secondary field does not necessarily invalidate established match presence.
- **FR-012**: An overall unreadable calendar structure or incorrect overall season or pool context MUST lead to rejection of the affected calendar while retaining previously accepted data. A locally unusable match or participant reference MUST instead isolate that match, provide the reason and preserve other usable matches. A calendar with an unresolved part of its match scope is incomplete for absence-based reconciliation; rejected rows MUST NOT implicitly be treated as absent sporting resources.
- **FR-013**: Contradictory rows for the same native reference within the same source and calendar context MUST isolate that match without arbitrary version selection, preserving other usable matches and reporting incomplete evidence for absence-based reconciliation. Identical repetition is not a contradiction. This rule defines neither universal match identity, code reuse across seasons nor provider priorities.
- **FR-014**: A malformed secondary field MUST be isolated and reported when the rest of the observation is usable. Its invalidity MUST NOT be represented as an explicitly empty value or a request to erase previously accepted data. Missing and invalid fields MUST remain distinct during handoff.
- **FR-015**: Normally absent values, including unpublished results or unconfirmed dates or times, MUST remain unknown. Acquisition MUST NOT invent statistics, scores, dates, times or coordinates. Recognized slots for future qualifiers or byes MUST retain source and phase context without being mistaken for malformed data or actual participants; they MUST NOT cause rejection of other identifiable matches. F02 defines when a match has sufficient identity to be viewable.
- **FR-016**: Sporting notations and special source values MUST be retained as evidence for F02 interpretation instead of automatically converted into ordinary results. Structural validity MUST NOT be confused with a business decision on forfeits, postponements, exceptional sets or sentinel values in official standings. FFVB numeric results in the qualified official results export follow [F02’s published-result rule](../002-sporting-data/spec.md#sources-results-and-time); special notations with ambiguous meaning remain unconfirmed. Acquisition MUST NOT calculate replacement official standings.
- **FR-017**: Official standings that are unreadable overall or impossible to attach to their context MUST be rejected independently of the calendar. The full qualified FFVB or LNV table MUST preserve the provider's published row sequence for [F02 integration](../002-sporting-data/spec.md#official-standings). Printed ranks, row labels and available statistics, including special values, MUST be preserved as source values. Missing ranks do not change the table's sequence or usability. Invalid or absent statistics MUST remain distinct from known values and MUST NOT become invented zeros. Inability to link a valid source row to a Blockout team belongs to F02 and does not justify discarding the acquired official table.
- **FR-018**: A failure MUST be isolated to the affected dependent scope. Pools, club sheets and independent FFVB/LNV calendar, standings and enrichment observations MUST remain separately usable. A failure MUST NOT make independent successes appear as failures, nor independent successes as evidence of recovery of the failing scope.
- **FR-019**: Rejected, partial, unavailable or stopped acquisition MUST NOT establish sporting absence. Repeated HTTP errors for a catalog-listed pool authorize no withdrawal. Send qualified catalog absence separately from a complete calendar observation. F02 decides visibility only after successful integration; discovery failure is not confirmed catalog absence.
- **FR-020**: One complete, valid, wholly empty calendar attributable to the correct current season and pool or phase, successfully integrated, MUST hide the pool, its matches and participations under F02 without erasing identities. No second confirmation or scheduled-only counter is required. A manual current collection follows the same qualification. All rejected rows, error pages, partial downloads and wrong context are not true empty observations. A still-referenced hidden pool remains eligible for collection. Closed seasons accept no new collection; already running cycles may finish once under FR-055. Wholly empty initial historical reconstruction preserves retained history.
- **FR-021**: Functional observation handoff MUST contain the source and its reference, expected season, competition, pack, pool and phase context where available, acquisition time, provider publication time if supplied, obtained scope, outcome and completeness, native identifiers, relevant observed values, classification context and actionable exclusion or rejection reasons. Any unknown context MUST be explicit; context MUST NOT be invented. Relevant values include participant references and names, dates and times, results and sets, venues and referees; official standings; professional phase, live-stream and qualified match-document references; club details and geocoding results. This is an information requirement, not a requirement for a public API or raw-data exposure. Observation order MUST be established before supplier reads and preserved at handoff; F02 owns calendar/detail precedence and acceptance of older observations. Report stale rejection without automatic replay of the integration pipeline.
- **FR-022**: Operators MUST distinguish validated acquisition from subsequent sporting integration, including successful match updates while other integrations remain failed or unconfirmed. Neither acquisition success nor partial integration success may be presented as complete synchronization of application data. F02 defines integration consistency and absence-reconciliation conditions; F13 defines shared operational visibility.
- **FR-023**: Diagnostics MUST expose source, scope, time, outcome, available counts and failure reasons without personal contact details, secret identifiers or automatic raw CSV or HTML archives. Useful normalized observations are distinct from raw-document archives. Diagnostic retention and delivery mechanisms belong to F11/F13; this specification chooses neither duration nor monitoring architecture.

#### Clubs and geocoding

- **FR-024**: Club-sheet acquisition MUST target clubs encountered in covered competitions, with a first attempt on discovery then collection every 24 hours. It MUST NOT independently browse the entire FFVB club directory. Club sheets describe the currently available club information independently of sporting seasons; geocoding likewise follows the current effective locality, not a selected historical season.
- **FR-025**: Failure to read a club sheet MUST remain distinct from club disappearance and MUST NOT trigger deactivation or erasure of prior information still authorized under F11. Available observations of name, address, municipality, postal code, contact and website MUST preserve distinctions between unknown and invalid fields; F11 governs authorized collection and retention.
- **FR-026**: A new geocoding need or one tied to a locality change passed by F02 MUST receive a collection attempt within one hour when collection is enabled and the provider available. F02 defines the effective address, municipality and postal context after manual corrections, intentional absences and restrictions. Acquisition MUST first seek the full address when present; an absent address or a qualified empty or ambiguous address response MUST use municipality lookup with the permitted postal context when available. A technical transport or malformed-response failure MUST remain a failure rather than trigger fallback. Without a permitted municipality, acquisition MUST make no provider request. A source change that leaves the effective context unchanged creates no new need; acquisition MUST NOT invent coordinates or heuristically choose an ambiguous address.
- **FR-027**: The final qualified response without a usable geocoding candidate or with ambiguous candidates MUST be distinguishable from a technical failure. Ordinary collection MUST NOT repeatedly query that same unchanged unresolved context. A located municipality fallback is a valid result and likewise creates no repeated automatic address lookup for unchanged context. A change in geocoding locality context, defined by F02, makes it eligible again.
- **FR-028**: An explicit manual rerun of the geocoding family MUST retry still-unresolved addresses, including unchanged addresses previously receiving an empty or ambiguous response, while respecting normal permissions and pauses. It MUST NOT recalculate valid results merely because the family was rerun.
- **FR-029**: Geocoding MUST pass address context and a success, no-candidate, ambiguity or technical-failure outcome to F02. A failed request MUST NOT become a definitive no-result observation or an instruction to erase accepted coordinates. A technical failure follows FR-037/FR-042, not FR-027 suppression of retries for an unchanged address.

#### Cadence and controls

- **FR-030**: Eligible calendars and standings MUST follow 30-minute scheduling, or five minutes when their pool/phase has a reliable match start within H−1 to H+4. Each LNV match detail MUST independently follow five minutes only inside its own reliable window and one hour otherwise, including after a final result. Newly discovered matches receive a first detail read. Unknown times create no fast window; missing results do not extend it. Corrected reliable times affect subsequent scheduling. A field not reread MUST NOT receive a new observation time; incompatible older sets must not survive a changed result as valid detail. LNV team logos MUST be retrieved during these calendar passages, once per distinct team per passage, without a separate schedule. Image failure MUST remain independent from usable match integration and preserve the last valid image under F02.
- **FR-031**: Catalog discovery MUST run every 30 minutes when enabled for qualified current-season sources and retained sources of active seasons, including while the current season is preparing or no pool is eligible for sporting collection. Discovery and FR-030 set scheduled times. Overruns, pauses and configuration unavailability follow FR-033–FR-035; unavailable sources remain visible under FR-050.
- **FR-032**: FFVB and LNV competition controls MUST be independent for each season. Club sheets and geocoding each retain an independent seasonless control. Pausing one season/provider does not pause another season, its other provider or either club control. These are functional controls, not separate services. Pause and resume act directly, retaining current permission, eligibility and pending-command checks. Shared provider access limits still apply. F12 access maintenance changes neither pause nor resumption state.
- **FR-033**: Pausing MUST allow the current cycle of the selected control scope to finish and prevent its next cycle. It cancels any manual relaunch request that has not started; resumption does not restore that request. A scope without a running cycle becomes paused immediately. Resumption restores eligibility without replaying missed ticks. Other scopes keep their own activation and pause states. Acknowledged mobile activity writes for a season/provider MUST present the applicable A25 confirmation once through [F13’s success feedback](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements). A failed state reread does not undo an accepted activity response; subsequent state is observed through ordinary reads.
- **FR-034**: A cycle MUST use reliable configuration selected at its start. Classification, division-activation and season changes MUST apply to acquisition from the next cycle. Without reliable configuration at startup, collection MUST NOT use remembered settings. Previously discovered references under FR-006 do not authorize this use of old configuration. Completing acquisition already in progress does not authorize F02 integration to undo current visibility or presentation rules.
- **FR-035**: Normal scheduling MUST prevent concurrent collection of the same target, skip missed ticks after an overrun and resume at the next eligible scheduled opportunity without catch-up. Backend atomic admission owns exclusion of running work. An admission without a successful response starts no provider reads; the next ordinary poll follows backend state. Lost completion acknowledgement does not block a backend-completed scope locally. A retained RUNNING cycle requires collector process replacement before a fresh admission; an unresolved live process still needs explicit inspection/restart. Each process attempts startup registration once before scheduling; failure ends that startup without application re-registration. Hosted native supervision may restart the failed process only within F13’s agreed bound, after which intervention is explicit. After a collector crash, preserve accepted integration and use a new ordinary collection; fine-grained resumption, custom crash supervision and automatic repair of a persistent bug are not required. Exceptional bounded overlapping reads may occur during failure but must respect supplier limits and cannot justify stale or duplicate business data. Monitoring exposes the interruption for manual correction. Eligible collection before closure can recover only data still supplied; it never recreates user corrections or irreplaceable logos.
- **FR-036**: A normal manual rerun MUST target the selected season/provider for competitions, or the selected seasonless club/geocoding control. Individual pool or club rerun commands are not required. An existing execution or pending request is returned without duplicate work. Otherwise one pending request is accepted and remains distinct from a confirmed cycle start; acceptance does not guarantee a start time or completion. On mobile, an acknowledged pending season/provider request MUST present the A27 confirmation once, leaving execution and result to the control’s read. Paused or pausing scopes reject relaunch until explicitly resumed. Eligibility and permissions remain required. A failed or lost response retains the selected target and ordinary action feedback; a later explicit action is permitted without a verification read or automatic replay. Closed seasons reject all new collection requests under FR-055.
- **FR-037**: Provider reads MUST share bounded active and pending work and configurable conservative access limits per provider. Avoid redundant retrieval of unchanged source material where the source permits it. Allow at most two additional attempts for transient read failures, honoring provider limits and any requested wait before retry; do not retry parsing errors or automatically replay an entire integration pipeline. Exhaustion produces explicit failure, never fabricated success or emptiness.
- **FR-038**: Collection Sources catalogue and operational reads, pause, resume, rerun, activation, closure, and controlled historical preparation and reconstruction MUST require the explicit collection-management permission. Ordinary users and live-stream moderators without the grant MUST be refused. [F12](../013-administration-app-configuration/spec.md#permissions-and-assignment) owns the independent grants for these operations and [F02 Mapping](../002-sporting-data/spec.md#identity-and-classification).

Suivi, Sources, source detail and the dedicated Bilan MUST identify the provider and season in the shared contextual heading. Bilan retains the contextual Support action. Suivi MUST separate activation, execution and observed outcomes. It MUST distinguish never collected, paused, pending and running work, and show the last attempt and last fully successful pass when known. Suivi MUST count successful, partial, failed and unconfirmed current results across both phases of the selected authorized control, independently of Bilan criteria and page. Commands retain their own authorization, prerequisites and FR-033/FR-036 acknowledgment feedback. Club sheets and geocoding retain independent seasonless controls. Catalogue consultation and season activation follow FR-050–FR-051. Closure follows FR-055. F02 owns separate mapping administration. Collection screens expose neither mapping readiness nor navigation to it.

  Bilan MUST present one paginated list of current results, one per control/scope/stage/phase, including while paused. Only a newer logical observation replaces the current result for its own key. Order results latest-attempt first with stable ties. The shared Search bar and filter sheet provide case- and accent-insensitive search across scope, source, stage and phase labels and combined Status, Source and Step filters before paging the authorized control. Status selection belongs to the filter sheet. The list omits a displayed result count.

A lone technical code appears inline with compact wrapping. Further diagnostics under FR-023, including measured counts, are available only when supplied. Unknown evidence MUST stay unknown, including causes or timeouts suggested by a machine category alone. Dated logs keep their own committed observations.

A non-successful result with an established catalogue attribution offers “Voir la source” and carries the known pack, pool or phase context with its binding. A source detail offers “Voir le bilan de cette source” and opens Bilan with that same binding selected as its Source filter. Back MUST restore the originating Bilan criteria and position or source catalogue expansion and position.

  The landing menu’s final required seasons-read failure MUST replace its whole body, including Competitions and Clubs links, with the common F13 card. Preserve header/Back and authorized cache in memory. Retry MUST keep that card with actual explicit-read progress, read only seasons and restore the menu after success, without a competing pull or command. Recorded acquisition or integration failure is result data. A result-read failure MUST leave independently authorized controls usable, including Pause. Catalogue lists and details, controls, results and closure follow [F13's scoped initial-read, refresh and Retry presentation](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#platforms-accessibility-and-bounded-continuity). Results refresh and Retry only reread their affected scope. A failed next-page read retains accepted rows and the native scroller with footer Retry of the failed page. The account prerequisite follows F05 independently of application navigation. Across dashboard landing, season/control scopes and catalogue consultation, a failed first account read MUST retain the same scoped card and mounted container through explicit Retry. Its button reflects only that action’s actual active read. Definitive closure retains exact typed confirmation and leaves closed seasons read-only. The current read error is presented directly. Forbidden data stays withdrawn independently of later technical failures.

- **FR-040**: A structural acquisition anomaly or season/context inconsistency MUST produce actionable evidence from its first accepted observation for the affected scope, without a consecutive-failure counter. Accepted-result diagnostics are emitted only after commit; a rolled-back observation produces none.
- **FR-041**: The first failed competition-collection cycle for an affected scope MUST produce actionable failure evidence. Repeated failures retain meaningful problem, scope, stage, phase and observation-time context; HTTP attempts within one cycle do not require separate incident identities. A confirmed manual-cycle failure follows the same rule. Unexecuted work is not an observed failure or recovery. Valid empty calendars are not technical failures. Recovery follows FR-043. This requirement selects no external alerting or delivery policy.
- **FR-042**: A technical club-sheet or geocoding failure MUST be retried at the next ordinary eligible passage of the affected family, without a dedicated recovery schedule, and produce actionable evidence from the first failed, partial or unconfirmed observation, like competition collection. Diagnostic failure does not change the accepted business outcome or next eligible passage. No exact outage-start or outage-duration value is promised. An operator pause prevents attempts without inventing recovery; no provider outage is inferred merely because work was intentionally not attempted. Valid empty or ambiguous geocoding responses are not outages.
- **FR-043**: Actual acquisition recovery MUST be established by a new usable observation of the affected scope showing that the reported problem has ended. A pause, closure, success elsewhere or successful acquisition with failed integration does not establish business recovery. A successful manual observation MAY establish it without fabricating scheduled observations. The recovery diagnostic describes that committed observation and its time for the exact control/scope/stage/phase key, even if a later outcome exists when it is emitted. No independent business-recovery state machine is required.
- **FR-044**: Application failure evidence MUST identify meaningful scope, stage, phase and observation time so repeated observations remain understandable. Logs carry safe facts from the committing operation; current projections retain current state. Diagnostic emission MUST NOT require fresh business transactions, season/control locks or projection rereads solely to synchronize a log with the latest state. Different problems remain identifiable; ending a network outage does not hide a content or integration defect. No reminder frequency, external notification, incident ledger or exact transition-delivery guarantee is selected.
- **FR-045**: A newly discovered or explicitly prepared historical binding and an accepted catalog observation MUST produce dated post-commit operator reports grouped by provider, season and subject. Report one actual source creation and available catalog counts from the operation. Leave unavailable counts absent and omit individual identities and outstanding classification counts. Voluntary pause and waiting for classification remain distinct from provider failure.
- **FR-046**: Initial V2 reconstruction MUST target the current season and three completed seasons, according to available source data, through an explicitly authorized exploitation command using prepared and verified historical references, with no dedicated mobile reconstruction journey. It MUST NOT require a generic product that automatically discovers all archives.
- **FR-047**: Initial historical reconstruction MUST use prepared and verified references while the historical season is PREPARING, before definitive closure. Neither periodic activation nor presence in today’s catalog is required. Classification, enabled divisions, coverage, permission, reliable configuration and source validation still apply. Use ordinary adapters and F02 integration, with one explicitly requested passage and a report; retry only an identified failure before closure. For a failed team logo, explicitly retry an eligible parent pool of that team under the same selected source. This ordinary pool passage may reread and integrate its currently authorized sporting facts and team images; it provides no single-image isolation and requires no whole-source rerun. No separate reconstruction engine, automatic archive crawler or pause of other seasons is required. Unavailable, unreadable, wrong-season or wholly empty historical input preserves retained data; no current feed may fill a historical gap. Complete nonempty authoritative input may reconcile individual facts before closure. F14 owns transition authorization and F07 suppresses initial-import announcements.
- **FR-048**: Closed seasons MUST have no recollection or reopening command; initial historical reconstruction occurs before terminal closure under FR-055.
- **FR-049**: Reconstruction MUST report requested coverage, obtained observations, rejected data and unavailable sources by season and source scope. Sampling a few available archives MUST NOT be presented as exhaustive reconstruction or evidence of migration readiness. F14 defines resulting launch, launch-refusal and recovery criteria; F02 defines reintegration consequences.

- **FR-050**: The provider's Sources tab MUST expose read-only official source catalogues before and after season activation. The list uses shared Search and its approved filter sheet, with Statut for both providers and Périmètre for FFVB. It shows the actual catalog health of each source and omits a result counter. Use published source names and verified FFVB entry perimeters when known. A binding with unknown presentation evidence keeps its exact native reference and remains visible in the unfiltered catalogue. The detail shows the known official link in its header and exposes the actual read outcome, latest attempt, latest complete read and factual reason when available. Distinguish no read, complete, incomplete and unavailable outcomes. After an incomplete or unavailable read, keep accepted entries visible and explain their retention when they exist. A stored provider failure is catalog information. A failed required application read uses the scoped [F13 feedback](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements).

  FFVB details show discovered native packs with expandable pools and the known pool counts. LNV championship details show only phases attested by the official structure, with one entry per established phase. Display its published phase name when established and its native phase reference otherwise. Group names remain attached to their sporting pools. Long catalogs remain browsable on the source detail. A direct or Bilan source link focuses only the corresponding pack, pool or phase present in that binding's catalogue. Source creation, URL editing, qualification and per-source launch have no ordinary operator journey. Controlled historical preparation follows FR-052. Catalog health is independent of calendar and standings collection. F02 owns classification and visibility independently of catalog health and season activation.

- **FR-051**: Season activation MUST be one action in the selected PREPARING season's context under current permission and lifecycle checks. Use the approved confirmation. Publish one green [F13 success notice](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements) after the command is acknowledged. Cancellation has no effect. An ordinary failed or lost response keeps the target available for a later explicit action with ordinary feedback. A subsequent read failure does not retract an acknowledged activation.

Check the exact target, typed label and current eligibility/authorization atomically; refuse incorrect or unauthorized requests without effects. No client editing revision is required. Acceptance prevents any new cycle, source validation or queued work from starting; only a cycle already RUNNING at acceptance may complete its selected work once, subject to existing order and visibility guards. <a id="key-entities-include-if-feature-involves-data"></a>

### Key Entities

- **Source reference**: Provider-specific location and native identifiers with season and competition context; neither a selected business primary key nor a permanent URL.
- **Discovery scope / catalog**: Official grouping whose season, provider and source boundaries determine which native references can be accepted and where authoritative absence can be established. The most recent read may be incomplete or unavailable while the earlier complete catalog remains usable.
- **Source pack / pool classification**: A native grouping of pools and effective classification by division, format and gender used for acquisition eligibility. It does not replace the durable Pool or Division concepts defined by F02.
- **Observation**: Evidence obtained for a source and instant, with scope, completeness, relevant values and reasons. It is distinct from accepted business state and downstream publication.
- **Collection family / cycle**: An independently controlled family of acquisition work and a bounded set of work selected at cycle start. No process, message queue or deployment mode is imposed.
- **Geocoding need**: A request concerning locality context, passed by F02, and its result; the acquisition domain does not own durable Club coordinates.
- **Acquisition incident**: A bounded problem requiring operator attention, with evidence establishing its opening and recovery. This concept does not impose a separate incident-management product.

<a id="assumptions"></a>

## Assumptions

- The current application remains the functional starting reference, not the implementation architecture to reproduce. Accepted scope intentionally changes the specified V1 behaviors; speculative social-network, fantasy-game, player-roster or additional-provider features are excluded.
- Official FFVB/LNV sources remain external dependencies and may change or omit data. A recognized catalog may be empty; recognizing its HTML structure does not prove the provider published everything it intended. Provenance and bounded outcomes make these limits explicit.
- Time-window examples use reliable start instants supplied by F02. Source time interpretation and user-facing time-zone/day semantics belong to F02/F03; no new midnight or default-time convention is chosen here.
- Cadence denotes scheduled times, not a measured delay after an execution ends. Pauses require separate explicit resumption; a manual rerun does not bypass eligibility. Normal duplicate prevention and operator control follow FR-035, including its explicitly accepted exceptional-overlap rule.
- The authorized reset concerns business-data reconstruction only. [identity continuity requirements](../005-accounts-identity/spec.md#v1-evidence-and-limitations), [Pro continuity evidence](../006-pro-subscriptions/spec.md#v1-evidence-and-limitations) and [transition requirements](../014-v1-v2-transition/spec.md) continues to constrain F14: this specification authorizes no deletion of Auth0 accounts or valid paid access rights.

## Evidence and decision traceability

<a id="repository-observations"></a>

### Repository observations

[Transition requirements](../014-v1-v2-transition/spec.md#v1-evidence) own V1 continuity and the limits of the historical functional reference; that reference establishes neither deployed configuration nor V2 qualification. Current coverage, acquisition, scheduling and recovery behavior is defined by this specification's requirements, with sporting truth owned by [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md). Discovery alone does not establish exhaustive catalogs, a completed passage does not establish successful integration, and source absence authorizes withdrawal only under the explicit completeness rules. Qualification remains in [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

<a id="official-source-observations-and-limits"></a>

### Official-source observations and limits

[Source research](research.md#ffvb-source-patterns) owns representative provider observations and their dates, URLs and coverage limits. They establish neither exhaustive catalogs/history nor stable future structures. Current source eligibility and interpretation follow the requirements above; [qualification](quickstart.md) separates those samples from running collector behavior.
