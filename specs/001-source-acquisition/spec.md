# Feature Specification: Source Acquisition and Observation Reliability

**Feature Branch**: `feature/257-source-acquisition`

**Created**: 2026-09-15

**Status**: Accepted

**Input**: Owner-approved scope for [#257](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/257): specify FFVB/LNV competition acquisition, club records, geocoding, reliable observations and operator controls; retain FFVB discovery and CSV exports, with explicit V2 changes and no technical implementation.

## User Scenarios & Testing _(mandatory)_

The primary actor is an operator with explicit collection permissions. Visitors and signed-in users benefit from trustworthy sporting data but cannot operate collection. A live moderator receives no collection permission merely by being a moderator. The sporting-data domain consumes observations; its decisions belong to F02, not to a second end-user role.

Scenario identifiers below are local acceptance references, not implementation task identifiers. Independent tests describe future acceptance evidence; no V2 runtime is claimed to exist.

### User Story 1 - Collect only eligible official competitions (Priority: P1)

As an operator, I want current official coverage to determine what is collected, so obsolete references and guessed classifications do not drive acquisition.

**Why this priority**: Source eligibility determines the trustworthiness and scope of all subsequent sporting data.

**Independent Test**: Supply representative catalogs and operator settings, then compare the eligible collection set and exclusion reasons without requiring sporting integration.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a readable supported catalog and an active season, **When** a classified, non-excluded pool is discovered, **Then** its calendar becomes eligible; each of the five excluded league codes remains outside coverage. (FR-001, FR-004, FR-008)
2. **A02** — **Given** a previously discovered pool, **When** a new usable catalog no longer lists it, **Then** further collection through its former reference stops immediately, without waiting for more absence cycles or deleting business data. **When** it reappears, **Then** current eligibility rules apply again. (FR-005, FR-019)
3. **A03** — **Given** known successful pool references for the same season, **When** their catalog is unreadable, partial or an error page, **Then** otherwise eligible collection may continue with those references while discovery is retried; the discovery failure remains visible and proves no absence. Without prior successful discovery, no reference is guessed. (FR-004, FR-006, FR-018)
4. **A04** — **Given** a successful discovery, **When** an intermediate five-minute collection is due, **Then** its same-season references may be reused subject to current eligibility, including after a failed discovery. **When** a later usable catalog confirms absence, **Then** the absent source reference stops being eligible even if its old URL responds. (FR-005, FR-006, FR-031)
5. **A05** — **Given** a newly detected season, **When** no operator has activated it, **Then** it is visible for preparation but not periodically collected. **When** activated while another season remains active, **Then** both may be collected. **When** one is closed, **Then** its periodic collection stops without deleting history. (FR-007, FR-019)
6. **A06** — **Given** a pack awaiting classification, **When** its pools also lack effective classification, **Then** calendars are not downloaded and no sporting creation is requested. **When** one pool receives a complete individual classification, **Then** only that eligible pool may proceed. Removing that classification without a pack replacement stops collection from the next cycle even though F02 retains previously published classification and visibility; a refused restoration does not resume collection. A disabled division suspends dependent collection from the next cycle; reactivation reevaluates eligibility without lifting exclusions. (FR-008, FR-009, FR-034)
7. **A07** — **Given** an individually classified pool, **When** its parent pack is excluded, **Then** the pool is excluded too. **When** the exclusion is lifted, **Then** eligibility is reevaluated in the next cycle. Pool-only exclusion leaves its siblings eligible. (FR-010, FR-034)

### User Story 2 - Keep unreliable input from becoming sporting truth (Priority: P1)

As a consumer of Blockout data, I want collection failures and ambiguous source input to remain distinguishable from actual sporting changes.

**Why this priority**: A successful download is insufficient evidence for trustworthy results or disappearance decisions.

**Independent Test**: Present complete, empty, malformed and conflicting observations and inspect their acceptance, reasons and functional handoff independently of persistence or display.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A08** — **Given** a 100-match calendar with one unusable match and usable global structure/context, **When** acquired, **Then** the other 99 matches remain usable, the affected match is isolated with reasons and the observation is incomplete for absence-based reconciliation. If global structure/context is unreadable, reject that calendar instead; independent pools still proceed. (FR-011, FR-012, FR-018)
2. **A09** — **Given** one source calendar and context, **When** two rows contradict each other for the same source reference, **Then** isolate that match without selecting a version and retain the other usable matches; this incomplete observation cannot justify absence-based withdrawal. Identical repetition, reuse in another season and disagreement between providers are distinct cases. (FR-013, FR-019)
3. **A10** — **Given** an otherwise usable record, **When** a secondary field such as a club website is malformed, **Then** that field is flagged and isolated without rejecting the whole record or requesting erasure of its previous value. (FR-014)
4. **A11** — **Given** a match with an unpublished result, unknown kickoff or recognized future qualifier, **When** acquired, **Then** normally missing values stay unknown and future-slot context is retained without fabricated teams or dates and without rejecting other identifiable fixtures. Special sporting notation is retained for F02 interpretation, not converted to zero. (FR-015, FR-016)
5. **A12** — **Given** a valid calendar, **When** its official standing is globally unreadable or unattributable, **Then** that standing is rejected independently. A usable table retains official order and labels even when individual statistics are missing or invalid; no fabricated zeros are supplied. An unmatched Blockout team is an F02 linkage outcome, not acquisition invalidity. (FR-017, FR-018)
6. **A13** — **Given** a previously populated pool, **When** three consecutive scheduled collections return a valid empty calendar, **Then** emptiness is recorded, existing data is preserved and an incident is raised on the third observation. A new pool or future phase that has always been empty does not raise this incident. (FR-019, FR-020, FR-041)
7. **A14** — **Given** an observation requested for one season, **When** the supplied content demonstrably belongs to another season, **Then** it is rejected and the context mismatch is reported immediately; current-season content cannot fill a historical gap. (FR-012, FR-040, FR-047)
8. **A15** — **Given** a validated acquisition, **When** some subsequent sporting integrations fail, **Then** operators can distinguish acquisition success, successful updates and remaining failures; the observation is not reported as fully integrated. (FR-021, FR-022, FR-043)

### User Story 3 - Obtain all covered professional phases (Priority: P1)

As a user following professional volleyball, I want acquisition to cover each official phase of the three supported championships rather than only their regular seasons.

**Why this priority**: The owner identifies incomplete V1 professional coverage and delayed enrichment as material limitations.

**Independent Test**: Use official entry pages and representative regular-season, play-in and playoff references with changing season identifiers, then inspect discovered coverage and separately sourced observations.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A16** — **Given** the three official professional championship entries, **When** season references or published phase links change, **Then** discovery follows the official entries and phases without treating previously observed codes as permanent or adding unrelated competitions. (FR-002, FR-003)
2. **A17** — **Given** an eligible professional phase, **When** the LNV page supplies dates, results, venue, officials or phase context, including a phase without an identifiable FFVB counterpart, **Then** acquisition supplies separately attributed evidence without guessing correspondence. FFVB reference absence stops that FFVB collection, not an independently eligible LNV phase. F02 owns identity, authority and visibility. (FR-002, FR-005, FR-018, FR-021)
3. **A18** — **Given** a new unsupported official page structure, **When** discovery cannot interpret it, **Then** an explicit diagnostic and structural incident require scraper maintenance; no guessed reference or normal-operation manual URL entry is used. (FR-003, FR-040)

### User Story 4 - Enrich covered clubs without repeated useless requests (Priority: P1)

As a user consulting a club or map, I want club information and coordinate observations to be refreshed without interpreting provider failure as disappearance.

**Why this priority**: Club details support consultation, while an unresolved address must not trigger perpetual identical geocoding requests.

**Independent Test**: Supply covered club references, changed addresses and geocoding outcomes; inspect requested work and observations without deciding final coordinate publication.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A19** — **Given** a newly encountered covered club, **When** its fiche is first collected and later refreshed, **Then** the first attempt follows discovery and subsequent attempts follow the 24-hour cadence; unrelated directory clubs are not added. Failure of another club page does not deactivate it or discard this result. (FR-024, FR-025)
2. **A20** — **Given** a new coordinate need or changed address supplied by F02, **When** collection is enabled and the provider is available, **Then** a geocoding attempt occurs within one hour and supplies the outcome and address context to F02. (FR-026, FR-029)
3. **A21** — **Given** a correct geocoding response with no usable candidate or an ambiguous result, **When** the address stays unchanged, **Then** routine collection does not request it again. **When** the F02 geocoding locality context changes or the operator relaunches the geocoding family, **Then** it is eligible for a new attempt. Existing valid results are not recalculated by that relaunch. (FR-027, FR-028)
4. **A22** — **Given** a technical club/geocoding failure, **When** no operator pause prevents work, **Then** it remains distinct from a valid empty/ambiguous response, receives another attempt before 24 hours, and raises an incident if the outage persists for 24 hours. (FR-029, FR-037, FR-042)

### User Story 5 - Control collection and cadence predictably (Priority: P1)

As an operator, I want to pause and relaunch collection without duplicate runs, hidden configuration fallback or an accumulated backlog.

**Why this priority**: Predictable controls bound provider traffic and permit safe maintenance.

**Independent Test**: Observe due work and operator outcomes over a simulated schedule with fast windows, overruns, configuration changes and denied commands.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A23** — **Given** an eligible pool, **When** no reliable kickoff is in its fast window, **Then** competition collection uses 30-minute due times. From one hour before through four hours after a reliable kickoff, it uses five-minute due times regardless of weekday. No result after the window does not extend it; an unknown kickoff does not create a window. (FR-030, FR-031)
2. **A24** — **Given** a collection due every five minutes and a run spanning later due times, **When** it finishes, **Then** missed due times are discarded and collection resumes at the next scheduled due time, without an immediate catch-up run or overlap for that target. (FR-035)
3. **A25** — **Given** a running family cycle, **When** an authorized operator pauses that family, **Then** its current cycle finishes and the next does not start; other enabled families remain eligible. Resuming does not replay missed due times. (FR-032, FR-033, FR-035)
4. **A26** — **Given** a running cycle, **When** classification, exclusion or season configuration changes, **Then** the existing cycle uses its starting configuration and the next uses the new configuration. If reliable configuration cannot be obtained at the next start, no collection starts on remembered settings. (FR-034)
5. **A27** — **Given** an authorized manual family relaunch, **When** that family is already collecting, **Then** its existing run state is returned without duplicate work. Otherwise the relaunch respects eligibility and does not silently unpause a paused family. (FR-036)
6. **A28** — **Given** an ordinary user or a live moderator without collection permissions, **When** they request pause, resume, relaunch, season control or a collection-setting change, **Then** the command is denied. Permission assignment is owned by F12. (FR-038)

### User Story 6 - Understand incidents and actual recovery (Priority: P2)

As an operator, I want grouped actionable signals, with a clear distinction between an outage, a deliberate pause and a genuine recovery.

**Why this priority**: Repeated or misleading notifications make real acquisition failures harder to notice.

**Independent Test**: Feed scoped observation sequences to the functional incident rules and compare the required incident state and notification events without selecting a monitoring product.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A29** — **Given** one scheduled collection containing three failed HTTP attempts, **When** the cycle ends, **Then** it counts as one failed scheduled cycle. Manual failed relaunches do not substitute for the second or third scheduled failures. On the third consecutive scheduled failure for that same scope, the incident is raised. (FR-039, FR-041)
2. **A30** — **Given** an open incident, **When** its family is paused, its pool excluded or another pool succeeds, **Then** no recovery is announced. **When** a new usable observation of the affected scope demonstrates the condition has ended, **Then** recovery is announced once. A still-empty calendar cannot resolve a populated-to-empty incident. (FR-043, FR-044)
3. **A31** — **Given** multiple newly detected seasons or unclassified entries, **When** they require operator action, **Then** signals are grouped rather than sent for every element; neither waiting for classification nor deliberate exclusion is reported as a provider outage. (FR-044, FR-045)
4. **A32** — **Given** an incident that remains open across several cycles, **When** the condition persists, **Then** it stays visible without periodic reminder notifications. Structural/context anomalies are signaled immediately instead of waiting for three failed cycles. (FR-040, FR-044)
5. **A33** — **Given** a failed acquisition with diagnostic metadata, **When** an operator inspects it, **Then** the source, scope, observation time, outcome, counts where available and reason are understandable without automatic raw-document archives, personal contact details or credentials in diagnostics. (FR-021, FR-023)

### User Story 7 - Reconstruct obtainable history through a bounded operation (Priority: P2)

As the operator preparing V2, I want an honest reconstruction of obtainable seasons and an exceptional recollection path that does not require a generic archive-discovery product.

**Why this priority**: Historical reconstruction is useful, but unavailable source data must not become a false coverage promise.

**Independent Test**: Prepare references spanning three completed seasons and the current season, including missing and wrong-season sources, then inspect the acquisition report and periodic-collection eligibility.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A34** — **Given** prepared and verified historical references, including a closed season whose pools are absent from the current catalog, **When** the initial reconstruction is run, **Then** those references are eligible without season activation or current-catalog discovery, provided classification, enabled-division status, coverage, exclusions and permissions permit the operation; it targets three completed seasons plus the current season with normal validation and reports obtained, rejected and unavailable coverage separately; partial source availability is not claimed as a complete rebuild. (FR-046, FR-047, FR-049)
2. **A35** — **Given** a closed season requiring exceptional recollection after launch, **When** an authorized operator schedules a controlled operation, **Then** the historical operation may run while the affected current-collection family is deliberately paused, the closed season remains closed for periodic work, and current collection resumes only on explicit resume without replaying missed cycles. Excluded, disabled-division or unclassified historical pools remain ineligible despite their prepared references. (FR-008, FR-010, FR-047, FR-048)
3. **A36** — **Given** a missing historical LNV source and an available current-season feed, **When** reconstruction is attempted, **Then** the current feed is not substituted; the gap is reported for the transition decision. (FR-047, FR-049)

### Edge Cases

- A URL still responding after catalog removal does not authorize continued current collection (A02). A failed catalog read does not prove removal and permits reuse of previously successful same-season references under current eligibility (A03).
- Overlapping active seasons retain distinct observation contexts even when provider codes repeat (A05, A09, A14).
- An individually classified pool cannot bypass pack exclusion; missing classification and exclusion remain different reasons for inactivity (A06–A07).
- A normally absent value and a malformed value have different meanings; neither authorizes an invented replacement (A10–A12).
- A future phase with no matches is not the same incident as a previously populated calendar becoming empty (A13).
- A normal manual family relaunch cannot bypass pause, exclusion, classification or permission requirements (A27–A28). Controlled historical acquisition has its own season/reference eligibility while retaining classification, exclusions and permissions (A34–A35).
- A valid observation may resolve a network outage while establishing a different data-quality incident; recovery is scoped to the condition that actually ended (A30).
- Exceptional historical work is separate from a current cycle and need not run concurrently with current collection (A35).

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional Requirements

All requirements below express the owner-approved V2 behavior. V1 evidence is separately identified under [Evidence and decision traceability](#evidence-and-decision-traceability).

#### Coverage and discovery

- **FR-001**: Acquisition MUST retain automatic FFVB pool-reference discovery and calendar CSV exports for covered national, regional and departmental competitions, excluding `LIGU`, `LIGY`, `LIMART`, `LIMY` and `LIRE`. Complementary official pages/feeds remain necessary for data not in calendar exports; coverage does not expand to unrelated competitions or disciplines.
- **FR-002**: Acquisition MUST cover all officially published phases of the three professional championships (women's top division, men's top division and men's second division), including regular seasons, play-ins and playoffs when applicable. Official LNV pages MAY supply dates, results, venues, officials, standings and phase context, including phases without an identifiable FFVB counterpart. F02 owns representation, reconciliation and precedence; FFVB CSV acquisition remains required for available FFVB calendars.
- **FR-003**: Normal current-season acquisition MUST derive seasonal and phase references from official entry pages and their relevant links, preserving season context rather than treating sampled codes/IDs as permanent. Unrecognized structures MUST produce explicit failure requiring scraper maintenance. Normal operator controls MUST NOT include arbitrary source-URL entry.
- **FR-004**: A usable discovery observation MUST establish that the expected catalog structure and affected scope were read and interpreted without missing portions. Successful HTTP status alone is insufficient. Error pages, unrecognized structures and partial reads MUST NOT establish confirmed absence. A genuinely empty recognized catalog is distinguishable from an unreadable one.
- **FR-005**: In normal current collection, after a usable source catalog omits a previously discovered pool/phase, collection through that source reference MUST stop immediately; there is no absence-cycle threshold and accessibility of the former URL does not preserve eligibility. Reappearance MUST trigger normal eligibility evaluation. Independent source eligibility is preserved: FFVB absence alone does not stop a still-published eligible LNV phase. F02 defines the authoritative catalog for sporting visibility.
- **FR-006**: A failed or unusable discovery attempt MUST NOT itself invalidate references from a previous successful discovery for the same season. Those references MAY support otherwise eligible intermediate and subsequent collection while discovery continues every 30 minutes when enabled. Without previous successful discovery, acquisition MUST NOT guess references. A subsequent usable catalog confirming absence ends reuse under FR-005. Prior references MUST NOT override pauses, exclusions, disabled divisions, closed seasons or unavailable reliable configuration. Discovery failures remain visible even when calendar requests succeed; no new time-to-live or fallback to another season is required.
- **FR-007**: New seasons MUST be detected automatically but activated and closed only by an authorized operator. Multiple seasons MAY be active concurrently. Only active seasons participate in normal periodic collection; closure MUST NOT delete history.
- **FR-008**: In normal current collection, calendars MUST be downloaded and sporting creation requested only when a discovered source reference permitted by FR-005–FR-006, active season, sufficient effective acquisition classification, enabled division and non-exclusion allow it. Controlled historical acquisition follows FR-047. Effective classification MUST identify division, format and gender; unclassified packs may register a need but MUST NOT create guessed sporting resources or a warehouse of pending calendars. Previously published classification retained by F02 MUST NOT substitute for missing current acquisition settings. Restored settings require acceptance under F02 correction rules before permitting collection. F02 owns division/classification semantics.
- **FR-009**: Native FFVB packs MUST remain acquisition groups with pack classification and individual pool classification overrides. An individually fully classified pool MAY be collected even when its pack is unclassified. [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md) owns complete overrides, division activation and correction semantics.
- **FR-010**: Pack/pool exclusion MUST be explicit and distinct from missing classification. Pack exclusion MUST override every member pool's classification. A pool-only exclusion MUST leave other eligible pools unaffected. Lifting exclusion MUST cause reevaluation in the next cycle, without bypassing remaining eligibility conditions.

#### Reliability and observation handoff

- **FR-011**: Acquisition MUST distinguish complete non-empty, valid empty, partial, invalid and unavailable observations. Partial means only part of the requested match scope was reliably obtained; it MUST identify excluded matches or missing portions rather than claim completeness. Usable matches in a partial calendar remain available for integration, but partial evidence MUST NOT authorize absence-based withdrawal. Isolated secondary-field invalidity need not invalidate the established match membership.
- **FR-012**: Globally unreadable calendar structure or wrong overall season/pool context MUST reject the affected calendar while retaining previous accepted data. A locally unusable match or participant reference MUST instead isolate that match, supply its reason and preserve the other usable matches. A calendar with any unresolved match portion is incomplete for absence-based reconciliation; rejected rows MUST NOT be silently treated as absent sporting resources.
- **FR-013**: Contradictory rows for the same native reference within one source/calendar context MUST isolate that match without arbitrary version selection, preserving other usable matches and reporting incomplete evidence for absence-based reconciliation. Identical repeated evidence is not a contradiction. This rule does not define universal match identity, cross-season code reuse or precedence between providers.
- **FR-014**: A malformed secondary field MUST be isolated and flagged when the remaining observation is usable. Its invalidity MUST NOT be represented as an explicit empty value or a request to erase previously accepted data. Missing and invalid fields MUST remain distinguishable in the handoff.
- **FR-015**: Normally absent values, including an unpublished result or unconfirmed date/kickoff, MUST remain unknown. Acquisition MUST NOT manufacture statistics, scores, dates, times or coordinates. Recognized future qualifier/bye placeholders MUST retain their source/phase context without being mistaken for malformed input or real participants; they MUST NOT reject other identifiable fixtures. F02 defines when a fixture has sufficient identity to become consultable.
- **FR-016**: Source sporting notation and special values MUST be retained as evidence for F02 interpretation rather than automatically converted to ordinary results. Structural validity MUST NOT be confused with a business decision about forfeits, postponements, exceptional sets or official ranking sentinels. Acquisition MUST NOT calculate a replacement official ranking.
- **FR-017**: A globally unreadable or unattributable official standing MUST be rejected independently of the calendar. In an otherwise usable table, invalid/missing statistics MUST remain distinguishable from known values and MUST NOT become fabricated zeros. Official ordering, positions when stated, labels and special values MUST remain available. Failure to link a valid source row to a Blockout team is an F02 outcome, not a reason to discard the acquired official table.
- **FR-018**: Failure MUST be isolated to the affected dependent scope. Independent pools, club records and FFVB/LNV calendar/ranking/enrichment observations MUST remain separately usable. One failure MUST NOT label independent successes as failures, or independent successes as proof that the failed scope recovered.
- **FR-019**: Empty, rejected, unavailable or stopped acquisition MUST NOT itself request sporting deactivation, deletion or history removal. Partial calendar evidence MUST NOT justify match/participation withdrawal based on absence; F02 additionally requires successful complete integration and defines which calendar has authority for withdrawals/reappearances when sources disagree. Independent acquisition success does not grant a secondary calendar membership authority. Catalog absence MUST be supplied as distinct evidence, not inferred from failed collection. F02 owns the sporting consequences of authoritative catalog absence and explicit administrative exclusions.
- **FR-020**: A valid empty calendar MUST be recorded as empty without discarding prior data. A pool or future phase that has never been populated MUST NOT raise an emptiness incident. For a previously populated pool, three consecutive scheduled empty observations MUST raise an incident; FR-041 defines counting boundaries.
- **FR-021**: The functional observation handoff MUST contain source/reference, expected season/competition/pack/pool/phase context where available, acquisition time, provider publication time if supplied, obtained scope, outcome/completeness, native identifiers, relevant observed values, classification context and actionable reasons for exclusion/rejection. Unknown context MUST be explicit; context MUST NOT be fabricated. Relevant values include calendar participant references/names, dates/times, results/sets, venue/referees; official standings; professional phase/live references; club details and geocoding outcomes. This is an information requirement, not a public API or raw-payload exposure requirement.
- **FR-022**: Operators MUST distinguish validated acquisition from subsequent sporting integration, including successful match updates with other integrations still failed or unconfirmed. Neither acquisition success nor partial integration success may be reported as full application-data synchronization. F02 owns integration consistency and guarded absence reconciliation; F13 owns common operational visibility.
- **FR-023**: Diagnostics MUST expose source, scope, time, outcome, available counts and failure reasons without personal contact data, credentials or automatic raw CSV/HTML archives. Useful normalized observations are distinct from raw-document archives. Diagnostic retention and delivery mechanisms belong to F11/F13; this specification does not choose a duration or monitoring architecture.

#### Clubs and geocoding

- **FR-024**: Club-fiche acquisition MUST target clubs encountered through covered competitions, with a first collection attempt on discovery and subsequent collection every 24 hours. It MUST NOT independently enumerate the entire FFVB club directory.
- **FR-025**: A failed club-fiche read MUST remain distinct from the club's disappearance and MUST NOT trigger club deactivation or erasure of prior details. Available name, address, city/postal code and contact/website observations MUST preserve field-level unknown/invalid distinctions; F11 governs permitted collection and retention.
- **FR-026**: A newly required or changed-locality geocoding need supplied by F02 MUST receive a collection attempt within one hour while collection is enabled and the provider is available. F02 defines municipality/postal context and coordinate changes; a street-only change is not a new municipal-geocoding need; acquisition MUST NOT invent coordinates.
- **FR-027**: A correct response with no usable geocoding candidate or ambiguous candidates MUST be distinguishable from technical failure. Routine collection MUST NOT repeatedly query that same unchanged unresolved address. A changed geocoding locality context, as defined by F02, makes it eligible again.
- **FR-028**: An explicit manual geocoding-family relaunch MUST retry still-unresolved addresses, including unchanged addresses with previous empty or ambiguous responses, while respecting normal permissions and pause rules. It MUST NOT recalculate valid results merely because the family was relaunched.
- **FR-029**: Geocoding MUST supply the address context and successful, empty, ambiguous or technical-failure outcome to F02. A failed request MUST NOT be transformed into a definitive no-result observation or an instruction to erase accepted coordinates. Technical failure follows FR-037/FR-042 rather than the unchanged-address suppression in FR-027.

#### Cadence and controls

- **FR-030**: Eligible competition collection MUST use a normal 30-minute cadence and a five-minute cadence for a pool having at least one reliably scheduled match from one hour before through four hours after kickoff, on any weekday. Unknown kickoff MUST NOT create a fast window. Missing results MUST NOT extend it. At subsequent cycles, corrected known kickoff information determines the applicable window; windows from any other eligible match still apply.
- **FR-031**: Discovery MUST run every 30 minutes while enabled, even when no pools are currently eligible. These and FR-030 cadences define scheduled due times, not guarantees of provider publication or application display freshness. Overruns, pauses and unavailable configuration follow FR-033–FR-035; unavailable sources remain visible under the incident rules.
- **FR-032**: Operators MUST have independently pausable families for FFVB competitions, LNV enrichments, club fiches and geocoding. These are functional controls, not a requirement for four services. A pause in one family MUST NOT implicitly pause another, although unsatisfied source prerequisites still apply.
- **FR-033**: Pause MUST allow the current family cycle to finish and prevent a subsequent one. A current cycle is the work selected at its start, not an entire historical reconstruction. A family with no running cycle becomes paused immediately. Resume MUST restore normal eligibility and scheduling without missed-cycle replay.
- **FR-034**: A cycle MUST use reliable configuration selected at its start. Classification, exclusion, division activation and season changes MUST apply to acquisition from the next cycle. Without reliable configuration at start, collection MUST NOT use remembered settings. Previously discovered references under FR-006 do not authorize configuration fallback. Finishing in-flight acquisition does not authorize F02 integration to undo current visibility or presentation rules.
- **FR-035**: The same collection target MUST NOT run concurrently with itself. When a run overruns due times, it MUST finish, discard missed due times and resume at the next scheduled due time, without an immediate catch-up run or accumulated backlog. The overrun MUST be visible to operators as a cadence deviation.
- **FR-036**: Normal manual relaunch MUST operate at family level, not through individual pool/club commands. A request during an existing run MUST return its current state without starting or queuing duplicate work. Relaunch MUST respect current eligibility and MUST NOT implicitly resume a paused family; explicit resume is a separate operation. Exceptional historical work follows FR-048.
- **FR-037**: Technical retries MUST be bounded and respect provider access/rate constraints. Exhausted attempts MUST yield an explicit scoped failure, not a success or fabricated empty response. Retry parameters and scheduling mechanics are technical-phase decisions constrained by this specification's observable cadence and incident outcomes.
- **FR-038**: Pause, resume, relaunch, season controls and acquisition-setting changes MUST require explicit exploitation permissions. Ordinary users and live moderators without those permissions MUST be denied. F12 owns assignment and common authorization behavior; F02 owns the administrative mapping semantics.

#### Incidents and history

- **FR-039**: Operators MUST be able to distinguish never collected, awaiting classification, excluded, paused, running, successfully observed, degraded/failed acquisition and failed/unconfirmed integration. These are required distinctions, not a prescribed state-machine or screen layout. Latest observation and reasons MUST remain understandable even when work is paused.
- **FR-040**: A structural acquisition anomaly or season/context mismatch MUST raise an immediate actionable incident. Notification must not wait for three scheduled failures in these cases.
- **FR-041**: Transient competition failures MUST raise an incident after three consecutive failed scheduled cycles for the same affected scope. HTTP retries within a cycle count only once, and failed manual relaunches MUST NOT substitute for scheduled failures. The same scheduled-observation rule applies to the empty-calendar threshold. An observed success ends a transient-failure sequence; a non-empty accepted calendar ends an empty sequence. Other completed scheduled outcomes break consecutiveness of that condition; skipped work does not add an observation. An already raised incident remains open until FR-043 establishes recovery.
- **FR-042**: Technical club/geocoding failure MUST receive another attempt before 24 hours and raise an incident if the technical outage persists for 24 hours. The age is measured from the initial failed observation, not from the ordinary daily refresh due time. An operator pause prevents attempts without inventing recovery; no provider-failure claim is inferred solely from work deliberately not attempted. Valid empty/ambiguous geocoding responses are not outages.
- **FR-043**: Recovery MUST require a new usable observation of the affected scope demonstrating that the reported condition has ended. Pause, exclusion, closure, another scope's success or acquisition success with an unresolved integration failure MUST NOT announce that incident as recovered. A manual successful observation MAY establish actual recovery; it does not manufacture missing scheduled failures.
- **FR-044**: Each incident MUST remain visible and produce one opening notification and one notification on actual recovery, without periodic reminder notifications. Persistent repeated failures MUST NOT create duplicate opening notifications. Different conditions remain distinguishable; ending a network outage does not conceal a newly observed content anomaly. Delivery and grouping mechanisms belong to F13.
- **FR-045**: Newly detected seasons and classification needs MUST be grouped into actionable operator signals rather than one notification per element. Deliberate pause/exclusion and ordinary classification waiting MUST NOT be labeled as provider outages.
- **FR-046**: Initial V2 reconstruction MUST target the current season and three completed seasons, subject to obtainable source data, through a controlled operation with prepared and verified historical references. It MUST NOT require a generic product that automatically discovers every archive.
- **FR-047**: Controlled historical acquisition MUST use prepared and verified references for the requested season; neither active-season status nor presence in the current catalog is required. Effective classification, enabled-division status, coverage and exclusion rules, exploitation permissions, reliable operation settings, observation validation and source-context checks MUST still apply. This operation MUST NOT serve as fallback for normal current collection after discovery failure. Missing history MUST NOT be replaced by a current-season feed or other wrong-season content. An unavailable source is an explicit gap, not a guarantee that the season contained no data.
- **FR-048**: Authorized exceptional recollection of a closed season MUST be possible without reactivating periodic collection for that season. It MAY run while the affected current-collection family is deliberately paused; the historical operation is explicitly authorized separately and MUST NOT resume current collection implicitly. The product does not require simultaneous uninterrupted current collection or a generic operator URL editor for this rare controlled operation.
- **FR-049**: Reconstruction MUST report requested coverage, obtained observations, rejected data and unavailable sources by season/source scope. Sampling some obtainable archives MUST NOT be presented as exhaustive reconstruction or migration readiness. F14 owns the resulting go/no-go and recovery criteria; F02 owns consequences of reintegration.

### Key Entities _(include if feature involves data)_

- **Source reference**: A provider-scoped locator and native identifiers with season and competition context; not a selected business primary key or permanent URL.
- **Discovery scope/catalog**: An official grouping whose successful interpretation establishes which child references are discoverable. Its boundaries determine which discovery failed and where confirmed absence can be established; previously successful same-season references remain subject to current eligibility.
- **Source pack / pool classification**: A native pool grouping and effective division/format/gender classification used for acquisition eligibility. It does not replace the durable Pool or Division concepts owned by F02.
- **Observation**: Evidence obtained for a source and time, with scope, completeness, relevant values and reasons. It is distinct from accepted business state and downstream publication.
- **Collection family / cycle**: An independently controlled family of acquisition work and its bounded set selected at cycle start. No process, queue or deployment topology is prescribed.
- **Geocoding need**: A locality-context request supplied by F02 and its result; the acquisition domain does not own durable Club coordinates.
- **Acquisition incident**: A scoped condition requiring operator attention, with evidence of opening and recovery. This concept does not prescribe a separate incident-management product.

## Success Criteria _(mandatory)_

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: Across A01–A07, eligible current or permitted last-known references are included and excluded, unclassified, disabled-division or inactive-season references are omitted, with zero acquisition-based deletion requests. Discovery failure never creates a reference or falsely confirms absence.
- **SC-002**: Across A08–A15, globally unusable calendars/standings are rejected, local match anomalies are isolated, and incomplete observations authorize zero absence-based withdrawals. A08 retains the 99 usable matches; no invented replacement values are supplied.
- **SC-003**: A16–A18 demonstrate discovery for all three professional championships and their supplied phase examples, including changed seasonal references; no fixed sampled identifier is required to remain valid in another season. Actual future phase availability remains a provider dependency.
- **SC-004**: Under enabled, available-provider, reliable-configuration and non-overrun conditions, A19–A24 satisfy the 30-minute discovery/base cadence, five-minute fast cadence, daily club refresh and one-hour geocoding-attempt bound. Deviations caused by failure or overrun are distinguishable from successful timely acquisition.
- **SC-005**: Across A25–A28, each pause blocks the next cycle, every unauthorized command is denied, and duplicate requests produce zero duplicate runs or missed-cycle backlog.
- **SC-006**: Across A13 and A29–A32, opening signals occur at the specified observation/time threshold, each incident has at most one opening and one actual-recovery notification, and deliberate pause/exclusion or unrelated successes produce zero false recovery notifications.
- **SC-007**: A21–A22 distinguish all four geocoding outcomes and issue zero automatic repeat requests for unchanged addresses already resolved as empty/ambiguous, while explicit family relaunch remains available.
- **SC-008**: Across A33–A36, operators can account for every requested reconstruction scope as obtained, rejected or unavailable without raw-document archives, personal diagnostic payloads or claims that sampled availability proves full reconstruction.

## Assumptions

- The current application remains the functional baseline, not the implementation architecture to reproduce. The accepted scope deliberately changes the V1 behaviors listed below; speculative social, fantasy, player-roster or additional-provider features are excluded.
- Official FFVB/LNV sources remain external dependencies and may change or omit data. A recognized catalog can be empty; HTML structure recognition cannot prove that a provider published everything it intended. Provenance and scoped outcomes express these limits.
- Time-window examples use reliable kickoff instants supplied by F02. Source-time interpretation and user-facing timezone/day semantics belong to F02/F03; no new midnight/default-time convention is selected here.
- Cadence denotes scheduled due times rather than a delay measured after completion. Pauses have separate explicit resume; a manual relaunch does not override eligibility. These defaults clarify the approved non-overlap and operator-control decisions.
- The allowed reset concerns business-data reconstruction only. [Identity and Pro continuity](../../docs/product/identity-and-pro-continuity.md) still constrains F14: this specification authorizes no deletion of Auth0 accounts or valid paid access.
- The [constitution](../../.specify/memory/constitution.md) and [V1 domain model](../../docs/architecture/blockout-domain-model-v1.md) remain applicable. This specification adds source-observation and control behavior, not new ownership of Club, Team, Pool or Match. [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md) defines mapping overrides, source authority and sporting lifecycle semantics; no V2 service topology or constitution amendment is selected.
- Completion of this document is not acceptance of the entire functional corpus, proof of provider/runtime acceptance tests, or permission to start technical planning. [#247](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/247) remains the whole-corpus gate.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-09-16

These are the owner's accepted clarifications incorporated into the requirements, not unanswered questions or a second execution workflow.

- Q: Does catalog absence need repeated confirmation? → A: No; discoverability determines current collection. A usable catalog omitting a pool stops it immediately, without deleting business data. (FR-005, FR-019)
- Q: Should a failed discovery reuse previous references? → A: Yes, previously successful same-season references remain usable under current eligibility while discovery is retried; confirmed absence still stops that source collection. (FR-005–FR-006)
- Q: Can an individual classification override pack exclusion? → A: No; exclusion dominates. A classified pool may proceed only when its parent is merely unclassified, not excluded. (FR-009–FR-010)
- Q: What is the rejection boundary for unreliable input? → A: Reject globally unusable calendar structure/context, isolate local matches including contradictory same-reference rows, and preserve usable secondary fields. Incomplete calendars never justify absence-based withdrawals. (FR-011–FR-014, FR-019)
- Q: How much historical tooling is required? → A: A controlled reconstruction of three completed seasons plus current, with prepared references and a gap report; exceptional later recollection may pause current collection. (FR-046–FR-049)
- Q: How should geocoding and relaunch remain simple? → A: Attempt new needs within one hour; use family-only relaunch and include unchanged unresolved addresses, without recomputing valid results. (FR-026–FR-028, FR-036)
- Q: What happens after an overrun? → A: Finish, discard missed due times and resume at the next scheduled due time, without immediate catch-up. (FR-035)
- Q: Should persistent incidents send reminders? → A: Notify opening and actual recovery only; retries are not separate scheduled cycles and pause/exclusion is not recovery. (FR-041–FR-044)
