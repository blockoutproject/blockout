# Functional specification: stream-link contributions and moderation

**Feature branch**: `feature/266-live-contributions-moderation`

**Created on**: 2026-09-22

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline under [planning authority #9](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/9); technical-dossier acceptance and implementation evidence remain separate.

**Requested scope**: [#266](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout-legacy/issues/266), F08 in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications). Define contribution, ownership, publication, quotas, reports and moderation of stream links associated with matches. [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md) owns sporting truth, [F03](../007-sporting-consultation/spec.md) consultation, [F05](../005-accounts-identity/spec.md) identity, [F11](../004-advertising-privacy-legal/spec.md) privacy and [F13](../003-shared-quality/spec.md) shared quality. No video hosting, permanent stream monitoring, technical contract or mockup is introduced.

## Clarifications — approved KISS revision, 2026-10-06

- Q: Does KISS remove proposal history or moderation rules? → A: No. Keep concurrent proposals requiring validation, the first eligible immediate live publication, seven-day account age, existing quotas, three/ten report thresholds and moderator reactivation of every retained old version. Reactivation replaces the active link, retains its author and starts a new report period, without renewing F07 match-level announcements. F08 publication eligibility at H−1 remains distinct from F07 announcement at H.

## User scenarios and validation _(mandatory)_

The actors are the visitor, the signed-in person with a usable profile, the contribution owner, the reporter and the person with applicable moderation permissions. Free or Pro entitlements do not alter contribution eligibility. FR, A and SC identifiers are local to F08. These scenarios express expected V2 outcomes, not already successful V1 tests.

### User story 1 — Propose a link at the authorized time (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to share a stream link for an eligible match and understand the conditions or refusal reasons.

**Priority rationale**: Publication must be useful without bypassing rights, sporting restrictions or moderation.

**Independent validation**: Present eligible and refused accounts, matches and links around account-age and publication boundaries.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a visitor, an unavailable profile or a person without publication permission, **when** a contribution is requested, **then** no mutation occurs: sign-in, profile retry and permission refusal are distinguished. Sign-in does not silently submit the link; a free user meeting the conditions has the same possibilities as a Pro user. (FR-001)
2. **A02** — **Given** a YouTube, Twitch or Facebook video, live stream, channel or streaming-page address, **when** proposed, **then** that type is accepted subject to other conditions. An empty, malformed address, another platform or a domain imitating an authorized platform is rejected without creation. The authorized link is presented as “Stream link”, without inferring evidence of live streaming or replay from its existence. (FR-002)
3. **A03** — **Given** a covered professional match, including an LNV phase without an identifiable FFVB equivalent, **when** a user or moderator proposes a contribution link, **then** the action is refused independently of the provider code; F03 professional media remains a separate journey. (FR-003)
4. **A04** — **Given** an Auth0 principal created less than seven days, exactly seven days or more ago, **when** ordinary publication is requested, **then** the first is refused and the others may proceed subject to other conditions. An already known reliable date remains usable during a provider outage; without reliable evidence, only the dependent action is suspended with retry. Recreating the business profile does not make the retained account younger. (FR-004, FR-005)
5. **A05** — **Given** a confirmed start H and no definitive result, **when** an eligible user publishes just before H−1 then at H−1, **then** the first attempt is refused and the second is published immediately in the absence of a moderation wait. In Paris and New York, the same instant yields the same decision; the display time zone does not shift the boundary. (FR-006)
6. **A06** — **Given** a dated match without a confirmed time, **when** ordinary publication is requested before a definitive result, **then** it is suspended until time confirmation; no assumed midnight authorizes H−1. Without a usable date, the F03 public restriction remains applicable. (FR-007, FR-033)
7. **A07** — **Given** a match with an F02 definitive result, **when** an eligible user proposes a first link or an authorized change, **then** the proposal awaits approval even if the time remains unknown. A provisional score, a publication whose definitive nature is unconfirmed or mere presence in “Completed” after six hours does not trigger this post-match journey. (FR-008)
8. **A08** — **Given** an already accepted link and a pending proposal, **when** the date or result is corrected, **then** subsequent publications reassess current conditions, the accepted link is not deleted solely for the date correction, and the proposal is never published automatically by the status change. (FR-009)
9. **A09** — **Given** a person with both publication and moderation permission, **when** they publish on an eligible match, **then** account age, the H−1 window and quotas do not block them, including with an unknown time; the link may be accepted directly after the match. Administration permission without the action-specific permissions is insufficient, and professional, address and visibility restrictions remain. (FR-027)

### User story 2 — Manage my link without losing one that remains useful (Priority: P1)

As a contributor, I want to correct or withdraw my link and understand what is published or pending.

**Priority rationale**: A proposal must not by itself interrupt consultation of an authorized link or grant access to another person’s contribution.

**Independent validation**: Replace a link before and after the match, reject or cancel a proposal, then act on an ownerless link.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A10** — **Given** an active link, **when** a pre-match replacement is submitted under immediate-publication conditions, **then** its authorized owner may replace it, but another ordinary user is refused. The old version remains historical and only one active link remains. (FR-010, FR-011, FR-012)
2. **A11** — **Given** the owner’s active post-match link, **when** they propose a new address, **then** the old link remains public while waiting. Approval replaces it; rejection leaves the authorized old link in place. Independent deletion or hiding of the old link while waiting is not undone by later rejection. (FR-013, FR-029, FR-030)
3. **A12** — **Given** an author’s pending proposal, **when** they submit another eligible address within their quotas, **then** the first becomes superseded. If current conditions require approval, the new one remains pending and the authorized active link stays unchanged; if they allow immediate publication, the new one becomes active under applicable ownership rights. The author retains at most one pending proposal for that match. A refused attempt preserves the previous proposal. (FR-009, FR-011–FR-014, FR-019)
4. **A13** — **Given** their own pending proposal, **when** the author views its state then cancels it, **then** cancellation is explicit, does not erase the retained public link and refunds no quota. They neither view nor cancel another person’s proposal and do not access moderation history. (FR-015)
5. **A14** — **Given** an active link and a possible replacement proposal, **when** the authorized owner withdraws the active link, **then** only that version is withdrawn; the proposal is neither implicitly published nor cancelled. Withdrawing the same version again does not withdraw another link that appeared meanwhile. (FR-016, FR-036)
6. **A15** — **Given** a useful link retained without an owner after account deletion, **when** a person registers again or wants to replace it, **then** they do not recover ownership; authorized moderation intervention remains possible. In the absence of an active link, an eligible user may propose one under current rules, without inherited ownership. (FR-011, FR-034)
7. **A16** — **Given** an active link and a pending post-match proposal, **when** the definitive result is withdrawn or the match rescheduled, **then** no pending replacement is implicitly approved; the active link remains subject to current visibility and restrictions. If the owner then submits a third eligible address after result withdrawal, with H−1 crossed, a confirmed time and no report-related wait, this new version immediately replaces the active link and supersedes their previous proposal. If a report-related wait persists, the new eligible version remains pending; if the new submission is refused, including before the new H−1 or because a quota is reached, the active link and previous proposal stay unchanged. (FR-006–FR-009, FR-012–FR-014, FR-019, FR-024, FR-033)

### User story 3 — Understand contribution limits (Priority: P1)

As a contributor, I want to know my limits without losing quota because of a refusal or network retry.

**Priority rationale**: Quotas must treat published versions and versions submitted to moderation consistently.

**Independent validation**: Submit versions on several matches and days, with refusals, cancellations and lost responses.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A17** — **Given** two recorded versions for an author and match, one pre-match and one post-match pending, **when** a third then fourth version are proposed, **then** the third may be recorded and the fourth is refused. Rejection, cancellation, replacement or hiding of previous versions does not free a slot. (FR-017, FR-019)
2. **A18** — **Given** versions recorded on three distinct matches during the Paris day, **when** a new version concerns a fourth match to which the author already contributed yesterday, **then** it is refused. Another version of one of the three matches already counted today remains possible within its version limit. (FR-018, FR-019)
3. **A19** — **Given** a reached daily quota, **when** the next Paris midnight is crossed, including on a daylight-saving change day, **then** the daily quota may restart without renewing the three versions per match. In New York, the deadline is understandable in local context but remains the same instant; changing the phone time zone renews no quota. (FR-018)
4. **A20** — **Given** an already recorded active contribution or pending proposal, **when** the same unchanged submission is repeated or retried after response loss, **then** no additional version or quota consumption occurs; this remains true when the first submission reached the quota. Repeating an old rejected or withdrawn submission does not reactivate it. (FR-020, FR-037)
5. **A21** — **Given** a refused attempt and an authorized moderator’s approval or reactivation of an existing version, **when** quotas are examined, **then** the attempt consumed nothing and moderation creates no new version charged to the author. Direct moderator publication follows their exception without granting it to the user. (FR-019, FR-020, FR-027)

### User story 4 — Report a link without circumventing moderation (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to report the problematic link I viewed and prevent its immediate republication after hiding.

**Priority rationale**: A report must target the correct version and produce an effect independent of circumvention attempts.

**Independent validation**: Report a version repeatedly, reach its thresholds, propose replacements then reactivate a version.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A22** — **Given** another person’s public link, **when** a signed-in authorized person reports it with a nonempty reason, **then** the report is accepted; the seven-day account age for publication is not required to report. A visitor must sign in, a missing reason is refused, and the owner uses link withdrawal rather than self-reporting. (FR-021)
2. **A23** — **Given** a report already accepted for a version and its current period, **when** the same user repeats it, **then** the count does not increase. If the link has been replaced since display, the action does not transfer to the new link; the situation must be refreshed before another action. (FR-022, FR-036)
3. **A24** — **Given** an active link, **when** distinct users record reports, **then** two reports do not hide it before the sporting end and the third hides it; after a definitive result, nine do not hide it and the tenth does. The threshold uses F02, not the F03 category. (FR-023)
4. **A25** — **Given** unresolved report-based hiding before the sporting end, **when** the former author or another user proposes the same or a new address, **then** every eligible proposal awaits approval even if H−1 has been crossed. Quotas, permissions and other ordinary conditions continue to apply. (FR-024)
5. **A26** — **Given** a hidden version with its report history, **when** a moderator reactivates it, **then** a new period begins with a zero count; old reports remain historical. A user who already reported may report once in the new period without reimporting the old count. (FR-026, FR-031)
6. **A27** — **Given** existing reports, **when** the sporting result changes, **then** that change neither reactivates a hidden link nor alone causes new hiding. The next newly accepted report evaluates the period total with the then-applicable threshold; a mere duplicate is not a new report. (FR-022, FR-023)
7. **A28** — **Given** unresolved hiding and a pending proposal, **when** that proposal is rejected or cancelled, **then** prior approval remains required for subsequent proposals. Approval, authorized direct publication by a moderator or explicit reactivation resolves this report-related wait; after the match, ordinary prior approval remains. (FR-024, FR-025)

### User story 5 — Moderate with an understanding of versions and decisions (Priority: P1)

As an authorized moderator, I want to examine proposals and reports, explicitly decide the retained link and preserve understandable history.

**Priority rationale**: Moderation decisions must resolve situations without hidden effects on another version.

**Independent validation**: Examine a queue with several proposals, approve, reject, withdraw or reactivate versions in varied states.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A29** — **Given** matches with active, pending or hidden versions, **when** an authorized person views moderation, **then** they can find matches, distinguish states and examine useful versions, reports and decisions. Missing permission prohibits this information even through old access; an error is not an empty queue. (FR-027, FR-028, FR-039)
2. **A30** — **Given** several pending proposals for the same match, **when** a moderator approves one, **then** it becomes the sole active link, the old active link is replaced and every other proposal becomes superseded. Their authors see that another proposal was selected, without access to others’ private details. (FR-029, FR-032)
3. **A31** — **Given** a pending proposal, **when** a moderator rejects it, **then** it becomes rejected without withdrawing the retained active link or resolving an independent wait arising from reports. A version no longer pending cannot be approved or rejected as though it were. (FR-030, FR-035)
4. **A32** — **Given** a rejected, voluntarily withdrawn, hidden, replaced or superseded version, **when** a moderator explicitly reactivates it, **then** replacement of any active link is identifiable, other proposals become superseded and a new reporting period begins. Merely reading history or replaying an old action does not cause reactivation. (FR-031, FR-032, FR-037)
5. **A33** — **Given** two concurrent approvals or publications, **when** they concern the same match, **then** at most one active link remains; the action that became obsolete does not silently replace the more recent decision. Simultaneous submissions cannot exceed quotas. (FR-010, FR-017, FR-018, FR-035)
6. **A34** — **Given** a match hidden for an F02/F03 reason, **when** a moderator examines or handles its contributions, **then** necessary information remains private and no link publication or reactivation re-exposes the match. Later sporting reappearance does not lift still-applicable moderation reasons. (FR-028, FR-033)

### User story 6 — Recover a reliable state after an interruption (Priority: P1)

As a user or moderator, I want to understand the actual outcome of my action and retry without duplicates or loss of rights.

**Priority rationale**: A lost response, account deletion or failed read must not implicitly change sporting or moderation decisions.

**Independent validation**: Interrupt responses, change a version from another session, delete an account then examine remaining authorized data.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A35** — **Given** an old view of a link, **when** deletion, cancellation or a report is sent after its replacement or reactivation, **then** the action stays tied to the targeted version and period without affecting the new link or period. A conflict is explicit and allows refresh. (FR-036)
2. **A36** — **Given** an accepted publication, report or decision whose response is lost, **when** the action is checked or retried, **then** its outcome is recovered without an unintended new version, consumption, report or reporting period. Uncertainty is not presented as definite failure. (FR-020, FR-022, FR-026, FR-037)
3. **A37** — **Given** an action confirmed successful followed by a failed page or history read, **when** the outcome is presented, **then** success remains confirmed and the read can be retried separately; no arbitrary return to the old link or mutation repetition follows. (FR-038)
4. **A38** — **Given** deletion of an account that contributed or reported, **when** F05/F11 apply erasure, **then** a useful contribution may remain ownerless, hiding remains applied and no new registration recovers ownership. Personal attribution is processed under applicable rules, without automatically erasing decisions or resetting counts because of that erasure. (FR-034)
5. **A39** — **Given** pending, refused, superseded, quota-reached or uncertain states, **when** the person views their journey, **then** the state, relevant reason and authorized retry are understandable in French and accessible under F13 without relying only on color. An independent outage does not erase still-authorized information and no other person’s history is exposed. (FR-015, FR-039)
6. **A40** — **Given** an actually accepted publication, replacement, approval, withdrawal, hiding or reactivation, **when** consuming scopes use that transition, **then** they can distinguish it from a refused attempt or unchanged repetition. F07 decides notifications; F08 promises neither recipients nor push receipt. Visual journeys remain to be covered by R02 before technical planning. (FR-040, FR-041)

### Edge cases

- An unknown start does not become midnight, classification in “Completed” does not become a definitive result, and a link does not become evidence of live streaming (A02, A06–A08).
- A pending proposal and active link may coexist; their deletion, rejection and replacement are distinct actions (A11–A14).
- Quotas remain consumed despite a later negative decision; approval of an existing version is not a new submission (A17–A21).
- Report-based hiding requires approval for all new authors; resolving it does not waive post-match approval (A25–A28).
- Reactivation opens a reporting period, not a new match identity or address version; replaying it does not reset the count (A26, A32, A36).
- Visibility, moderation and privacy restrictions accumulate; neither reappearance nor account deletion implicitly erases them (A34, A38).

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional requirements

#### Eligibility and time

- **FR-001**: Contribution MUST be accessible to signed-in people with a usable profile and publication permission, without a Pro condition. A visitor MUST be able to enter the sign-in journey without automatic publication on return. Unavailable profile, missing permission and absence of sign-in MUST remain distinct. Checks MUST apply to the action even if its entry point was previously displayed.
- **FR-002**: Valid YouTube, Twitch and Facebook web addresses for a video, live stream, channel or streaming page MUST be accepted subject to other conditions. An empty, malformed address or one outside authorized domains MUST be refused; a similar name or the platform name appearing in another address is insufficient. The link MUST reuse F03’s neutral “Stream link” label. No permanent availability, live or replay monitoring, automatic video search or new platform is required.
- **FR-003**: Contributions to professional matches covered by F02 MUST be forbidden, including for moderators and phases without an FFVB match. The prohibition comes from sporting context, not an isolated provider code. Professional media acquired and presented by F01/F03 remains distinct from this contribution.
- **FR-004**: Ordinary publication MUST require at least seven elapsed days since creation of the retained Auth0 principal under F05. The exact boundary is eligible. Business profile creation or recreation, migration, the linked sign-in method and reinstall MUST NOT replace this evidence. Voluntary deletion followed by new registration follows F05 without recovering erased account age.
- **FR-005**: Already known reliable evidence MUST remain usable during provider unavailability. Without reliable evidence, dependent publication MUST be suspended with retry, without an invented date or blocking other authorized actions. The account-age exception requires established moderation permission under FR-027.
- **FR-006**: Before an F02 definitive result, ordinary immediate publication MUST be allowed from one elapsed hour before the confirmed start, subject to other conditions and outside the FR-024 moderation wait. Before that boundary it MUST be refused. The same instant yields the same decision regardless of display time zone; F03 owns local presentation.
- **FR-007**: A known date without a confirmed time MUST suspend ordinary publication before a definitive result until time confirmation. H−1 MUST NOT be calculated from an assumed midnight. Absence of any usable date remains subject to F03 public hiding. The window exception for a person authorized to moderate follows FR-027, without a visibility exception.
- **FR-008**: After a definitive result recognized under F02, every eligible ordinary addition or replacement MUST await prior approval, even without a known time. F03’s “Completed” category, elapsed time alone, a provisional score or unconfirmed definitive nature MUST NOT trigger this journey. F02 also interprets special sporting results; no universal match-end arbitration is added.
- **FR-009**: A date or result correction MUST reassess conditions for subsequent publications. Date correction alone MUST NOT withdraw an already accepted link, and a sporting status change MUST NOT automatically publish a pending proposal. Current visibility and moderation restrictions remain applicable.

#### Versions, ownership and proposals

- **FR-010**: A match MUST have at most one active contribution version at a time. Public visibility of this version remains conditional on FR-033. A pending proposal MUST NOT become a second public link.
- **FR-011**: An ordinary user MUST neither replace nor withdraw another owner’s active link, including after the match. In the absence of an active link, any eligible person MAY propose a link under current rules. An ownerless contribution does not become any new user’s property; only authorized moderation intervention allows action on that active link.
- **FR-012**: Before a definitive result and outside the FR-024 wait, a new eligible version submitted by the owner MUST immediately replace their active version. The previous version MUST remain identifiable as replaced in moderation history. A refusal MUST alter neither the active link nor an already recorded proposal.
- **FR-013**: When an ordinary replacement awaits post-match approval, the active version MUST remain visible while still authorized. Approval alone performs that replacement; rejection or cancellation of the proposal MUST neither delete the active link nor restore a link independently withdrawn or hidden while waiting.
- **FR-014**: An author MUST have at most one pending proposal per match. Recording a different new eligible version MUST supersede their previous pending proposal. Current conditions MUST determine the new version’s outcome: awaiting approval without changing the authorized active link, or immediate publication under FR-006/FR-012 and FR-011 rights. The mere presence of a pending proposal MUST NOT impose approval on a new submission that has become eligible for immediate publication after a sporting correction; that correction alone does not publish the previous proposal. Proposals from different authors may coexist when eligible, including after hiding; resolution follows FR-032.
- **FR-015**: The author MUST be able to know their proposal’s state and cancel one still awaiting approval, with the applicable withdrawal permission. Cancellation MUST neither withdraw the retained public link nor refund quotas. The author MUST access neither others’ proposals nor moderation history; a rejected, cancelled or superseded state must remain understandable within their own journey.
- **FR-016**: The owner with withdrawal permission MUST be able to withdraw their active version without account-age, window or publication-quota conditions. Withdrawal MUST preserve the version in authorized history and targets no separate proposal. Repeated deletion of this version MUST NOT withdraw another link; others’ contributions require FR-027 permissions.

#### Quotas and repetitions

- **FR-017**: An ordinary user MUST be limited to three recorded versions per match, counting all their pre- and post-match versions. The third version may be recorded; the fourth MUST be refused. This limit is not renewed daily.
- **FR-018**: An ordinary user MUST be limited to three distinct matches to which they submit a new version during the Paris calendar day. A match already contributed to on another day counts upon a new version today; multiple versions of the same match today count as one daily match. The period runs from one Paris midnight to the next, including 23- or 25-hour days. The user’s time zone does not change it; the retry deadline MUST be understandable to them under F03.
- **FR-019**: Every new recorded version, active or pending, MUST consume applicable quotas. Later rejection, cancellation, replacement, superseding, hiding or withdrawal MUST NOT refund that consumption. A refused attempt MUST consume nothing and invalidate no previously authorized state.
- **FR-020**: Resending an unchanged already recorded active version or pending proposal and retrying the same submission MUST recover its outcome without a new version or consumption, even if recording it reached a quota. A repetition MUST neither reactivate a withdrawn or rejected version nor overwrite a more recent state. Approving or reactivating an existing version MUST NOT count as a new submission by its author.

#### Reports and moderation wait

- **FR-021**: A signed-in person with a usable profile and reporting permission MUST be able to report another person’s public link with a mandatory nonempty reason. Publication account age and contribution quotas MUST NOT be required to report. The owner uses link withdrawal instead of self-reporting; the visitor goes through F05. Report content remains within the authorized moderation process.
- **FR-022**: Reports MUST target the viewed version and its reporting period. Each new version begins its own period with a zero count, without inheriting reports from another version. Only one report per user MUST count for that version and period; a duplicate or retry MUST neither increase the count nor target another version or period. FR-036 governs changes during the action.
- **FR-023**: A newly accepted report MUST evaluate the period total with the current threshold: three distinct users before an F02 definitive result, ten afterward. Reaching the threshold MUST hide the version. A result change alone MUST neither recalculate automatic hiding nor reactivate a link; a duplicate is not a new report. The F03 calendar category MUST NOT select the threshold.
- **FR-024**: After unresolved report-based hiding for a match, every new eligible ordinary proposal MUST await approval, regardless of author or address, even before the sporting end. Changing users or republishing the same address MUST NOT bypass this wait. Other ordinary conditions, including account age, window and quotas, remain required.
- **FR-025**: Approval of a replacement, authorized direct publication by a moderator or explicit reactivation MUST resolve the match’s report-related wait. Mere rejection or cancellation of a proposal MUST NOT resolve it. Resolution MUST waive no independent restriction, particularly ordinary post-match prior approval.
- **FR-026**: Explicit reactivation of a version MUST open a new reporting period with a zero count. Old reports MUST remain historical without contributing to the new threshold; a former reporter may report once in the new period. Retrying the same reactivation MUST NOT create yet another period or erase more recent reports.

#### Moderation and transitions

- **FR-027**: Permissions MUST be action-specific. Direct publication and replacement of another person’s link require publication and moderation permissions; withdrawing another person’s active link requires withdrawal and moderation permissions. Moderation consultation, history, approval, rejection and reactivation require moderation permission. For authorized moderator publication, V1 account-age, window and quota exceptions are retained, along with direct post-match publication. They MUST bypass neither FR-002/FR-003, visibility or privacy, nor concurrency checks. An administrative role or Pro does not implicitly supply these permissions; F12 owns assignment.
- **FR-028**: Authorized people MUST be able to view matches concerned by moderation, distinguish states, find a match and examine the history of its useful versions, reports and decisions. States must remain understandable, including multiple proposals or an erased author, without inventing an identity. This information remains private; it MUST NOT publicly re-expose a hidden match. This requirement selects no new technical audit system.
- **FR-029**: Approving a still-pending proposal MUST select it as the active version and explicitly replace any previous active version. Ownership of the approved proposal MUST NOT transfer to the moderator merely through approval. Other proposals follow FR-032; independent visibility restrictions remain applicable.
- **FR-030**: Rejecting a still-pending proposal MUST make it rejected without withdrawing the retained active version or resolving an independent report-related wait. Approval and rejection MUST NOT act as if a proposal were still pending when it no longer is.
- **FR-031**: Rejected, voluntarily withdrawn, report-hidden, replaced or superseded versions MUST be reactivatable through an explicit authorized action. Replacement of any active link MUST be identifiable; the reactivated version retains its identity and remaining ownership. Reporting periods follow FR-026 and proposals follow FR-032. Reactivation MUST bypass neither professional restrictions nor visibility and privacy restrictions.
- **FR-032**: When a link is selected by approval, authorized direct moderation publication or reactivation, other pending proposals for that match MUST become superseded and leave the items awaiting review. They remain historical; their authors MUST understand that another proposal was selected without accessing others’ private information. Reuse requires explicit reactivation, not late approval as if they were still pending.

#### Visibility, identity and reliability

- **FR-033**: F02/F03 restrictions MUST prevail over link visibility: a withdrawn match, hidden parent, qualified calendar-empty restriction, inactive division or missing usable date MUST NOT be bypassed by publication or reactivation. Public contribution journeys follow match viewability; private moderation handling re-exposes no hidden resource. Sporting reappearance MUST NOT lift an independent moderation reason.
- **FR-034**: Account deletion MUST apply F05/F11: a useful contribution may remain ownerless, without ownership recovery on registration or arbitrary transfer to a third party. Erasure of personal attribution MUST preserve decisions and nonpersonal information needed for moderation under applicable rules, without resetting hiding or counts because of that erasure. Personal data in the address or reason remains subject to applicable requests. No global retention duration or indefinite retention of personal identifiers is imposed.
- **FR-035**: Concurrent publications or decisions MUST preserve active-link uniqueness, quotas and more recent accepted decisions. An obsolete action MUST NOT silently overwrite a replacement or decision; its outcome must be explicit and allow refresh before a new intentional action. No technical concurrency mechanism is selected.
- **FR-036**: Withdrawal, cancellation and reporting MUST target the chosen version and, for reporting, the relevant period. Another version or period appearing between display and action MUST NOT be affected. Repetition of an already accepted action may recover that outcome; an unresolved conflict must request refresh without automatically transferring the action.
- **FR-037**: Confirmed success, refusal, confirmed failure and uncertain outcome MUST be distinguished. After response loss, the outcome MUST be checkable or recoverable without an unintended new version, consumption, report or period. Retry MUST NOT undo a more recent restriction or decision; F13 owns shared quality and F07 notification deduplication.
- **FR-038**: Retrieval failure after a confirmed mutation MUST remain distinct from mutation failure. Reading must be retryable separately, without arbitrary restoration of the old link or unsafe action repetition. Still-authorized information remains usable under shared freshness and visibility rules.
- **FR-039**: Journeys MUST distinguish loading, absence, pending work, rejection, cancellation, obsolete intent, restrictions, quota, success, error and uncertainty with understandable French reasons and authorised retries. F13 governs accessibility, honest freshness and diagnostics; an outage is not an empty queue. Private moderation data MUST NOT appear to another person, in public diagnostics or technical user errors.
- **FR-040**: Actually accepted publication, replacement, approval, withdrawal, hiding and reactivation transitions MUST be distinguishable for F07 from refused attempts and unchanged repetitions. F07 owns precise triggers, recipients, preferences, deduplication and delivery; F08 MUST NOT promise a notification for each of its transitions or guaranteed receipt.
- **FR-041**: F08 MUST retain contribution and reporting entry points contextualized with F03/F05, pass action-specific permissions to F12 and personal proposal, active/pending coexistence, quota, conflict and moderation journeys to R02. F10 retains general problem reporting, distinct from F08 link reporting. This specification supplies neither an approved visual design nor a contract, technical plan or implementation task.

### Main entities

- **Match**: F02 sporting identity and context; its viewability and definitive result remain independent of contribution presence and its calendar category.
- **Contribution version**: Stream address, platform, match, possible owner, state and useful time references; reactivation creates no new address version.
- **Pending proposal**: Nonpublic version to review, at most one per author and match; it may coexist with the retained active link.
- **Report**: Reason and report attributable under applicable rules, targeting a version and period; personal data is distinct from the count and hiding decision.
- **Reporting period**: Deduplication and counting scope for a version, renewed by explicit reactivation without erasing authorized history.
- **Report-related approval wait**: Consequence of hiding on new match proposals; distinct from post-match approval and sporting restrictions.
- **Moderation decision**: Authorized action and its understandable effect on versions; functional history does not prescribe an audit architecture.

#### Transition table

| Initial state or situation                                  | Eligible action                                          | Outcome                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| No active link, no moderation wait                          | Ordinary publication before a definitive result from H−1 | New active version.                                                                                                                                          |
| Owner’s active link                                         | Eligible ordinary replacement before a definitive result | New active version; old one replaced.                                                                                                                        |
| After a definitive result or a wait arising from reports    | New eligible ordinary proposal                           | Pending; any authorized active link retained.                                                                                                                |
| Pending proposal by the same author                         | Different new eligible version                           | Old proposal superseded; new one active or pending according to current conditions and ownership rights; active link replaced only on immediate publication. |
| Pending proposal                                            | Cancellation by the authorized author                    | Voluntarily withdrawn; separate active link unchanged.                                                                                                       |
| Pending proposal                                            | Authorized rejection                                     | Rejected; separate active link and report-related wait unchanged.                                                                                            |
| Pending proposal                                            | Authorized approval                                      | Active; old active version replaced; other pending proposals superseded; report-related wait resolved.                                                       |
| Active version                                              | Authorized withdrawal                                    | Voluntarily withdrawn; separate proposals unchanged.                                                                                                         |
| Active version                                              | New report reaching the current threshold                | Hidden; new ordinary proposals subject to approval.                                                                                                          |
| Rejected, withdrawn, hidden, replaced or superseded version | Authorized reactivation                                  | Active; new reporting period; other active version replaced; pending proposals superseded.                                                                   |
| Any contribution context eligible for moderation            | Authorized direct moderation publication                 | New active version owned by the publishing moderator; old active version replaced; pending proposals superseded.                                             |
| Any state                                                   | Sporting reappearance, date or result change             | No implicit moderation approval or reactivation; visibility and subsequent actions reassessed.                                                               |

All transitions respect permissions, targeted versions, concurrency and independent restrictions. “Active” is a contribution state, not evidence of ongoing streaming or a guarantee of public match viewability.

## Success criteria _(mandatory)_

### Measurable outcomes

- **SC-001**: A01–A09 produce zero publications forbidden by account age, window, permission, professional context or unknown time, with only the explicit FR-027 exceptions; exactly H−1 and seven days are eligible subject to other conditions. Paris and New York make the same decision at the same instant; no F03 threshold replaces F02.
- **SC-002**: A10–A16 produce at most one active link and one pending proposal per author and match. Merely making a proposal pending, rejecting it or cancelling it causes zero withdrawal of the still-authorized active link; no other person’s ownership is implicitly acquired. After a sporting correction, every newly recorded version follows current conditions and supersedes the same author’s previous proposal; a correction alone or refused attempt neither publishes nor invalidates that proposal.
- **SC-003**: A17–A21 accept at most three ordinary versions per author and match and three distinct matches per Paris day. Refusals, repetitions and decisions on an existing version consume zero additional versions; pending versions count and negative decisions refund nothing.
- **SC-004**: A22–A28 count at most one report per reporter, version and period, hide at the applicable thresholds of three or ten and produce zero immediate ordinary publications bypassing an unresolved wait. Each intentional reactivation opens only one new period; old reports are not counted again in it.
- **SC-005**: A29–A34 make decisions and versions understandable without obsolete approval or two active links. Selecting a link through moderation leaves zero other pending proposals for that match; superseded versions remain historical and every independent sporting restriction remains applied.
- **SC-006**: A35–A38 produce zero mutations transferred to another version or period, zero duplicated effects on retry and zero ownership recovery after registration. A failed read after confirmed success becomes neither mutation failure nor restoration of older data.
- **SC-007**: A29/A39/A40 distinguish states, partial outcomes and F07/F10/F12/F13/R02 responsibilities without exposing private history, falsely promising notifications or declaring design or implementation complete.

## Assumptions and limits

- Sporting-end and visibility rules belong to F02/F03. F08 adds no maximum interval after H−1 or maximum match age; proposals after a definitive result go through moderation.
- Platforms are recognized by their authorized address; recognition certifies neither the submitter’s rights nor current video accessibility. Official professional links are not user contributions.
- “Three versions” includes the first submission and its subsequent versions on that match, not three additional changes. An identical address actively published or pending is not an additional version; intentionally republishing an old withdrawn version is not a way to reactivate it without current rules and quotas.
- The daily period is based on Paris; displayed dates and deadlines remain consistent with the person’s time zone under F03. No arbitrary 24-hour period replaces the calendar day.
- Superseding distinguishes a replaced or no-longer-relevant proposal from a moderation rejection. These meanings impose neither technical enumeration names nor a new general management interface.
- No guaranteed human-decision deadline, automatic abuse detection, account blocking, video hosting or additional platform integration is required.
- Minimal moderation data and its personal attribution remain subject to F05/F11; no numerical duration or automatic raw archive is inferred from functional history retention.
- The delivery is functional. Global consistency review R01, Figma review R02 and acceptance under #247 precede any technical plan or implementation.
