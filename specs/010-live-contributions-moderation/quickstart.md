### Ordinary interruption and diagnostic boundaries

Lose a version-mutation response and fail its dependent read: ordinary settlement invalidation neither proves rollback nor undoes acknowledged success. A report acknowledgement remains distinct from read success. After response loss, a current authorized reported:true for the exact account/version establishes “already reported” without recovering that acknowledgement. A false or failed read retains the reason and permits deliberate retry of the same account/version; false cannot exclude delayed POST completion. Current authorization refusal removes private data before an outage. F03 match refusal removes the report address and disables sending while preserving the local reason; refusal of only the old report member keeps its exact-target retry.

Inspect safe local feedback and shared diagnostics: business refusal/conflict emits no unexpected-failure event; unexpected SQL/read failure has one bounded shared HTTP diagnostic. Exclude URLs, query strings, reasons, identities, credentials and payloads. F08 adds no telemetry sink or cleanup-content exception; [F13](../003-shared-quality/plan.md#application-diagnostics-and-health) owns the shared diagnostic boundary; crash reporting remains disabled.
