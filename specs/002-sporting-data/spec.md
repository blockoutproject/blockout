# Feature Specification: Sporting Data Identity and Lifecycle

**Feature Branch**: `feature/258-sporting-data`

**Created**: 2026-09-16

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: Owner-approved functional scope for [#258](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/258), including the proportionate-reliability decisions: trust official sporting content, establish its identity and context, isolate local anomalies, and preserve useful successful updates. This document owns F02 in the [specification map](../../docs/product/specification-perimeters.md).

## User Scenarios & Testing _(mandatory)_

Visitors and signed-in users consume sporting information. Operators with explicit domain permissions manage sporting presentation, classifications, divisions and exclusions. Authorized technical operators handle exceptional identity reconciliation; ordinary users and live moderators receive none of these permissions implicitly. Source acquisition supplies observations under [F01](../001-source-acquisition/spec.md), not already accepted sporting state.

Scenario identifiers are local acceptance references, not implementation tasks. The independent tests describe future acceptance evidence; no implemented V2 or production result is claimed.

### User Story 1 - Recognize the same sporting resources (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want corrections and rescheduling to preserve the right teams and matches, so my links and follows do not accidentally refer to replacements.

**Why this priority**: Every score, following relationship and historical view depends on correct attribution.

**Independent Test**: Compare identities and references across controlled calendars with repeated teams, changed labels, rescheduled matches and separate seasons.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a known club entering a new season, **When** its teams and pools are acquired, **Then** the club retains its identity while seasonal teams and pools are distinct. Two named teams of that club in the same division remain distinct; one team in two pools of the same classification retains one identity. (FR-001, FR-002, FR-003, FR-004)
2. **A02** — **Given** a known match, **When** its official date changes, **Then** its identity and attached relationships remain; a repeated encounter or a reused code in another season/context is not merged on date or participant pair alone. (FR-005)
3. **A03** — **Given** source names differing only by case, redundant whitespace or typographic apostrophes, **When** compared in the same identity context, **Then** those differences are equivalent; digits, accents, periods and hyphens remain significant. A verified scoped alias resolves its declared variants while retaining their source labels. (FR-006, FR-007)
4. **A04** — **Given** an existing match whose participant acquires an unrecognized significant name change, **When** the identity cannot be established, **Then** that match retains its previous state and is diagnosed, other usable matches proceed, and no absence-based withdrawal occurs from that incomplete treatment. (FR-008, FR-019)
5. **A05** — **Given** a classified phase with known participants and another fixture labelled with an undetermined qualifier, **When** acquired, **Then** known fixtures can be published, the undetermined fixture creates no fictitious team or consultable match, and ranking-only names create no teams. A recognized future slot is not a malformed participant reference. (FR-009, FR-053)

### User Story 2 - Correct classifications without losing continuity (Priority: P1)

As an authorized operator, I want explicit classification controls and understandable conflict refusals without a general-purpose identity merge interface.

**Why this priority**: Classification changes can affect shared teams and existing user relationships.

**Independent Test**: Apply pack and pool settings to established participations and compare effective classifications, identities and denied-operation outcomes.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A06** — **Given** an unclassified pack, **When** one pool receives a complete division/format/gender override, **Then** that pool can become eligible while the other pools wait. Later pack edits do not change its override. Removing it restores pack inheritance subject to correction checks. If the pack supplies no replacement, collection stops under F01 cycle rules while published resources keep their last accepted classification, identities and visibility; exclusions and inactive divisions still apply. That retained classification does not authorize collection. (FR-011, FR-012)
2. **A07** — **Given** an erroneously classified pool whose affected teams have no conflicting outside participation or target identity, **When** an authorized correction is applied, **Then** pool, teams, matches, follows and presentation overrides retain their identities/relationships under the corrected classification. After a classification gap, restoring the same classification or safely correcting it permits collection again subject to normal eligibility; neither operation lifts an exclusion or inactive-division restriction. (FR-012, FR-013, FR-016, FR-041)
3. **A08** — **Given** a correction affecting several pools, including a team shared with a pool outside the correction or an existing corresponding target team, **When** requested, **Then** the whole requested correction is refused with the conflicting scopes identified; no safe subset is silently applied. If this was an attempt to restore missing acquisition classification, collection remains ineligible and published resources retain their last accepted classification. A narrower request or controlled technical intervention remains possible. (FR-012, FR-014)
4. **A09** — **Given** an authorized division operator, **When** they create or edit a division's name, colors or logo, **Then** its presentation is maintained without replacing its identity. An inactive division cannot be selected for a new classification. (FR-015, FR-047)
5. **A10** — **Given** a populated division, **When** deactivated, **Then** dependent collection stops under F01 cycle rules and its pools, teams and matches become hidden without erasure. **When** reactivated, **Then** resources hidden solely for that reason return immediately with known freshness; other withdrawal reasons still apply. (FR-016, FR-042)

### User Story 3 - Receive useful updates despite isolated failures (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want valid matches to stay current even when another record is unusable, without failures being mistaken for sporting withdrawals.

**Why this priority**: Official data is generally usable; disproportionate rejection reduces its value.

**Independent Test**: Submit mixed-validity calendars, interrupted integrations and replayed observations, then compare accepted matches, retained records and withdrawal permissions.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A11** — **Given** valid references from an earlier discovery, **When** the next catalog read fails, **Then** otherwise eligible collection may continue with those references in the same season while discovery is retried. Without prior successful discovery there is no invented reference; confirmed absence in a later usable catalog stops that source collection. (FR-017)
2. **A12** — **Given** a calendar with 100 matches and one unusable match, **When** treated, **Then** the other 99 may update and the failed match retains prior state. Contradictory rows for one reference isolate that match; identical repetitions create no duplicate. A globally unreadable document or wrong season/pool rejects the calendar instead. (FR-018, FR-019)
3. **A13** — **Given** a usable match with an invalid secondary field and another with normally absent information, **When** integrated, **Then** invalidity preserves the previous usable field, while absence remains explicit rather than becoming zero or a fabricated date. A known official withdrawal of a value follows its field's rule. (FR-020)
4. **A14** — **Given** successful updates followed by an integration failure, **When** treatment ends and is retried, **Then** successes remain visible with valid team/pool references, failures are distinguishable and retried without duplicates, and absence-based withdrawals wait for a complete successful treatment. (FR-019, FR-021, FR-022, FR-048)
5. **A15** — **Given** a newer accepted observation, a manual display override or a currently hidden resource, **When** older work is replayed, **Then** it cannot overwrite the newer accepted state, erase the override or bypass current visibility reasons. (FR-022, FR-045)

### User Story 4 - Read official results and rankings (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want the published sporting information, including professional phases and partial values, without Blockout inventing a replacement result or ranking.

**Why this priority**: The application conveys official information rather than independently adjudicating the sport.

**Independent Test**: Compare accepted values against attributed official observations with conflicts, outages, withdrawn results and unlinked standing rows.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A16** — **Given** a correctly identified official LNV phase without an identifiable FFVB counterpart, **When** its classification and participants are established, **Then** it can be represented from LNV; missing correspondence remains explicit and another phase/season is not substituted. (FR-023)
2. **A17** — **Given** conflicting usable professional observations correctly attributed to the same match, **When** date, result, venue or officials are selected, **Then** the LNV page prevails over LNV XML, which prevails over FFVB. Results and set details are not assembled from incompatible observations. (FR-024)
3. **A18** — **Given** a value confirmed by the higher-priority source, **When** that source is unavailable and a lower-priority source disagrees, **Then** the confirmed value is retained with its freshness; a genuinely unknown value may be complemented. A withdrawn result is not repopulated from a lower-priority source. (FR-025, FR-026, FR-027)
4. **A19** — **Given** a published official result, an explicitly provisional score and an ambiguous score publication, **When** interpreted, **Then** they are respectively final, provisional and finality-unconfirmed; no universal three-set threshold or elapsed-time rule overrides their source meaning. (FR-028)
5. **A20** — **Given** an official overall result of 3–1 with inconsistent or unusable set details, **When** integrated, **Then** 3–1 remains available, the unusable details are not displayed, and the anomaly is diagnosed without invalidating independent date/venue changes. (FR-029)
6. **A21** — **Given** a recognized official forfeit/penalty notation, **When** integrated, **Then** its sporting meaning is retained without invented numeric scores or locally calculated standings. An unrecognized notation is exposed as uninterpreted source evidence to operations, not silently converted to zero. (FR-030)
7. **A22** — **Given** an accepted result, **When** the authoritative source supplies a usable observation of the same match with the result withdrawn, **Then** the displayed result and its attached set details are removed and the match has no confirmed result. A later corrected result can be accepted on the same identity. (FR-026, FR-031)
8. **A23** — **Given** an identifiable match with no date, date only, or FFVB time 00:00 without contrary evidence, **When** integrated, **Then** known date information is retained without inventing an instant or triggering a fast window from an unknown kickoff. Ordinary local source times use their applicable time zone, including daylight-saving changes. (FR-032, FR-033)
9. **A24** — **Given** an exploitable official standing with a team absent from the calendar or not matched to a consultable team, **When** published, **Then** official rows, order, positions and available statistics remain, and that row uses its source label without a Blockout team link. No team is created and no ranks are locally recalculated. (FR-034, FR-035)
10. **A25** — **Given** an unusable or unavailable standing but a usable calendar, **When** updated, **Then** the calendar proceeds and the last usable standing remains with known freshness, or the standing is unavailable if none exists. Missing columns/special ratios do not become numeric zeros or fabricated statistics. A phase publishing no standing does not acquire an invented one. (FR-034, FR-036)

### User Story 5 - Understand disappearance and recover preserved history (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want withdrawn resources to disappear from ordinary consultation without losing identities or history on an outage or seasonal closure.

**Why this priority**: Visibility, sporting outcome and data preservation express different facts.

**Independent Test**: Apply scoped discovery, calendar and administrative observations across current/closed seasons and compare visibility, retained information and reappearance outcomes.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A26** — **Given** a previously visible pool, **When** the usable authoritative catalog for its season omits it, **Then** the pool and its matches become hidden but are retained. For a phase identified on LNV, LNV catalog absence governs; FFVB absence alone does not hide a phase still published by LNV. (FR-037)
2. **A27** — **Given** a complete valid non-empty authoritative calendar successfully integrated, **When** an old match is omitted, **Then** it is marked withdrawn from that calendar and removed from upcoming matches without being declared cancelled; its identity and acquired results remain. Apply the calendar-authority examples below: a secondary calendar cannot contradict the authoritative membership decision. Empty/partial/failed treatment causes no such withdrawal. (FR-019, FR-024, FR-038)
3. **A28** — **Given** a team omitted from that complete authoritative calendar, **When** its participation is withdrawn, **Then** its other pools remain unaffected. Apply the same calendar-authority examples to its participation. A team with no visible pool is hidden; its club remains consultable even with no visible teams. A stale official ranking row does not reinstate participation. (FR-024, FR-039, FR-040)
4. **A29** — **Given** a populated pack or pool, **When** explicitly excluded, **Then** collection and visibility cease for that scope without erasure. Pack exclusion dominates pool overrides; a family pause alone does not hide sporting resources. (FR-041)
5. **A30** — **Given** preserved resources hidden by source absence and possibly other reasons, **When** a usable authoritative catalog rediscovers the pool, **Then** its last known data becomes consultable immediately only if no other reason remains; ordinary collection can refresh it. A known match/participation reappearing in the authoritative calendar reuses its identity; presence only in a secondary calendar does not clear its withdrawal. Reappearance leaves exclusions and inactive-division restrictions intact. Removing an exclusion or reactivating a division does not clear an independent source withdrawal. (FR-024, FR-042)
6. **A31** — **Given** two active seasons and an older acquired season, **When** one season is closed or absent from a different season's catalog, **Then** no historical data is erased or falsely withdrawn. Exceptional historical recollection uses F01 controls and the same sporting rules. V1 reset loss remains confined to the separate migration exception. (FR-010, FR-043)
7. **A32** — **Given** a hidden resource with an existing follow or direct link, **When** a user consults ordinary lists, search or calendars, **Then** it is absent and its old link reports unavailability; its identity and relationships remain for reappearance without automatic transfer to another resource. (FR-044)

### User Story 6 - Maintain presentation and useful club locations (Priority: P2)

As an authorized operator, I want durable presentation corrections; as a user, I want a useful municipal location rather than an unsupported claim about a match venue.

**Why this priority**: Names, logos and location help navigation without redefining sporting identity.

**Independent Test**: Apply source refresh, manual changes, reactivation and geocoding responses to controlled club/team/pool examples, including denied operations.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A33** — **Given** manually corrected club display names and team/pool full or short display names, **When** source labels change or resources reactivate, **Then** each explicit override persists without changing identity or source labels. Removing an override restores the current automatic value for that field. (FR-045)
2. **A34** — **Given** teams with and without their own logos in multiple seasons, **When** the club logo changes, **Then** all teams without their own logo inherit the current club logo; a team override remains and removing it restores inheritance. Unsupported/oversized images or a failed replacement do not replace the last valid logo and can be corrected/retried. (FR-046)
3. **A35** — **Given** an ordinary user or moderator without the relevant domain permission, **When** they attempt a presentation/classification/division/exclusion change or exceptional identity correction, **Then** it is denied without mutation. Authorized corrections produce understandable affected-scope outcomes. (FR-047, FR-048)
4. **A36** — **Given** an existing club, **When** its fiche fails or contains an invalid contact field, **Then** the failure neither withdraws the club nor erases valid details; exploitable permitted source changes update automatic fields while preserving display overrides. (FR-049)
5. **A37** — **Given** usable municipality and postal information, **When** an unambiguous corresponding geocoding response is received, **Then** the club receives a municipal point, not a claimed gymnasium location. Unknown/ambiguous locations create no arbitrary point. (FR-050, FR-052)
6. **A38** — **Given** a published municipal point, **When** municipality or postal code changes, **Then** the old point is hidden until a valid response for the new context arrives. A street-only change does not move it. An outdated response cannot restore the old location; failure without a location change does not erase a valid point. (FR-051, FR-052)

### Edge Cases

- Local anomalies are isolated, whereas an unknown overall season/pool context invalidates the calendar; neither case authorizes guessed identity or absence-based withdrawal from incomplete evidence.
- Recognized future qualifiers/byes do not create fictitious resources. Incomplete identifiable matches may have unknown dates, times or results without being malformed.
- A partial update may be useful without proving full synchronization. Global acquisition and integration success are distinct from per-match success.
- A result can be corrected or withdrawn independently of a match's visibility. Source absence is not proof of cancellation, and a final result is not proof that every source has synchronized.
- An official ranking row may remain visible after calendar participation disappears; the ranking is not an alternative authority for team creation or reactivation.
- Pool/team visibility can have several simultaneous reasons. A collection response cannot undo an operator exclusion, inactive division or manual display override.
- An unknown club code change, merger or ambiguous cross-provider match is not an automatic identity merge; controlled technical reconciliation is the bounded recovery path.

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional Requirements

#### Identity and classification

- **FR-001**: Sporting identity, source reference/context, original source labels and display presentation MUST remain distinguishable. Source or display changes MUST NOT by themselves replace Blockout identities.
- **FR-002**: A Club MUST represent a persistent organization across seasons. Known provider references identify its observations; a new reference MUST NOT be merged with another club on name/address similarity alone. Exceptional verified reference continuity or merger treatment requires controlled technical reconciliation, not an inferred history transfer.
- **FR-003**: A Team MUST belong to one club, season, division, format and gender. Distinct named teams in that context remain distinct; a team may participate in multiple pools of the same classification. Genuinely different divisions or seasons define different teams; correcting erroneous classification follows FR-013–FR-014.
- **FR-004**: A Pool MUST have season, competition context and an accepted sporting classification independent of its display name. Its last accepted classification remains attached to acquired sporting state when acquisition classification is missing under FR-012. FFVB references are interpreted in league/season context; LNV phase references in championship/season/phase context. A pack is a provider grouping, not another team identity or an automatically created sporting division.
- **FR-005**: Match identity MUST be established from attributable source references within the proper provider, season and competition/pool/phase context. Rescheduling preserves identity. Date or participant pair alone MUST NOT cause a merge, and repeated encounters/code reuse across contexts MUST remain distinct. Ambiguous cross-source correspondence MUST leave the existing match unchanged by that unassigned enrichment and produce a diagnostic rather than a duplicate guessed match.
- **FR-006**: Team-name comparison MUST ignore case, redundant whitespace and typographic-versus-straight apostrophes, while preserving digits, accents, periods and hyphens. The original source label MUST remain available independently of comparison and presentation.
- **FR-007**: Verified name correspondences MUST be explicit and scoped to the relevant sporting identity/context. Maintenance is a controlled technical operation; no fuzzy matching, cross-club name-only merge or new alias-management screen is required. A correspondence causing an identity collision MUST use the same exceptional-reconciliation boundary as FR-014.
- **FR-008**: A significant unrecognized participant-name change on a known match MUST NOT silently merge or replace its team. That match's prior state MUST be retained until correspondence or an actual participant replacement is verified; other usable matches may proceed. Verification/retry is the recovery path, with no generic score/match editor.
- **FR-009**: Teams MUST be created from attributable calendars with established club and classification context, not solely from standings. A consultable Match MUST have two identified teams. Recognized undetermined qualifiers/byes MUST remain source/phase context without fictitious teams or consultable fixtures and MUST NOT invalidate unrelated identifiable fixtures.
- **FR-010**: Seasons MUST preserve their provider-confirmed sporting context rather than being guessed from a match's calendar year. Detection, activation, overlap, closure and exceptional historical acquisition MUST follow F01; no automatic cross-season team/follow transfer is implied.
- **FR-011**: Effective pool classification MUST supply division, format and gender from its native pack unless a complete individual override exists. An individually classified pool may proceed under an unclassified pack; pack exclusion still dominates. Missing effective classification MUST prevent guessed sporting creation and acquisition as defined by F01.
- **FR-012**: A pool override MUST set all three classification values together and remain independent of subsequent pack edits. Removing it MUST restore pack inheritance subject to correction checks. If no effective acquisition classification remains, collection MUST stop under F01 cycle rules while published resources retain their last accepted sporting classification, identities and visibility, subject to all other visibility reasons. That retained classification MUST NOT substitute for missing acquisition settings; explicit exclusion remains the way to hide the scope. Restoring classification MUST pass FR-013–FR-014 before collection becomes eligible; restoring the same classification preserves state, and a refused correction leaves collection ineligible if classification was missing.
- **FR-013**: An unambiguous correction of erroneous classification MUST preserve pool/team/match identities, existing relationships and presentation overrides when every affected participation can consistently follow that correction and no target identity collision exists. This is distinct from a genuine different-division team.
- **FR-014**: A requested classification correction requiring team separation, identity merging or changes to shared participations outside the requested scope MUST be refused in its entirety, preserving effective classification and data. Operators MUST receive the conflicting scopes/reasons and may narrow the request or use a controlled technical intervention. No automatic partial application or generic split/merge interface is required.
- **FR-015**: Authorized operators MUST retain division creation and presentation editing, including names, colors and logos, without replacing division identity. Duplicate division names differing only by case MUST be rejected on creation or renaming. An inactive division MUST be unavailable for new classification selections.
- **FR-016**: Division deactivation MUST preserve its data, hide dependent pools/teams/matches and suspend their collection under F01 cycle rules. Reactivation MUST restore resources hidden solely for this reason immediately, with known freshness and all other eligibility/visibility constraints still applied. It MUST NOT hide the clubs themselves.

#### Proportionate acquisition and integration

- **FR-017**: Failed/unusable catalog discovery MUST NOT itself invalidate previously successful references for the same season. Otherwise eligible collection may continue and discovery retries follow F01 cadence. No successful prior discovery means no guessed references. Confirmed absence stops that source's collection; remembered references MUST NOT override pauses, exclusions, inactive divisions/seasons or unreliable configuration.
- **FR-018**: Globally unreadable structure or wrong overall season/pool context MUST reject that calendar. Local unusable matches, ambiguous participants or contradictory rows for one source reference MUST isolate the affected match and retain its previous accepted state while allowing other usable matches to proceed. Identical repeated evidence MUST NOT create duplicates.
- **FR-019**: Absence-based match or participation withdrawals MUST occur only after a complete, usable, non-empty calendar holding membership authority under FR-024 has been successfully treated in full. Any failed/unattributable match, essential missing portion or integration failure prevents those withdrawals for that treatment. A later successful complete authoritative treatment may perform them. This does not prevent a separately established authoritative catalog absence or an explicit operator exclusion from affecting visibility.
- **FR-020**: Invalid secondary fields MUST be isolated without erasing prior usable values. Normally absent fields MUST remain distinguishable from invalid fields, without fabricated dates, scores or zeros. An explicit usable source clearing follows the field's meaning, including FR-026/FR-031 and FR-049; inability to parse a field is never an erasure request.
- **FR-021**: Successfully integrated matches MUST remain updated when another match fails. Every published match MUST reference existing, correctly attributed teams and pool in one consistent season/context. Failed matches MUST be recoverable without duplicates; no whole-pool indivisible publication or collection-by-collection version archive is required.
- **FR-022**: Replaying an observation MUST NOT duplicate identities/participations, generate a fresh sporting change when accepted state is unchanged, or replace newer accepted source state with older work. Current exclusions, division visibility and manual overrides MUST survive in-flight or retried acquisition. Observation age is assessed for the same source/context; a changed sporting date is not evidence of an older observation.

#### Sources, outcomes and time

- **FR-023**: Correctly identified phases of the three covered professional championships MAY be represented from official LNV evidence without an identifiable FFVB counterpart, provided season, classification and participants are established. FFVB CSV acquisition remains the method for available FFVB calendars. Unknown correspondence MUST remain explicit rather than borrowing another phase's identity or data.
- **FR-024**: For attributable professional match dates, results, venues and officials, usable LNV page values MUST take precedence over LNV XML, then FFVB. Ordinary FFVB competition values come from FFVB. The same order MUST govern match-membership and participation withdrawals/reappearances: authority belongs to the highest-priority calendar established by complete usable acquisition and successful integration for the same season and pool/phase. Until a higher-priority calendar is established, a lower-priority calendar may hold authority. Failure, invalidity or partial treatment of an established authoritative calendar MUST NOT transfer authority downwards or itself justify a withdrawal; known membership remains unless usable attributable reappearance under FR-042 establishes presence. A secondary calendar MUST neither contradict authoritative presence by withdrawing an element nor reactivate an element withdrawn by that authority. A result's set details MUST correspond to the selected overall-result observation; no incompatible composite result is allowed. XML remains an available observation/reference source. No confidence-scoring engine or heuristic estimate of which publication is actually newest is required.
- **FR-025**: When a higher-priority source becomes unavailable or invalid, its last usable confirmed value MUST remain instead of being overwritten by a contradictory lower-priority value. Lower-priority observations may fill genuinely unknown information; operators and consumers MUST retain the selected source and known freshness. A more recently fetched document alone does not establish more recently published information.
- **FR-026**: An explicit withdrawal from the authoritative usable source MUST remain distinct from unavailable evidence and from information never supplied. A withdrawn result MUST NOT be repopulated by a lower-priority source. Invalid detail alone MUST NOT clear the overall result.
- **FR-027**: Accepted values MUST retain sufficient source/context and observation-time attribution to explain selection and freshness, including provider publication time when supplied. Missing provider timestamps MUST stay unknown. This requires neither every historical raw response nor a complete timeline of every value.
- **FR-028**: A result published as such by the official source MUST be trusted as final unless indicated otherwise; Blockout MUST NOT require a separate universal finality proof or implement general competition-rule adjudication. Explicit provisional scores MUST be qualified as provisional; genuinely ambiguous score-publication meaning MUST remain finality-unconfirmed. Neither elapsed kickoff nor a universal number of won sets establishes completion. These distinctions do not promise continuous live scoring.
- **FR-029**: An exploitable official overall score MUST remain usable when its set detail is missing, invalid or contradictory. Unusable detail MUST be withheld and diagnosed without rejecting that overall result or unrelated date/venue updates. Blockout MUST NOT reconstruct a different winning score from the detail.
- **FR-030**: Recognized official special outcomes, including forfeits and penalties, MUST retain their source meaning without invented numeric substitutions, winners or standing calculations. Recognizable sporting annotations MUST NOT be treated as a new team identity. Unknown notation MUST be retained as uninterpreted evidence for diagnosis rather than silently converted to a normal result.
- **FR-031**: A usable authoritative observation that still identifies the match but withdraws its result MUST remove the displayed result and attached set detail and return the match to no confirmed result, even after its scheduled date. It MUST NOT infer cancellation or live play. Later corrections retain the same match identity. F07 owns resulting notification policy, including repeated/corrected result handling.
- **FR-032**: An identifiable match MAY exist without a usable date or confirmed time. Known date-only information MUST be preserved without fabricating an instant. FFVB 00:00 MUST mean unknown kickoff unless explicit contrary evidence confirms midnight. Unknown kickoff MUST NOT trigger F01 fast cadence.
- **FR-033**: Sporting dates/times MUST retain their source-local meaning and applicable time zone; ordinary covered metropolitan FFVB/LNV local timestamps use Europe/Paris, including seasonal offset changes, unless explicit source context establishes otherwise. Unresolvable time-zone/clock ambiguity MUST remain unknown rather than be guessed. Season ownership is independent of rescheduling across civil-year boundaries. Display time zones and day grouping belong to F03.

#### Official standings

- **FR-034**: A standing MUST reproduce one usable official observation for its pool/phase, with LNV page priority for professional standings, then LNV XML and FFVB, and FFVB authority elsewhere. Source ordering, stated positions, available statistics and special labels/ratios MUST be preserved. Missing numbers MUST remain unknown, and values such as an unlimited ratio MUST NOT become a fabricated finite coefficient. No mixing of tables/dates or local recalculation of ranks, penalties or tie-breaks is permitted. A phase without an official standing MUST NOT acquire an invented table.
- **FR-035**: A usable official standing MUST remain publishable when a row cannot be matched to a currently consultable Blockout team or disagrees with current calendar participation. Such a row MUST retain its official label and position without a team-navigation link. It MUST NOT create/reinstate a team/participation or cause local row removal/rank renumbering.
- **FR-036**: A globally unreadable/unattributable standing MUST be isolated from calendar integration. Keep the last usable official table with its freshness, or report standing unavailable if none exists. Invalid/missing individual statistics remain unavailable without invented zeros when the table's identity and order remain usable. Source outage MUST NOT silently cause a conflicting lower-priority table to replace an already selected higher-priority table.

#### Visibility, history and recovery

- **FR-037**: A pool omitted by a usable authoritative catalog for its own season MUST be hidden with its matches immediately, without erasing identities/data or declaring cancellation. FFVB catalog evidence governs FFVB pools; LNV catalog evidence governs professional phases identified on LNV. FFVB absence alone MUST NOT hide a phase still published by its authoritative LNV catalog. An unreadable/partial catalog proves no absence.
- **FR-038**: A match omitted from a fully treated authoritative calendar satisfying FR-019 MUST be marked withdrawn from that calendar and excluded from upcoming matches while retaining identity and acquired results, even if secondary sources still include it. This is not a cancellation result or a request to erase its historical result; result/history consultation remains subject to parent visibility. Empty calendars or stopped collection MUST NOT withdraw matches.
- **FR-039**: A team entirely absent from the authoritative calendar satisfying FR-019 MUST lose its visible participation in that pool, not its identity or other pool participations. Ranking-only or secondary-calendar presence MUST NOT prevent this withdrawal. An attributable participant reappearing in the authoritative calendar MUST reuse its known identity and participation rather than duplicate it, subject to FR-042 visibility rules.
- **FR-040**: A team without any visible pool participation MUST be hidden while retained. Clubs MUST remain consultable even when they have no visible teams, including across seasons. A club-fiche failure MUST NOT be interpreted as the club's disappearance.
- **FR-041**: Explicit pool/pack exclusion MUST suspend collection and hide the affected pools/matches/participations without erasure; pack exclusion dominates individual classification and pool-only exclusion leaves siblings unaffected. A collection pause alone MUST NOT hide resources. Lifting an exclusion MUST restore only resources with no other remaining visibility reason and reevaluate collection under F01.
- **FR-042**: Usable authoritative catalog rediscovery MUST remove the pool's source-absence visibility reason and restore its preserved data immediately if no other reason remains, with known freshness rather than an assertion of a newly fetched calendar. For a withdrawn match or participation, attributable usable reappearance in the authoritative calendar under FR-024 MUST remove that element's calendar-withdrawal reason and reuse its identity; secondary-calendar presence MUST NOT do so. Either form of reappearance MUST preserve unrelated exclusions and inactive-division restrictions. Pool reactivation MUST NOT clear independent withdrawals of individual matches/participations.
- **FR-043**: Seasonal closure or disappearance from another season's catalog MUST NOT erase or withdraw acquired history. Preserve the latest useful accepted sporting state and identities without requiring every collection version. A closed season is periodically inactive, not historically hidden by default. Exceptional historical recollection follows F01 and these same sporting rules; verified withdrawal within that season still affects visibility. F14 alone owns allowed loss of unrecoverable V1 sporting data during reset, not routine loss of V2 history.
- **FR-044**: Hidden resources MUST be absent from ordinary lists/search/calendars, and their existing direct links MUST report unavailability rather than expose the hidden resource or navigate to a guessed replacement. Existing follows/relationships MUST remain tied to preserved identities for reappearance, without automatic transfer; F06 owns their list presentation and actions. FR-035 permits the independent official label in a standing without a hidden-team link.

#### Presentation, administration and club location

- **FR-045**: Authorized manual club display-name and team/pool full-name or short-name overrides MUST survive source refresh, classification correction and reactivation without rewriting original source names or sporting identity. Removing an override MUST restore the current automatic value for that field, including an applicable verified naming correspondence. Each full/short-name override is independent; a source name change is not permission to erase another field's override.
- **FR-046**: A team's own logo MUST override its club logo. Without an override, it MUST use the club's current logo across all seasons; changing the club logo updates that inheritance, and removing a team override restores it. Missing source logos MUST NOT erase manual logos. Club, team and division logo uploads MUST retain the existing PNG/JPEG support and maximum size of 5 MiB (5,242,880 bytes); unsupported or oversized input is refused. A failed replacement MUST leave the last valid presentation available and permit retry. No season-specific logo archive is required.
- **FR-047**: Sporting presentation, classification, division lifecycle, exclusion and exceptional identity correction MUST require explicit permissions for the action and affected scope. An ordinary user or live moderator receives no implied right. Denial MUST leave state unchanged; F12 owns assignment and common administration access. No new generic manual score/match editor, alias UI or split/merge console is authorized.
- **FR-048**: Operators MUST distinguish accepted/rejected/partially integrated observations and identify affected source, season, pool/match context, time and actionable reasons without personal diagnostic payloads or raw-document archives. Successful acquisition or some successful match updates MUST NOT be reported as full calendar integration. Classification refusals MUST explain conflicting scopes; exceptional technical reconciliation MUST be authorized and its intended identity effects verified. F13 owns common visibility/recovery and F11 diagnostic retention.
- **FR-049**: Covered clubs MUST retain available permitted fiche details, including source/display name, locality/address, contact and website information, subject to F11 minimization. Usable automatic-field corrections or explicit removals MUST update those fields, invalid fields MUST preserve previous usable values, and an unavailable fiche MUST preserve prior details. Manual display overrides and independent logo inheritance remain protected. Fiche processing does not authorize club withdrawal or whole-directory collection.
- **FR-050**: Published club coordinates MUST locate its municipality using sufficiently identifying, consistent municipality/postal information, not claim a gymnasium or match venue. Unknown or ambiguous locality MUST have no arbitrary point. Teams on pool maps use the club location; no team-venue reconciliation feature is required.
- **FR-051**: A changed municipality or postal code MUST invalidate the old published point until a usable response for the new context is received. A street-only change in the same municipality/postal context MUST NOT move the municipal point or create a new need for that reason alone. New/changed eligible locality needs follow F01's one-hour attempt bound and its pause/retry rules.
- **FR-052**: Only an unambiguous usable geocoding outcome matching the current locality context MUST be published. Empty/ambiguous outcomes remain unresolved with F01 suppression of automatic unchanged retries and authorized family relaunch. Technical failure without locality change MUST NOT erase a valid point; an old-context response MUST NOT republish the previous location after a change.
- **FR-053**: An attributable calendar club reference MAY establish the persistent club needed by a team before its fiche succeeds, without inventing unavailable details. Source-only future-slot references MUST NOT create clubs. Unknown/ambiguous club attribution prevents creation of that match/team and follows local isolation rather than guessed linkage.

### Key Entities

- **Club**: Persistent sporting organization, source references, presentation and permitted fiche information; its municipality is independent of match venues and team visibility.
- **Division / classification**: Maintained sporting category and the division/format/gender context shared by teams and pools. A native source pack supplies acquisition defaults; a complete pool override supplies exceptions. The last accepted classification of published sporting state remains distinct from the current settings required to authorize acquisition.
- **Season**: Sporting context confirmed by the source, with operator-controlled collection lifecycle rather than an identity inferred from civil match dates.
- **Team**: Seasonal club team in one classification, distinct from its names and from any particular pool participation.
- **Pool / professional phase**: Classified competition grouping in a season, backed by attributable official references; professional source coverage need not have a one-to-one FFVB counterpart.
- **Participation**: Relationship between an identified team and a pool, with retained identity and visibility. It is not created solely by a standing row.
- **Match**: Identified encounter between two teams within one pool/season, with potentially unknown date/time/result and independently qualified source outcome and visibility.
- **Official standing**: An attributed official table for a pool/phase, preserving row identity/order and optional links to Blockout teams. It is not a local recalculation from match results or merely the set of current participations.
- **Observation / visibility reason / presentation override**: Source evidence is distinct from accepted sporting state; source withdrawal, administrative exclusion and division inactivity affect visibility without erasure; explicit presentation overrides do not redefine identity.

These are the owner-approved V2 sporting semantics refining the [shared domain model](../../docs/architecture/blockout-domain-model-v1.md). They select no runtime service boundaries, database keys, protocols or public contracts.

## Assumptions

- This is a functional specification for the existing sporting capabilities and the explicit owner-approved changes. No fantasy game, social system, roster model, new provider, automatic merger tool or generic match editor is added.
- Official data is generally usable. The product trusts attributed official sporting content, while distinguishing uncertain identity/context, unsupported notation and actual acquisition/integration failures. It does not adjudicate federation rules.
- Source examples establish observed fields/behavior, not exhaustive coverage, permanent identifiers or proof of future availability. The accepted source-priority policy is not an assertion that the preferred page always publishes first.
- Simple name/display resets restore the current automatic value, not a historical snapshot. The historical guarantee concerns retained identities and latest useful state, not an audit copy of every scrape.
- Pool visibility reasons are cumulative. Operator settings become acquisition-effective under F01 cycle boundaries, while current visibility and manual overrides cannot be bypassed by late work.
- Personal data purposes/retention, measurable global freshness/performance, notification behavior, user-facing day grouping and design are owned by the referenced perimeters; this document supplies their required sporting meanings rather than declaring their specifications complete.
- No technical planning is authorized until the full functional corpus and global consistency/Figma review are accepted under [#247](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/247). No constitution change or V2 topology is selected.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-09-16

The approved planning decisions below are integrated into the normative requirements. They describe the selected behavior, not implementation completion.

- Q: How should unreliable calendars be handled? → A: Reject globally unusable context/structure, isolate local match anomalies, retain successful updates and prohibit absence-based withdrawal from incomplete treatment. (FR-018–FR-022)
- Q: May collection continue when catalog discovery fails? → A: Yes, with previously successful references from the same season and current eligibility; a confirmed absence still stops that source's collection. (FR-017)
- Q: How are ordinary results and standings trusted? → A: Trust published results, retain overall scores when detail is invalid, and show usable official standing rows even without Blockout team links. (FR-028–FR-036)
- Q: What requires exceptional identity intervention? → A: Ambiguous correspondences and classification changes requiring separation/merging; refuse the entire requested administrative correction if any affected scope conflicts. (FR-007–FR-014)
- Q: What happens when a pool override is removed without a pack replacement? → A: Stop collection under F01 cycle rules; keep published resources visible with their last accepted classification and identities, subject to other visibility reasons. Restoring classification must pass correction checks before collection can resume. (FR-004, FR-011–FR-014)
- Q: Which calendar decides conflicting withdrawals and reappearances? → A: The highest-priority established calendar for the same season and pool/phase, using LNV page, then XML, then FFVB for professional competitions and FFVB elsewhere. Failure does not demote its authority, and secondary sources cannot contradict its membership decisions. (FR-019, FR-024, FR-038–FR-042)
- Q: Does retained history imply permanent visibility? → A: No; confirmed source withdrawals and explicit exclusions/division deactivation hide resources without erasing them. Closed seasons remain consultable unless another reason applies. (FR-037–FR-044)
- Q: What does the map locate? → A: The club municipality, with the old point hidden after locality changes and no claimed gymnasium location. (FR-050–FR-052)
