# Functional specification: sporting data identity and lifecycle

**Feature branch**: `feature/258-sporting-data`

**Created on**: 2026-09-16

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline under [planning authority #9](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/9); technical-dossier acceptance and implementation evidence remain separate.

**Requested scope**: Functional scope approved by the product owner for [#258](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout-legacy/issues/258), including proportionate reliability decisions: trust official sporting content, establish its identity and context, isolate local anomalies and preserve successful useful updates. This document defines F02 in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications).

<a id="user-scenarios--testing-mandatory"></a>

## Clarifications — approved KISS revision, 2026-10-06

- Q: What changes in source and visibility rules? → A: LNV pages alone own professional facts; ordinary competitions use FFVB. One qualified current empty calendar hides the pool and dependent resources, while preserving collection of still-listed references and identities. Reappearance clears only its own reason. Manual pack/pool exclusion and empty-confirmation counters are withdrawn; closed-season history and atomic classification corrections remain protected.

### Session 2026-10-09

- Q: How are teams shared across independent championship pools recognized and displayed? → A: Keep club/name/season/division/gender/format identity and the F02 name-comparison rule; dots and hyphens remain significant. A team has one identity and one team follow across compatible pools. Its existing tab bar offers one standings tab per visible pool, while upcoming/finished matches include all its visible participations with their pool context. No qualification graph, progression calculation or automatic cross-season/division transfer is required.

## User scenarios and validation _(mandatory)_

Visitors and signed-in users view sporting information. Operators with explicit domain permissions manage sporting presentation, classifications and divisions. Authorized technical operators handle exceptional identity reconciliation; ordinary users and live-stream moderators implicitly receive none of these permissions. Source acquisition supplies observations under [F01](../001-source-acquisition/spec.md), not an already accepted sporting state.

Scenario identifiers are local acceptance references, not implementation tasks. Independent validations describe acceptance evidence to obtain; they do not claim that V2 is implemented or that a production result has been achieved.

<a id="user-story-1---recognize-the-same-sporting-resources-priority-p1"></a>

### User story 1 — Recognize the same sporting resources (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want match corrections and postponements to preserve the correct teams and matches so that my links and follows do not accidentally refer to replacements.

**Priority rationale**: Every score, follow relationship and historical view depends on correct association.

**Independent validation**: Compare identities and references in controlled calendars with repeated teams, changed labels, postponed matches and distinct seasons.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a known club entering a new season, **when** its teams and pools are acquired, **then** the club retains its identity while seasonal teams and pools are distinct. Two named teams of that club in the same division remain distinct; a team present in two pools with the same classification retains a single identity. (FR-001, FR-002, FR-003, FR-004)
2. **A02** — **Given** a known match, **when** its official date changes, **then** its identity and associated relationships are preserved; a repeated encounter or a code reused in another season or context causes no merge solely from the date or participant pair. (FR-005)
3. **A03** — **Given** source names differing only in letter case, excess spaces or typographic apostrophes, **when** compared in the same identity context, **then** these differences are considered equivalent; digits, accents, dots and hyphens remain significant. A verified, bounded alias resolves the variants it declares while retaining their source labels. (FR-006, FR-007)
4. **A04** — **Given** an existing match whose participant undergoes an unrecognized significant name change, **when** identity cannot be established, **then** that match retains its previous state and receives a diagnostic, other usable matches continue to be processed, and this incomplete processing produces no absence-based withdrawal. (FR-008, FR-019)
5. **A05** — **Given** a classified phase, **when** its calendar is integrated, **then** creation and publication follow these cases. (FR-009, FR-019, FR-049, FR-053)

   - **Identified club, unavailable page**: A club not yet in Blockout is unambiguously identified by its calendar reference. If other conditions are met, the club and its teams are created and identified matches published without waiting for the page. Unavailable details remain unknown; later page retrieval completes authorized information without replacing identities or erasing customizations.
   - **Unknown or ambiguous club association**: The affected team and match are not created from an assumed link; the anomaly remains local and other usable matches are processed. This incomplete processing authorizes no absence-based withdrawal.
   - **Undetermined qualifier or recognized bye**: The future place remains in source context without creating a club, fictional team or viewable match. It is not a malformed participant reference and does not block identifiable matches.
   - **Participant present only in standings**: Its label is insufficient to create a team.

<a id="user-story-2---correct-classifications-without-losing-continuity-priority-p1"></a>

### User story 2 — Correct classifications without losing continuity (Priority: P1)

As an authorized operator, I want explicit classification commands and understandable conflict refusals, without a generic identity-merging interface.

**Priority rationale**: Classification changes may affect shared teams and existing user relationships.

**Independent validation**: Apply pack and pool settings to established participations, then compare effective classifications, identities and refused-operation outcomes.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A06** — **Given** an unclassified pack, **when** a pool receives a complete division, format and gender override, **then** it may become eligible while other pools remain pending. Later pack changes do not alter its override. Removing it restores pack inheritance subject to correction checks. If the pack supplies no replacement, collection stops under F01 cycle rules while published resources retain their last accepted classification, identities and visibility; source hiding and inactive divisions remain applicable. This retained classification does not authorize collection. (FR-011, FR-012)
2. **A07** — **Given** a wrongly classified pool whose affected teams have neither conflicting outside participation nor target-identity collision, **when** an authorized correction applies, **then** the pool, teams, matches, follows and presentation customizations retain their identities and relationships under the corrected classification. After a period without classification, restoring the same classification or correcting it without conflict allows collection again subject to normal eligibility; none of these operations lifts a source-absence or inactive-division restriction. (FR-012, FR-013, FR-016, FR-041)
3. **A08** — **Given** a correction covering several pools, including a team shared with a pool outside the correction scope or an already existing matching target team, **when** correction is requested, **then** the entire request is refused with conflicting scopes identified; no conflict-free subset is implicitly applied. If restoring a missing acquisition classification was intended, collection remains ineligible and published resources retain their last accepted classification. A narrower request or controlled technical intervention remains possible. (FR-012, FR-014)
4. **A09** — **Given** an operator authorized to manage divisions, **when** they create a division or change its presentation, **then** these cases apply. (FR-015, FR-016, FR-047)

   - **Authorized change**: Changing the name, colors or logo preserves division identity and active or inactive state. An inactive division remains unavailable for a new classification; editing it restores neither resource visibility nor collection.
   - **Duplicate creation**: If “National 2” exists, creating “national 2” is refused without creating an additional division or changing the existing one.
   - **Duplicate rename**: If “National 2” exists, renaming another division to “national 2” is refused; its name, identity and active or inactive state remain unchanged.

5. **A10** — **Given** a division containing data, **when** an authorized operator acts on its lifecycle, **then** these cases apply. (FR-016, FR-042)

   - **Deactivation**: Dependent collection stops under F01 cycle rules; pools, teams and matches become hidden without erasure. Clubs themselves are not hidden for this reason.
   - **Explicit reactivation**: Resources hidden solely for this reason return immediately with their known freshness. Collection may resume under F01 cycle rules if other eligibility conditions are met.
   - **Another restriction still present**: A source-withdrawn or calendar-empty pool remains hidden after division reactivation; lifting one reason bypasses no other reason.

<a id="user-story-3---receive-useful-updates-despite-isolated-failures-priority-p1"></a>

### User story 3 — Receive useful updates despite isolated failures (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want valid matches to stay current even when another record is unusable, without treating failures as sporting withdrawals.

**Priority rationale**: Official data is generally usable; disproportionate rejection reduces its usefulness.

**Independent validation**: Submit calendars mixing valid and invalid items, interrupted integrations and replayed observations, then compare accepted matches, retained records and withdrawal authorizations.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A11** — **Given** valid references from earlier discovery, **when** the next catalog read fails, **then** otherwise eligible collection may continue with those references in the same season during discovery retries. Without earlier successful discovery, no reference is invented; confirmed absence in a later usable catalog stops collection of that source. (FR-017)
2. **A12** — **Given** a calendar of 100 matches with one unusable match, **when** processed, **then** the other 99 may be updated and the failed match retains its previous state. Contradictory rows for one reference isolate that match; identical repetitions create no duplicate. In contrast, a globally unreadable document or one from the wrong season or pool causes calendar rejection. (FR-018, FR-019)
3. **A13** — **Given** a usable match with an invalid secondary field and another with normally absent information, **when** integrated, **then** invalidity preserves the old usable field, while absence remains explicit instead of becoming zero or an invented date. A known official withdrawal of a value follows that field’s rule. (FR-020)
4. **A14** — **Given** successful updates followed by an integration failure, **when** processing ends then is retried, **then** successes remain visible with valid team and pool references, failures are distinct and retried without duplicates, and absence-based withdrawals await complete successful processing. (FR-019, FR-021, FR-022, FR-048)
5. **A15** — **Given** a more recent accepted observation, a manual display customization or a currently hidden resource, **when** old work is replayed, **then** it can neither overwrite the more recent accepted state, erase customization nor bypass current visibility reasons. (FR-022, FR-045)

<a id="user-story-4---read-official-results-and-rankings-priority-p1"></a>

### User story 4 — Read official results and standings (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to read published sporting information, including professional phases and partial values, without Blockout inventing a substitute result or standings.

**Priority rationale**: The application conveys official information rather than adjudicating sporting rules itself.

**Independent validation**: Compare accepted values with official observations attributed to their source, including conflicts, outages, result withdrawals and standings rows without a team link.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A16** — **Given** a correctly identified official LNV phase without an identifiable FFVB equivalent, **when** its classification and participants are established, **then** it may be represented from LNV; the missing mapping remains explicit and no other phase or season is used as a replacement. (FR-023)
2. **A17** — **Given** a professional match, **when** integrating its LNV calendar and match pages, **then** use verified contextual identity and compatible result/set facts. XML and FFVB observations do not supplement or compete for this match. (FR-023–FR-024)
3. **A18** — **Given** accepted professional page data, **when** the page fails or required detail is absent, **then** retain accepted values with freshness or report unknown data. Do not fall back to XML or FFVB or fabricate a result. (FR-025–FR-027)
4. **A19** — **Given** a published official result, an explicitly provisional score and an ambiguous score publication, **when** interpreted, **then** they are respectively definitive, provisional and unconfirmed as definitive; no universal three-set threshold or elapsed-time rule replaces their meaning in the source. (FR-028)
5. **A20** — **Given** an official overall result of 3–1 with inconsistent or unusable set detail, **when** integrated, **then** 3–1 remains available, unusable detail is not displayed and the anomaly is diagnosed without invalidating independent date or venue changes. (FR-029)
6. **A21** — **Given** a recognized official forfeit or penalty notation, **when** integrated, **then** its sporting meaning is retained without an invented numeric score or locally calculated standings. An unrecognized notation is exposed to operations as uninterpreted source material without implicit conversion to zero. (FR-030)
7. **A22** — **Given** an accepted result, **when** the authoritative source supplies a usable observation of the same match with result withdrawal, **then** the displayed result and its set detail are removed and the match no longer has a confirmed result. A later corrected result may be accepted under the same identity. (FR-026, FR-031)
8. **A23** — **Given** an identifiable match without a date, with only a date, or with FFVB time 00:00 without contrary evidence, **when** integrated, **then** the known date is retained without inventing an instant or triggering a fast window from an unknown time. Ordinary source local times use their applicable time zone, including seasonal daylight-saving changes. (FR-032, FR-033)
9. **A24** — **Given** usable official standings with a team absent from the calendar or unmatched to a viewable team, **when** published, **then** official rows, order, positions and available statistics are retained, and that row uses its source label without a Blockout team link. No team is created and no rank is recalculated locally. (FR-034, FR-035)
10. **A25** — **Given** unusable or unavailable standings but a usable calendar, **when** an update occurs, **then** the calendar is processed and the last usable standings retained with known freshness; if none exist, standings are unavailable. Missing columns and special ratios do not become zeros or invented statistics. A phase without published standings receives no invented standings. (FR-034, FR-036)

<a id="user-story-5---understand-disappearance-and-recover-preserved-history-priority-p1"></a>

### User story 5 — Understand disappearances and recover retained history (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want withdrawn resources to disappear from ordinary consultation without losing identity or history during an outage or season closure.

**Priority rationale**: Visibility, sporting result and data retention express different facts.

**Independent validation**: Apply bounded discovery, calendar and administration observations to current or closed seasons, then compare visibility, retained information and reappearance outcomes.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A26** — **Given** a previously visible pool, **when** the usable catalog authoritative for its season omits it, **then** the pool and its matches become hidden but retained. For a phase identified on LNV, absence from the LNV catalog is authoritative; FFVB absence alone does not hide a phase still published by LNV. (FR-037)
2. **A27** — **Given** known matches, **when** the authoritative calendar is processed, **then** these cases apply without erasing identities or internally retained results. (FR-019, FR-024, FR-038, FR-043)

   - **Individual withdrawal**: A complete, usable, nonempty and successfully integrated calendar hides an absent match everywhere, with or without a result: calendars, personal feed and page through an old link. Neither team nor pool following bypasses this withdrawal, which does not mean sporting cancellation.
   - **Empty calendar**: One qualified wholly empty current observation hides the pool, matches and participations, retaining identities and catalog-based collection eligibility.
   - **Outage or incomplete observation**: Partial, failed or wrong-context integration authorizes no absence-based withdrawal.
   - **Source and catalog**: The designated source is LNV pages for professional competition and FFVB elsewhere; qualified catalog absence is distinct from failed discovery.

3. **A28** — **Given** a team absent after qualified absence reconciliation, **when** its pool participation is hidden, **then** other participations remain. One qualified empty current calendar hides that pool and all its participations/matches; the team hides only when no visible participation remains, while its club stays consultable. Closed seasons reject new acquisition and retain history. (FR-019, FR-038–FR-040, FR-043)
4. **A29** — **Given** retained sporting data, **when** a family is paused, **then** collection stops without hiding that data. Division inactivity remains an independent visibility restriction; no manual pack/pool exclusion command exists. (FR-016, FR-041)
5. **A30** — **Given** source-hidden identities, **when** qualified catalog or calendar evidence reappears, **then** clear only its corresponding hiding reason and reuse recognized identities immediately. Catalog presence alone does not clear calendar-empty hiding; a usable nonempty calendar does. Other division/item restrictions remain. (FR-024, FR-042)
6. **A31** — **Given** two active seasons and an older acquired season, **when** one is definitively closed, **then** history remains consultable under its existing visibility restrictions. New acquisition and recollection are refused. Only a cycle already running at closure may finish once with normal order/visibility guards. Initial historical reconstruction occurs before closure and never substitutes current-season content for an unavailable archive. The V1 reset exception remains separate. (FR-010, FR-019, FR-024, FR-043; F01 FR-055)

7. **A32** — **Given** a hidden resource with an existing follow or direct link, **when** a user views ordinary lists, search or calendars, **then** it is absent and its old link indicates unavailability; its identity and relationships remain retained for reappearance without automatic transfer to another resource. (FR-044)

**Calendar-authority examples for A27–A30**: Each row applies to match presence and team participations in the same season and pool or phase. Every absence-based withdrawal remains subject to FR-019; observations from another phase or season cannot substitute for it.

| Source observation                                                | Expected presence outcome                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Complete usable nonempty designated-source calendar omits a match | Hide it after complete integration; retain identity                              |
| Partial, wrong-context or failed calendar                         | Preserve accepted presence; no absence reconciliation                            |
| One complete valid empty current calendar                         | Hide pool, matches and participations; keep collecting if catalog-listed         |
| Qualified catalog absence                                         | Stop old reference and hide affected pool; no inferred deletion                  |
| Failed discovery                                                  | No confirmed absence; previously qualified eligible references may remain usable |
| Catalog rediscovery after calendar-empty hiding                   | Clear catalog absence only; calendar-empty reason remains                        |
| Attributable nonempty reappearance                                | Reuse identities and clear the relevant absence/empty reason                     |
| New acquisition requested after season closure                    | Refuse the request; preserve history                                             |

### User story 6 — Maintain presentation and useful club locations (Priority: P2)

As an authorized operator, I want durable presentation and contact-detail corrections; as a user, I want a useful municipality-level location rather than an unsupported claim about a match venue.

**Priority rationale**: Names, logos and location facilitate navigation without redefining sporting identity.

**Independent validation**: Apply source refreshes, manual changes, reactivations and geocoding responses to controlled club, team and pool examples, including refused operations.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A33** — **Given** manually corrected club display names and full or short team or pool names, **when** source labels change or resources are reactivated, **then** every explicit customization persists without changing identity or source labels. Removing a customization restores the current automatic value of that field. (FR-045)
2. **A34** — **Given** teams with and without their own logo across several seasons, **when** the club logo changes, **then** all teams without their own logo inherit the current club logo; a team customization stays in place and its removal restores inheritance. An unsupported or oversized image, or failed replacement, does not replace the last valid logo and can be corrected or retried. (FR-046)
3. **A35** — **Given** an ordinary user or moderator without the relevant business permission, **when** they attempt a presentation, classification, division change, exceptional identity correction, or correction, deliberate absence or return to source for club contact details, **then** the action is refused without modification. Authorized corrections produce understandable outcomes for affected scopes. (FR-047, FR-048)
4. **A36** — **Given** a club page and authorized operator, **when** contact details are corrected or recollected, **then** these cases apply. (FR-045, FR-047–FR-050)
   - **Durable value**: For each address, city, postal code, email, phone and website field, a recorded correction survives multiple collections and reactivation, even if the source changes. Club identity, teams, participations and follows remain unchanged.
   - **Deliberate absence**: The field remains absent despite source presence. This state is confused neither with acquisition failure nor return to source.
   - **Independence**: A corrected phone, deliberately absent email and source-managed website may coexist. Changing one field does not change the others’ mode.
   - **Return to source**: An explicit action resumes the current usable, authorized automatic value of the targeted field alone. Without an eligible value, it remains absent; no old contact is arbitrarily recovered. An unavailable page or invalid field does not replace the last still-valid authorized automatic value with an invented value.
   - **Privacy**: An F11 restriction prevents publication of the affected value, including after correction, return to source, collection or restoration. Old personal contact details do not become an archive.
   - **Refusal, failure and recovery**: Without permission, no change is accepted. Confirmed failure preserves the last state; a lost response can be checked then retried without an additional effect. An old request does not silently replace a more recent decision. The same guarantees cover all three modes.
   - **Source page**: Its failure does not withdraw the club or erase still-authorized details. Valid source changes update automatic values without removing name or logo customizations.
5. **A37** — **Given** usable municipality and postal information, **when** a corresponding unambiguous geocoding response arrives, **then** the club receives a municipality-level point without claiming to locate a gymnasium. An unknown or ambiguous locality creates no arbitrary point. (FR-050, FR-052)
6. **A38** — **Given** a published municipality-level point, **when** location context changes, **then** these cases apply. (FR-049–FR-052)
   - **Effective change**: A new municipality or postal code from the source, a manual correction or return to source invalidates the old point until a valid response for the new context. Deliberate absence leaving the locality unknown, inconsistent or ambiguous removes the misleading point without inventing another.
   - **Unchanged context**: A street-only correction does not move the point. A source change masked by a durable correction also does not move the club or create a geocoding request for that reason alone.
   - **Late response or failure**: A response targeting the old context does not restore the old location, even after a return to source. Technical failure without a locality change does not erase a valid point. Attempts for the current context respect F01 delay, pauses and retries.
7. **A39** — **Given** retained associations between V1 clubs and their logo URLs, **when** clubs are rebuilt for V2, **then** a certain identity match allows reapplying the logo as manual presentation, with normal team inheritance. An absent or ambiguous club keeps its association in recovery evidence without a fictional club or name-only matching. A missing file is reported; retaining its URL alone does not prove preservation. F14 requires verified retention of associations and files before any reset. (FR-002, FR-043, FR-046)

## Owner clarifications — 2026-10-04

These clarifications refine the accepted scope for the F02 technical plan without selecting new product screens.

- **Formats and genders (FR-003, FR-011)**: Supported formats are 6x6, 4x4 and 2x2; supported genders are female, male and mixed. The owner identified 2x2 as used for poussin. This does not introduce automatic age-group rules or classify 2x2 as beach volleyball.
- **Secondary-calendar publication rule withdrawn (FR-024, FR-038–FR-042)**: The former cross-provider priority is superseded by designated authority: LNV pages for professional competitions and FFVB for other covered competitions. There is no secondary XML/FFVB professional candidate queue. Partial-integration and absence safeguards still apply within the designated source.

<a id="edge-cases"></a>

### Edge cases

- Local anomalies are isolated, while an unknown global season or pool context invalidates the calendar; neither case authorizes a guessed identity or absence-based withdrawal from incomplete evidence.
- Recognized future qualifiers or byes create no fictional resources. Incomplete identifiable matches may have unknown dates, times or results without being malformed.
- A partial update may be useful without proving complete synchronization. Overall acquisition and integration success is distinct from per-match success.
- A result may be corrected or withdrawn independently of match visibility. Source absence does not prove cancellation and a definitive result does not prove synchronization of every source.
- An official standings row may remain visible after calendar participation disappears; standings are not a substitute authority for creating or reactivating a team.
- Pool and team visibility may depend on several simultaneous reasons. A collection response cannot undo an independent source restriction, an inactive division or a manual display customization.
- An unrecognized club-code change, a merger or an ambiguous provider mapping causes no automatic identity merge; controlled technical reconciliation is the bounded resolution path.

### Functional requirements

<a id="identity-and-classification"></a>

#### Identity and classification

- **FR-001**: Sporting identity, source reference and context, original source labels and displayed presentation MUST remain distinct. A source or display change MUST NOT by itself replace Blockout identities.
- **FR-002**: A Club MUST represent an organization persisting across seasons. Known provider references identify its observations; a new reference MUST NOT be merged with another club solely from name or address similarity. Verified exceptional reference continuity or handling of a merger requires controlled technical reconciliation, not an automatically inferred history transfer.
- **FR-003**: A Team MUST belong to a club, season, division, format and gender. Teams with distinct names in that context remain distinct; a team may participate in several independent pools with the same classification, retaining one identity and one team follow. F03 owns their presentation and navigation. Genuinely different divisions or seasons define different teams; correction of a mistaken classification follows FR-013–FR-014.
- **FR-004**: A Pool MUST have a season, competition context and accepted sporting classification independent of its display name. Youth qualifying pools use this ordinary model without required progression links. When an FFVB code denotes distinct groups across published tours, each qualified tour/group/pool context is a distinct pool; a normal matchday is not. A shared CSV is partitioned only for a demonstrated source format. Its last accepted classification remains attached to acquired sporting state when acquisition classification is missing under FR-012. FFVB references are interpreted in their league and season context; LNV phase references in their championship, season and phase context. A pack is a provider grouping, not another team identity or an automatically created sporting division.
- **FR-005**: Match identity MUST be established from attributable source references in the correct provider, season and competition, pool or phase context. Postponement preserves identity. The date or participant pair alone MUST NOT cause a merge, and repeated encounters or codes reused in different contexts MUST remain distinct. An ambiguous source mapping MUST leave the existing match unchanged by that unassociated enrichment and produce a diagnostic rather than an assumed duplicate match.
- **FR-006**: Team-name comparison MUST ignore letter case, excess spaces and the difference between typographic and straight apostrophes while preserving digits, accents, dots and hyphens. The original source label MUST remain available independently of comparison and presentation.
- **FR-007**: Verified name mappings MUST be explicit and limited to the relevant sporting identity and context. Maintaining them is a controlled technical operation; no approximate matching, name-only cross-club merge or new alias-management screen is required. A mapping causing an identity collision MUST follow the same exceptional-reconciliation boundary as FR-014.
- **FR-008**: An unrecognized significant participant-name change on a known match MUST NOT implicitly merge or replace its team. The match’s previous state MUST be retained until the mapping or an actual participant replacement is verified; other usable matches may be processed. Verification and retry constitute the resolution path, without a generic score or match editor.
- **FR-009**: Teams MUST be created from attributable calendars with established club and classification context, not from standings alone. A viewable Match MUST have two identified teams. Undetermined qualifiers and recognized byes MUST remain elements of source and phase context without fictional teams or viewable encounters and MUST NOT invalidate other identifiable encounters.
- **FR-010**: Seasons MUST preserve their provider-confirmed sporting context rather than being inferred from a match’s calendar year. Detection, activation, overlap, definitive closure and initial historical reconstruction before closure MUST follow F01; no automatic team or follow transfer between seasons is implicit.
- **FR-011**: A pool’s effective classification MUST supply division, format and gender from its native pack unless a complete individual override exists. An individually classified pool may be collected under an unclassified pack; division/season eligibility remains applicable. Absence of effective classification MUST prevent assumed sporting creations and acquisition under F01.
- **FR-012**: A pool override MUST define all three classification values together and remain independent of later pack changes. Removing it MUST restore pack inheritance subject to correction checks. If no effective acquisition classification remains, collection MUST stop under F01 cycle rules while published resources retain their last accepted sporting classification, identities and visibility, subject to all other visibility reasons. This retained classification MUST NOT replace missing acquisition settings. Restoring classification MUST satisfy FR-013–FR-014 before collection becomes eligible again; restoring the same classification preserves state, and a refused correction leaves collection ineligible if classification was missing.
- **FR-013**: An unambiguous correction of a mistaken classification MUST preserve pool, team and match identities, existing relationships and presentation customizations when all affected participations can follow the correction consistently and no target-identity collision exists. This case is distinct from a team that truly differs by division.
- **FR-014**: A requested classification correction requiring team separation, identity merging or changes to shared participations outside the requested scope MUST be refused in full, preserving effective classification and data. Operators MUST receive conflicting scopes and reasons and may narrow the request or use controlled technical intervention. No automatic partial application or generic split/merge interface is required.
- **FR-015**: Authorized operators MUST retain division creation and presentation editing, including names, colors and logos. A presentation change MUST preserve division identity and active or inactive state; it MUST NOT restore visibility or collection suspended by inactivity. Duplicate division names differing only by letter case MUST be refused on creation or rename. An inactive division MUST be unavailable for new classification selections.
- **FR-016**: Division deactivation MUST preserve data, hide dependent pools, teams and matches and suspend their collection under F01 cycle rules. This hiding and suspension reason MUST be lifted only by an explicit reactivation action. It MUST immediately restore resources hidden solely for this reason, with their known freshness; collection resumption remains subject to F01 cycle rules and all other eligibility and visibility constraints remain applied. Deactivation MUST NOT hide clubs themselves.

<a id="proportionate-acquisition-and-integration"></a>

#### Proportionate acquisition and integration

- **FR-017**: Failed or unusable catalog discovery MUST NOT by itself invalidate references already successfully discovered for the same season. Otherwise eligible collection may continue and discovery retries follow F01 cadence. Without earlier successful discovery, no reference is guessed. Confirmed absence stops collection of that source; remembered references MUST NOT bypass pauses, inactive divisions or seasons, or unreliable configuration.
- **FR-018**: A globally unreadable structure or incorrect global season or pool context MUST cause calendar rejection. Locally unusable matches, ambiguous participants or contradictory rows for one source reference MUST isolate the affected match and retain its previously accepted state while allowing other usable matches to be processed. Identical repetitions MUST NOT create duplicates.
- **FR-019**: Absence reconciliation MUST run only after complete successful processing of a complete, correctly contextualized observation from the designated source. A nonempty calendar may withdraw absent matches/participations; one wholly empty current calendar hides the pool and all its matches/participations without erasing identities. No counter is required. Any failed or unassociable match, missing essential section or incomplete integration prevents absence reconciliation. Closed seasons reject new cycles; only a cycle already running at closure can finish once under F01. Wholly empty initial historical reconstruction preserves history; qualified nonempty input may reconcile individual facts before closure. Discovery failure and HTTP errors prove no absence.
- **FR-020**: Invalid secondary fields MUST be isolated without erasing usable previous values. Normally absent fields MUST remain distinct from invalid fields, without invented dates, scores or zeros. Usable explicit erasure in the source follows the field’s meaning, including FR-026/FR-031 and FR-049; inability to parse a field is never an erasure request.
- **FR-021**: Successfully integrated matches MUST remain current when another match fails. Each published match MUST reference existing teams and a pool, correctly associated with a consistent season and context. Failed matches MUST be retryable without duplicates; neither indivisible publication of an entire pool nor a versioned archive of every collection is required.
- **FR-022**: Replaying an observation MUST NOT duplicate identities or participations, produce a new sporting change when accepted state is unchanged, or replace a more recent accepted source state with old work. Current source hiding, division visibility and manual customizations MUST withstand ongoing or retried acquisition. Observation age is assessed for the same source and context; a changed sporting date does not prove that an observation is older.

<a id="sources-outcomes-and-time"></a>

#### Sources, results and time

- **FR-023**: Correctly identified phases of the three covered professional championships MAY be represented from official LNV evidence without an identifiable FFVB equivalent if season, classification and participants are established. FFVB CSV acquisition remains the method for available FFVB calendars. An unknown mapping MUST remain explicit rather than borrow another phase’s identity or data.
- **FR-024**: Professional matches and their presence MUST come from LNV pages only, using grouped calendars and match details with verified context and identity. Do not merge or fall back to XML LNV or FFVB for these encounters. FFVB supplies other covered competitions. Match result and sets must be compatible within the accepted page observation. Missing or unqualified professional coverage remains explicit; no multi-source priority/authority-election mechanism is required.
- **FR-025**: When the designated source is unavailable or invalid, retain its last accepted values with provenance and freshness. Do not fill professional facts from another source. Unknown values remain unknown until usable authorized evidence is available; a newer download alone does not prove a newer sporting publication.
- **FR-026**: An explicit withdrawal from the usable authoritative source MUST remain distinct from an unavailable observation and information never supplied. A withdrawn result MUST NOT be reintroduced from an obsolete observation or unauthorized source. Invalid detail alone MUST NOT erase the overall result.
- **FR-027**: Accepted values MUST retain sufficient source, context and observation-instant attribution to explain selection and freshness, including provider publication time when supplied. Missing provider timestamps MUST remain unknown. This requires neither every old raw response nor a complete chronology of all values.
- **FR-028**: A result published as such by the official source MUST be considered definitive unless indicated otherwise; Blockout MUST NOT require separate universal proof of match completion or implement general adjudication of competition rules. Explicitly provisional scores MUST be marked provisional; if the meaning of a score publication is truly ambiguous, its definitive nature MUST remain unconfirmed. Neither elapsed time since match start nor a universal number of sets won establishes its end. These distinctions do not promise continuous live-score tracking.
- **FR-029**: A usable official overall score MUST remain usable when its set detail is absent, invalid or contradictory. Unusable detail MUST be hidden and diagnosed without rejecting that overall result or independent date or venue changes. Blockout MUST NOT reconstruct a different winning score from the detail.
- **FR-030**: Recognized special official results, including forfeits and penalties (`F`, `P`, `3/F`, `3/P`, `F/F`, `2/F`), MUST retain their source meaning without invented numeric substitutions, winners or standings calculations. Recognizable sporting annotations MUST NOT be treated as a new team identity. An unknown notation MUST be retained as uninterpreted material for diagnostics rather than implicitly converted to a normal result.
- **FR-031**: A usable authoritative observation still identifying the match but withdrawing its result MUST remove the displayed result and associated set detail and return the match to a state without a confirmed result, even after its scheduled date. It MUST NOT infer cancellation or a match in progress. Later corrections retain the same match identity. F07 defines the resulting notification policy, including treatment of repeated or corrected results.
- **FR-032**: An identifiable match MAY exist without a usable date or confirmed time. A known date without a time MUST be retained without inventing an instant. FFVB time 00:00 MUST mean an unknown match start unless explicit contrary evidence confirms midnight. An unknown start MUST NOT trigger F01 fast cadence. [F03 FR-010–FR-011](../007-sporting-consultation/spec.md) hides dateless matches from all public surfaces while retaining their data; a known date without a time remains displayable.
- **FR-033**: Sporting dates and times MUST retain their local source meaning and applicable time zone; ordinary local timestamps of covered metropolitan FFVB/LNV competitions use Europe/Paris, with seasonal offset changes, unless explicit source context indicates otherwise. Unresolvable time-zone or time ambiguity MUST remain unknown rather than guessed. Season membership is independent of postponement across a calendar-year boundary. Display time zones and daily grouping belong to [F03 FR-009–FR-012](../007-sporting-consultation/spec.md); a calendar category is not a confirmed sporting end.

<a id="official-standings"></a>

#### Official standings

- **FR-034**: Standings MUST reproduce one usable official table for their pool/phase: LNV pages for professional competitions and FFVB elsewhere. Preserve order, stated positions, available statistics and special labels/ratios. Unknown values remain unknown, unlimited ratios are not invented finite numbers, and no local ranking recalculation or mixed-source table is allowed. A phase without published standings receives no fabricated table.
- **FR-035**: Usable official standings MUST remain publishable when a row cannot be associated with a currently viewable Blockout team or contradicts current calendar participation. That row MUST retain its official label and position without a team-navigation link. It MUST NOT create or restore a team or participation, or cause local row deletion or rank renumbering.
- **FR-036**: Globally unreadable or unassociable standings MUST be isolated from calendar integration. Retain the last usable official table with its freshness, or mark standings unavailable if none exists. Invalid or missing individual statistics remain unavailable, without invented zeros, when table identity and order remain usable. A source outage MUST NOT replace the last usable table with fabricated or unauthorized-source data.

<a id="visibility-history-and-recovery"></a>

#### Visibility, history and recovery

- **FR-037**: A pool omitted by a usable catalog authoritative for its own season MUST immediately be hidden with its matches, without erasing identities and data or declaring cancellation. FFVB catalog observations govern FFVB pools; LNV catalog observations govern professional phases identified on LNV. FFVB absence alone MUST NOT hide a phase still published by its authoritative LNV catalog. An unreadable or partial catalog proves no absence.
- **FR-038**: An absent match in a complete successfully integrated designated-source calendar MUST be hidden everywhere without erasing identity, results or relationships. A qualified wholly empty current calendar hides every match and the pool under FR-019. Following or retained scores do not bypass hiding. Outage, partial integration and pauses establish no absence.
- **FR-039**: Withdrawal removes only the team’s participation in the affected pool, preserving its identity and other participations. A wholly empty qualified current calendar hides all those participations and the pool. A team remains visible if it has another visible participation; its club stays consultable. Standings alone do not re-establish participation. Qualified attributable calendar reappearance reuses the existing identities.
- **FR-040**: A team without any participation in a visible pool MUST be hidden while retained. Clubs MUST remain viewable even without a visible team, including between seasons. A club-page failure MUST NOT be interpreted as club disappearance.
- **FR-041**: Withdrawn: manual pack/pool exclusion and its commands are removed. Collection pauses alone do not hide resources. Division inactivity, qualified source absence and current calendar emptiness remain independent visibility reasons; lifting one does not lift another.
- **FR-042**: Qualified catalog rediscovery clears only the catalog-absence reason. If no other reason remains, retained data may become visible with known freshness. A pool hidden by a qualified empty calendar requires usable nonempty calendar evidence to clear that reason; catalog presence alone is insufficient. Attributable reappearance of known matches and participations reuses their identities and clears the relevant withdrawal without a second confirmation. Independent division and item restrictions remain.
- **FR-043**: Season closure or disappearance from another season’s catalog MUST NOT erase or withdraw acquired history. Preserve the last useful accepted sporting state and identities without requiring every collection version. A closed season is terminal for acquisition, not historically hidden by default. Refuse any new cycle or recollection; only already running work may finish once under F01 FR-055. Initial historical reconstruction takes place before closure: complete nonempty qualified input may reconcile individual facts; wholly empty input preserves retained data. The last authorized data remains viewable without lifting an independent hiding reason. An inaccessible, unreadable or wrong-season archive preserves previously accepted data. F14 alone defines authorized loss of irrecoverable V1 sporting data during reset, not routine loss of V2 history. This exception MUST NOT erase manual associations between V1 clubs and logos: club references, URLs and corresponding files MUST be verifiably retained before any reset. F14 defines controlled recovery; a certain mapping allows association, while an absent or ambiguous mapping preserves the association for resolution without an invented club or name-only matching. This manual-recovery obligation concerns club logos without requiring migration of other V1 customizations.
- **FR-044**: Hidden resources MUST be absent from ordinary lists, search and calendars, and their existing direct links MUST indicate unavailability rather than expose the hidden resource or navigate to an assumed replacement. Existing follows and relationships MUST remain tied to retained identities for reappearance, without automatic transfer; F06 defines list presentation and associated actions. FR-035 allows the independent official label in standings without a link to the hidden team.

<a id="presentation-administration-and-club-location"></a>

#### Presentation, administration and club location

- **FR-045**: Authorized manual customizations of club display names and team and pool full or short names MUST withstand source refreshes, classification corrections and reactivations without rewriting original source names or sporting identity. Removing a customization MUST restore the current automatic value of that field, including an applicable verified name mapping. Each full- or short-name customization is independent; a source-name change does not authorize erasing another field’s customization. Club contact-detail corrections belong to FR-049: manual value, deliberate absence and return to source are distinct and independent per field.
- **FR-046**: A team’s own logo MUST take priority over its club’s. Without customization, it MUST use the current club logo across all seasons; changing the club logo updates this inheritance and removing team customization restores it. A missing source logo MUST NOT erase manual logos. A club logo recovered from V1 under FR-043 MUST retain this protection and inheritance. Club, team and division logo uploads MUST retain existing PNG/JPEG support and the maximum size of 5 MiB (5,242,880 bytes); unsupported or oversized inputs are refused. Failed replacement MUST leave the last valid presentation available and allow retry. No seasonal logo archive is required.
- **FR-047**: Sporting presentation, classification, division lifecycle and exceptional identity corrections MUST require explicit permissions for the relevant action and scope. An ordinary user or live-stream moderator receives no implicit right. Refusal MUST leave state unchanged; F12 defines assignment and shared administration access. No new generic manual score or match editor, alias interface or split/merge console is authorized. Club contact-detail correction, deliberate absence and return to source MUST remain possible for an operator with the corresponding correction permission, without requiring a new mobile form. These actions MUST change neither club identity nor its teams, participations or follows.
- **FR-048**: Operators MUST distinguish accepted, rejected and partially integrated observations and identify the relevant source, season, pool and match context, time and usable reasons without personal data in diagnostics or raw-document archives. Successful acquisition or a few successful match updates MUST NOT be presented as complete calendar integration. Classification refusals MUST explain conflicting scopes; exceptional technical reconciliations MUST be authorized and their intended identity effects verified. F13 defines shared visibility and recovery, F11 diagnostic retention. For contact-detail corrections, refusal or confirmed failure MUST preserve the last accepted state; a lost response MUST allow establishing the outcome before retry. Under F13, repetition produces no additional effect and an old request cannot silently overwrite a more recent correction. Diagnostics describe the outcome without copying personal contact details.
- **FR-049**: Covered clubs MUST retain available, authorized page details, including source and display names, locality and address, contacts and website, subject to F11 minimization. Usable corrections or explicit removals of automatic fields MUST update those fields, invalid fields MUST preserve usable previous values, and an unavailable page MUST preserve previous details. For each address, city, postal code, email, phone and website field, an authorized operator MUST be able to choose a durable manual correction, durable deliberate absence or source management. All three states MUST remain distinct and independent per field. Collection and reactivation MUST remove neither a correction nor deliberate absence; automatic changes remain subject to validity and privacy rules. Explicit return to source MUST use the current usable, authorized automatic value without arbitrarily restoring old contact details or changing other fields; without an eligible value, the field remains absent. F11 restrictions prevail in all three states, including after restoration; no archive of old personal contact details is added. Manual display customizations and independent logo inheritance remain protected. Page processing authorizes neither club withdrawal nor collection of the entire directory.
- **FR-050**: A club’s published coordinates MUST locate its municipality from consistent and sufficiently identifying municipality and postal information, without claiming to designate a gymnasium or match venue. An unknown or ambiguous locality MUST receive no arbitrary point. Teams on pool maps use club location; no team–practice-venue matching feature is required. The municipality and postal context used MUST be the one actually retained after applying corrections, deliberate absences and restrictions. Absent, inconsistent or insufficient information MUST NOT preserve an old point that has become misleading.
- **FR-051**: A municipality or postal-code change MUST invalidate the old published point until receipt of a usable response for the new context. A street-only change within the same municipality and postal context MUST NOT move the municipality-level point or create a new need for that reason alone. New eligible or changed locality needs follow F01’s one-hour deadline for an attempt and its pause and retry rules. This rule concerns the effective change, whether from the source, a correction, deliberate absence or explicit return to source. A source change masked by a durable correction MUST NOT move the club or trigger new geocoding for that reason alone.
- **FR-052**: A published geocoding result MUST be usable, unambiguous and match the current locality context. Empty or ambiguous results remain unresolved, with automatic retries suppressed for unchanged context under F01 and authorized family-level relaunch. Technical failure without a locality change MUST NOT erase a valid point; a response for the old context MUST NOT republish the old location after a change. Matching MUST be checked against the effective locality at publication, including after an administrative change or return to source.
- **FR-053**: When the calendar unambiguously identifies a club and other creation and publication conditions are met, its reference MUST allow establishing the persistent club needed by its teams and publishing identified matches without waiting for page retrieval. Page unavailability MUST NOT block these operations. Only available, attributable information MUST be used; unknown details remain absent until authorized retrieval under FR-049. Source references designating only future places MUST NOT create clubs. An unknown or ambiguous club association prevents creation of that match or team and causes local isolation rather than an assumed link, without blocking other usable encounters.

<a id="key-entities"></a>

### Main entities

- **Club contact detail**: Address or contact field managed by the source, manually corrected or deliberately absent. These independent modes govern the authorized effective value; they do not change club identity or constitute contact history.
- **Club**: Persistent sporting organization, source references, presentation and authorized page information; its municipality is independent of match venues and team visibility.
- **Division / classification**: Administered sporting category and division, format and gender context shared by teams and pools. A native source pack supplies acquisition defaults; a complete pool override supplies exceptions. The last accepted classification of published sporting state remains distinct from current settings needed to authorize acquisition.
- **Season**: Source-confirmed sporting context with an operator-controlled collection lifecycle rather than an identity inferred from matches’ calendar dates.
- **Team**: Seasonal club team within a classification, distinct from its names and any particular pool participation.
- **Pool / professional phase**: Classified competition grouping within a season, supported by attributable official references; professional-source coverage does not require an unambiguous FFVB mapping.
- **Participation**: Relationship between an identified team and a pool, with retained identity and visibility. Not created from a standings row alone.
- **Match**: Identified encounter between two teams within a pool and season, with possibly unknown date, time or result, and independently qualified source result and visibility.
- **Official standings**: Official table attributed to its source for a pool or phase, preserving row identity and order and optional Blockout team links. Neither a local recalculation from match results nor merely the set of current participations.
- **Observation / visibility reason / presentation customization**: Source evidence differs from accepted sporting state; source withdrawal, calendar emptiness and division inactivity affect visibility without erasure; explicit presentation customizations do not redefine identity.

## Success criteria _(mandatory)_

<a id="measurable-outcomes"></a>

### Measurable outcomes

- **SC-001**: A01–A05 preserve intended identities and create zero fictional participants, cross-season merges or duplicates; every unresolved mapping has a bounded outcome. In A05, zero teams or matches meeting other conditions are blocked solely by an unambiguously identified club’s unavailable page; no missing detail is invented.
- **SC-002**: A06–A10 retain identities in every conflict-free correction and apply zero partial changes for a refused administrative correction; inactive-division visibility and eligibility agree. A09 produces zero active/inactive state changes during presentation editing and refuses duplicate names both on creation and rename. In A10, only explicit reactivation lifts the inactivity reason without bypassing other restrictions. Removing the sole acquisition classification causes neither hiding due to that removal nor loss of the last accepted classification, and authorizes no later collection while settings are missing; a refused restoration does not resume collection.
- **SC-003**: In A12’s 100-match example, all 99 usable matches remain eligible for update despite one invalid match, and incomplete processing produces zero absence-based withdrawals. A11–A15 demonstrate no invented references, duplicate effects on replay or inconsistent published references.
- **SC-004**: A16–A23 select the specified official values, retain zero invented scores or dates and distinguish definitive, provisional and unknown results without rejecting a usable overall score solely because of invalid set detail.
- **SC-005**: A24–A25 preserve every usable official row and its supplied order or position, including unlinked rows, with zero local rank recalculation and zero team creation from standings alone.
- **SC-006**: A26–A32 hide a pool, matches and participations after one qualified wholly empty current observation, retaining identities. A team with another visible participation remains visible; clubs stay consultable. Partial integration, false empty and wrong context cause no absence-based hiding. Catalog absence stops old references; failed discovery does not. Reappearance clears only the corresponding reason, without a second confirmation. Closure preserves history and refuses new acquisition. (FR-019, FR-037–FR-044)
- **SC-007**: A33–A39 preserve every applicable customization and all three independent club contact-detail modes. A36 produces zero collection overwrites of a correction or deliberate absence, zero F11 bypasses and zero additional effects on retry; every return to source concerns only the requested field. These scenarios refuse every unauthorized change and publish zero arbitrary coordinates or coordinates from obsolete context; municipality geocoding requests inherit F01 deadlines. A39 reapplies only certain club-logo associations, retains every unresolved association and distinguishes available from missing files without equating a URL with retention evidence.

<a id="assumptions"></a>

## Assumptions

- This functional specification covers existing sporting capabilities and changes explicitly approved by the product owner. No fantasy game, social system, roster model, new provider, automatic merge tool or generic match editor is added.
- Official data is generally usable. The product trusts official sporting content attributed to its source while distinguishing uncertain identities and contexts, unsupported notations and actual acquisition or integration failures. It does not adjudicate federation rules.
- Source examples establish observed fields and behavior, not exhaustive coverage, permanent identifiers or evidence of future availability. The accepted designated-source policy does not claim that the preferred page always publishes first.
- Explicit returns to source for names, display or club contact details restore the current usable, authorized automatic value, not a historical state. Deliberate absence of a contact-detail field is a distinct decision. Contact-detail corrections create no additional V1 recovery obligation; F14 retains its approved scope. The historical guarantee concerns retained identities and the last useful state, not an audit copy of every collection.
- Pool visibility reasons accumulate. Operator settings affect acquisition under F01 cycle boundaries, while current visibility and manual customizations cannot be bypassed by late work.
- Personal-data purposes/retention, functional freshness, notifications, displayed day grouping and visual design remain owned by their referenced scopes. This specification supplies their sporting semantics without introducing performance/capacity or monthly availability qualification.
- No technical planning is authorized before acceptance of the complete functional corpus and global consistency and Figma review under [#247](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout-legacy/issues/247). No constitution change or V2 topology is selected.

## Clarifications

<a id="session-2026-09-16"></a>

### Session 2026-09-16

The approved preparatory decisions below are incorporated into normative requirements. They describe selected behavior, not implementation completion.

- Q: How should unreliable calendars be handled? → A: Reject globally unusable contexts and structures, isolate local match anomalies, retain successful updates and prohibit absence-based withdrawals after incomplete processing. (FR-018–FR-022)
- Q: May collection continue when catalog discovery fails? → A: Yes, with references already successfully discovered for the same season and under current eligibility; confirmed absence still stops collection of that source. (FR-017)
- Q: How should ordinary results and standings be trusted? → A: Trust published results, retain overall scores when their detail is invalid and display usable official standings rows even without a Blockout team link. (FR-028–FR-036)
- Q: Which cases require exceptional identity intervention? → A: Ambiguous mappings and classification changes requiring separation or merging; refuse the entire requested administrative correction if an affected scope conflicts. (FR-007–FR-014)
- Q: What happens when a pool override is removed without a replacement from the pack? → A: Stop collection under F01 cycle rules; keep published resources visible with their last accepted classification and identities, subject to other visibility reasons. Restoring classification must satisfy correction checks before collection resumes. (FR-004, FR-011–FR-014)
- Q: Which source governs professional matches and standings? → A: LNV pages only, with no XML or FFVB supplementation. FFVB remains authoritative for other covered competitions. (FR-019, FR-024, FR-034)
- Q: Does retained history imply permanent visibility? → A: No; source-confirmed withdrawals, calendar emptiness and division deactivations hide resources without erasing them. Closed seasons remain viewable unless another reason applies. (FR-037–FR-044)
- Q: What does the map locate? → A: The club’s municipality, hiding the old point after a locality change and without claiming to locate a gymnasium. (FR-050–FR-052)

## Approved F01 handoff clarification — 2026-10-06

The [F01 specification](../001-source-acquisition/spec.md), A38–A45 and FR-050–FR-054, owns supported pre-launch source preparation, locked season bindings, ordinary youth pools, qualified tour contexts and initial reconstruction before definitive closure. `Jo` is not universally a tour. Calendar/standings cadence follows the pool or phase; each LNV detail follows its own reliable H−1/H+4 window at five minutes and otherwise thirty minutes, including final results. No field reread means no new freshness. Rollover of a current LNV entry is not absence from an older bound championship. F02 retains match transactions, seasonal identity, initial historical corrections before closure, retained history after terminal closure and notification-transition suppression for initial imports. Human season confirmation cannot validate a contradictory or malformed source.

Acceptance supplements A01/A16/A17/A21: one persistent club with new teams/pools each season and no automatic follow transfer; independent covered championship pools sharing one team versus ordinary N2 matchdays; same-club distinct teams; per-match LNV cadence and retained detail freshness; special-score fixtures distinguish observed F examples from a synthetic 3/P regression case.
