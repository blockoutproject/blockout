# Feature Specification: Sporting data, identity and lifecycle

**Feature Branch**: `develop`

**Created**: 2026-09-16

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline; [plan.md](plan.md) owns technical design and [tasks.md](tasks.md) tracks implementation.

**Input**: Functional scope approved by the product owner, including proportionate reliability decisions: trust official sporting content, establish its identity and context, isolate local anomalies and preserve successful useful updates. This document defines F02 in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications).

<a id="user-scenarios--testing-mandatory"></a>

## User Scenarios & Testing

Design input: the [approved prototype and Figma references](plan.md#consumers-and-approved-design) cover pool mapping, professional teams without a club, optional club navigation, partial maps and automatic logo restoration. Design approval does not qualify production behavior.

Visitors and signed-in users view sporting information. Operators with explicit domain permissions manage sporting presentation, classifications and divisions. Authorized technical operators handle exceptional identity reconciliation; ordinary users and live-stream moderators implicitly receive none of these permissions. Source acquisition supplies observations under [F01](../001-source-acquisition/spec.md), not an already accepted sporting state.

Scenario identifiers are local acceptance references, not implementation tasks. Independent validations describe acceptance evidence to obtain; they do not claim that V2 is implemented or that a production result has been achieved.

<a id="user-story-1---recognize-the-same-sporting-resources-priority-p1"></a>

### User Story 1 — Recognize the same sporting resources (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want match corrections and postponements to preserve the correct teams and matches so that my links and follows do not accidentally refer to replacements.

**Why this priority**: Every score, follow relationship and historical view depends on correct association.

**Independent Test**: Compare identities and references in controlled calendars with repeated teams, changed labels, postponed matches and distinct seasons.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a known club entering a new season, **when** its teams and pools are acquired, **then** the club retains its identity while seasonal teams and pools are distinct. Two named teams of that club in the same division remain distinct; a team present in two pools with the same classification retains a single identity. (FR-001, FR-002, FR-003, FR-004)
2. **A02** — **Given** a known match, **when** its official date changes, **then** its identity and associated relationships are preserved; a repeated encounter or a code reused in another season or context causes no merge solely from the date or participant pair. (FR-005)
3. **A03** — **Given** source names differing only in letter case, excess spaces or typographic apostrophes, **when** compared in the same identity context, **then** these differences are considered equivalent; digits, accents, dots and hyphens remain significant. A verified, bounded alias resolves the variants it declares while retaining their source labels. (FR-006, FR-007)
4. **A04** — **Given** an existing match whose participant undergoes an unrecognized significant name change, **when** identity cannot be established, **then** that match retains its previous state and receives a diagnostic, other usable matches continue to be processed, and this incomplete processing produces no absence-based withdrawal. (FR-008, FR-019)
5. **A05** — **Given** a classified phase, **when** its calendar is integrated, **then** creation and publication follow these cases. (FR-009, FR-019, FR-049, FR-053)

   - **Identified club, unavailable page**: A club not yet in Blockout is unambiguously identified by its calendar reference. If other conditions are met, the club and its teams are created and identified matches published without waiting for the page. Unavailable details remain unknown; later page retrieval completes authorized information without replacing identities or erasing customizations.
   - **Unknown or ambiguous FFVB club association**: The affected team and match are not created from an assumed link; the anomaly remains local and other usable matches are processed. This incomplete processing authorizes no absence-based withdrawal.
   - **Undetermined qualifier or recognized bye**: The future place remains in source context without creating a club, fictional team or viewable match. It is not a malformed participant reference and does not block identifiable matches.
   - **Professional team without a club**: Qualified LNV participants establish their teams and matches without a club, club link or invented municipality. A later controlled club association preserves identities and follows.
   - **Participant present only in standings**: Its label is insufficient to create a team.

<a id="user-story-2---correct-classifications-without-losing-continuity-priority-p1"></a>

### User Story 2 — Correct classifications without losing continuity (Priority: P1)

As an authorized operator, I want to find a discovered pack and its pools, see their current mapping and make explicit classification corrections with understandable conflict refusals.

**Why this priority**: Classification changes may affect shared teams and existing user relationships.

**Independent Test**: Find a pack in a preparing season with the classification grant, read its current mapping and apply pack and pool settings to established participations. Compare effective classifications, identities and refused-operation outcomes.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A06** — **Given** discovered packs and pools, including in a preparing season, **when** an operator reads or corrects their mapping, **then** these cases apply. (FR-004, FR-011, FR-012, FR-047)

   - The sporting-classification grant alone opens “Mapping des poules” from Profile. Collection-only access is refused. A discovered unclassified pack and its pools are available before season activation, using their existing identities and established source and season labels.
   - A pack without a default has one classified custom pool and one unclassified inherited pool. Its row shows “Sans défaut” and “1/2 classées”. Each pool's detail shows its effective classification and mode. Filter by partial status, source and season and search for a matching pool beyond the first unfiltered page. The matching pack is returned and its detail still allows traversal of all its children. The season and source choices remain available after an empty filtered result, including the most recent catalogued season if it is preparing. Compare a pack whose children are all classified by inheritance or override, a pack with no classified children and childless packs with and without a default.
   - The list starts on the newest catalogued season. Choose “Toutes les saisons” or another season in the shared filter sheet. Each row presents the title and source followed by two badges aligned with the text above. “Sans défaut” and “5/5 classées” can appear together for a pack with five custom-classified pools. Open a pack and read its source and season in the header. Back retains the criteria, loaded results and reading position.
   - A complete division, format and gender override may make one pool eligible under an unclassified pack while other pools remain pending. Later pack changes preserve the override. Removing it restores inheritance subject to correction checks. If the pack supplies no replacement, collection stops under F01 cycle rules while published resources retain their last accepted classification, identities and visibility, subject to source hiding and inactive divisions. This retained classification does not authorize collection.
   - Edit the N2F pack default and see an inheriting pool pick up the accepted values. Set a “Personnalisé” classification for CFA Tour 2 with Division, Format and Genre, then verify that a later pack change preserves it. “Du pack” applies inheritance independently of the division catalogue and shows actual absence when the pack has no default. A final failure of a read required by the active form masks the entire editor. Retry only reads and recovers the same draft.

2. **A07** — **Given** a wrongly classified pool whose affected teams have neither conflicting outside participation nor target-identity collision, **when** an authorized correction applies, **then** the pool, teams, matches, follows and presentation customizations retain their identities and relationships under the corrected classification. An acknowledged Mapping save closes its sheet, reflects the accepted classification and shows one green confirmation at the top. After a period without classification, restoring the same classification or correcting it without conflict allows collection again subject to normal eligibility; none of these operations lifts a source-absence or inactive-division restriction. (FR-012, FR-013, FR-016, FR-041)
3. **A08** — **Given** a correction covering several pools, including a team shared with a pool outside the correction scope or an already existing matching target team, **when** correction is requested, **then** the entire request is refused with conflicting scopes identified; no conflict-free subset is implicitly applied. A refusal or technical write failure retains the draft for an explicit correction or retry. If restoring a missing acquisition classification was intended, collection remains ineligible and published resources retain their last accepted classification. A narrower request or controlled technical intervention remains possible. (FR-012, FR-014)
4. **A09** — **Given** an operator authorized to manage divisions, **when** they create a division or change its presentation, **then** these cases apply. (FR-015, FR-016, FR-047)

   - **Catalogue recovery**: A final first/full catalogue-read failure masks the results with the common F13 card while preserving search/activity criteria and independently authorized creation. Retry keeps the card, shows actual explicit-read progress and rereads only the catalogue; a successful response restores the applicable results and criteria.
   - **Authorized change**: Creation opens without first reading the division catalogue. During creation and editing, choosing a manual division logo requests the shared native crop confirmation and keeps the result in the draft until Save. Saving the name, colors and logo acts directly and preserves the accepted values as the form reference; discarding later edits or a failed subsequent read does not restore the empty creation form. Presentation editing preserves division identity and active or inactive state. An inactive division remains unavailable for a new classification; editing it restores neither resource visibility nor collection.
   - **Duplicate creation**: If “National 2” exists, the server refuses creating “national 2” without creating an additional division or changing the existing one. The name error appears at the field and preserves the entered name, colors and logo candidate.
   - **Duplicate rename**: If “National 2” exists, the server refuses renaming another division to “national 2”; its accepted name, identity and active or inactive state remain unchanged, and the editable draft is retained.

5. **A10** — **Given** a division containing data, **when** an authorized operator acts on its lifecycle, **then** these cases apply. (FR-016, FR-042)

   - **Deactivation**: Dependent collection stops under F01 cycle rules; pools, teams and matches become hidden without erasure. Clubs themselves are not hidden for this reason.
   - **Explicit reactivation**: Resources hidden solely for this reason return immediately with their known freshness. Collection may resume under F01 cycle rules if other eligibility conditions are met.
   - **Another restriction still present**: A source-withdrawn or calendar-empty pool remains hidden after division reactivation; lifting one reason bypasses no other reason.

<a id="user-story-3---receive-useful-updates-despite-isolated-failures-priority-p1"></a>

### User Story 3 — Receive useful updates despite isolated failures (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want valid matches to stay current even when another record is unusable, without treating failures as sporting withdrawals.

**Why this priority**: Official data is generally usable; disproportionate rejection reduces its usefulness.

**Independent Test**: Submit calendars mixing valid and invalid items, interrupted integrations and replayed observations, then compare accepted matches, retained records and withdrawal authorizations.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A11** — **Given** valid references from earlier discovery, **when** the next catalog read fails, **then** otherwise eligible collection may continue with those references in the same season during discovery retries. Without earlier successful discovery, no reference is invented; confirmed absence in a later usable catalog stops collection of that source. (FR-017)
2. **A12** — **Given** a calendar of 100 matches with one unusable match, **when** processed, **then** the other 99 may be updated and the failed match retains its previous state. Contradictory rows for one reference isolate that match; identical repetitions create no duplicate. In contrast, a globally unreadable document or one from the wrong season or pool causes calendar rejection. (FR-018, FR-019)
3. **A13** — **Given** a usable match with an invalid secondary field and another with normally absent information, **when** integrated, **then** invalidity preserves the old usable field, while absence remains explicit instead of becoming zero or an invented date. A known official withdrawal of a value follows that field’s rule. (FR-020)
4. **A14** — **Given** successful updates followed by an integration failure, **when** processing ends then is retried, **then** successes remain visible with valid team and pool references, failures are distinct and retried without duplicates, and absence-based withdrawals await complete successful processing. (FR-019, FR-021, FR-022, FR-048)
5. **A15** — **Given** a more recent accepted observation, a manual display customization or a currently hidden resource, **when** old work is replayed, **then** the older observation is ignored in full, including any previously missing field, and can neither overwrite the more recent accepted state, erase customization nor bypass current visibility reasons. (FR-022, FR-045)

<a id="user-story-4---read-official-results-and-rankings-priority-p1"></a>

### User Story 4 — Read official results and standings (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to read published sporting information, including professional phases and partial values, without Blockout inventing a substitute result or standings.

**Why this priority**: The application conveys official information rather than adjudicating sporting rules itself.

**Independent Test**: Compare accepted values with official observations attributed to their source, including conflicts, outages, result withdrawals and standings rows without a team link.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A16** — **Given** a correctly identified official LNV phase without an identifiable FFVB equivalent, **when** its classification and participants are established, **then** it may be represented from LNV; the missing mapping remains explicit and no other phase or season is used as a replacement. (FR-023)
2. **A17** — **Given** a professional match, **when** integrating its LNV calendar and match pages, **then** use verified contextual identity and compatible result/set facts. XML and FFVB observations do not supplement or compete for its sporting facts; the document-only exception in FR-024 preserves this authority. (FR-023–FR-024) Each detail keeps the F01 per-match collection window and the freshness of fields actually read; absent detail does not receive the calendar observation time.
3. **A18** — **Given** accepted professional page data, **when** the page fails or required detail is absent, **then** retain accepted values with freshness or report unknown data. Do not fall back to XML or FFVB for sporting facts or fabricate a result; a qualified document reference is not such a fallback. (FR-025–FR-027)
4. **A19** — **Given** a published official result, an explicitly provisional score and an ambiguous score publication, **when** interpreted, **then** they are respectively definitive, provisional and unconfirmed as definitive; no universal three-set threshold or elapsed-time rule replaces their meaning in the source. (FR-028)
5. **A20** — **Given** an official overall result of 3–1 with inconsistent or unusable set detail, **when** integrated, **then** 3–1 remains available, unusable detail is not displayed and the anomaly is diagnosed without invalidating independent date or venue changes. (FR-029)
6. **A21** — **Given** a recognized official forfeit or penalty notation, **when** integrated, **then** its sporting meaning is retained without an invented numeric score or locally calculated standings. An unrecognized notation is exposed to operations as uninterpreted source material without implicit conversion to zero. (FR-030) Provider-observed examples and synthetic regression fixtures, such as 3/P, must remain distinguishable as evidence.
7. **A22** — **Given** an accepted result, **when** the authoritative source supplies a usable observation of the same match with result withdrawal, **then** the displayed result and its set detail are removed and the match no longer has a confirmed result. A later corrected result may be accepted under the same identity. (FR-026, FR-031)
8. **A23** — **Given** an identifiable match without a date, with only a date, or with FFVB time 00:00 without contrary evidence, **when** integrated, **then** the known date is retained without inventing an instant or triggering a fast window from an unknown time. Ordinary source local times use their applicable time zone, including seasonal daylight-saving changes. (FR-032, FR-033)
9. **A24** — **Given** usable official standings, including a row for a team absent from the current calendar or unmatched to a viewable team, **when** published or refreshed, **then** every row is retained in the latest published sequence even if only the order changed. Printed ranks, when present, and available statistics remain source values. The unlinked row keeps its source label without creating a team, participation or navigation link. (FR-034, FR-035)
10. **A25** — **Given** a usable calendar, **when** standings acquisition fails, **then** the calendar is processed and the last usable standings keep their sequence and freshness. With no usable table, qualified non-publication remains distinct from unavailability. Missing columns and special ratios do not become zeros or invented statistics. (FR-034, FR-036)

### User Story 5 — Understand disappearances and recover retained history (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want withdrawn resources to disappear from ordinary consultation without losing identity or history during an outage or season closure.

**Why this priority**: Visibility, sporting result and data retention express different facts.

**Independent Test**: Apply bounded discovery, calendar and administration observations to current or closed seasons, then compare visibility, retained information and reappearance outcomes.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A26** — **Given** a previously visible pool, **when** a complete authoritative catalog for its source and season omits it, **then** the pool and its matches become hidden but retained. For a phase identified on LNV, absence from the LNV catalog is authoritative; FFVB absence alone does not hide a phase still published by LNV. (FR-037)
2. **A27** — **Given** known matches, **when** the authoritative calendar is processed, **then** these cases apply without erasing identities or internally retained results. (FR-019, FR-024, FR-038, FR-043)

   - **Individual withdrawal**: A complete, usable, nonempty and successfully integrated calendar hides an absent match everywhere, with or without a result: calendars, personal feed and page through an old link. Neither team nor pool following bypasses this withdrawal, which does not mean sporting cancellation.
   - **Empty calendar**: One qualified wholly empty current observation hides the pool, matches and participations, retaining identities and catalog-based collection eligibility.
   - **Outage or incomplete observation**: Partial, failed or wrong-context integration authorizes no absence-based withdrawal.
   - **Source and catalog**: The designated source is LNV pages for professional competition and FFVB elsewhere; qualified catalog absence is distinct from failed discovery.

3. **A28** — **Given** a team absent after qualified absence reconciliation, **when** its pool participation is hidden, **then** other participations remain. One qualified empty current calendar hides that pool and all its participations/matches; the team hides only when no visible participation remains, while its club stays consultable. Closed seasons reject new acquisition and retain history. (FR-019, FR-038–FR-040, FR-043)
4. **A29** — **Given** retained sporting data, **when** a family is paused, **then** collection stops without hiding that data. Division inactivity remains an independent visibility restriction; no manual pack/pool exclusion command exists. (FR-016, FR-041)
5. **A30** — **Given** catalog-hidden identities with assigned mappings and an independent calendar-empty or division restriction, **when** a catalog read is incomplete, unavailable or wrongly attributed, **then** known entries, mappings and all reasons remain. **When** the complete authoritative same-season catalog lists an identity again, **then** reuse the identity and mapping and clear only its catalog-absence reason. A usable nonempty calendar is still required to clear its qualified calendar-empty reason. (FR-019, FR-024, FR-037, FR-042)
6. **A31** — **Given** two active seasons and an older acquired season, **when** one is definitively closed, **then** history remains consultable under its existing visibility restrictions. New acquisition and recollection are refused. Only a cycle already running at closure may finish once with normal order/visibility guards. Initial historical reconstruction occurs before closure and never substitutes current-season content for an unavailable archive. The V1 reset exception remains separate. (FR-010, FR-019, FR-024, FR-043; F01 FR-055)

7. **A32** — **Given** a hidden resource with an existing follow or direct link, **when** a user views ordinary lists, search or calendars, **then** it is absent and its old link indicates unavailability; its identity and relationships remain retained for reappearance without automatic transfer to another resource. (FR-044)

**Authority examples for A26–A30.** A complete FFVB catalog covers its organizer's pools for the admitted season, including pools in separate packs. A complete LNV catalog covers the groups and phases of its admitted championship and season. Each calendar has authority over its own pool or phase. Absence follows FR-019 and FR-037.

| Source observation                                                | Expected presence outcome                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Complete usable nonempty designated-source calendar omits a match | Hide it after complete integration; retain identity                            |
| Partial, wrong-context or failed calendar                         | Preserve accepted presence; no absence reconciliation                          |
| One complete valid empty current calendar                         | Hide pool, matches and participations; keep collecting if catalog-listed       |
| Complete authoritative catalog absence                            | Stop old reference and hide affected pool; preserve identities and mappings    |
| Incomplete, unavailable or wrongly attributed catalog             | Preserve accepted entries, mappings and visibility; retain eligible references |
| Catalog rediscovery after calendar-empty hiding                   | Clear catalog absence only; calendar-empty reason remains                      |
| Attributable nonempty reappearance                                | Reuse identities and clear the relevant absence/empty reason                   |
| New acquisition requested after season closure                    | Refuse the request; preserve history                                           |

<a id="user-story-6---maintain-presentation-and-useful-club-locations-priority-p2"></a>

### User Story 6 — Maintain presentation and useful club locations (Priority: P2)

As an authorized operator, I want durable presentation corrections and the ability to correct club contact details from mobile administration. As a user, I want the club's useful address-based location, with a municipality fallback when needed, without an unsupported claim about a match venue.

**Why this priority**: Names, logos and location facilitate navigation without redefining sporting identity.

**Independent Test**: Apply source refreshes, manual changes, reactivations and geocoding responses to controlled club, team and pool examples, including refused operations. Correct a collected club postal code from mobile administration and verify the effective locality through geocoding and a later collection.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A33** — **Given** manually corrected club display names and full or short team or pool names, **when** source labels change or resources are reactivated, **then** every explicit customization persists without changing identity or source labels. Removing a customization restores the current automatic value of that field. Save acts directly; an older currently authorized valid form may replace newer manual values for submitted fields without Relire. Contract-optional PATCH omissions preserve current values and explicit null retains its meaning. Accepted values become the form reference for subsequent changes and discard. A final later form-data read failure masks all editable content and save commands with the common F13 Error Card without erasing accepted values, dirty fields or applicable prepared files. Retry keeps the card during its real active progress and returns to the same draft on success. (FR-045, FR-048)
2. **A34** — **Given** nonprofessional teams with and without their own logo across several seasons, **when** the club logo changes, **then** teams without their own logo inherit the current club logo; a team customization stays in place and its removal restores inheritance. A professional team instead uses its customization, accepted LNV logo, optionally associated club logo, then ordinary missing artwork in that order. Removing its customization restores the current automatic choice; failed LNV image retrieval leaves the last valid image and match integration independent. An unsupported or oversized image, or failed replacement, does not replace the last valid logo and can be corrected or retried. Editor images remain passive. A refused private-image read withdraws its bytes; a later network failure reports the current failure without restoring the refused image or removing an admissible local candidate. (FR-046–FR-048)
3. **A35** — **Given** an ordinary user or moderator without the relevant business permission, **when** they attempt a presentation, classification, division change, exceptional identity correction, or correction, deliberate absence or return to source for club contact details, **then** the action is refused without modification. Authorized corrections produce understandable outcomes for affected scopes. When a refused read is followed by a technical failure, the current error is shown and withdrawn data and commands remain unavailable under their actual permissions. (FR-047, FR-048)
4. **A36** — **Given** a club page and authorized operator, **when** contact details are corrected or recollected, **then** these cases apply. (FR-045, FR-047–FR-051)
   - **Mobile administration**: The authorized operator edits the street address, municipality, postal code, email, phone and website alongside the club's name and logo. The text inputs start at their permitted effective values and show the eligible source value underneath when available. Editing a contact, leaving it empty and explicitly returning to source select the corresponding independent mode. One Save applies the changes. The club name shows its current effective value and original source name, and an unrelated edit preserves its existing customization mode.
   - **Durable value**: For each address, city, postal code, email, phone and website field, a recorded correction survives multiple collections and reactivation, even if the source changes. Club identity, teams, participations and follows remain unchanged.
   - **Deliberate absence**: The field remains absent despite source presence. This state is confused neither with acquisition failure nor return to source.
   - **Independence**: A corrected phone, deliberately absent email and source-managed website may coexist. Changing one field does not change the others’ mode.
   - **Return to source**: An explicit action resumes the current usable, authorized automatic value of the targeted field alone. Without an eligible value, it remains absent; no old contact is arbitrarily recovered. An unavailable page or invalid field does not replace the last still-valid authorized automatic value with an invented value.
   - **Privacy**: An F11 restriction prevents publication of the affected value, including after correction, return to source, collection or restoration. Old personal contact details do not become an archive.
   - **Refusal, failure and recovery**: Without permission, no change is accepted. Confirmed failure preserves the last state; a failed or lost response retains input with ordinary failure feedback and permits explicit manual retry without a verification prerequisite. Current authorization, privacy restrictions and resulting-state validation remain authoritative. An older valid command may replace newer manual values only for the fields it submits; omitted PATCH fields preserve current values. The same guarantees cover all three modes.
   - **Source page**: Its failure does not withdraw the club or erase still-authorized details. Valid source changes update automatic values without removing name or logo customizations.
   - **Postal-code correction**: An operator corrects an incorrect FFvolley postal code in mobile club administration. The next eligible automatic geocoding attempt uses the corrected effective locality. When a later FFvolley collection supplies a different eligible postal code, the source value updates and the saved manual code remains effective.
5. **A37** — **Given** currently permitted effective club address, municipality and available postal information, **when** a corresponding unambiguous geocoding response arrives after F01's full-address-first lookup, **then** the club receives that address point. If the address is absent or its qualified lookup is empty or ambiguous, an unambiguous municipality fallback is a valid point. No permitted municipality or no unambiguous final result creates no arbitrary point. Neither point claims to locate a gymnasium or match venue. (FR-050, FR-052)
6. **A38** — **Given** a published club point, **when** location context changes, **then** these cases apply. (FR-049–FR-052)
   - **Effective change**: A new effective address, municipality or postal code from the source, a manual correction, restriction, deliberate absence or return to source invalidates the old point until a valid response for the new context. Removing the address permits a new municipality fallback; removing the municipality prevents geocoding. Unknown, inconsistent or insufficient context creates no arbitrary replacement.
   - **Unchanged context**: A source change masked by a durable correction does not move the club or create a geocoding request for that reason alone. A valid municipality fallback is not repeatedly upgraded while its effective context remains unchanged.
   - **Late response or failure**: A response targeting the old context does not restore the old location, even after a return to source. Technical failure without a locality change does not erase a valid point. Attempts for the current context respect F01 delay, pauses and retries.
7. **A39** — **Given** retained associations between V1 clubs and their logo URLs, **when** clubs are rebuilt for V2, **then** a certain identity match allows reapplying the logo as manual presentation, with normal team inheritance. An absent or ambiguous club keeps its association in recovery evidence without a fictional club or name-only matching. A missing file is reported; retaining its URL alone does not prove preservation. F14 requires verified retention of associations and files before any reset. (FR-002, FR-043, FR-046)

<a id="edge-cases"></a>

### Edge Cases

- Local anomalies are isolated, while an unknown global season or pool context invalidates the calendar; neither case authorizes a guessed identity or absence-based withdrawal from incomplete evidence.
- Recognized future qualifiers or byes create no fictional resources. Incomplete identifiable matches may have unknown dates, times or results without being malformed.
- A partial update may be useful without proving complete synchronization. Overall acquisition and integration success is distinct from per-match success.
- A result may be corrected or withdrawn independently of match visibility. Source absence does not prove cancellation and a definitive result does not prove synchronization of every source.
- An official standings row may remain visible after calendar participation disappears; standings are not a substitute authority for creating or reactivating a team.
- Pool and team visibility may depend on several simultaneous reasons. A collection response cannot undo an independent source restriction, an inactive division or a manual display customization.
- An unrecognized club-code change, a merger or an ambiguous provider mapping causes no automatic identity merge; controlled technical reconciliation is the bounded resolution path.

## Requirements

<a id="functional-requirements"></a>

### Functional Requirements

The [F11 club-contact restriction](../004-advertising-privacy-legal/spec.md#purposes-minimization-and-shared-restrictions) removes source and manual values for the targeted field and prevents their republication. Ordinary manual editing, deliberate absence and return to source do not lift this separate restriction. Handling is manual without a new administration journey; there is no generic person-matching subsystem.

#### Identity and classification

- **FR-001**: Sporting identity, source reference and context, original source labels and displayed presentation MUST remain distinct. A source or display change MUST NOT by itself replace Blockout identities.
- **FR-002**: A Club MUST represent an organization persisting across seasons. Known provider references identify its observations; a new reference MUST NOT be merged with another club solely from name or address similarity. Verified exceptional reference continuity or handling of a merger requires controlled technical reconciliation, not an automatically inferred history transfer.
- **FR-003**: A Team MUST belong to a season, division, format and gender. Nonprofessional FFVB teams MUST have an established club. Professional teams use the native LNV team reference within their qualified season/championship context and MAY have no club; an optional technical association to an existing club changes neither sporting identity nor matches or follows, is never rewritten by ingestion and does not automatically propagate between seasons. No club-linking interface or automatic cross-provider matching is required. FFVB teams with distinct names in that context remain distinct; a team may participate in several independent pools with the same classification, retaining one identity and one team follow. F03 owns their presentation and navigation. Genuinely different divisions or seasons define different teams; correction of a mistaken classification follows FR-013–FR-014.
- **FR-004**: A Pool MUST have a season and competition context independent of its display name. A qualified catalogue may establish the pack and pool identities before sporting classification or seasonal activation. Acquired sporting state MUST carry its accepted classification and retain it when current acquisition settings are missing under FR-012. Youth qualifying pools use this ordinary model without required progression links. When an FFVB code denotes distinct groups across published tours, each qualified tour/group/pool context is a distinct pool; a normal matchday is not. A shared CSV is partitioned only for a demonstrated source format. FFVB references are interpreted in their league and season context; LNV phase references in their championship, season and phase context. A pack represents the provider grouping and creates neither another team identity nor a sporting division.
- **FR-005**: Match identity MUST be established from attributable source references in the correct provider, season and competition, pool or phase context. Postponement preserves identity. The date or participant pair alone MUST NOT cause a merge, and repeated encounters or codes reused in different contexts MUST remain distinct. An ambiguous source mapping MUST leave the existing match unchanged by that unassociated enrichment and produce a diagnostic rather than an assumed duplicate match.

### Key Entities

- **Club contact detail**: Address or contact field managed by the source, manually corrected or deliberately absent. These independent modes govern the authorized effective value; they do not change club identity or constitute contact history.
- **Club**: Persistent sporting organization, source references, presentation and authorized page information; its address-based location or municipality fallback is independent of match venues and team visibility.
- **Division / classification**: Administered sporting category and division, format and gender context shared by teams and pools. A native source pack supplies acquisition defaults; a complete pool override supplies exceptions. The last accepted classification of published sporting state remains distinct from current settings needed to authorize acquisition.
- **Season**: Source-confirmed sporting context with an operator-controlled collection lifecycle rather than an identity inferred from matches’ calendar dates.
- **Team**: Seasonal sporting team within a classification, distinct from its names and any particular pool participation. FFVB teams have a club; professional LNV teams may have none and retain their native contextual identity after a technical club association.
- **Pool / professional phase**: Classified competition grouping within a season, supported by attributable official references; professional-source coverage does not require an unambiguous FFVB mapping.
- **Participation**: Relationship between an identified team and a pool, with retained identity and visibility. Not created from a standings row alone.
- **Match**: Identified encounter between two teams within a pool and season, with possibly unknown date, time or result, and independently qualified source result and visibility.
- **Official standings**: Official table attributed to its source for a pool or phase, preserving row identity and order and optional Blockout team links. Neither a local recalculation from match results nor merely the set of current participations.
- **Observation / visibility reason / presentation customization**: Source evidence differs from accepted sporting state; source withdrawal, calendar emptiness and division inactivity affect visibility without erasure; explicit presentation customizations do not redefine identity.

## Assumptions

- This functional specification covers existing sporting capabilities and changes explicitly approved by the product owner. No fantasy game, social system, roster model, new provider, automatic merge tool or generic match editor is added.
- Official data is generally usable. The product trusts official sporting content attributed to its source while distinguishing uncertain identities and contexts, unsupported notations and actual acquisition or integration failures. It does not adjudicate federation rules.
- Source examples establish observed fields and behavior, not exhaustive coverage, permanent identifiers or evidence of future availability. The accepted designated-source policy does not claim that the preferred page always publishes first.
- Explicit returns to source for names, display or club contact details restore the current usable, authorized automatic value, not a historical state. Deliberate absence of a contact-detail field is a distinct decision. Contact-detail corrections create no additional V1 recovery obligation; F14 retains its approved scope. The historical guarantee concerns retained identities and the last useful state, not an audit copy of every collection.
- Pool visibility reasons accumulate. Operator settings affect acquisition under F01 cycle boundaries, while current visibility and manual customizations cannot be bypassed by late work.
- Personal-data purposes/retention, functional freshness, notifications, displayed day grouping and visual design remain owned by their referenced scopes. This specification supplies their sporting semantics without introducing performance/capacity or monthly availability qualification.
- Technical planning follows the [constitution](../../.specify/memory/constitution.md); this specification selects no V2 topology.
