# Functional specification: notifications, personal inbox and delivery

**Feature branch**: `codex/267-notification-retention`

**Created**: 2026-09-22

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline under [planning authority #9](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/9); technical-dossier acceptance and implementation evidence remain separate.

**Requested scope**: [#267](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout-legacy/issues/267), F07 in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications). Define result and stream-link announcements, their recipients, the personal inbox, reading, badges and device delivery. [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md) owns sporting truth, [F03](../007-sporting-consultation/spec.md) viewing, [F05](../005-accounts-identity/spec.md) identity, [F06](../009-following-personal-feed/spec.md) follows, [F08](../010-live-contributions-moderation/spec.md) contributions, [F11](../004-advertising-privacy-legal/spec.md) privacy and [F13](../003-shared-quality/spec.md) shared quality. This specification expresses V2 behavior; it chooses no contract, storage, transport or mockup.

## Clarifications — approved KISS revision, 2026-10-06

- Q: Which mechanisms remain? → A: A persisted inbox, best-effort push, live announcement at H, composition fixed at match-result acceptance, and non-personal match-level announcement facts. Persistent push retries and permanent per-person history are removed; rare duplicates after restoration losing match facts are accepted. Editorial variants and inbox retention remain unchanged.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-09-25

- Q: Which date starts retention and which entries are covered? → A: Original creation in the personal inbox, for every family and both read states; reading, correction, delayed push receipt, maintenance, season changes and re-following never reset it.
- Q: What remains after notification content is purged? → A: Non-personal match-level processed live/result/replay facts, without recipient identity or content. Ordinary repeats are prevented; restoration losing those facts may exceptionally duplicate an announcement.
- Q: Which simple purge schedule respects a maximum of 30 days? → A: Daily batch purge of entries aged at least 29 elapsed days, with removal no later than 30 elapsed days. This replaces exact per-entry expiry at 30 days; manual individual and bulk deletion are removed.
- Q: Must backup retention change? → A: Keep F13's existing protected backup cycle, allowing up to 30 additional days after active purge, with expired notifications removed before any restored data becomes usable.
- Q: When must an offline device clean its copy? → A: Before display on opening or resuming the app, even offline; an app that is stopped or a device that is powered off cannot be remotely cleaned.

## User scenarios and validation _(mandatory)_

The actors are the signed-in person with a usable profile, recipient and owner of their inbox, and the visitor without personal access. Free or Pro entitlements do not change these rules. People authorized to publish or moderate a link act under F08; this power grants no access to another person’s inbox. A personal notification and its push delivery are two distinct outcomes. FR, A and SC identifiers are local to F07. Scenarios describe expected outcomes, not V1 tests already passed.

### Journey 1 — Learn a relevant result without repetition (Priority: P1)

As a person following a team or pool, I want to learn new results for followed matches and find their corrected information without receiving the same announcement several times.

**Priority rationale**: The result is the main sporting fact announced; historical discovery or correction must not become false news.

**Independent validation**: Evolve already known matches, matches discovered completed and historical matches, with overlapping follows; observe personal entries and delivery requests.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a known match without a final result and a confirmed follow of one team, **when** F02 accepts its first final result, **then** a result notification is created for the eligible recipient. A professional match follows the same rule. (FR-002, FR-011, FR-039)
2. **A02** — **Given** an unknown match, **when** its first accepted observation already contains a final result, with or without an available link, **then** it is viewable under F03 without a result announcement, replay notice inferred from that discovery alone or corresponding push. (FR-002, FR-003, FR-007, FR-008)
3. **A03** — **Given** a known match without a final result, **when** a provisional score arrives, six hours pass or F03 categorizes it as “Completed”, **then** no result notification is created; its later final result may trigger the announcement, without an H+4/H+6 limit. (FR-002)
4. **A04** — **Given** an initial import or historical reconstruction before closure, **when** results are integrated or corrected, **then** no new announcement or push burst is produced; any existing notifications reflect authorized corrections. (FR-003, FR-004)
5. **A05** — **Given** a retained read or unread result notification, **when** the score is corrected, withdrawn and restored, **then** the same entry reflects authorized facts without a new entry or push; identity, original order, read state and retention origin stay unchanged. A purged entry remains absent. (FR-004, FR-019, FR-038, FR-043)
6. **A06** — **Given** a confirmed follow of one team, both teams or both teams and their pool, **when** the same eligible event is observed repeatedly, **then** each person receives at most one corresponding entry. A still-uncertain follow request does not establish eligibility. (FR-011, FR-012, FR-038)
7. **A07** — **Given** a triggered event, **when** a person subsequently follows the target, **then** they do not catch up on that event. If a recipient removes their last eligible follow before a remaining delivery, that delivery is cancelled; another retained eligible follow permits sending. The already created entry remains in their inbox under current restrictions. (FR-012, FR-013)
8. **A08** — **Given** confirmed follows across several seasons, **when** the personal-feed season changes, **then** this filter does not change recipients. A closed season alone does not hide history. (FR-014, FR-031)

### Journey 2 — Hear about a link at the right time (Priority: P1)

As a person following a match, I want to distinguish a link offered before the result from one offered to watch the match again, without repeated or artificially grouped announcements.

**Priority rationale**: Announcement timing and public link availability determine usefulness; private proposals must not appear published.

**Independent validation**: Apply F08 publications and decisions before/after the result, with different times and arrival orders; count personal announcements.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A09** — **Given** a link made public before the final result, **when** conditions are met at H or later, **then** at most one live announcement is created per person and match. Before H, no announcement is created. (FR-005, FR-009)
2. **A10** — **Given** a link published several days before the match by a moderator, **when** H arrives, **then** conditions are rechecked and follows at that instant determine recipients. A follow added before H is eligible; a link withdrawn or match hidden in the meantime produces no announcement. (FR-005, FR-006, FR-012, FR-026)
3. **A11** — **Given** a public link without a reliable match time, **when** eligibility is evaluated, **then** the link remains consultable but its live announcement waits. A corrected reliable time determines H before announcement. Later correction never resets a processed match announcement or creates delivery retries. (FR-005, FR-006, FR-009, FR-026, FR-042)
4. **A12** — **Given** a public link at acceptance of the first eligible final result, **when** the event is recorded, **then** every eligible result recipient receives one enriched result-and-replay notification, including after an earlier live announcement. Personal creation order cannot change this family. (FR-007, FR-009, FR-011–FR-012)
5. **A13** — **Given** no public link at final-result acceptance, **when** a link is published later, **then** the plain result remains plain and a separate replay event reaches currently eligible followers. A later follower receives replay only. An already consumed match replay fact prevents replacement/reactivation repeats, even after personal inbox purge. (FR-003, FR-008–FR-013, FR-043)
6. **A14** — **Given** nearly simultaneous result acceptance and link publication, **when** their accepted match transitions are ordered, **then** a link public at result acceptance yields the enriched family; a later link yields plain result plus separate replay. Concurrent personal creation or delayed delivery cannot change that decision. No artificial waiting or per-person composition rule is introduced. (FR-007–FR-009, FR-038, FR-043)
7. **A15** — **Given** a pending or rejected proposal or an unchanged repeat, **when** it is examined, **then** no announcement follows. An actually accepted direct publication, approval or reactivation may make the link eligible, subject to already processed match-level announcements and sporting restrictions. (FR-009, FR-010, FR-031)
8. **A16** — **Given** a link already announced before or after the result, **when** it is replaced, hidden then reactivated, **then** entry information is refreshed according to authorized state, without repeating the announcement for the already announced period. F08 reactivation of its reporting counter does not reset F07 allowances. (FR-009, FR-032, FR-038)
9. **A17** — **Given** an enriched result or replay entry, **when** its push fails or its retention ends, **then** the post-match announcement remains consumed; retry, time-zone change, correction or result restoration creates no second announcement. (FR-009, FR-019, FR-024, FR-038, FR-042–FR-043)

### Journey 3 — Manage the inbox and unread entries (Priority: P1)

As a recipient, I want to consult recent notifications, open the relevant match and have old entries removed automatically, with consistent read state and counts across my devices.

**Priority rationale**: The inbox is the personal reference even without push receipt; reading must have a single meaning.

**Independent validation**: Use an inbox with several pages, change entries from two devices and simulate refusals, lost responses and viewing restrictions.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A18** — **Given** a guest, an unusable account under F05 or an attempt to access another inbox, **when** list, count or read is requested, **then** no foreign personal data is accessible. Pro or moderation permissions grant no wider inbox access; manual notification deletion is not an available action. (FR-001, FR-019, FR-037)
2. **A19** — **Given** unread entries, **when** the list is displayed or a push merely presented by the system, **then** they remain unread. Tapping an entry in the list or its push marks it read for the account; no second action or “opened” state is needed. (FR-017, FR-018)
3. **A20** — **Given** an accepted read action, **when** opening the detail sheet fails or the read response is lost, **then** navigation and reading are distinguished; the accepted read is not cancelled and retry adds no effect. If reading is not yet confirmed, its outcome remains to be checked without claiming success. (FR-018, FR-021, FR-038)
4. **A21** — **Given** a read entry or an entry already purged, **when** a read is repeated, content is corrected or an old push is tapped, **then** read state never returns to unread and the purged entry is not recreated. The old push may open only the currently authorized match in the appropriate account context, without fabricating a read update for the missing entry. (FR-004, FR-017–FR-019, FR-030, FR-034, FR-043)
5. **A22** — **Given** a multipage inbox with entries sharing a creation date, **when** it is browsed, refreshed or content is corrected, **then** initial newest-to-oldest order remains stable for these entries and no entry is duplicated across pages; correction does not artificially move the entry up. (FR-004, FR-020)
6. **A23** — **Given** an empty inbox, initial loading, a failed next page or an uncertain read, **when** the user consults or retries, **then** these states remain distinct. Still-retained authorized data remains usable; a failure is not shown as empty and an old response cannot restore purged or expired data. No deletion confirmation, deletion button or manual cleanup recovery is offered. (FR-019–FR-021, FR-036, FR-038, FR-044–FR-046)
7. **A24** — **Given** seven consultable unread entries with only two loaded, **when** synchronized, **then** the notification tab and supported system icon count seven. Reading one, purging another and hiding a third reduces the count to four regardless of their page. Purging all remaining consultable entries produces the empty state and removes the zero-count badge. (FR-019, FR-022–FR-023, FR-031)
8. **A25** — **Given** two devices including an offline one, **when** reading or purge changes the first device, **then** synchronization applies the current inbox and count without an old response restoring a removed entry or old total. Offline opening/resume applies local age cleanup before display under FR-044, without claiming instantaneous remote freshness or an exact remote total from loaded pages alone. (FR-017, FR-023, FR-038, FR-044)

### Journey 4 — Receive best-effort push on devices (Priority: P1)

As a recipient, I want to retain information when push fails and avoid intentional duplicates or old notices that are no longer relevant.

**Priority rationale**: Devices and providers can fail; absence of push must neither erase the inbox nor authorize infinite repeats.

**Independent validation**: Deliver an entry to several installations, simulate system denial, invalid destinations, partial successes and lost responses, then observe normal provider handling without persistent retries.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A26** — **Given** an eligible recipient without a destination, with system permission denied or an unavailable provider, **when** the notice is created, **then** the entry exists in their inbox without claiming push receipt; these states do not change their right to view the inbox. During F12 maintenance, the same eligible events create their entries without push and without lifting the ordinary-access block; operator bypass provides no delivery exception. (FR-024, FR-026, FR-027)
2. **A27** — **Given** an eligible new inbox entry, **when** its ordinary push attempt fails or maintenance prevents sending, **then** the inbox remains available under normal access rules. No persistent retry deadline, queue or maintenance catch-up is created. (FR-024–FR-026)
3. **A28** — **Given** an ordinary push about to be sent, **when** the match becomes hidden, the account is deleted, the destination is invalid or the last eligible follow is removed, **then** sending is refused. A final result prevents an obsolete live push. (FR-012, FR-026, FR-030–FR-032)
4. **A29** — **Given** provider acceptance without receipt evidence, **when** delivery state is examined, **then** only that acceptance is asserted. Explicit rejection and a lost response remain distinct; retrying an uncertain outcome checks what can be checked and recreates neither a notice nor an already confirmed effect. (FR-027, FR-028, FR-038)
5. **A30** — **Given** three installations with accepted, rejected and invalid-destination provider responses, **when** ordinary response handling completes, **then** only the invalid destination is removed, the inbox remains and no per-device persistent retry state is created. (FR-024–FR-029)
6. **A31** — **Given** two installations with the same operating-system version, **when** they are registered or one changes destination, **then** they remain distinct; rotation or repeated reassociation does not create multiple equivalent active destinations for one installation or resend a notice already served to it. (FR-028, FR-029)
7. **A32** — **Given** an installation associated with an account then signed out or switched to another account, **when** an old response, push or retry arrives, **then** no inbox, read state, destination or badge from the old account is assigned to the new one. Other devices of the first account remain associated after a simple local sign-out. (FR-030, FR-035, FR-037, FR-038)
8. **A33** — **Given** account deletion, **when** F05/F11 erasure is applied and the person registers again, **then** old notifications, pending deliveries and personal inbox data are not transferred to the new account. Failed destination invalidation remains retryable without authorizing it for the new account. The 29–30-day notification policy does not delay account erasure. (FR-030, FR-037, FR-043)

### Journey 5 — View information that remains authorized (Priority: P1)

As a recipient, I want the inbox, badges and detail sheet to reflect current restrictions, including when I use old access.

**Priority rationale**: An announcement must not become a way to bypass sporting withdrawal or a moderation decision.

**Independent validation**: Restrict and restore an announced resource, change its link and attempt old access, including from an offline device.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A34** — **Given** an announced match, **when** it is withdrawn by sporting authority, attached to an inactive division or deprived of a usable date, **then** its content is no longer viewable in the inbox and no longer contributes to the badge. A cache, old link or retained follow does not bypass this restriction. Content already known to be restricted does not reappear during a network error. (FR-031, FR-034, FR-036)
2. **A35** — **Given** a notification mentioning a link, **when** F08 withdraws or hides it, **then** the entry no longer presents it as available and the detail sheet gives no access to the old address. A still-viewable result may remain displayed; current absence of the link is explicit. (FR-032, FR-034)
3. **A36** — **Given** a hidden match reappearing under its existing identity, **when** sporting restrictions lift, **then** a retained entry may reappear with its original read state, order and creation date. A purged or expired entry stays absent, no announcement or push is repeated, and independent moderation restrictions still apply. (FR-009, FR-019, FR-031–FR-033, FR-043)
4. **A37** — **Given** a message already handed to the phone system before hiding, **when** the restriction becomes known, **then** the application and still-controllable sends apply it; the application does not promise certain recall of that system message. Opening goes to the detail sheet subject to current rules, never directly to an old video. A push handed over before maintenance may still be received; opening applies the current access block without promising recall. (FR-026, FR-034–FR-036)

### Journey 6 — Understand accurate messages and local presentation (Priority: P2)

As a recipient, I want lively but accurate French messages and understandable times wherever I am.

**Priority rationale**: Editorial variety and time zones must change neither sporting truth nor announcement counts.

**Independent validation**: Present the four variant families with complete and missing data on several devices and time zones; verify their facts and instants.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A38** — **Given** a result with a reliable score, then a special result without a usable numeric score or with a missing label, **when** a message is chosen, **then** suitable variants use only attributable facts; no victory, fictitious value, assumed identity or temporal proximity is invented. (FR-015, FR-036)
2. **A39** — **Given** a notification and several devices, **when** its message is delivered or retried, **then** the chosen variant remains the same. A factual correction may refresh values or remove a phrase that has become false, without a new event, new read or gratuitous variation. (FR-004, FR-015, FR-016, FR-038)
3. **A40** — **Given** an unverified public link, **when** a live or replay notice is presented, **then** it indicates the shared link without claiming an active broadcast, tested replay or free access. The detail sheet uses “Stream link” and unread states remain understandable with accessibility aids. (FR-015, FR-036, FR-041)
4. **A41** — **Given** the same match at 20:00 in Paris and a recipient in New York, **when** H is reached, **then** both use the same reliable instant, including differing daylight-saving weeks. Local display differs, announcement facts and elapsed retention do not. (FR-005, FR-042)
5. **A42** — **Given** a professional match with a result or provider code, **when** F07 qualifies the announcement, **then** the result follows A01–A04 and the code alone does not become a video address. No automatic LNV TV collection or new professional-media announcement is inferred from exploratory examples. (FR-039, FR-040)
6. **A43** — **Given** inbox, badge, error, system-permission and navigation needs, **when** overall R02 design is prepared, **then** it receives the states specified here, without a generic campaign, new setting, quiet period or personal moderation alert. No V1 screen or writing checklist constitutes visual approval or V2 qualification. (FR-040, FR-041)

### Journey 7 — Keep a recent inbox without manual cleanup (priority: P1)

As a recipient, I want old notifications to disappear automatically while my current match history and future eligible announcements remain available.

**Independent validation**: Exercise all four content families, read/unread and visible/hidden states, across daily purge boundaries, multiple devices, interruptions and protected restores. One day is 24 elapsed hours.

1. **A44** — **Given** entries just below 29 days, exactly 29 days and between 29 and 30 days old at a daily purge, **when** the run succeeds, **then** only the entries aged at least 29 days are purged for retention, and each entry is removed no later than age 30 days. The same outcome holds for every family, read state and visibility state, in Paris/New York and across daylight-saving changes. Account/privacy erasure may require earlier removal. (FR-019, FR-042)
2. **A45** — **Given** a retained entry, **when** it is read, corrected, received late, hidden/restored, carried across a season or maintenance, or its match is unfollowed/re-followed, **then** its original creation time and 29–30-day retention remain unchanged. A push arriving after inbox purge cannot recreate it. (FR-004, FR-013, FR-018–FR-019, FR-024–FR-025, FR-031–FR-034)
3. **A46** — **Given** a usable account on the Notifications screen, **when** actions and empty/error states are inspected, **then** no individual/bulk deletion, delete confirmation, archive, restore or manual purge action is available. Automatic purge can empty the list and removes its zero-count badge; account deletion remains available under F05. (FR-001, FR-019–FR-022, FR-041)
4. **A47** — **Given** a purged plain result notice and no post-match link announcement yet, **when** a genuinely new eligible F08 publication occurs, **then** one replay notice is created with its own creation time and retention, without restoring the result. If an enriched result or replay had already consumed that allowance, zero new notices are created. Repeated old result/live/link events do not regain allowances, and match results/history remain governed by F02/F03. (FR-002–FR-010, FR-019, FR-043)
5. **A48** — **Given** entries across several pages and two devices, **when** reads, pagination, correction, purge and refresh race and stale responses arrive afterwards, **then** surviving entry order/read state stays consistent, purged entries never return, synchronized unread totals exclude every removed entry regardless of page, and complete purge yields an empty inbox. (FR-017–FR-023, FR-038, FR-044)
6. **A49** — **Given** a device offline for more than 30 days, **when** the app opens/resumes, **then** aged local content is removed before display without waiting for connection or marking it read. Known-purged entries stay absent; reconnection reconciles the whole count without resurrection. An app left active across age 30 also stops displaying/counting expired entries. Switching accounts exposes no foreign data; stopped apps and system-delivered messages retain the explicit FR-035/FR-044 limitations. (FR-023, FR-030, FR-035–FR-036, FR-044)
7. **A50** — **Given** a daily purge interrupted after partial success or missed beyond the 30-day limit, **when** the inbox/count is consulted and processing resumes, **then** entries at or beyond 30 days are never returned or counted, completed removals stay removed, failed physical cleanup remains observable and is retried to completion without duplicate effects. Diagnostics contain no personal payload and no partial result is labelled complete. (FR-019, FR-022, FR-037–FR-038, FR-046)
8. **A51** — **Given** a restored backup with expired notices, a since-erased account and possibly missing match announcement facts, **when** reopening is considered, **then** purge due content and apply current privacy/erasure obligations first. Unknown obligations keep affected access/sends closed. Preserve available match facts; their loss may exceptionally duplicate a sporting announcement. No erased personal data, expired inbox entry or renewed backup lifetime is permitted. (FR-019, FR-030, FR-037, FR-043, FR-045)
9. **A52** — **Given** purged personal notifications, **when** remaining announcement information is inspected, **then** only match-level processed categories/composition remain, with no account reference, text, score, read state, variant or delivery payload. A new account inherits no personal history and receives no backfill of processed events. Hiding a match does not erase its facts. (FR-019, FR-030, FR-037, FR-043, FR-045)

### Edge cases

- A final result without a numeric score is not a provisional score: F02 decides the sporting fact, A03/A38 its consumption.
- H is an announcement condition, not a prohibition on retaining a moderator-published link. A10/A11 distinguish availability, deadline and correction.
- The post-match announcement counter counts an enriched result as well as a late notice. F08 quotas and reporting periods do not become F07 counters (A12–A17).
- Automatic notification purge, unfollowing, sporting hiding, destination invalidation and account erasure have distinct effects. Removing inbox content does not erase match results/history, consume a never-announced event or preserve data against account erasure (A07, A21, A30, A33–A36, A47).
- An offline device may not know a new remote restriction or the most recent daily purge, but it knows the age of cached entries. Local cleanup precedes display on opening/resume; while active, it must not keep displaying entries at or beyond 30 days. No instantaneous remote cleanup of a stopped app or recall of system-delivered messages is promised (A25, A34, A37, A49).

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional requirements

#### Access and results

- **FR-001** : The inbox, count and read mutations MUST be restricted to the authenticated usable account under F05 and applicable permissions. Each person may consult and mark read only their own entries. Pro and moderation roles MUST NOT grant access to another inbox. Manual individual or bulk notification deletion is excluded by FR-019; account erasure remains governed by F05/F11.
- **FR-002**: A result notification MUST be triggered only when an already known match without a final result receives a final result recognized by F02. Discovery directly with a result, a provisional score, the F03 “Completed” category or elapsed time MUST NOT trigger this notice. No H+4/H+6 limit is added to this trigger.
- **FR-003**: Initial imports and historical reconstructions before closure MUST NOT create new personal announcements or push notifications from rediscovered results or link availability. They may refresh authorized information in existing entries. Availability already present when a completed match is discovered MUST NOT be repurposed as a replay event; a later new F08 publication follows FR-008.
- **FR-004** : Corrections, withdrawal and restoration of a result MUST update a still-retained entry without a new entry or push. Identity, original creation order, read state and retention origin MUST stay unchanged. Withdrawn results MUST NOT be presented as acquired facts; current unavailability must be understandable. Purged or expired entries MUST NOT reappear.

#### Link announcements and recipients

- **FR-005**: An F08 contribution link made public before the final result may trigger a live announcement from H, where H is the reliable match-start instant. There MUST NOT be more than one live announcement per user and match, regardless of link author, address or versions. A live notice indicates a link offered to follow the match, not a verified active broadcast.
- **FR-006**: A link made public by moderation before H MUST remain available under F08; its announcement waits for H and a new check. Without a reliable time, it waits for confirmation without assuming midnight. Before announcement creation, a date or time correction MUST recalculate the deadline; after creation, it does not reset the announcement allowance and remaining sends follow FR-025–FR-026.
- **FR-007**: At acceptance of the first eligible final result, determine once at match-event level whether a public F08 link already exists. If it does, emit one result-and-replay event and create an enriched result notification for each eligible recipient, even if live was previously announced. If not, emit a plain result event. Personal-entry creation order, reading, push and delayed workers MUST NOT change this composition; no artificial grouping delay is permitted.
- **FR-008**: If no public link existed at result acceptance, a later eligible F08 publication MUST produce a separate replay event, subject to the match-level post-result announcement fact. A follow added between result and publication may receive this new replay, without result catch-up. A match discovered finished permits only a genuinely later new publication event under FR-003. Do not retroactively enrich the earlier plain result or recreate a purged entry.
- **FR-009**: Record processed live, result and post-result-link announcements at match level. An enriched result consumes the result and replay facts together. Ordinary repeats, corrections, replacement, reactivation, result withdrawal/restoration and inbox purge MUST NOT produce another announcement for a consumed category. Record processing even with no eligible recipients; later follows receive no backfill. Exceptional duplicates after a restoration that lost these facts are accepted under FR-045.
- **FR-010**: An authorized direct publication, approval or explicit F08 reactivation may make a link eligible. A pending proposal, refusal or unchanged repeat MUST NOT trigger an announcement. Withdrawal or hiding updates availability without a publication announcement. F08 retains ownership of contribution acceptance and moderation.
- **FR-011**: Recipients MUST be selected from the union of confirmed eligible follows of both teams and the pool under F06. Overlaps MUST NOT produce multiple entries or deliveries of the same notice to the same recipient on one installation.
- **FR-012**: An eligible follow MUST exist at event triggering and sending. For an advance link, triggering is the H deadline when all conditions become satisfied, not publication several days earlier; for an unknown time, it waits for a reliable time and entry into that window. Removing the last eligible follow MUST cancel remaining sends; retries MUST NOT add new recipients who followed only after triggering.
- **FR-013** : A new follow MUST NOT backfill earlier events. Unfollowing MUST NOT itself erase existing notifications; their current visibility and FR-019 retention still apply. Re-following MUST NOT reset cancelled deliveries, announcement limits or entry age.
- **FR-014**: The season selected in the F06 feed MUST remain a display filter and MUST NOT become a notification preference. Authorized follows and restrictions determine recipients independently of that filter.

#### Content, reading and inbox

- **FR-015**: The four message families MUST each have at least three warm, slightly playful French variants with the same facts and triggers. Sporting variables MUST be reliable and attributable. A missing value requires adapted wording, never an assumed score, winner, favorite, name or sense of urgency. Special results without a usable score MUST be covered. Video availability, free access, active status or replay status MUST NOT be presented as verified. The detail sheet retains “Stream link” under F03.
- **FR-016**: Editorial choice MUST remain stable for the same notification across its devices and retries. A factual correction may update values or remove a fragment that has become false, without gratuitous variant changes, a new entry or a new push. Editorial variety MUST NOT create an event.
- **FR-017**: The inbox MUST retain a single personal read/unread state shared across account devices. A new entry is unread. The distinct functional “opened” state is not carried forward to V2; it constitutes neither a second counter nor an additional reading condition. Content correction MUST NOT mark the entry unread again. Technical mapping and old-client compatibility are reserved for subsequent phases.
- **FR-018** : Tapping a retained consultable notification or its push MUST mark it read for its owner and request match navigation. Showing the list or receiving a push alone MUST NOT mark entries read. Failed navigation MUST NOT undo an accepted read; uncertain reads remain reconcilable without repeating an accepted mutation. For a purged or expired entry, an old push follows FR-034 without recreating the entry, read state or unread count.
- **FR-019** : The inbox MUST NOT provide manual individual or bulk deletion, deletion confirmations or a user-triggered purge. All four families, read/unread and visible/hidden entries alike, MUST undergo daily automatic purge: remove entries aged at least 29 elapsed days at each run, no later than 30 elapsed days after original inbox creation during normal operation. One day means 24 elapsed hours, independently of calendar dates or time zones. Reading, correction, delayed receipt, maintenance, season changes, re-following and visibility changes MUST NOT reset age. Purge removes personal content, editorial payload, read state and associated per-entry delivery details from active controlled copies; neither diagnostics nor delivery history may act as a content archive. Preserve only FR-043 anti-duplicate facts and independently necessary installation associations. Account/privacy erasure may require earlier removal. Caches, protected backups and failed cleanup follow FR-044–FR-046. Missing entries cannot be sent again or recreated by old events.
- **FR-020** : The inbox MUST provide a paginated list of currently consultable, still-retained entries, newest first by original creation with stable tie ordering. Corrections, pagination and concurrent purge MUST NOT reorder surviving entries arbitrarily, duplicate entries across pages or expose expired entries. Each entry targets its match; an empty retained history uses the ordinary empty state, with no archive, pin, restore or manual deletion option.
- **FR-021** : Loading, empty inbox, initial unavailability, next-page failure, accepted read, denial, confirmed failure and uncertain read MUST remain distinct. Known, still-retained authorized entries remain usable after partial failure under F13 freshness rules. Reconciliation and old responses MUST NOT restore purged data or overwrite newer read state. Cleanup failures follow FR-046; no user deletion workflow is introduced.

#### Badges and delivery

- **FR-022** : The notification-tab badge and supported/authorized app-icon badge MUST represent all currently consultable unread entries of the account, across every page. Reading, purge, maximum-age exclusion and sporting hiding update this total; the badge disappears at zero. Loading only part of the inbox MUST NOT turn that page count into the whole-inbox count. System and offline limitations remain explicit under FR-023/FR-044.
- **FR-023** : Devices MUST reconcile current retained inbox, read state and counts for their account. Old responses MUST NOT restore purged/expired entries or stale totals. Local age cleanup applies before display under FR-044; an offline device MUST NOT claim a freshly synchronized or exact total if it cannot establish it from its retained data. System permissions/capabilities remain explicit; instantaneous synchronization of all devices is not promised.
- **FR-024** : Personal-entry creation MUST remain independent of push success: refused permission, missing/invalid destination or provider failure neither removes the entry before its normal retention boundary nor invalidates the announcement. Absence of push is not absence of an inbox entry. During F12 maintenance, eligible events still create entries while ordinary access and pushes stay blocked. Maintenance neither pauses retention/purge nor resets announcement limits.
- **FR-025**: Withdrawn: the persistent fifteen-minute push retry window is removed. Attempt ordinary best-effort push for a newly created eligible notification; keep the inbox regardless of push failure. There is no persistent delivery retry queue, per-device success ledger for retries or catch-up after maintenance.
- **FR-026**: Before the ordinary push attempt, reassess follows, viewability, account, entry existence, destination and content relevance. Do not send a live notice after a final result or when its corrected time makes it premature. Active F12 maintenance prevents every new sporting push, including operator accounts, without suppressing eligible inbox creation. Ending maintenance does not trigger a backlog replay.
- **FR-027**: Delivery MUST distinguish provider acceptance, established failure, uncertain outcome and actually demonstrated receipt. Acceptance or absence of errors MUST NOT be presented as proven receipt. A lost response MUST NOT suffice to presume failure; what can be verified must be reconciled before an unsafe repeat, without guaranteeing receipt or absolute absence of system duplicates.
- **FR-028**: Process ordinary provider responses and receipts, including rejection and invalid destinations. Invalidate only affected destinations; another device’s failure does not erase an accepted inbox entry or disable other valid destinations. Do not persist success per device for future delivery retries. Native transport outcomes remain best effort and acceptance is not proof of receipt.
- **FR-029**: Installations MUST remain distinct even with the same operating-system version. Rotation and reassociation MUST avoid equivalent active duplicate destinations and retain the current account association. A destination is a delivery address, never identity or authorization evidence; its change does not trigger replay of previous pushes.
- **FR-030**: Sign-out, account change and deletion MUST apply F05: removal or invalidation of the affected old association, isolation of data and badges, retry of failures without reassignment to the new account. A simple local sign-out retains other devices. Old responses, pushes and retries MUST NOT mutate or expose another account’s inbox. Re-registration does not recover notifications deleted with the old account.

#### Restrictions and boundaries

- **FR-031** : F02/F03 restrictions MUST govern inbox content, messages, counts, caches and destinations. Withdrawn matches, inactive divisions or unusable dates MUST NOT be exposed through notifications. Known hiding removes the entry from consultation and unread counts without itself constituting retention purge or pausing its age. Seasonal closure alone does not hide authorized sporting history, but notification entries still follow FR-019.
- **FR-032**: A link withdrawn or hidden under F08 MUST no longer be presented as available. Retained content MUST reflect link absence and may continue presenting a viewable result. Eligible replacement updates availability without a new announcement for an already announced period. A final result ends any current live claim; retained entries must not support an old situation that has become false.
- **FR-033** : A sporting reappearance MAY restore visibility only to a still-retained entry with its original identity, read state, order and creation date. It MUST NOT recreate purged/expired entries or itself trigger an announcement or delivery. Independent moderation restrictions remain applicable.
- **FR-034** : Notifications MUST open the match page under current F03 rules, never an old video address directly. A push for a purged/expired entry may still open the currently authorized match in the appropriate account context, without recreating the entry or fabricating a read update. If the target cannot be resolved or is unavailable, use the existing unavailable-target outcome, distinct from network failure and without exposing a hidden resource. Old access MUST NOT bypass restrictions or account isolation.
- **FR-035**: Messages already handed to the phone system MUST NOT be presented as certainly recallable. Known restrictions MUST apply to data and sends still controllable by the application; this system limit does not authorize new intentional exposure.
- **FR-036**: F11/F13 requirements for French language, accessibility, privacy, freshness, retry and diagnostics MUST apply. Reading and actions MUST be understandable without color alone; no private data, destination address or internal error must be exposed as a user diagnostic. Offline, neither perfect freshness nor instant knowledge of a remote restriction is promised; a cache MUST NOT undo an already known restriction.
- **FR-037**: Notifications and installation associations MUST remain within their authorized purposes and F05/F11 erasure. The match-level announcement facts under FR-043 contain no account reference and retain no personal announcement history after inbox purge. Diagnostics must not reproduce notification content or bypass erasure. Notification retention does not set sporting-history or log retention.
- **FR-038** : Repeated events, concurrent processing, retries, reads and purge MUST preserve identities, consumed announcements and newer decisions. A lost response MUST NOT create an entry or repeat a confirmed effect. Uncertain outcomes remain distinct from failures; refresh, paging and delayed responses MUST NOT resurrect purged/expired entries or reset read state. FR-043 preserves announcement history without retaining inbox content.
- **FR-039**: Professional matches MUST retain result-notification eligibility under shared rules. F07 MUST NOT add automatic collection of LNV TV pages or their dedicated notifications; that evolution remains separate. A Data Project `liveCode` MUST NOT be equated with a video URL. Exploratory public examples guarantee neither exhaustive coverage, free access nor video availability.
- **FR-040**: F07 MUST NOT add generic campaigns, new notification settings, quiet periods or personal moderation alerts. F01/F13 operator incidents remain distinct from this sporting inbox. F08 transitions useful for eligibility do not constitute a promise of an announcement for every decision.
- **FR-041** : R02 MUST cover the Notifications entry point, accessible read/unread distinctions, tab/system badges, four content families, pagination, empty/error/uncertain states, current match destinations, permissions and cross-device/account synchronization. Replace all manual deletion affordances and confirmation/recovery states with automatic retention behavior, including count reduction and an empty recent history after purge. Affected prototype/Figma evidence MUST be revalidated before technical planning; no historical screen or document checklist constitutes visual approval.
- **FR-042**: Paris schedules MUST be resolved to reliable instants before evaluating H, the planned start. Travel and daylight-saving changes MUST NOT change that shared instant, elapsed inbox retention or match-level announcement facts. A date without reliable time never becomes a guessed midnight instant.

- **FR-043**: Preserve minimal processed-announcement facts on the match for live, result and post-result link, including the composition chosen at result acceptance. These facts contain no recipient identity, message text, read state, editorial variant or delivery payload. They remain when inbox entries are purged and prevent ordinary re-announcement. Hiding a match does not erase them; later follows and account recreation do not backfill processed events. No permanent per-person announcement ledger is required.
- **FR-044** : On opening or resuming the app, including offline, controlled local notification copies aged at least 29 elapsed days MUST be removed before display. Known purges/restrictions MUST also be applied; synchronization reconciles any purge not yet known locally without resurrection. An active app MUST cease displaying/counting entries no later than age 30 days even without a new resume or network connection. A stopped app or powered-off device is cleaned at its next use; system-delivered messages follow FR-035 and are not a guaranteed remotely erasable cache. Local age cleanup never creates read events or changes accounts.
- **FR-045**: Protected backups follow F13 retention. Before reopening restored data, remove inbox entries aged at least 29 elapsed days and apply current erasure, privacy and rights obligations, preserving original creation dates. Preserve available match-level announcement facts; if restoration lost some facts, an exceptional duplicate sporting announcement is an accepted limitation. This exception does not permit restoring erased personal data, expired inbox content or bypassing unresolved privacy obligations: keep affected access and sends closed when those obligations cannot be established.
- **FR-046** : A missed, interrupted or partially failed purge MUST remain observable to the operator under F13, with safe diagnostics and a retryable outcome that preserves completed removals. It is a retention failure, not permission to retain longer or a successful cleanup. Entries at or beyond 30 elapsed days MUST be excluded from controlled consultation, counts and sends despite physical cleanup failure; failed physical removal must be retried until resolved, without recreating entries or resetting ages/announcement limits. No user-facing manual cleanup control, duplicate incident stream or guaranteed human intervention time is added.

### Main entities

- **Eligible event**: An actually accepted sporting fact or public availability, with match, trigger instant and context distinguishing initial discovery, history and transition. Technical repeats are not new facts.
- **Personal notification**: An account-owned match entry with identity, immutable initial creation time, stable content family, authorized content and read state. Retention purge, current visibility, account erasure and push delivery are distinct lifecycles.
- **Announcement fact**: Non-personal match-level record of processed live, result and replay categories and result-event composition, under FR-043.
- **Link announcement**: Information already conveyed to the recipient before or after the result. An enriched result and a late notice consume the same post-match announcement; the pre-result allowance is independent.
- **Eligible recipient**: An account with a confirmed eligible follow at triggering, whose conditions are reassessed at sending. The union of follows avoids duplicates.
- **Installation and destination**: A functionally identifiable installed device, current account association and replaceable delivery address. A shared operating-system version does not merge installations.
- **Delivery**: Best-effort ordinary provider attempt and response/receipt handling, distinct from the persisted inbox; no persistent delivery retry ledger.

## Success criteria _(mandatory)_

### Measurable outcomes

- **SC-001** : A01–A08 produce exactly eligible result announcements, zero notices for initial finished discovery, provisional scores or historical imports, and zero follow-overlap duplicates. A05 preserves identity, order, read state and initial age after correction; purged entries remain absent.
- **SC-002**: A09–A17 use H for live eligibility and match-event result acceptance for composition. A public link at acceptance yields one enriched result, even after live; a later publication yields a separate replay. Personal creation order never changes the family, and corrections/reactivations do not renew processed categories. (FR-005–FR-013, FR-043)
- **SC-003**: A18–A25 yield zero access to another person’s inbox, zero reads caused solely by displaying the list and zero reversions to unread on correction. The synchronized count of viewable unread entries is accurate across all pages and disappears at zero, subject to explicitly recognized system capabilities.
- **SC-004**: A26–A33 preserve the inbox despite best-effort push failures, process normal provider outcomes and invalid destinations, and isolate account changes. There is no persistent fifteen-minute retry mechanism or per-device success ledger for it. Maintenance prevents new sends without creating catch-up. (FR-024–FR-030)
- **SC-005** : A34–A37 allow no known restriction bypass through inbox, count, cache, follow, old link or reappearance. System-delivered messages are not promised to be recallable; a purged/expired entry never returns. Offline limitations do not waive the local age cleanup required by FR-044.
- **SC-006**: A38–A43 have at least three variants for each of the four families, without invented facts, gratuitous editorial changes or video promises. Paris/New York and daylight-saving changes yield the same trigger instants and retention age; no additional out-of-scope professional collection or interface is inferred.

- **SC-007** : A44–A46 and A48–A49 remove all due entries in each successful daily purge, retain none past 30 elapsed days in active operation, expose zero manual deletion actions and produce exact synchronized all-page unread totals. No correction, timezone change, maintenance or old response resets age or restores purged content. Offline limitations are explicit.
- **SC-008**: A47 and A50–A52 preserve match-level announcement facts across personal purge and ordinary retries. Restoration may exceptionally duplicate an announcement if required match facts were lost; it must never restore expired or erased personal data or bypass obligations before reopening. No permanent per-person announcement history remains. (FR-043–FR-046)

## Assumptions

- F02 qualifies definitive results, special scores, corrections, identity and visibility; F03 qualifies viewability and local presentation. Calendar categories do not constitute sporting events.
- Confirmed F06 follows and accepted F08 transitions are available for functional qualification; no transport form, global technical order or V1 projection is assumed. FR-007–FR-009 define the observable outcome of concurrent orders.
- Push is best effort. An already handed-off provider message may arrive later; no persistent retry window or delivery guarantee is imposed.
- F13 governs accessibility, honest freshness, failure states, diagnostics and safe operations. No throughput, latency, capacity or monthly availability qualification is required. F07 delivery deadlines, retention and privacy rules remain applicable, without a device-receipt guarantee.
- V2 has a single read model. [F14](../014-v1-v2-transition/spec.md) defines the personal reset boundary, without promising to restore the old inbox, and destination transition without replaying V1 sends. Old-client compatibility and technical mechanisms follow from these requirements; they do not imply retaining the “opened” functional state.
- R01 checks the full corpus, R02 performs the global visual review, and acceptance under #247 precedes technical planning. This documentary delivery certifies neither a provider, V2 software nor a completed migration.
