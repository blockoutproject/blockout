# Functional specification: V1-to-V2 transition and user welcome

**Branch**: `feature/270-v1-v2-transition`

**Created**: 2026-09-23

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline under [planning authority #9](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/9), with welcome-help clarification approved in [#38](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/38). The complete affected [prototype](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/38#issuecomment-6096091323) and corresponding [Figma translation](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/38#issuecomment-6096348752) were separately approved on October 10. No technical dossier or runtime qualification is implied.

**Requested scope**: F14, [#270](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout-legacy/issues/270). Define authorized reset outcomes, continuity, welcome and cutover conditions, consuming [F05 identity requirements](../005-accounts-identity/spec.md), [F09 paid-right continuity](../006-pro-subscriptions/spec.md) and [F13 protection/recovery](../003-shared-quality/spec.md), without executing migration or choosing its architecture.

The actors are the V1 user, visitor, holder of a recognized account, subscriber or Pro beneficiary, authorized operator and product owner. Recreating a business profile during migration does not create a new person or constitute voluntary deletion. Opening V2 means making it available for ordinary use; preparation, store publication, maintenance, reconstruction and opening are distinct stages.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-10

- Q: Should restoration help add a welcome action or move the V2 welcome after onboarding? → A: Keep the existing pre-sign-in welcome order and preserve completed onboarding. Explain in the existing panel that a person whose applicable Pro rights are not recovered after sign-in can use “Restore my purchases” in the ordinary Pro area. Add no restoration button, new navigation or automatic restoration. This decision and the separate affected-prototype/Figma approvals are owned by [consolidation #38](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/38).

### Session 2026-10-05

- Q: Does the transition require dedicated emergency or automatic recovery machinery? → A: No. Preserve identities, valid rights, required club/logo data and current privacy obligations; use controlled preparation and standard manual V2 repair/restoration. F12 has no dedicated outside-mobile emergency circuit, and no generic resumable migration framework is imposed.

## User scenarios and validation

### Journey 1 — Understand the transition and regain personal access (Priority: P1)

The person understands the fresh start before signing in again, without losing their identity or having to repeat all onboarding.

**Priority rationale**: Avoid personal access under an old session and assumed loss of entitlements.

**Independent validation**: Compare an updated installation, a new installation, a guest and a failed sign-in.

1. **A01** — **Given** an installation identified as having moved from V1 to V2, **when** ordinary access becomes possible, **then** a short welcome appears before sign-in, explaining sign-in, business data not carried over and Pro continuity, without a follow-selection step. It does not present a subscription as lost. (FR-006–FR-008)
2. **A02** — **Given** a transition welcome displayed then dismissed or completed, **when** the application returns to the foreground, restarts or resumes sign-in, **then** this welcome is not replayed on that installation; a mere interruption before dismissal does not constitute completion. Already completed ordinary onboarding remains completed. (FR-007, FR-009)
3. **A03** — **Given** a new installation without evidence of transition from V1, **when** the person opens the application, **then** the ordinary F05 journey applies without assuming V1 origin; Pro help remains available after identification through ordinary journeys. (FR-010)
4. **A04** — **Given** an old personal V1 session still available, **when** the person requests personal access in V2, **then** explicit Google or Apple sign-in is required once for that installation; neither an old local profile nor an old token alone opens personal access. After success, ordinary F05 session resumption applies without mandatory sign-in at each launch. (FR-006)
5. **A05** — **Given** cancelled or failed sign-in, or successful sign-in with an unavailable profile, **when** the person retries, **then** the states remain distinct; guest access and support remain accessible under F12, without a fictitious ready profile, duplicate, purchase restoration or entitlement transfer triggered by sign-in success alone. (FR-006, FR-011)
6. **A06** — **Given** a signed-out person reading the welcome's restoration help, **when** they proceed through ordinary sign-in and their applicable Pro rights are not recovered, **then** the help identifies “Restore my purchases” in the ordinary Pro area; restoration requires their explicit action on the usable account. The welcome adds no restoration button or new navigation, and sign-in silently triggers neither restoration nor entitlement transfer. (FR-011)

### Journey 2 — Recover entitlements and preserve continuity evidence (Priority: P1)

The recognized account recovers applicable entitlements; difficulties have a retry path without requiring a new purchase.

**Priority rationale**: The reset must not make a paid subscription inaccessible or delete evidence of its beneficiary.

**Independent validation**: Compare Google, Apple, existing associations, historical aliases and restorations on both stores.

1. **A07** — **Given** an already known Google or Apple account holder, including linked methods with different addresses, **when** they sign in again after reset, **then** linked methods resolve to the same account and its applicable entitlements; the profile is automatically recreated, account age retained and no form or email matching replaces this resolution. (FR-002, FR-013)
2. **A08** — **Given** distinct unlinked principals sharing a usable provider email, or a principal without a usable provider email, **when** recreation is requested, **then** the former remain distinct accounts under F05; only missing usable email suspends first creation with retry, guest access and assistance. The address is neither reserved nor evidence of a new association. (FR-002, FR-013)
3. **A09** — **Given** a recognized account with a valid paid or manual entitlement, **when** V2 checks its rights, **then** the applicable entitlement is recovered without repurchase; migration and sign-in constitute neither provider-customer deletion nor the end of a gift. Independent entitlements remain distinct. (FR-012, FR-019)
4. **A10** — **Given** a former subscriber whose entitlement is not found, failed verification or uncertain restoration, **when** the Pro journey is presented, **then** help distinguishes these states without automatically announcing expiry. F09 restoration and support remain available; an outage displays neither advertising nor a repurchase invitation under unknown-entitlement rules. (FR-012)
5. **A11** — **Given** a purchase on the phone’s store and then a purchase on the other store, **when** the person attempts to recover Pro, **then** native restoration uses the paying store account and F09 transfer rules; the other store receives no native-restoration promise and has verified assisted recovery. (FR-012, FR-016)
6. **A12** — **Given** subscribers linked to primary, secondary or historical identities or anonymous aliases, **when** continuity preparation is examined, **then** every necessary association is explained and preserved or reconstructible; a few tested accounts, a dashboard total or a familiar identifier format does not prove complete coverage. (FR-014, FR-015)
7. **A13** — **Given** an unexplained indispensable association or an unqualified necessary provider outcome, **when** a reset is considered, **then** destruction that would lose this evidence remains prohibited; offering later restoration does not lift this block. Recovery evidence covers every affected category and both stores. (FR-015, FR-016, FR-034)
8. **A14** — **Given** a purchase, renewal, transfer, revocation or logo change after initial preparation, **when** final state is established for cutover, **then** changes up to the transition are accounted for; an earlier record alone does not prove current continuity and an old event does not restore superseded state. (FR-017)
9. **A15** — **Given** an already accepted voluntary deletion, still partial or with a lost response, **when** migration then sign-in or retry occurs, **then** deletion continues under F05 without resurrecting the account or losing the request; a delayed operation does not affect a new account and purchase restoration follows the required resolution. (FR-004, FR-018)

### Journey 3 — Rebuild data without losing protected elements (Priority: P1)

The user regains usable sporting data; retained logos remain associable without invented identities.

**Priority rationale**: Distinguish the authorized reset from prohibited losses and source failures.

**Independent validation**: Compare absent old follows, complete or ambiguous logos and complete, partial or unavailable sporting observations.

1. **A16** — **Given** personal V1 business data not carried over, **when** the account is recreated, **then** no restoration of old follows, notifications or profile customizations not carried over is promised; new states are honest, without pretending to load forever. Follow renewal remains voluntary under F06. (FR-001, FR-005)
2. **A17** — **Given** an old administrative permission or private response retained on the device, **when** V2 establishes account access, **then** current F12 permissions govern the action; migration grants no privilege from a local declaration and does not mix data from two accounts. (FR-003, FR-006)
3. **A18** — **Given** an external support case and applicable privacy restrictions, **when** business data is reset, **then** neither the case nor obligations are deemed erased; access remains restricted and collection does not republish a prohibited field. (FR-004)
4. **A19** — **Given** manual logos associated with V1 clubs, **when** preservation is examined before reset, **then** each affected association and its file are retained independently of the state to be destroyed, verifiable and attributable; a URL alone does not satisfy this condition. (FR-020)
5. **A20** — **Given** a missing logo file or unusable copy, **when** preparation is assessed, **then** the absence is explicit and does not pass as successful preservation; the affected destruction remains blocked. Disaster-loss tolerance does not permit this prepared loss. (FR-021, FR-034)
6. **A21** — **Given** a preserved logo and a certain, absent or ambiguous rebuilt club, **when** association is processed, **then** a certain match receives the logo with F02 protections; other associations remain preserved and identifiable for resolution, without an invented club or name-based matching. Pending association is not presented as complete. (FR-022, FR-023)
7. **A22** — **Given** the targeted current season and three completed seasons, **when** controlled reconstruction runs, **then** F01/F02 automatically determine acquisition, integration, rejection and visibility; every requested scope receives an explicit outcome without manual pool approval or an invented global percentage. (FR-024, FR-025)
8. **A23** — **Given** an unavailable, partial or historically unrecoverable source calendar, **when** the report is produced, **then** the source gap remains distinct from a V2 defect and a valid empty calendar; usable independent elements remain processed under F01/F02. The V1 loss exception does not become a V2 history-loss rule. (FR-025, FR-026)
9. **A24** — **Given** results or links recovered during initial import, then a new eligible event after opening, **when** F07 processes them, **then** import creates no announcement or push burst; the new event follows V2 triggers and follows without artificially reusing old follows or sends. (FR-005, FR-027)

### Journey 4 — Cut over without letting V1 alter the new state (Priority: P1)

Users on both platforms can update; old clients do not disrupt V2.

**Priority rationale**: Publication, a blocking screen and effective retirement of old operations are three different pieces of evidence.

**Independent validation**: Compare store availability, sign-in dependency, old sessions and deferred work.

1. **A25** — **Given** V2 downloadable on only one store or merely approved without established availability, **when** retirement of ordinary V1 access is considered, **then** cutover waits for actual availability on both platforms for affected users; no global retirement based solely on the first store occurs. (FR-028)
2. **A26** — **Given** maintenance, a mandatory update or unavailable configuration, **when** an old or new client reaches the service, **then** F12 journeys and priorities apply, including legal information and support; the transition welcome does not bypass blocking. No message promises an unknown resumption time. (FR-029)
3. **A27** — **Given** an already authenticated old client, direct call or deferred V1 response, **when** V2 takes authority over the affected operations, **then** old access does not allow an incompatible mutation, new identity association or exposure of another account; displaying an update screen alone does not constitute this evidence. (FR-030)
4. **A28** — **Given** a sign-in dependency on V1 default-role assignment, **when** the corresponding backend is retired, **then** V2 sign-in with and without a prior role remains qualified, without automatic privilege; the dependency is addressed before retirement instead of discovering blocked sign-in afterwards. (FR-031)
5. **A29** — **Given** V1 collection, accepted mutations, events and retries still possible, **when** responsibilities transition, **then** old operations do not rewrite V2 state; accepted obligations follow their domain and no payment or erasure is cancelled by assumption. Maintenance alone does not constitute stopping collection. (FR-017, FR-032)
6. **A30** — **Given** an old notification destination, local badge or already delivered push, **when** updating then signing in establishes the V2 installation, **then** associations and counters are reconciled under F05/F07 without blindly resuming V1 sends, another person’s inbox or duplicates; an already delivered message cannot be presented as certainly recallable. (FR-033)
7. **A31** — **Given** failed or unevidenced preparation, protection or qualification, **when** a destructive step or opening is requested, **then** the unmet condition remains blocking and partial state is not announced as ready. Source availability is described under F01; no manual validation per calendar is added. (FR-034, FR-035)

### Journey 5 — Recover V2 and qualify the transition (Priority: P1)

A difficulty is observable and recoverable without reintroducing data that has become prohibited.

**Priority rationale**: V2 availability must not depend on an undemonstrated return to V1 or silent loss.

**Independent validation**: Examine abandoned preparation, an incident after opening and restoration involving newer restrictions.

1. **A32** — **Given** failed preparation before opening, **when** it is retried or abandoned, **then** neither destructive continuation nor partial opening is automatic; protected elements and already accepted results remain preserved. No V1 reopening after incompatible changes is assumed safe. (FR-035)
2. **A33** — **Given** a serious outage after opening and already accepted V2 actions, **when** service is recovered, **then** the intended path is V2 repair or restoration under F12/F13, without requiring a functional return to V1, silently resetting new data or permitting protection bounds to be exceeded. (FR-036, FR-037)
3. **A34** — **Given** an old copy containing a deleted account or revoked entitlement, **when** it is used for restoration, **then** current restrictions are reapplied before access; if they cannot be established, affected access remains suspended. Reinstating an old application does not constitute undoing data changes. (FR-037)
4. **A35** — **Given** a transition incident or partial qualification, **when** outcomes are examined, **then** F13 distinguishes stages, results, gaps and recovery without personal data in diagnostics; merely launching a command, a sample or checklist success does not constitute global qualification. (FR-038, FR-039)
5. **A36** — **Given** welcome, sign-in, reconstruction and recovery journeys, **when** future design and validation are prepared, **then** R02 receives the states and Pro help, V1 evidence remains historical and technical choices follow R01/R02 and #247; no executed procedure or approved mockup is inferred from this spec. (FR-040)

### Edge cases

- Migration sign-in is neither a new identity nor F05 voluntary deletion; the two cycles must not share consequences merely through similarity.
- The welcome explains preserved entitlements, not a supposedly lost subscription; verification error remains distinct from expiry.
- An unavailable source, an integration defect and an unpreserved protected element are three different outcomes; no success percentage makes them interchangeable.
- A preserved logo association without a certain match remains available for resolution. A lost file is not merely a pending association.
- Changes up to cutover, lost responses and old retries remain subject to the latest identities, decisions and restrictions.

## Requirements

### Functional requirements

#### Reset scope

- **FR-001**: The transition MUST distinguish reset V1 business data from protected information needed for continuity. Recovery of old follows, the inbox and its states, contributions and unretained profile customizations MUST NOT be promised. Business profiles are recreated under F05; F02 subsequently governs V2 history retention. This exceptional operation does not become an ordinary erasure feature.
- **FR-002**: Auth0 principals, their existing identity associations, reliable account-age reference and necessary RevenueCat mappings MUST be preserved or resolved under F05 before reconstruction. Distinct unlinked principals may share an email; no address reservation or business uniqueness claim is carried over. Never merge or infer ownership by email. Migration does not delete identity accounts or create new associations.
- **FR-003**: Permissions MUST be established from current authorized assignments under F12. An old profile, local role or V1 session MUST NOT grant implicit privileges. Ordinary capabilities follow F12 under their business conditions; only authorized processes may establish the privileges needed for operations, without new migration powers for a moderator.
- **FR-004**: Accepted deletion requests, privacy obligations and external support cases MUST continue to be handled under F05/F10/F11. A business-data reset MUST NOT announce erasure of an external case, lose an ongoing deletion or republish prohibited data. Only evidence needed for these obligations remains protected according to its purpose, without a new global retention period.
- **FR-005**: Personal V1 data not carried over MUST produce honest V2 states, without false recovery loading or a promise of reappearance. New follows are voluntarily chosen under F06. Old links, counters, caches and responses MUST NOT make an old reference point to a rebuilt resource without a certain match, transfer relationships by similarity or fabricate V2 recipients.

#### Welcome and sign-in

- **FR-006**: Explicit Google or Apple sign-in MUST be required for first personal access to V2 on an installation moving from V1 to V2, even if a V1 session remains. Migration requires neither a new provider identity nor a Blockout password; it does not require re-entering a secret if the provider signs in another way. After success, ordinary F05 rules resume. Cancellation, failure and unavailable profile remain distinct; guest access and support remain possible under F12. Old-session data and responses remain isolated.
- **FR-007**: An installation identified as updated from V1 MUST present a short transition welcome before sign-in when F12 restrictions permit the ordinary journey. It MUST be dismissible and shown only once after dismissal or completion on that installation. Interruption alone does not constitute completion; resumption, restart, foreground return or sign-out does not repeat a completed welcome. Completion does not waive sign-in for personal actions.
- **FR-008**: The welcome MUST explain sign-in, business data not carried over and Pro entitlement continuity, with recovery help. It MUST NOT announce loss of an account or subscription, or recovery of old follows or notifications. It MUST NOT add any follow-selection step, campaign or transition notification. The person may continue without a mandatory subscription or restoration.
- **FR-009**: The transition welcome MUST remain distinct from ordinary F05 onboarding. Already known completion of onboarding MUST NOT be reset merely because of migration, sign-in or business-profile recreation. No new profile form or global consent is added.
- **FR-010**: A new installation without evidence of transition from V1 MUST follow ordinary F05 welcome, without assumed V1 origin or a transition welcome imposed solely because of account age. Ordinary restoration and support journeys remain accessible even if no transition welcome was shown.
- **FR-011**: The existing welcome panel MUST explain that a person whose applicable Pro rights are not recovered after sign-in can use “Restore my purchases” in the ordinary Pro area. This informational help adds no restoration button, new navigation or change to the welcome/onboarding order. Restoration requires authentication and a usable account under F09. From a signed-out state, proceeding to sign-in MUST NOT silently trigger restoration after success; restoration remains an explicit action on the current account. Cancellation, sign-in failure and unavailable profile allow return or retry, without automatic purchase.
- **FR-012**: Sign-in MUST first allow automatic recovery of applicable entitlements under F09. Restoration help particularly addresses a former subscriber whose access is not recovered, without presuming their purchase is currently valid. Confirmed entitlement, unavailable verification, restoration in progress, absence of an applicable purchase and uncertain outcome MUST remain distinct. An outage MUST NOT become expiry, advertising or encouragement to repurchase. Restoration follows the paying store account and F09 transfer rules; for the other store or an unestablished outcome, verified support remains available without promising universal native restoration.

#### Complete account and entitlement continuity

- **FR-013**: Resolution after sign-in MUST cover Google, Apple and their existing associations, including different addresses; an already linked method finds the same account and its entitlements. Profile recreation is automatic under F05 and imposes neither a new form nor loss of account age. Missing provider fields, conflicting addresses and unavailable profiles remain governed by F05, without new linking or a duplicate business profile.
- **FR-014**: Prior reconciliation MUST cover all subscribers and associations needed for their access, with the categories actually present: Auth0 principals, linked secondary identities, identified, original and historical RevenueCat references and relevant anonymous aliases. A reference alone does not prove its beneficiary. Unknown matches and contradictions MUST be identifiable within a private process; a few samples or dashboard totals do not prove coverage.
- **FR-015**: Before destruction of the state holding it, indispensable information MUST be verifiably retained or its reliable reconstruction demonstrated: identity and address resolution, account age, entitlement beneficiary, purchase mappings and associations present only in V1. An unexplained dependency blocks the affected destruction; a promise of later restoration or the help welcome MUST NOT replace this evidence. No full-profile export or technical mapping schema is imposed.
- **FR-016**: Continuity qualification MUST demonstrate F05/F09 outcomes for affected categories and separately on Apple and Google: return to the same account, linked methods, reinstallation, platform change, restoration and transfer within the standard RevenueCat/store scope accepted in F09 FR-019–FR-026 (potentially receipt-wide), without merging business profiles or transferring gifts; assisted recovery when needed. Actual provider offers, entitlements and settings MUST be checked against these outcomes. A test purchase, dashboard screenshot or successful ordinary restoration alone does not qualify all categories, especially deletion followed by restoration.
- **FR-017**: Preparation MUST account for changes accepted up to cutover: identities, purchases, renewals, transfers, expirations, revocations, deletions and protected logos. Previously prepared state does not prove completeness at the transition. Repeats and old events MUST NOT duplicate effects or overwrite a newer decision; a transition of responsibilities must preserve already accepted operations under their domain rules, without an imposed technical mechanism.
- **FR-018**: An already accepted voluntary deletion MUST continue under F05 despite the transition. Migration MUST NOT recreate the account, lose the request or restore an erased provider record to facilitate access. New registration/restoration exposed to still-conflicting operations waits for their resolution; old retries do not affect a new account. This cycle remains distinct from identity-preserving migration recreation.
- **FR-019**: Still-applicable paid and manual entitlements MUST remain assigned to the recognized beneficiary under F09, with current validity and restrictions. Migration MUST NOT delete a RevenueCat customer as in voluntary deletion or treat business-profile recreation as the end of a gift. Old evidence does not extend an established expiry, undo revocation or transfer a gift with a purchase.

#### Club-logo preservation

- **FR-020**: Before any reset affecting them, all manual V1 club-to-logo associations MUST be verifiably preserved, with club references, URLs and corresponding files, independently of the destroyed state. Presence of a URL or external storage does not suffice to establish file existence and recovery. Evidence must cover final state under FR-017.
- **FR-021**: A missing file, lost association or unusable copy MUST be identified and MUST NOT be declared preserved; the affected destruction remains blocked while this obligation cannot be satisfied. The F13 backup schedule and possible disaster-recovery gaps do not permit deliberate loss of protected information during a prepared transition.
- **FR-022**: A retained logo MUST be reapplied only to a certain club-identity match under F02. An association without a rebuilt club or with ambiguity MUST remain retained and identifiable for resolution, without a fictitious club, matching solely by name or an association presented as successful. This waiting state does not constitute loss of the preserved file and must not be hidden in the transition outcome.
- **FR-023**: The reapplied logo MUST follow F02 customization protections and team/club inheritance. This presentation-migration obligation concerns club logos only; no transfer of other names, team or division logos or V1 customizations is imposed.

#### Sporting reconstruction and notifications

- **FR-024**: Reconstruction MUST target the current season and three completed seasons under F01, controlled references and available source data. Eligibility, classifications, completeness, integration, withdrawals and visibility MUST be decided automatically under F01/F02, without human approval per pool or manual validation of missing calendars. Existing operational permissions remain required for the controlled operation; no new scraping setting is added.
- **FR-025**: The report MUST distinguish obtained, integrated, rejected and partial observations and unavailable sources for each requested season and scope. A source gap is not a V2 defect and does not constitute sporting absence. A V2 collection or integration defect must not be reclassified as an unavailable source to present reconstruction as successful. No global minimum percentage or exhaustive provider availability is added; usable independent parts follow F01/F02.
- **FR-026**: V1 history unrecoverable from sources MAY be absent from this reconstruction; this exception MUST NOT authorize loss of protected identities/entitlements and logos or routine erasure of V2 history. Historical empty calendars, inaccessible sources, reappearances and independent restrictions follow F01/F02, without assumed seasons or fabricated history.
- **FR-027**: Initial reconstruction MUST NOT produce new personal announcements or push notifications from rediscovered results or links, under F07. New eligible events after transition to ordinary use follow V2 triggers and follows; retrying an import does not become a new event. Old V1 sends and recipients not carried over are not caught up.

#### Availability and effective V1 retirement

- **FR-028**: Retirement of ordinary V1 access MUST wait until V2 is actually downloadable on iOS and Android for affected users. Approval alone, a well-formed URL or availability on only one platform does not satisfy this condition. No new automatic store-monitoring system is imposed; F12 defines versions and links, while actual evidence belongs to qualification.
- **FR-029**: Maintenance, minimum version, unavailable configuration and restriction priority MUST follow F12. Legal information and assistance remain accessible under F10/F11; an old client or transition welcome cannot bypass current restrictions. No dedicated outside-mobile emergency circuit is required. Relevant distributed client versions remain subject to actual transition qualification, without promising a provider recovery time.
- **FR-030**: The transition MUST NOT plan lasting functional V1/V2 coexistence. Once V2 takes authority over affected operations, old clients, sessions, direct calls and V1 operations MUST no longer alter shared state incompatibly with V2 rules, particularly by creating a prohibited identity link. The mere presence of a mandatory-update screen is not evidence that server operations are neutralized.
- **FR-031**: V1 retirement MUST address dependencies still needed for identity, particularly default-role assignment called at sign-in. V2 sign-in MUST be qualified with and without a prior role before removing the dependency; handling it MUST NOT grant privileges beyond F12. No endpoint, provider action or replacement mechanism is chosen here.
- **FR-032**: V1 collection, deferred operations and retries MUST be controlled before they can contradict V2 state. F12 maintenance does not implicitly stop them; F01 cycle-completion rules and rules specific to accepted mutations remain applicable. An accepted purchase or erasure MUST NOT become cancelled by assumption. No general scheduler or lasting V1/V2 double processing is required.
- **FR-033**: Installation transition MUST respect F05/F07: old destination associations, caches, badges and responses grant no access to another person’s account and do not blindly restart old deliveries. Sign-in establishes the current context and system permissions remain respected. A push already delivered to the phone must not be presented as certainly recallable; its old access bypasses no restriction.

#### Transition conditions and recovery

- **FR-034**: A reset MUST be conditional on evidence of preservation of protected information it would destroy; opening MUST be conditional on qualified continuity, availability in both stores, effective removal of V1 interference, reconstruction handling under F01/F02 and applicable F13 journeys/protections. Missing evidence, an uncertain outcome or a qualification defect MUST NOT be announced as satisfied. Source gaps remain the automatic outcome of FR-024–FR-026, without new validation per calendar or equating them with lost subscription or logo evidence.
- **FR-035**: Before opening, failed preparation MUST allow stopping, correction or retry without automatic destructive continuation, exposing partial state as ready or losing protected elements or already accepted results. Preparation state and its blockers MUST be distinguishable. No V1 reopening on data that has become incompatible is assumed safe; F13 instructions must establish preconditions for the contemplated recovery.
- **FR-036**: After opening, recovery means repairing or manually restoring V2 with standard tools under F13. No functional return to V1, generic recovery service or automatic reconstruction of every interrupted operation is required. Report any actual recovered gap; do not silently reset user data. Ordinary collection may refresh available supplier data; protected identity/rights/club-logo continuity and privacy obligations remain.
- **FR-037**: Before restored data becomes available, check and apply current deletion, identity, rights and privacy obligations under F05/F09/F11/F13; standard provider checks and authorized manual correction are acceptable. Unresolved affected access remains suspended. A software rollback does not undo data changes; verify compatibility, recovered scope and actual loss before reopening. No synchronous external evidence store or automatic replay framework is prescribed.
- **FR-038**: Preparation, reconstruction, cutover and recovery MUST provide F13 operational outcomes: scope, stages, last successes, failures, uncertainties, remaining work and demonstrated recovery. Continuity evidence containing personal information remains in its private processes; diagnostics and public artifacts do not reproduce its content. Alerts, safe diagnostics and manual intervention remain governed by F13, without monthly availability accounting, on-call duty or a guaranteed human response time.
- **FR-039**: Transition conditions MUST be verifiable from relevant outcomes, not an executed command, existence of an unreviewed backup or a writing-quality checklist. F14 MUST NOT invent a maintenance duration, sporting-coverage threshold or automatic destructive return when a deadline expires. Procedures and mechanisms selected later will need to demonstrate these outcomes before the real transition.
- **FR-040**: Journeys MUST apply F13 for French language, accessibility, errors and retry. R02 MUST cover the transition welcome before sign-in, dismissal, sign-in, reset explanations, Pro/restoration/support states and F12 restrictions. V1 evidence remains historical observations; no V2 screen, provider, procedure or software is deemed qualified. R01/R02 and global acceptance #247 precede technical plans; this spec delivers no code, contract, schema or production operation.

### Functional entities

- **Retained identity and recreated profile**: A person and already associated methods, distinct from their rebuilt business representation and from registration after voluntary deletion.
- **Continuity evidence**: Attributable information establishing an identity, beneficiary, entitlement, association or protected operation; retaining it does not mean public exposure.
- **Preserved logo association**: V1 club reference, logo address and recoverable file; certain association, awaiting a match and missing file are distinct.
- **Transition welcome**: Information specific to an installation moving from V1 to V2, with completion distinct from ordinary onboarding and authentication.
- **Transition outcome**: Scope, stage, success, source gap, defect, uncertainty or remaining work; it constitutes neither a new public resource nor a mandatory dashboard.

## Success criteria

- **SC-001**: A01–A06 produce zero personal access through the V1 session alone, zero restorations silently triggered after sign-in and zero repeats of a completed welcome on the installation. Transition welcome and ordinary onboarding remain distinct; a new installation receives no invented origin.
- **SC-002**: A07–A15 cover both methods and stores and all identity categories present, without mandatory repurchase, email matching, loss of account age or restoration of a revoked entitlement. Every unexplained indispensable association remains blocking before destruction; no accepted deletion is lost.
- **SC-003**: A16–A21 produce zero promised personal restorations without carryover, zero privileges from a cache and preservation evidence for every affected logo association and file before destruction. No ambiguous association is announced as successful; file loss is not an identity-waiting state.
- **SC-004**: A22–A24 report on every targeted reconstruction scope without manual pool validation, invented percentages, false integration success or import notifications. Missing sources and V2 defects remain distinguished; V2 history retains its normal rules.
- **SC-005**: A25–A31 produce zero ordinary V1 retirements based on a single store, zero authorized old interference with V2 state and zero sign-in dependencies removed without qualified continuity. No badge, old push or destination transfers an inbox to another account.
- **SC-006**: A32–A36 continue no destruction with missing evidence, publish no partial state as ready and assume no safe return to V1 after opening. V2 recovery respects protection and current restrictions; a command or checklist does not constitute recovery evidence.

## Assumptions and limits

- The framing authorizes an exceptional business-data reset. This spec does not require migration of old follow relationships, notifications, contributions or profile customizations; neither does it turn this operation into voluntary deletion of the principal or its rights. Independent privacy obligations remain applicable.
- The message may, for example, explain: “If your applicable Pro rights are not recovered after sign-in, use ‘Restore my purchases’ in your Pro area.” This help text does not constitute entitlement evidence or announce its loss; F09 owns applicable states, paying-store-account requirements and restrictions.
- Evidence that an installation comes from V1, retention of welcome completion, private mappings and neutralization of old access are functional needs. Their representation, technical identifiers, architecture and migration mechanisms remain for later plans.
- Qualification concerns actually distributed versions and actually applicable settings. The code and provider observations listed below do not replace it; this delivery performs no new production inspection or provider change.
- Scraping and integration retain their automatic F01/F02 decisions. Prior evidence protects continuity and cutover; it creates no human approval of missing calendars, coverage percentage or arbitrary maintenance schedule.
- V1 retirement waits for both stores and transition conditions. After opening, the product requires V2 recovery; neither maintaining a second functional application nor returning to V1 is requested. The F13 backup policy does not permit deliberate loss of protected information during preparation.

## Approved F01 source-lifecycle clarification — 2026-10-06

Initial sports reconstruction uses F01 adapters and F02 integration for the current season and three completed seasons according to demonstrated availability, without a separate engine or day-long completeness criterion. Prepare and verify unavailable-in-discovery historical references before launch. Initial historical acquisition takes place while the season is PREPARING, before its definitive closure. It uses the same adapters and bounded provider execution without changing other seasons’ controls. Report gaps and retry identified failures before closure; closed seasons have no recollection or reopening operation. Preserve clubs and source correspondences without automatically linking seasonal teams or following them across seasons. F07 still suppresses initial historical-import notifications; F14 continuity, files and erasure obligations remain required. See [F01 A38–A45](../001-source-acquisition/spec.md).
