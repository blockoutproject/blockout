# Feature Specification: Following and personal feed

**Feature Branch**: `develop`

**Created**: 2026-09-20

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline; the technical dossier is available for review. Implementation qualification remains pending.

**Input**: F06 in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications). Define following teams and pools, associated lists and counters, and match selection for the personal calendar. [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md) owns identities and visibility, [F03](../007-sporting-consultation/spec.md) calendars and [F05](../005-accounts-identity/spec.md) accounts. No club follows or automatic seasonal transfer is introduced. The [technical plan](plan.md) defines the implementation boundaries and qualifications.

## User Scenarios & Testing

The actors are the visitor and the signed-in person with a usable profile, free or Pro. Follows are personal; the public follower count does not provide access to follower identities. Scenarios express expected V2 outcomes, not V1 qualification. FR, A and SC identifiers are local to F06.

### User Story 1 — Manage my follows with a reliable outcome (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to manage my follows with a reliable outcome.

**Why this priority**: Following a resource must produce a single relationship and an understandable state, even after a failure.

**Independent Test**: Add and remove follows, repeat requests and interrupt their responses.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a guest or a person without a usable matching account, **when** a follow action is requested, **then** the guest receives a sign-in invitation without implicitly executing the follow after sign-in; an unusable account suspends the personal action without closing deliberate public viewing. A technical reread failure does not alone revoke a known matching account or confirmed Follow state in its owner. For a signed-in Team/Pool page, a failed required relationship read presents the common page Error card with a safe relationship Retry; deliberate guest viewing requires no private read. No Pro subscription is required. (FR-001, FR-002, FR-029)
2. **A02** — **Given** an unfollowed viewable team or pool, **when** a follow is confirmed, **then** a single relationship is created for the current account; follow state, the applicable list and match selection reflect the outcome. Following a pool does not individually follow its teams, or vice versa. (FR-003, FR-005)
3. **A03** — **Given** an already followed resource or an already absent follow, **when** the same request is repeated, including after a double tap, **then** the outcome remains followed or absent respectively, without an additional relationship or counter change. Each mounted control retains its current confirmed label and selection while showing progress and admitting one pending action; an independent visit or control can send the same target another desired-state request before it settles. A confirmed response changes the displayed state; an ordinary failure preserves the previous state. Other controls retain their confirmed state until an ordinary update or reread. No mutation is automatically replayed. The last server-recorded state wins, including a delayed explicit request; no global gesture order is promised. (FR-003, FR-004, FR-023)
4. **A04** — **Given** an existing accessible follow, **when** removal is confirmed, **then** the relationship is removed without deleting the sporting resource; the list and calendar are reassessed without removing matches still selected by another follow. (FR-004, FR-019)
5. **A05** — **Given** a team whose name or classification is corrected without changing its identity, **when** data is refreshed, **then** the follow is retained. A team in the new season, even with the same name and club, is not automatically followed. (FR-006, FR-007)
6. **A06** — **Given** a mutation in progress for an account, **when** the person switches accounts or signs out before the response, **then** no private data or late response from the first account affects the new context; no guest follow is created. (FR-002, FR-023)

### User Story 2 — Choose a shared season for my personal space (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to choose a shared season for my personal space.

**Why this priority**: A single selection prevents calendars and their follows from referring to different seasons.

**Independent Test**: Change the season across the three screens, select All seasons and add a follow from another season.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A07** — **Given** viewable follows from 2025/2026 and 2026/2027, and a catalog season of 2027/2028, **when** the personal space opens without a retained choice, **then** 2026/2027 is selected. Without a viewable follow, 2027/2028 is offered; without an available season, no year is invented. A retrieval error does not prove the absence of seasons. (FR-009, FR-010, FR-022)
2. **A08** — **Given** several seasons followed or viewable in the catalog, **when** a season is chosen in Upcoming, Completed or Following, **then** the same selection applies to all three screens and the Teams/Pools lists; F04 search filters and F03 club sheets do not change. Choices are combined without duplicates or fixed years. (FR-008, FR-009)
3. **A09** — **Given** viewable follows from several seasons, **when** All seasons is selected, **then** their lists and eligible matches become accessible together under their ordering rules, without follows transferred between seasons. (FR-008, FR-014, FR-018)
4. **A10** — **Given** a filter on 2025/2026, **when** a team from 2026/2027 is followed from its detail sheet, **then** the follow is confirmed with its season but the filter remains on 2025/2026; the resource can be found in 2026/2027 or All seasons. A new opening without a retained choice recalculates the default. (FR-010, FR-011)
5. **A11** — **Given** a valid selected season, **when** a detail sheet is opened then left, or a newer season appears, **then** the current choice and still-valid context are retained. A reset returns to the default among viewable follows, then the catalog if none exist. (FR-010, FR-015)
6. **A12** — **Given** a selection that has become unavailable, **when** an ordinary successful choices reload establishes its disappearance, including after opening the existing season selector, **then** the shared selector returns automatically to the FR-010 default, or All seasons if none is available, without a warning or dedicated repair action. A failed selected-season data read remains an ordinary error until an explicit retry or selector opening; it does not reload choices automatically. Failure to retrieve choices preserves uncertainty instead of declaring the selection gone. (FR-012, FR-022)

### User Story 3 — Find my follows without exposing a hidden resource (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to find my follows without exposing a hidden resource.

**Why this priority**: Personal history remains useful while respecting sporting restrictions.

**Independent Test**: Browse identical names and old seasons, hide then restore a followed resource.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A13** — **Given** viewable followed teams and pools, including identical names, **when** the lists are browsed, **then** each list applies the shared filter and alphabetical public-name ordering, with stable tie-breaking and known season/classification context. All matching follows remain accessible, without a silent total cap. (FR-013, FR-014)
2. **A14** — **Given** a followed resource that becomes hidden under F02, **when** their ordinary lists or calendar reads complete, **then** those responses omit the hidden target without an unavailable section, generic entry or hidden-target removal control. An already-loaded public sporting card may remain until its own ordinary read or foreground refresh; opening it applies current F03 unavailability. The relationship remains retained. (FR-016)
3. **A15** — **Given** a retained relationship to a hidden resource, **when** the same identity becomes viewable again, **then** its follow and eligible effects reappear without a new addition or double increment; a remaining independent restriction prevents this reappearance. (FR-006, FR-016, FR-020)
4. **A16** — **Given** only hidden follows for a season, with no other viewable data from that season, **when** choices and lists are presented, **then** those follows alone do not add this season to the selector. If the catalog independently justifies it, it may remain offered. Empty states refer to follows available for the selection without claiming that retained relationships have been erased. (FR-009, FR-016, FR-017)
5. **A17** — **Given** a catalog season without personal follows or follows without eligible matches, **when** the calendar is viewed, **then** the first case explains the absence of available follows for the selection and offers search; the second explains the absence of applicable matches. No general calendar is presented as personal. (FR-017, FR-018, FR-022)

### User Story 4 — See each relevant match once (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to see each relevant match once.

**Why this priority**: Several follows may refer to the same match without multiplying matches in the feed.

**Independent Test**: Combine participant and pool follows, then remove them successively and walk through F03 time cases.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A18** — **Given** several follow relationships and known matches, **when** the feed is read, **then** the following cases apply. Already-loaded public sporting projections retain the FR-012/FR-016 ordinary-freshness boundary. (FR-016, FR-018, FR-019, FR-021)

- **Union and deduplication**: Following both teams and their pool presents each currently viewable match in the selected season once.
- **Withdrawn match with a result**: Even if the team remains viewable in another pool, a match withdrawn from its former calendar under F02 is absent from the feed and its public detail sheet; its internally retained result does not make it displayable. Following the former pool does not bypass withdrawal.
- **Confirmed empty calendar**: One qualified wholly empty current observation hides the pool, its matches and participations under F01/F02 without deleting follows. A team hides only if no visible participation remains elsewhere. False empty and partial observations preserve accepted presence.
- **Failure and reappearance**: A download failure alone removes no match. An authorized reappearance restores retained identities and follows without duplicates and without waiting for two new collections, subject to the selected season and other restrictions.

2. **A19** — **Given** several follows selecting a match, **when** one and then the last of these follows are removed, **then** the match remains present as long as an applicable follow selects it; it disappears from the personal feed after the last is removed, without sporting deletion of the match. (FR-004, FR-019)
3. **A20** — **Given** a dated match without a result, a postponement, a match without a time and a match without a date, **when** the personal calendar is browsed in Paris and New York, **then** days/times, Upcoming/Completed categories, the unavailable-result indication, shared boundaries and hiding without a date follow F03. The filter concerns the F02 sporting season, not the local calendar year. (FR-018, FR-021)
4. **A21** — **Given** a multipage calendar and a change in follows or season, **when** responses arrive out of order or an additional page fails, **then** old batches are not added to the new context; already retrieved authorized matches and their controls/scroller stay mounted through next-page failure and shared footer Retry; full-reread failure and safe recovery follow [F13 FR-031/FR-036–FR-038](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements) without duplicates or a false end of list. (FR-019, FR-022, FR-023)

### User Story 5 — Understand counters and recover after an error (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to understand counters and recover after an error.

**Why this priority**: A responsive interface must not announce a definitive state without evidence of the outcome.

**Independent Test**: Simulate confirmed failures, lost responses and failure after success, then check counters and shared rules.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A22** — **Given** a known or unavailable public count, **when** a follow is confirmed, repeated, hidden then restored, **then** a relationship counts once; repetition or hiding neither creates nor removes a relationship. An unknown count is not zero and the number of filtered results does not become the follower count. After an initial or retained public-count read reaches final failure, its icon remains and `Indisponible` replaces the number with the accessible label `Nombre d’abonnés indisponible`. There is no dedicated count Retry; a later ordinary count read restores a successful number. (FR-020)
2. **A23** — **Given** a certainly refused request, **when** its outcome is shown, **then** the previous confirmed state remains applicable, with an error and an appropriate retry option, without invented success or count. A target that has become hidden cannot be followed through old access. (FR-001, FR-024)
3. **A24** — **Given** a potentially saved addition or removal whose response is lost, **when** the interface receives a transport error, **then** ordinary mutation failure feedback preserves the last confirmed state and permits an explicit manual retry. Existing invalidation and normal reads may update current state; no verification prerequisite, dedicated recovery mode or invented outcome is added. Repeated desired-state commands cause no duplicate relationship. (FR-024, FR-025)
4. **A25** — **Given** an accepted mutation followed by failed profile retrieval, **when** presentation is reconciled, **then** the read failure does not prove cancellation of the mutation or erase its confirmed outcome. Progress alone never establishes a saved follow. (FR-025)
5. **A26** — **Given** retrieved follows but a required read fails, **when** the applicable journey is viewed or refreshed, **then** [F13 FR-031/FR-036–FR-038](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements) determines its scoped Error, first-load, refresh and same-card Retry presentation. Still-authorized pages and selections remain in their existing owner, and each failure stays distinct from zero follows, resources or matches. An unrelated Home tab remains accessible; a missing common prerequisite follows A30 without removing the pager. Teams and Pools are collapsible sections of the single Following tab: an initial or full-reread failure for an open section replaces that tab content with one Error card, while a collapsed section waits for activation. Card Retry relaunches failed necessary reads. A next-page failure retains the native list and both sections’ authorized rows, with the shared compact error/Retry line at the end of the failed section. Its explicit Retry resumes only that section’s failed page; the sibling’s valid continuation stays independent. (FR-008, FR-022, FR-026, FR-029)
6. **A27** — **Given** a confirmed then removed follow, **when** the relationship is consumed by notification features, **then** F07 selects confirmed recipients once without duplicates and checks a current qualifying follow before a prepared push. An already selected recipient who re-follows can qualify; later followers are not added and no additional send or replay is created. The feed season filter does not become a notification preference; following alone promises neither system permission nor delivery. (FR-023, FR-027)
7. **A28** — **Given** a free user, Pro user or user with unknown entitlements, **when** follows and personal calendars are used with applicable accessibility aids, **then** F05/F09/F11/F13 rules apply: no payment required to follow, no interstitial on filtering/refresh/return, sporting navigation according to F11, states readable without color alone, relationship privacy and contextual reporting. (FR-026, FR-028)

8. **A29** — **Given** a signed-in Team/Pool page, **when** its Follow relationship or another required read is pending or fails, **then** [F13 FR-041](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#first-useful-composition) and [F03 FR-029](../007-sporting-consultation/spec.md#recovery-restrictions-and-quality) determine the indispensable common context and tab scope. The private relationship is a common prerequisite of the signed-in page, so its final failure replaces dependent page content with the common Error card and its Retry reads that relationship. Guests have no private relationship prerequisite; public counts stay independent. Technical failure preserves matching confirmation in Query and confirmed writes remain confirmed; it does not keep an inverse action visible over the common Error card. Read Retry follows [F13 FR-031/FR-036–FR-038](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements) and never repeats a mutation. Refusals, original-account/target isolation and pending commands retain their actual guards. (FR-002, FR-023–FR-026, FR-029)
9. **A30** — **Given** Home with an established personal owner, **when** an indispensable common prerequisite is not yet usable or its required read reaches final failure, including the first season read, **then** Upcoming, Completed and Following retain the same native pager, tab presses and horizontal swipes. Each scene presents its applicable F13 read feedback; changing tabs starts no additional common read. Calendars and lists do not load before their common prerequisites are usable. Explicit Retry retains the card; only the initiating scene's button shows progress while its safe read is active, without active progress while suspended. Returning to that scene observes the same attempt; other scenes retain their idle card action while sharing the read's outcome. Success restores applicable content or confirmed Empty with the valid season and selected tab retained. Current refusals still withdraw affected private data, and guest/account changes retain F05 ownership. (FR-008, FR-022, FR-026, FR-029)

### Edge Cases

- A completed season remains viewable under F02; a new season does not replace followed identities.
- All follows may be retained but hidden: current reads expose no unavailable list and the interface does not claim relationships have been deleted. Older public cards may remain until ordinary rereading; their destination enforces current access.
- Removing the last follow from the selected season does not silently change seasons: it remains selectable if the catalog justifies it; otherwise FR-012 applies.
- A follow from another season is saved without changing the current filter; the filter is neither a follow mutation nor a notification preference.
- An absent response, an established refusal and a read failure after success are three distinct situations.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

#### Access, relationships and renewal

- **FR-001**: Following MUST concern only viewable teams and pools, for a signed-in person with a usable profile, with no Pro requirement. A nonexistent or hidden target MUST NOT receive a new follow through old access. No editing right or club follow is added.
- **FR-002**: A guest MUST receive the F05 sign-in invitation without implicitly executing a follow afterwards. Without a usable matching account under F05, personal actions remain suspended and deliberate public viewing remains reachable. A failed technical reread alone MUST NOT revoke a known matching account, remove its confirmed Follow state or turn it into guest identity. Follows and responses MUST remain isolated by native principal and business account UUID, including replacement of that UUID under the same principal; sign-out, account change and deletion follow F05.
- **FR-003**: A confirmed addition MUST create a single account/type/identity relationship. Repeating an already satisfied addition MUST NOT create a duplicate or repeat its counter effects. Concurrent requests or retries MUST respect this uniqueness.
- **FR-004**: Confirmed removal of an accessible follow MUST delete that personal relationship without deleting the sporting resource. Removing an already absent relationship MUST NOT repeat counter effects. State, list and match selection MUST be reassessed after confirmation.
- **FR-005**: Following a pool MUST NOT create individual follows for its teams; following a team MUST NOT create follows for its pools. Each relationship retains its meaning and may select relevant matches independently of the others.
- **FR-006**: A follow MUST remain attached to the F02 identity, including during a name/classification correction that preserves it and an authorized reappearance. No similarity of name, club or classification transfers the relationship to another identity.
- **FR-007**: Seasonal follow renewal MUST remain manual. A new seasonal team or pool does not inherit old follows; still-viewable historical resources remain accessible. The exceptional V1 reset follows [F14](../014-v1-v2-transition/spec.md): no recovery of old follows is promised or inferred from resource similarity; new follows remain voluntarily chosen.

#### Shared season and navigation

- **FR-008**: Upcoming, Completed and Following MUST share a single season selection, also shared by the Teams and Pools lists. Within the established personal context, their three native pages and tab/swipe navigation MUST remain available during common-prerequisite loading or failure as defined by FR-029. “All seasons” MUST be offered. This filter is independent of F04 search and F03 club sheets.
- **FR-009**: Offered seasons MUST combine those from viewable follows and the viewable catalog, without duplicates or fixed years. A season justified only by hidden follows MUST NOT be exposed through those relationships; it remains possible if the catalog independently justifies it. No year is invented without available data.
- **FR-010**: Without a retained choice, the latest season among viewable follows MUST be selected; failing that, the latest in the catalog. A valid selection MUST persist during the journey, even if a newer season appears. Resetting reapplies this rule; a new opening without a retained choice recalculates the default.
- **FR-011**: Adding a follow from another season MUST NOT change the current shared filter. Confirmation MUST identify the follow’s season, where it can be found, or in All seasons. This rule does not transfer the follow or erase navigation choices.
- **FR-012**: A successful choices reload that removes the selected season MUST automatically apply the FR-010 default, or All seasons if no season is available. Use the ordinary selector without a warning, unavailable option or dedicated repair action. A failed selected-season list/calendar read MUST retain the selection and ordinary error/retry; it does not automatically reload choices or repair the choice. The holder may open the existing season selector to reload choices and establish disappearance. A failed choices read MUST preserve the prior selection rather than prove disappearance. Successful omission changes the shared choice without traversing or rewriting other retained lists or calendars. Already-loaded public sporting cards may remain until their own ordinary read or foreground refresh; opening a destination applies its current F03 authority. Relationships remain retained.

#### Personal lists and hiding

- **FR-013**: The Teams and Pools lists MUST apply the shared filter and sort viewable follows alphabetically by public name, with stable tie-breaking for identical names on unchanged data. Season, classification and other available contextual information MUST allow resources to be distinguished without invented details.
- **FR-014**: All viewable follows matching the selection MUST be accessible, without a silent total cap. An identity appears once in its list. All seasons combines eligible historical follows without changing their identities.
- **FR-015**: Opening a resource MUST use its F03 detail sheet. Returning MUST retain selection, tab and position when the context remains valid; an ordering or visibility change may require recomposition without guaranteeing the exact position.
- **FR-016**: Ordinary lists and calendar reads MUST omit resources hidden under F02, even if followed. Their current responses MUST NOT add an unavailable section, generic card or hidden-target removal control. An already-loaded public card may remain until its own ordinary read or foreground refresh; another projection learning about hiding MUST NOT synchronously rewrite it. The relationship remains retained for reappearance of the same identity without a new addition; no other restriction reason is lifted. An old link follows F03 unavailability.
- **FR-017**: Empty states MUST distinguish no follows available for the selection from no applicable matches despite available follows. They MUST NOT assert deletion of hidden relationships. A season without available follows MUST offer access to search to choose follows manually.

#### Personal calendar and counters

- **FR-018**: Current feed reads MUST select only matches viewable under F02/F03 in the chosen sporting season, linked to at least one applicable followed team or pool. A team selects its matches within its viewable participations; a pool selects its viewable matches. A followed team or pool never makes a withdrawn match viewable, even with an internally retained result. Full withdrawal after one qualified, complete, current and successfully integrated empty-calendar observation follows F01/F02; a read failure does not trigger it. All seasons removes only the seasonal restriction. Without an applicable follow, no substitute general calendar is presented as personal.
- **FR-019**: A match selected by several follows MUST appear once. Removing a follow MUST NOT remove it if another still selects it; removing the last removes it from the feed without a sporting mutation. Pagination and refresh MUST avoid duplicates and lasting omissions in accordance with F03. A confirmed follow change reloads affected personal lists and calendars from their first page when displayed, retaining the selected season and calendar category. Until that succeeds, ordinary loading/error/retry replaces the previous selection; an unchanged-context refresh retains authorized pages and traversal in their existing owner, while its final read failure follows [F13 FR-031/FR-036–FR-038](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements).
- **FR-020**: The public follower count MUST represent existing follow relationships for this identity, each once, without exposing followers. Season filtering, hiding and reappearance neither create nor delete a relationship. An unknown count MUST NOT be presented as zero; a quantity of displayed follows does not become the follower count or an implicit exhaustive total. After the count read reaches final failure, retain its icon and replace the number with `Indisponible`, labelled accessibly `Nombre d’abonnés indisponible`, independently of the private relationship and sporting content. There is no count-specific Retry, polling or replacement refresh loop. A later ordinary count read can restore the number; a current target refusal retains its access/withdrawal scope.
- **FR-021**: Personal calendars MUST reuse F03 for local times and days, ordering, shared boundaries, absent results, postponements, unknown dates and navigation. A past match without a result falls under F03 handling, not a feed-specific exclusion. The season comes from F02, never from the display calendar year.

Following a contextual pool such as CFA tour 01 does not follow CFA tour 02. Following the same seasonal team covers its compatible participation across those pools; another division or season defines a distinct team under F02. See [F01 A38–A45](../001-source-acquisition/spec.md).

#### Errors, concurrency and shared responsibilities

- **FR-022**: Loading relationships, retrieving resources and loading matches MUST remain distinguished. A failed stage MUST NOT produce zero follows or zero matches. [F13 FR-031/FR-036–FR-038](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements) owns first load, refresh, continuation, final failure and explicit Retry presentation. Still-authorized pages and selections remain in their existing owner for recovery, while an actual private refusal withdraws the forbidden data; a next-page failure retains the authorized rows, controls and native scroller with compact footer Retry for that cursor, never exhaustion. This applies to an accepted page set even when current filtering renders no rows.
- **FR-023**: Requests and responses MUST be tied to the applicable account, target and context. An obsolete response MUST NOT replace a current selection or add an old batch to the new feed. Each mounted follow control MUST retain its current confirmed label and selected state while showing progress and admitting one pending action without queueing. A confirmed response changes the displayed follow state; an ordinary failure preserves the previous state. Other controls retain confirmed state until an ordinary update or read; independent controls or visits may send desired-state requests for the same target concurrently. A mutation response MUST NOT restore private data withdrawn after its applicable access refusal; a successful authorized read is required. A public sporting refusal in another projection does not change this relationship reader or other retained collections. Actual account departure and private refusal still remove forbidden data. The single team-follow action required by F03 remains shared by that team's tabs. Other targets remain usable. Mutations MUST NOT be automatically replayed. The last server-recorded state wins, including across devices or delayed explicit requests; no global order of user gestures is promised.
- **FR-024**: A failed or lost follow response MUST use ordinary mutation failure feedback, retain the last confirmed label/state and permit an explicit manual retry. Existing invalidation and normal reads may observe current state without becoming a verification prerequisite. Repeating the desired state creates no duplicate relation; no custom uncertainty mode, confirmation read, retry gate or automatic write replay is introduced. A later read proves neither cancellation nor the exact historical command outcome. A definite refusal preserves the last confirmed state; unconfirmed success is never announced.
- **FR-025**: Failed profile retrieval after a mutation MUST NOT suffice to announce its cancellation or erase established write success. Pending presentation MUST NOT imply that the requested state is saved or invent a relationship, count or success.
- **FR-026**: Apply F13 honest freshness, accessibility, failure isolation and diagnostics without a performance or monthly availability target. Follow state, selections and retries MUST be understandable without colour alone. Current destination authority and private refusals override affected retained data; the public projection freshness allowed by FR-012/FR-016 remains ordinary.
- **FR-027**: Confirmed relationships MUST constitute the follow reference consumable by F07 without duplicates; a removed and later recreated follow is a new relationship. F07 may use that current qualifying follow for an already selected recipient's prepared push, without adding later followers or creating a replay. [F07](../011-notifications-delivery/spec.md#link-announcements-and-recipients) owns trigger/send eligibility, cancellation and delivery; following alone proves neither system permission nor delivery. The display season MUST NOT implicitly become a notification preference.
- **FR-028**: F05/F09/F11 MUST govern accounts, entitlements, advertising and privacy. Follows and the personal calendar do not become paid features. Filtering, refreshing and returning do not trigger an interstitial; sporting navigation follows F11. Personal relationships are not exposed to other users. Reporting retains its context and F10/F11 limits without implicit new collection.
- **FR-029**: Signed-in Team/Pool composition MUST use the current holder’s Follow relationship as defined by [F03 FR-029](../007-sporting-consultation/spec.md#recovery-restrictions-and-quality) and [F13 FR-041](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#first-useful-composition). The private relationship remains an indispensable common prerequisite: its final read failure presents the common page Error card and read-only relationship Retry, including after a confirmed write. Guests require no private relationship read. Public counts remain independent under FR-020. Technical account or relationship failure preserves matching still-authorized confirmation and established command outcomes in their existing owner; read presentation and safe Retry follow [F13 FR-031/FR-036–FR-038](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements). No read Retry replays a mutation. Current-account and exact-target refusals retain their actual scope, and the server remains authoritative for each command. Home retains its three paged scenes during indispensable common account/season loading or final failure: each dependent scene presents the applicable F13 feedback, without replacing the pager with one shared non-paged body. Pending background reads retain usable content under F13. The existing common read remains single-owned; changing scenes MUST NOT start it again. Calendar/list reads wait for usable common prerequisites; an unavailable season read does not select All seasons by default. Once ready, each active tab owns its required content reads under [F13 FR-041](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#first-useful-composition); independent tabs do not share a failure solely because their queries are mounted together. Following contains two collapsible Teams/Pools sections. An initial or full-reread failure for an open section presents one Error card in Following; a collapsed section starts no read until activation. Card recovery reads only failed necessary sections. A next-page-only failure instead retains the single native list and both sections’ authorized pages, controls and scroll position. Each section places F13’s shared continuation line after its own rows; footer Retry resumes only that section’s failed cursor without blocking the sibling’s valid paging. Query retains both traversals.

### Key Entities

- **Follow relationship**: A personal link between an account and a team or pool identity, retained independently of visibility and without implicit seasonal transfer.
- **Personal selection**: A single season or All seasons, shared by calendars and both follow lists, distinct from F03/F04 filters.
- **Viewable follow**: A relationship whose target is currently viewable under F02; visibility does not define the existence of the relationship.
- **Feed match**: A viewable F02 match selected by at least one applicable relationship, presented under F03 and deduplicated by identity.
- **Follower count**: The number of existing relationships on a resource; it denotes neither loaded list items nor a public user list.
- **Mutation outcome**: Confirmed state or ordinary mutation failure with explicit retry; pending or lost responses do not establish a saved change, cancellation or a separate verification state.

## Success Criteria

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A01–A06 produce at most one relationship per account/type/identity, no guest or implicit follow and no cross-account leakage. Repeats produce zero additional effects. (FR-001–FR-007, FR-023)
- **SC-002**: A07–A12 apply the same selection across the three screens and both lists, without changing external filters or silently replacing a valid choice. Successful season omission selects a valid default without rewriting retained exact-season or All-seasons collection caches. (FR-008–FR-012, FR-015)
- **SC-003**: A13–A17 make all matching viewable follows accessible, with current read responses omitting hidden targets, retained public cards following FR-016 ordinary freshness, and zero relationship transfers on reappearance. (FR-013–FR-017)
- **SC-004**: A18–A21 show each eligible followed match once. Current feed reads and destination openings cannot bypass hiding after a qualified single current empty observation; already-loaded public cards follow the ordinary-freshness boundary.
