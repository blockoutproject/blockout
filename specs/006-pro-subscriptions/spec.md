# Feature Specification: Pro subscriptions

**Feature Branch**: `develop`

**Created**: 2026-09-18

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline with owner-agreed planning corrections and approved guest-entry prototype and Figma translation; the technical decisions are recorded in [plan.md](plan.md).

**Input**: F09 in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications). Define paid and gifted Pro entitlements, their refresh, purchases, restorations, transfers and assisted recovery, in continuity with [F05](../005-accounts-identity/spec.md) and [F11](../004-advertising-privacy-legal/spec.md). No new benefit, subscription tier or web purchase is added. The existing monthly subscription becomes 1.99 EUR at V2 launch through native store pricing, coordinated by F14.

## User Scenarios & Testing

The actors are the visitor, the holder of a usable account and the project owner, the only person authorized at launch to manage manual benefits and recovery in RevenueCat. A Blockout moderator receives none of these powers automatically. Stores manage billing; assignment of a Blockout entitlement is distinct from the paying Apple/Google account and the F05 sign-in identity.

These scenarios define required outcomes; [tasks](tasks.md) record implementation and remaining qualification. Their presence proves neither an actual purchase, store configuration nor success of the deletion/restoration lifecycle. A, FR and SC identifiers are local to F09. Later mutative tests must use authorized qualification accounts; no real customer data is published in evidence.

Qualification follows [F13's proportionate scope](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#platforms-accessibility-and-bounded-continuity): deterministic tests cover entitlement, eligibility, error and obsolete-session variants; representative native observations establish the SDK, provider UI and store boundaries. Review the complete retained territory/product/cohort/terms configuration, then inspect representative native formats rather than every store locale. Gift qualification uses the supported owner procedure and focused SDK reflection on each platform, without an exhaustive console-failure campaign. These limits preserve all payment, beneficiary, billing, privacy and continuity outcomes below; unobserved provider results remain pending at their owning tasks.

### User Story 1 — Understand my access and recover my benefits (Priority: P1)

As an account holder, I want to recover the same benefits on my devices and understand whether my access is active, free or pending, without unnecessary payment.

**Why this priority**: Entitlements determine the advertising exemption, never access to sporting consultation. Missing evidence does not mean the person must buy.

**Independent Test**: Cover entitlement/access and delayed-response variants deterministically; observe ordinary native account transitions and same-account recovery across platforms at the SDK boundary.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a valid paid subscription or valid manual benefit, **when** entitlements are established, **then** the sole Pro benefit is the absence of advertising served by Blockout. The pool map and full calendars of clubs’ upcoming and completed matches are free for everyone, including guests. Advertising from external video services is excluded from this promise; a mere client declaration proves no entitlement. (FR-001–FR-003)
2. **A02** — **Given** a guest or an account that is SDK-inactive, paid active, gifted, expired or has unknown entitlements, **when** they open public sporting consultation, including a pool map or full club calendar, **then** no Pro block, wait for entitlement verification or purchase invitation interrupts access. Personal and administrative actions retain their account and permission conditions. Every existing connected Pro entry follows FR-017; only an established free account receives its existing banner. Guests receive the shared sign-in invitation without a price; cancelling preserves the guest context and completing sign-in follows the ordinary journey without reopening Pro. Purchase and restoration require a usable signed-in account. Unknown entitlements suppress advertising and automatic purchase encouragement without preventing a voluntary Pro entry. F13 accessibility applies. (FR-001, FR-003–FR-005, FR-017, FR-033)
3. **A03** — **Given** paid and promotional entitlements in RevenueCat, **when** native SDK state changes after expiry, revocation or restoration, **then** that state determines Pro without a Blockout recomputation. A gift changes no billing date; restoration follows the accepted native receipt-wide scope. (FR-002, FR-020, FR-028)
4. **A04** — **Given** the same Blockout account on another device or the other platform, **when** the person signs in, **then** their applicable entitlements are recovered without a new purchase. An already linked Google/Apple method leads to the same outcome. Native restoration is distinct from this account-based recovery. (FR-005, FR-016)
5. **A05** — **Given** account A with a purchase, restoration or entitlement read in progress, **when** the session switches to B or guest mode and a response from A arrives, **then** it assigns no entitlement to the new session. A payment already made remains recoverable in its legitimate context; the session change does not portray it as a store cancellation. (FR-006, FR-014)

### User Story 2 — Continue during an outage without retaining an entitlement indefinitely (Priority: P1)

As a subscriber, I want verification outages not to cut off my benefits immediately, using the provider’s standard state and understandable recovery.

**Why this priority**: A verification outage, a certain end date and a billing problem with an entitlement still active must be distinguished.

**Independent Test**: Supply active, inactive, cached and unavailable SDK state, then change account and exercise native purchase/restoration outcomes.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A06** — **Given** active use, foreground return or an opened Pro provider interface, **when** the RevenueCat SDK supplies cached or refreshed state, **then** the application uses that state without a custom refresh clock or backend verification. Public sporting consultation remains usable. (FR-003, FR-007, FR-008)
2. **A07** — **Given** a provider outage, **when** the SDK still supplies an active entitlement under its cache rules, **then** Pro remains active. When it supplies an inactive state, F11 advertising eligibility may resume subject to consent. No separate Blockout outage-tolerance period applies. (FR-003, FR-004, FR-009)
3. **A08** — **Given** an unavailable SDK with no current-account state, **when** the application restarts or an ordinary entitlement read fails, **then** no advertising or Pro banner is shown and no inactive state is invented. The existing voluntary Pro entry can open the configured paywall after account association; association or opening failure gives brief feedback on the originating screen, whose independent support entry remains available. No verification page, timestamp or tolerance counter is introduced. (FR-004, FR-007–FR-009, FR-017)
4. **A09** — **Given** a changed entitlement, **when** the SDK refreshes, **then** its current state governs Pro. A response for an earlier account session cannot grant rights to the new account. (FR-006, FR-010)
5. **A10** — **Given** a gift expiry, sign-out or accepted deletion, **when** state is evaluated, **then** native SDK expiry rules apply and the previous account’s rights never populate a guest or another account. There is no separate Blockout grace period. (FR-005, FR-009, FR-010, FR-027)
6. **A11** — **Given** renewal disabled but a paid period still active, **when** the person views Pro, **then** entitlements remain applicable until their actual end. A payment problem with active store grace also preserves access; expiry, suspension without an active entitlement or confirmed revocation removes only the relevant entitlement. An event label alone is insufficient to decide the outcome. (FR-011)

### User Story 3 — Purchase and manage my subscription without ambiguity (Priority: P1)

As an account holder, I want to know the offer terms, understand the payment outcome and find subscription management.

**Why this priority**: Payment, entitlement verification and display of benefits may complete separately; confusing them may lead to paying again.

**Independent Test**: Exercise an available then unavailable offer, cancel, defer or confirm payment and trigger a verification failure after payment.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A12** — **Given** a usable connected account, free, paid, gifted or without current rights information, **when** an existing Pro entry is opened, **then** successful current-account SDK association and explicit offering resolution lead directly to the same configured native paywall. Its products, localized prices, periods and applicable terms come from the provider. Missing offering or failed pre-presentation resolution/association gives brief originating-screen feedback; errors rendered after native mounting remain SDK-owned; no catalogue page, implicit current offering, invented price or automatic payment is introduced. (FR-005, FR-012, FR-013, FR-017)
2. **A13** — **Given** a purchase journey, **when** the person cancels or the store indicates a pending payment, **then** no new Pro access is granted on that outcome alone. Cancellation is not a technical error; pending payment remains a native SDK/store outcome and Blockout never replays it automatically. An independent valid entitlement remains applicable. Ordinary purchase errors keep the standard native feedback without a duplicate Blockout message and a later explicit SDK action without an app-owned Verify state or mandatory read before retry. An error establishes neither a charge nor its absence. (FR-008, FR-014)
3. **A14** — **Given** payment confirmed by the store, **when** entitlement activation is not yet established, **then** confirmed payment remains distinct from activation and is not presented as failed. The standard provider journey and ordinary current-account SDK updates permit recognition and supported restoration/assistance without Blockout replaying payment or introducing a verification screen. Provider purchase controls remain native, including any generic Buy control still displayed. A lost response proves neither failure nor cancellation. (FR-008, FR-014, FR-015)
4. **A15** — **Given** a confirmed purchase and valid entitlement established for the current account, **when** the result is applied, **then** the absence of advertising is confirmed without requiring a restart. No sporting content is presented as newly unlocked. Blockout starts no additional purchase automatically; explicit purchase, already-owned recognition and restoration use the SDK/store’s standard controls and outcomes without an application interceptor. (FR-006, FR-014, FR-015)
5. **A16** — **Given** any existing connected Pro entry on a free, paid, gifted or unknown-rights account, **when** it is tapped, **then** the same configured RevenueCat Paywall opens directly in a standard native Modal after the useful account prerequisite and SDK association. The Modal contains only the official Paywall component, with no commercial destination, Blockout header or intermediate controls. Closing returns to the originating screen; logout, accepted erasure and account replacement remove the host without claiming store cancellation. Restoration, management and assistance use the configured journey’s supported native actions. Observe the actual paid/gifted presentation and original-store limits; a generic Buy control may remain visible as an accepted native limitation. Do not hide, disable or intercept it through Blockout code or replace the provider UI. Blockout consumes known SDK origin/validity truthfully and invents no billed renewal for gifts; limited gift/unknown distinctions in the provider UI remain accepted native presentation limits. (FR-005, FR-017–FR-019)

### User Story 4 — Restore a purchase and obtain verified assistance (Priority: P1)

As a purchase holder, I want to recover it after reinstalling, switching accounts or deletion, without merging profiles or losing other entitlements.

**Why this priority**: Holding a Blockout account and owning the purchase are distinct; support must neither require repurchasing nor arbitrarily assign a subscription.

**Independent Test**: Restore from each store onto the original account then another account, with an independent gift, and qualify evidence for assisted recovery.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A17** — **Given** a signed-in account, free, paid, gifted or without current rights information, **when** the person opens Pro and looks for restoration, **then** the configured paywall uses its standard explicit restore action independent of purchase. Native copy, action availability and return behavior are observed with accepted library limitations, without an app restore fallback, confirmation dialog or direct active-account Customer Center branch. Receipt-wide association remains part of existing private assistance guidance. If opening fails, brief source feedback leaves independent F10 support available. (FR-005, FR-017, FR-019)
2. **A18** — **Given** explicit restoration within the configured paywall journey, **when** the SDK supplies no active entitlement, supplies active restored rights or reports failure, **then** those outcomes remain distinct. A successful operation without an active Pro entitlement does not announce “Pro active”; returned current-account CustomerInfo is sufficient without a second confirmation read. Failure keeps standard native feedback without a duplicate Blockout message and permits a later explicit native action, without an app Verify state or automatic restore replay. (FR-008, FR-019)
3. **A19** — **Given** an authenticated account B and store purchases previously associated with A, **when** the user explicitly restores, **then** the standard RevenueCat/store transfer scope applies, potentially receipt-wide. No Blockout profile is merged and no gift transfers as a purchase. Observe both accounts on refresh without a purchase-by-purchase guarantee. (FR-010, FR-020, FR-028, FR-032)
4. **A20** — **Given** an Apple purchase and a person now on Android without access to their old Blockout account, **when** they request recovery, **then** no native restoration of an Apple receipt on Google Play is promised; verified assisted recovery is offered. The reverse case is covered. If the same Blockout account remains accessible, its entitlements are recovered without requiring a store change. (FR-016, FR-021)
5. **A21** — **Given** a deleted business account and a still-valid store purchase, **when** the person creates an account then restores, **then** only the paid entitlement may be restored. The old profile, follows, contribution attribution and gifts do not return. No cleanup-pending gate delays registration or restoration; ordinary provider errors and manual assistance cover concurrent cleanup and return under F05. Qualify Apple and Google separately, with the relevant offers, notifications, historical associations and actual transfer effects; a customer sample does not constitute complete reconciliation. (FR-022, FR-032)
6. **A22** — **Given** assisted recovery, **when** the owner verifies the recipient account, the purchase, its validity and corroborating ownership evidence, **then** they may perform the compatible operation, check both assignments and communicate the outcome. The request reference, reason, decision and outcomes are retained in the private process. (FR-023–FR-025)
7. **A23** — **Given** a declared email, an isolated transaction number, an uncorroborated screenshot, an uncertain recipient or contradictory evidence, **when** the request is examined, **then** no automatic transfer or gift occurs. The response specifies what is missing or the refusal reason without disclosing another customer’s information; additional evidence may be submitted. (FR-023, FR-024, FR-034)
8. **A24** — **Given** a manual transfer that could affect an independent entitlement or whose outcome is uncertain, **when** the owner intervenes, **then** they do not bypass guarantees: they verify the scope, contact the provider if necessary and reread the result before repetition. A later restoration by the paying store account may transfer the purchase again; the manual transfer has not changed that paying account. (FR-020, FR-025, FR-026)

### User Story 5 — Grant and withdraw a gifted benefit without changing billing (Priority: P2)

As the owner, I want to gift Pro to an identified account, set its validity and revoke it from RevenueCat, without an additional Blockout screen.

**Why this priority**: Gift administration follows correct existing entitlements, purchase and restoration. The accepted capability remains required at V2 launch.

**Independent Test**: Review and exercise the supported gift procedure, observe representative validity changes and paid/gift coexistence through each native SDK, and reuse transfer/deletion evidence from its owning qualification. Test application unknown/error/expired/gift variants deterministically.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A25** — **Given** the owner in RevenueCat and an unambiguously identified beneficiary, **when** they grant Pro with or without an end date, **then** its visibility follows the SDK refresh and its known validity is identifiable. An address alone is insufficient to choose between accounts; a Blockout moderation role does not authorize the operation. (FR-027, FR-029)
2. **A26** — **Given** a temporary benefit, **when** its end date is reached, **then** that entitlement stops granting Pro under SDK expiry behavior, without a Blockout extension. An explicit change or extension produces identifiable final validity, without implicitly adding durations. A failed or uncertain operation receives no false confirmation and can be checked before retry. (FR-009, FR-027, FR-030)
3. **A27** — **Given** a gift and paid subscription simultaneously, **when** the gift is revoked, **then** the valid subscription continues granting Pro. Its price, billing date and renewal remain unchanged; gifting Pro has suspended no payment and creates no refund. (FR-002, FR-028, FR-030)
4. **A28** — **Given** an open-ended gift, **when** the account is deleted or a purchase transferred elsewhere, **then** the gift stops benefiting the deleted business account or stays with its beneficiary during transfer, as applicable. Failed customer cleanup may leave the old provider gift until manual handling; it is never assigned to the new business UUID. It does not return on recreation and is not presented as a billed subscription. (FR-022, FR-028)
5. **A29** — **Given** a confirmed console change at RevenueCat, **when** the SDK refreshes, **then** the application reflects the supplied state without a Blockout propagation deadline. Existing provider/private intervention evidence identifies the operation; no extra grant ledger is created. (FR-004, FR-007, FR-029–FR-031, FR-034)

### User Story 6 — Recognize my Pro experience without losing sports context (Priority: P2)

As a Pro subscriber or gift recipient, I want a restrained premium treatment in neutral app chrome without losing readable matches or competition colors.

**Why this priority**: Recognizable Pro presentation supports the subscription experience without blocking core sporting consultation.

**Independent Test**: Test all entitlement/session accent variants deterministically; compare representative active and neutral native compositions, both themes and a live appearance transition. Observe current-account accent removal and reliable SDK entitlement change without multiplying every state by every layout and platform.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A30** — **Given** an active Pro right supplied by the SDK, **when** its owner browses neutral screens, **then** subtle gold accents complement black/white foundations in both themes. Match cards and division contexts keep their existing colors. Unknown, expired and free rights do not receive this treatment; logout or account change immediately clears the previous owner's premium styling. (FR-005, FR-035)

### Edge Cases

- Expected expiry with renewal still undetermined does not by itself constitute a confirmed end. A certain end, revocation or the known end date of a gift receives no new interval. (A07–A11)
- SDK cache/expiry behavior is authoritative; no additional freshness or outage clock is maintained. (A06–A10)
- A transaction may be paid without benefits yet established; a successful restoration may contain no active Pro entitlement. (A14, A18)
- An account’s entitlement, the store receipt and historical RevenueCat associations are not the same identity; F09 authorizes no new Auth0 linking. (A04, A19–A24)
- A refund or cancellation record must be interpreted with the effective entitlement, not as an instruction to remove all access. (A03, A11, A27)
- Deleting the business account or provider customer does not cancel the store purchase. F05 adds no cleanup-pending gate; provider failures and concurrent cleanup/return use ordinary errors and manual assistance. (A21)
- Closing the provider interface, losing an offer or changing account while it is open MUST NOT imply a confirmed purchase or entitlement or retain the previous owner's premium styling. (A05, A12–A16, A30)

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

An already known valid Pro entitlement avoids unnecessary advertising age/consent prompts under [F11](../004-advertising-privacy-legal/spec.md#entitlements-and-advertising-choices); privacy information and correction remain accessible. F11 EU27 store distribution does not revoke existing entitlements outside those countries. Offer availability must remain consistent with that distribution before release.

#### Entitlements and states

- **FR-001**: Blockout Pro MUST have a single benefit: the absence of advertising served by Blockout. Its name, existing products, subscriptions and gifts MUST be retained. At V2 launch, the monthly EUR price MUST decrease to 1.99 EUR for new and existing subscribers through the stores’ ordinary pricing and renewal rules; review localized prices, taxes and existing subscriber cohorts before activation. Display the actual localized provider price, not a Blockout-calculated conversion. F14 coordinates activation; cross-store timing is not atomic and no compensation process is added. V1 and V2 MUST use the same existing `default` offering and the same published `Blockout Pro` Paywall in the existing RevenueCat project, with the same products, entitlement and platform keys. Adapt that shared paywall to the V2 ad-free-only benefit with V1 compatibility review; no separate offering, duplicate paywall or new commercial tier is introduced. Existing store prices remain in effect until the explicitly authorized V2 cutover; preparing the shared paywall does not activate the price decrease. The pool map and full calendars of clubs’ upcoming and completed matches MUST be free, including for guests, in all entitlement states; no sporting consultation may require Pro, entitlement verification or a purchase invitation. Account and permission conditions for personal and administrative actions remain unchanged. F03 owns sporting content; F11 owns advertising. Advertising served by external video services is explicitly excluded from the Pro promise.
- **FR-002**: Pro access MUST follow the active entitlement supplied by RevenueCat, accounting for supported paid and promotional grants. Gifts do not postpone or suspend subscription billing. Blockout does not recompute or persist a separate entitlement decision; native transfer scope follows FR-020.
- **FR-003**: The mobile MUST consume RevenueCat SDK entitlement state for the current account, distinguishing active, inactive, unavailable/no state and pending purchase. SDK cache and expiration behavior apply. No backend entitlement projection, separate grant ledger, custom refresh clock or Blockout outage tolerance is required. These states MUST NOT block sporting consultation.
- **FR-004**: When the SDK supplies no entitlement state for the current account, the application MUST suppress advertising and automatic purchase encouragement, including the Pro banner. It MUST NOT infer inactive rights from a transport failure. An existing voluntary Pro entry remains available under FR-017 after current-account association, without a verification destination or mandatory entitlement reread before opening. Ordinary SDK listener/foreground reads continue; source-screen support remains independent of RevenueCat. An inactive state supplied by the SDK, including cached state under its standard behavior, MAY permit advertising under F11 consent, availability and frequency rules. No advertising debt accumulates.
- **FR-005**: Purchase and restoration MUST require authentication and a usable account as defined by F05. Opening Pro as a guest MUST show the shared F05 sign-in invitation with Pro-specific copy, without prices or a provider offer. Signing in continues the ordinary account journey without automatically reopening Pro, purchasing, restoring or retaining purchase intent. Dismissal leaves the guest context unchanged. Guests inherit no previous-account SDK state. Paid, gifted and unknown-rights accounts use the same explicit native paywall entry; Blockout MUST add no automatic purchase or extra repurchase invitation. The configured native journey supplies its supported restoration, management and assistance actions. Its standard UI, including a generic Buy control for active/gifted customers or limited cross-store guidance, is accepted with its actual limitations. Observe and report those limits without a purchase interceptor, custom control suppression, rebuilt provider screen or replacement journey. The independent private F10 support entry remains available.
- **FR-006**: Every entitlement, purchase or restoration response MUST remain attributed to the relevant account and operation. Sign-out, account switching and late responses MUST NOT assign A’s outcome to B or a guest. An already made payment is not inferred to be cancelled from a session change; its recovery follows the account and purchase actually concerned.

#### Freshness, outage and billing

- **FR-007**: RevenueCat SDK refresh and cache behavior MUST govern when entitlement changes become visible. Use its standard integration for sign-in, foreground use, purchase, restoration and provider-managed UI. No Blockout refresh or global propagation deadline applies; sporting consultation does not wait.
- **FR-008**: Purchase and restoration MUST consume the outcome and current-account entitlement state supplied by the RevenueCat SDK. Keep payment success, entitlement activation and unavailable verification distinct. A genuinely unfinished purchase does not authorize blind repurchase. Ordinary SDK purchase/restoration errors keep standard native feedback without duplicate Blockout messages and permit a later explicit SDK action without a dedicated uncertainty mode or confirmation read. No additional server verification endpoint or custom evidence timestamp is required.
- **FR-009**: Standard SDK cache/expiration behavior and FR-004 no-state handling MUST govern outages; no separate Blockout outage-tolerance clock applies.
- **FR-010**: The mobile MUST apply the current-account state supplied by the SDK and reject responses from an earlier account session. Sign-out and accepted deletion immediately invalidate that account’s local rights and private data. The V2 business account identity established by F05 is the RevenueCat customer association, never an email-derived identity. Establish that association only for a usable account; an accepted erasure prevents reassociating its old customer on a later startup or re-entry. During reassociation, only Pro remains unavailable until the current account is confirmed; Blockout navigation and local logout remain usable. Use standard provider transfer, refresh and entitlement ordering, with sequential native identity calls; do not build an independent entitlement projection or recovery system.
- **FR-011**: Effective entitlement validity MUST govern access, not merely the name of a payment event. Disabling renewal preserves the still-valid period; active store grace preserves the entitlement. Expiry, suspension without an active entitlement or effective revocation removes only the relevant entitlement. Store billing grace and SDK cache behavior remain provider-owned; this spec prescribes no change to grace settings.

#### Purchase and subscription consultation

- **FR-012**: The explicitly selected existing `default` offering and its published `Blockout Pro` Paywall MUST present actually available products, localized prices, periods and applicable terms, including any configured trial or renewal. Resolve the configured offering identifier to a real offering without falling back to current or displaying a hardcoded price. Historical observations establish no price, trial, annual offer or sharing setting. No new tier, web purchase or HTTP offering endpoint is introduced.
- **FR-013**: Opening MUST distinguish a resolved explicit offering, no applicable offering and failed pre-presentation resolution/association. A detected pre-presentation failure leaves brief originating-screen feedback and permits a later explicit entry; native loading/rendering errors after mounting keep the SDK’s standard feedback and dismissal behavior without application error translation or recovery; it creates no catalogue Query, empty/error page or recovery route. Opening never starts payment. Purchase or restoration starts only through an explicit SDK action after current-account association, without a required verification read before another ordinary failed action.
- **FR-014**: The journey MUST distinguish cancellation, SDK-reported pending payment, ordinary SDK error, confirmed payment and activated entitlement. Cancellation or waiting grants no new entitlement and removes no independent right. Confirmed payment with activation not yet established remains confirmed; Blockout adds no second payment action or automatic replay, while the provider keeps its standard controls. Ordinary purchase/restoration errors keep standard native feedback without duplicate Blockout messages and a later explicit SDK action, without app-owned Verify/uncertainty states, a refresh-before-action gate or automatic payment replay. An error establishes neither a charge nor its absence. The SDK/store owns concurrency: removing a view creates no cancellation, operation outcome or global purchasing/restoring reservation waiting for a disposed-view callback. Surviving current-account listeners and foreground reads establish subsequent rights, rejecting obsolete owners. A gift does not prove a purchase completed.
- **FR-015**: Activation MUST be announced only after a current-account entitlement is established and the sole ad-free benefit MUST apply without restart. Payment confirmation alone does not establish activation, and activation does not unlock sporting content. Paid and already-owned purchases use standard SDK/store recognition, restoration and available configured assistance. Blockout adds no extra acquisition or custom admission control around the native library; limited provider guidance or visible generic purchase controls remain accepted native limitations. No Blockout verification page or mandatory duplicate read is required.
- **FR-016**: The same Blockout account MUST recover its applicable entitlements on its devices and platforms without repurchasing. Identity associations already retained by F05 remain equivalent; no new Auth0 association or business profile merge is introduced. The paying Apple/Google account remains distinct from the Blockout sign-in method.
- **FR-017**: The profile MUST show “Blockout Pro” before “Privacy” in “My account”, with meaningful distinct icons. Every existing connected Pro entry MUST open the same configured native RevenueCat Paywall directly for free, paid, gifted and unknown-rights accounts, after the useful F05 usable-account prerequisite and successful current SDK association. The existing Pro owner hosts a standard React Native Modal containing only the official Paywall component: no Blockout commercial route, Page/Header, intermediate buttons, recreated provider screen or recovery destination. Closing returns to the originating screen; logout, accepted erasure and account replacement remove the host. Detected pre-presentation association/offering failure gives brief originating-screen feedback; mounted provider loading/error behavior remains native. Existing entry visibility is preserved: only a usable current SDK-inactive account without unresolved purchase facts receives the nonblocking banner above an existing visible bottom tab bar; guests, active/unknown rights and pending/already-owned/paid-but-unverified purchase facts receive no added banner or automatic encouragement. [F13 FR-039](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#functional-requirements) owns its route coverage and single measured bottom reservation. The native configured paywall journey owns localized commercial content and its supported explicit restoration, management/Customer Center and assistance actions; no app restore fallback or direct active-account Customer Center entry remains. Its offer describes only “An ad-free experience”, excluding external video-service ads; Blockout consumes paid/gifted/unknown SDK evidence truthfully without inventing dates or billed renewal. Provider labels, purchase proposals and limited gift/unknown distinctions remain standard native presentation, with observed limitations accepted and reported. Observe and report actual subscribed/gifted content and original-store actions. A generic Buy control may remain for an active account, and Paywall does not expose Customer Center’s Custom Action callback to Blockout: these standard provider limitations are accepted without a custom purchase gate, hidden/disabled native controls, recreated state or substitute. Configure useful supported actions without promising unsupported behavior. Provider configuration changes retain their own authorization. Assistance does not automatically attach receipts or customer payloads; the independent profile F10 entry remains available after provider failure. No new retention questionnaire, retention offer or support channel is added. The separate Privacy row retains the F11 settings sheet and Google UMP where applicable.
- **FR-018**: Billing remains owned by the original store. Use management/Customer Center actions actually supported by the configured native paywall; observe and report original-store routing and any unavailable cross-store guidance as accepted native limitations. Do not add a Blockout management screen, redirect/interceptor, payment editor or refund process to compensate. Gifting Pro, deleting a Blockout account or changing platforms constitutes neither cancellation nor refund. The existing independent F10 support entry and private assisted process remain available.

#### Restoration, transfer and return

- **FR-019**: Use the configured paywall’s standard explicit restoration action independent of starting a purchase, with the supported SDK/store behavior and receipt-wide transfer scope. Preserve that scope in the existing private assistance guidance; add no app restore fallback or confirmation dialog to compensate for limited native copy. Current returned inactive rights, valid restored rights and failure remain distinct in SDK consumption; successful restoration without active entitlement MUST NOT cause a Blockout “Pro active” announcement. Consume returned current CustomerInfo without a second read. SDK-owned failure feedback and another explicit native action need no duplicate Blockout message, Verify state or replay. Neither an empty result nor an error invents inactive rights or replaces independent payment facts.
- **FR-020**: Explicit restoration MUST accept the standard RevenueCat/store transfer scope, which can include all purchases on a receipt. Do not promise transfer purchase by purchase or that other paid purchases on that receipt remain with the old account. No Blockout business profiles are merged; manual gifts are not transferred as store purchases. Actual behavior must be qualified on both stores before launch.
- **FR-021**: The journey MUST NOT promise native restoration of a receipt from another store. When the original Blockout account remains accessible, FR-016 applies; otherwise, the person is directed to verified assisted recovery. Impossible native restoration proves neither absence of a purchase nor an obligation to repurchase.
- **FR-022**: After F05 business deletion, a still-valid store purchase MUST be restorable on a new usable account without recovering old profile data, follows, contribution attribution, personal grants or gifts. No persisted cleanup state or provider-completion polling blocks recreation or restoration. External failures and concurrent provider cleanup/return use ordinary errors and manual assistance; no stronger provider-race guarantee is inferred. Qualify the complete lifecycle separately on Apple and Google.

#### Assisted recovery

- **FR-023**: Assisted recovery MUST use the private F10/F11 process and, for compatible interventions, RevenueCat; at launch, only the owner is authorized to intervene. The recipient account must be authenticated and usable. The operator MUST establish the link between the request, that account, the actual purchase, its current validity and its beneficiary before transferring; a declared address or identifier alone does not constitute evidence.
- **FR-024**: Supplied evidence, such as store documentation and its references, MUST be checked against transaction data and corroborating ownership evidence obtained through the authorized process. An isolated transaction number or uncorroborated screenshot is not automatically sufficient. In case of contradiction, an uncertain recipient or insufficient evidence, no transfer or automatic compensatory gift is authorized; explain the reason or missing elements without disclosing others’ data and allow a completed request. F11 proportionate verification still applies, without systematic identity documents.
- **FR-025**: Before intervention, the owner MUST verify the relevant entitlements and effects on the old and new beneficiary. After intervention, they MUST verify the outcome and record privately the request reference, operator, reason, necessary decision evidence, date, scope and outcome, including uncertain or pending. A reread precedes repetition of an operation with an unknown outcome; the communicated result must not claim completion of what remains pending.
- **FR-026**: Assisted recovery MUST use the provider’s supported scope with verified ownership and an understandable outcome. If necessary evidence or a supported operation is unavailable, report the block and use provider support rather than fabricating success or merging profiles. Standard receipt-wide transfer is accepted under FR-020; no bespoke per-purchase transfer mechanism is required.

#### Gifted benefits and operations

- **FR-027**: The owner MUST manage gifts directly in the RevenueCat console: grant, extend/change using supported operations or revoke an entitlement, with an explicit expiry or no expiry. There is no additional Blockout grant ledger or administration screen. Mobile visibility follows standard SDK refresh and expiration; no immediate cross-device propagation guarantee is imposed.
- **FR-028**: A gift MUST be independent of the beneficiary’s charges, prices, renewals, refunds and other entitlements. It is not automatically converted to a subscription and does not suspend an existing subscription. It stays attached to its beneficiary account on purchase transfer; it grants no benefit to a new business account after deletion unless explicitly assigned anew. Failed provider cleanup may leave it attached to the original customer until manual handling, without copying it to the new UUID. Revoking it does not remove a valid subscription.
- **FR-029**: At launch, manual assignments, changes, revocations and recovery MUST be reserved for the owner in RevenueCat and have an identifiable private record. RevenueCat’s Support role MUST NOT be described as a permission limited to gifts: it has broader powers. Any later delegation requires an explicit decision; no Blockout moderation role or support-record access right implicitly grants these powers.
- **FR-030**: Operator actions MUST distinguish request, confirmed provider result, failure and uncertainty. Inspect the provider result before repeating an uncertain operation; do not arbitrarily extend a gift or remove a valid subscription. The mobile reflects changes on SDK refresh under FR-007, without claiming earlier success.
- **FR-031**: Opening failures use brief originating-screen feedback; payment, restoration and management stay in the configured native paywall journey. SDK-pending payment, confirmed payment without established activation, ordinary failures and blocked interventions MUST remain understandable and addressable under F13 without an app recovery destination or exposure of personal data, receipts or secrets. Native entitlement refresh and provider propagation remain distinct; no human on-call coverage or provider response deadline is guaranteed.
- **FR-032**: Qualification before launch MUST verify offer/entitlement mappings, effective restoration and store notification settings, historical identities and purchase/gift transfer limits. Observing a few customers, a Test Store purchase or a dashboard interface does not prove continuity of all subscriptions. F14 owns full reconciliation and transition; this requirement makes no production change. The F14 common onboarding explains recovery and points to explicit native restoration in the ordinary Pro area after sign-in to the new business identity; it adds no restoration action of its own. Verified manual assistance handles exceptions, including unavailable original-store access; no automatic migration engine is required. Valid gifts need separate preservation because store restoration does not transfer them. This assistance waives neither prior reconciliation nor preservation of indispensable historical references for continuity and erasure, and does not classify a verification outage as subscription loss.
- **FR-033**: Journeys MUST apply F13 for accessibility, language, understandable errors and degraded states, and F05 for a usable account and isolation. Entitlement validation and its availability MUST NOT block public content or support. Qualification evidence must not be presented as behavior already delivered in V1.
- **FR-034**: Payment evidence, account mappings, supporting documents and intervention records MUST remain in processes authorized for their purpose, under F11. Traceability justifies neither public copying of a record, retention of a complete customer after deletion nor unlimited retention. Data requests and backups apply current restrictions before making data available again; an old restored state does not restore a revoked entitlement.
- **FR-035**: An established active Pro right supplied by the SDK MUST give neutral application chrome a restrained black/white/gold appearance in both light and dark themes. This visual recognition MUST NOT gate any capability beyond the sole ad-free benefit. Gold is a subtle accent, not a brown replacement for surfaces or a sporting-division color. Match cards and division-specific contexts MUST retain their light/dark and division treatments. Guest, SDK-inactive, expired and unknown-rights states MUST use the neutral base theme. Account or reliable entitlement changes MUST update the appearance without retaining a previous account's premium styling.

### Key Entities

- **Beneficiary account**: F05 business account to which an entitlement is assigned, distinct from linked identities and the paying store account.
- **Store purchase / subscription**: Transaction and validity periods tied to the original store; their billing is not changed by a gift or Blockout transfer.
- **Paid Pro entitlement**: Advertising exemption from a valid purchase; its transfer transfers neither business profile data nor sporting access.
- **Manual benefit**: Gifted advertising exemption, temporary or open-ended, revocable and independent of paid entitlements; it does not govern sporting access.
- **Entitlement state**: Current-account RevenueCat SDK information, including its standard cached state; no separate Blockout evidence clock or rights ledger.
- **Offer**: Presentation of actually available products and their terms; constitutes neither purchase evidence nor an entitlement.
- **Purchase/restoration operation**: Explicit action whose payment, receipt of the outcome and entitlement establishment may have distinct outcomes.
- **Recovery / intervention record**: Private request, proportionate evidence, verified recipient, decision, scope and outcome; does not replace the F05 account.
