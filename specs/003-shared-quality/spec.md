# Feature Specification: Shared application quality

**Feature Branch**: `develop`

**Created**: 2026-09-17

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline; [plan.md](plan.md) owns technical decisions, [design scope](#evidence-scope) identifies current approved sources and exceptions, and [tasks.md](tasks.md) tracks implementation.

**Input**: F13 shared application quality in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications). Preserve F01/F02 correctness, safe diagnostics, authorization, data integrity and native usability. F14 owns V1 continuity. Server hosting and release tooling, external monitoring and backup policies are outside this scope. Native build qualification and F14 mobile continuity remain applicable.

## User Scenarios & Testing

Visitors and signed-in users view sporting data; authorized users perform actions within their domain. Operators inspect application outcomes and use authorized business recovery actions. Tools and providers are implementation means, not additional business roles.

The scenarios define required application outcomes; implementation and qualification are tracked in [tasks](tasks.md) and [quickstart](quickstart.md). Identifiers are local to this specification. Local evidence does not establish provider or public-release qualification.

### User Story 1 — Consult correct, current authorised data (Priority: P1)

Users consult sporting details, calendars and search with accepted changes reflected
in the relevant views, without restrictions being bypassed or supplier failure being
presented as a sporting deletion.

**Why this priority**: Correct content and understandable failures are required for
ordinary use; no workload, latency benchmark or capacity qualification is required.

**Independent Test**: Accept a correction, consult/refresh its related views,
then exercise a supplier failure and a visibility restriction.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** an application increment, **when** its qualification is prepared, **then** functional correctness and domain timing are checked without a synthetic workload, latency or capacity programme. (FR-001–FR-003)
2. **A02** — **Given** an accepted sporting correction, **when** affected views are consulted/refreshed and derived projection processing completes, **then** they use the accepted state rather than restoring an older version. Current visibility and permissions apply even before a projection catches up; no continuous screen refresh or global propagation stopwatch is required. (FR-004, FR-005)
3. **A03** — **Given** an unavailable supplier and retained consultable data, **when** a user opens its details or calendar, **then** the accepted data remains usable under F02 with honest freshness. The supplier failure is visible to operations, not interpreted as sporting absence or successful integration. (FR-005, FR-008)

### User Story 2 — Understand application failures (Priority: P1)

As an operator, I want useful application diagnostics to distinguish accepted results, incomplete work and failures without exposing private content.

**Why this priority**: A failed or missing observation must not be presented as a successful business outcome.

**Independent Test**: Exercise bounded application failures and inspect safe structured diagnostics and the ordinary recovery actions of the affected domain.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A04** — **Given** application diagnostics, **when** an observation is absent or incomplete, **then** no successful execution or healthy business outcome is inferred. (FR-006, FR-007, FR-010)
2. **A05** — **Given** a failed application or database health check, **when** its result is read, **then** only minimal readiness is public and private diagnostic details remain protected. (FR-010, FR-013)
3. **A06** — **Given** a failed acquisition scope that is later paused, **when** its application state is inspected, **then** pause does not prove recovery; an acquisition success alone does not prove a failed integration recovered. (FR-005, FR-010)
4. **A07** — **Given** partial integration or a refused retry, **when** the operator views diagnostics and ordinary instructions, **then** successful elements, failed elements, affected scope, reason and authorized business action remain distinguishable. Useful information exposes no secret, personal content or raw provider document. Only the scoped original-target references for private F05 manual cleanup are permitted under FR-013; they never appear in public output. (FR-010, FR-011, FR-013, FR-014)

### User Story 3 — Preserve rights and action effects (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want failures and retries neither to give another account access to my data nor to allow unauthorized actions, and not to duplicate an already accepted action.

**Why this priority**: Cached information never replaces current authorisation decisions.

**Independent Test**: Submit authorized and prohibited requests, change accounts during a deferred response, then replay an operation whose confirmation was lost.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A08** — **Given** a visitor, an account without the required right or a request concerning another account’s data, **when** a protected action is requested directly or from an old interface, **then** it is refused without a prohibited effect. Absent or unusable entitlement evidence grants no implicit right; still-valid evidence and tolerances explicitly defined by F05/F09 remain applicable. (FR-015, FR-017, FR-018)
2. **A09** — **Given** a still-pending response intended for account A, **when** the user signs out or uses account B, **then** this response does not populate session B and account A’s private data or rights do not remain usable by B. (FR-016, FR-018)
3. **A10** — **Given** an ordinary accepted action whose response was lost, **when** its result is reread or a safe authorized retry occurs, **then** ordinary duplicate effects are prevented and uncertainty is not called cancellation. Apply domain-specific exceptional delivery limits, including manual resolution of a rare duplicate F10 issue. No generic recovery protocol is an acceptance condition. (FR-008, FR-019)

### User Story 4 — Recover complete state after interrupted processing (Priority: P1)

As an operator, I want to rerun processing or rebuild derived data without losing correct results or bringing obsolete state back into use.

**Why this priority**: Partially rebuilt search must not be presented as a complete empty result.

**Independent Test**: Interrupt integration and rebuilding at several stages, replay observations and compare retained data, identities and final visibility.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A11** — **Given** accepted partial results and a visible failure, **when** the next normal collection, standard rerun or authorized manual correction occurs, **then** accepted data and identities are preserved, current restrictions apply and completion is reported honestly. Resuming the exact interrupted step is not required. (FR-019, FR-021)
2. **A12** — **Given** usable search and an interrupted rebuild, **when** derived data has been partially prepared, **then** it does not replace usable results with an empty or incomplete state presented as complete. Currently hidden or prohibited data is not re-exposed through retained results. Without usable prior state, unavailability is explicit rather than a false “no results”. (FR-020)
3. **A13** — **Given** an old observation or work interrupted before a division restriction, **when** it is replayed, **then** it does not overwrite newer accepted state or bypass current restrictions. Rebuilding alone does not re-emit processed announcements; F07 accepts an exceptional duplicate if restoration lost match-level facts. (FR-019–FR-021)

### User Story 5 — Reuse data without reviving prohibited state (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want retained or reconstructed data to respect current rights, privacy and domain expiry.

**Why this priority**: An older value cannot restore an erased account, revoked right or expired notification.

**Independent Test**: Introduce retained data predating an erasure, rights change or notification purge and verify current restrictions before reads or sends are allowed.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A17** — **Given** retained data predating applicable erasure, rights changes or notification purge, **when** it is considered for use, **then** current obligations apply before affected reads or sends. Missing evidence leaves affected capabilities unavailable. (FR-027; F05/F09/F11 and F07 A51)
2. **A18** — **Given** an application version incompatible with current schema or data, **when** its use is considered, **then** incompatibility prevents unsafe use. Replacing application code does not reverse accepted data changes. (FR-028)

As an iOS or Android user, I want the approved Blockout interface in my phone’s light or dark appearance, with French content, accessible actions and understandable waiting or error states.

FR-039 and the scenarios below own the approved shared presentation; [F03](../007-sporting-consultation/spec.md) owns sporting facts and the single team-follow action. [Evidence scope](#evidence-scope) records the prototype approval and remaining design/native stages.

2. **A20** — **Given** enlarged text or reduced motion enabled on either platform, **when** the user follows representative journeys within FR-030's qualification scope, **then** actions have an understandable name, role and state; essential information remains readable and errors identifiable without depending on color or animation alone. Ordinary form keyboard/focus and final actions remain usable. Image selection, replacement and removal use the same control in profile, sporting and support forms while preserving their distinct rules. (FR-030, FR-033)
3. **A21** — **Given** lost connectivity or failed loading, **when** the user views data or submits an action, **then** they can leave the waiting state and distinguish retained data, absent data, error and an uncertain outcome. A retry respects current rights and does not repeat indefinitely. No new offline editing capability is implicit. (FR-008, FR-016, FR-019, FR-031)
4. **A23** — **Given** an approved Figma component or screen and its corresponding content, state, theme and reference width, **when** the native interface is compared with that source, **then** its dimensions, spacing, Outfit typography, colors, variants and useful composition relationships follow the approved design, with equivalent actions and states. Any necessary native adaptation is explained and qualified; a material design change follows the existing approval process, and accessibility is not silently weakened. (FR-030, FR-034)

No theme selector or saved appearance preference exists; sporting identity and current Pro eligibility remain independent. (FR-035; F02/F09)

### User Story 7 — Preserve logo associations during transition (Priority: P1)

As an operator who has associated club logos, I want the authorized reset not to destroy this manual work.

**Why this priority**: A file without its certain club association is not usable continuity evidence.

**Independent Test**: Examine a controlled recovery set with an identified club, an ambiguous association and a missing file, according to F02 rules and the transition prerequisites handed to F14.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A22** — **Given** associations between V1 club references and logo URLs, **when** their protection and recovery are verified for F14, **then** each expected association and file is retained or explicitly flagged as missing. A storage URL alone does not prove retained bytes and associations. (FR-024, FR-032; F02 FR-002, FR-043, FR-046)

### Edge Cases

- Traffic anecdotes are historical context, not workload requirements or capacity evidence.
- A fast error, hidden data served from a cache or obsolete information presented as fresh does not satisfy a quality objective.
- Timely collection, successful integration and propagation to views remain three distinct observations.
- Missing diagnostics do not establish successful execution or current business state.
- Retained or reconstructed values may contain rights or data that have become prohibited. Current restrictions precede making them usable again.
- An old club absent from rebuilt competitions retains its logo association in recovery material; that association alone does not create a new viewable club.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

#### Functional quality and freshness

- **FR-001**: No concurrency or action-rate target is retained in the current scope or deferred roadmap.
- **FR-002**: No load-test, benchmark or capacity-study protocol is required for this scope or release.
- **FR-003**: No numerical API latency or percentile acceptance target is retained. Ordinary correctness and bounded user waits remain required by FR-008.
- **FR-004**: Accepted sporting changes MUST update their affected views and derived projections. New consultation/refresh MUST respect current permissions and visibility; a stale projection never authorises disclosure. Keep freshness honest and update failed projections through normal processing/manual correction. No global numerical propagation objective or continuous screen refresh is required.
- **FR-005**: Distinguish supplier observation, acquisition, accepted integration and completed projection. Success at one step MUST NOT prove the next; failed or pending integration/projection remains diagnosable. F01 collection timing and domain-owned expiry/freshness rules remain applicable without a global performance qualification.

#### Application diagnostics and bounded failures

- **FR-006**: Application diagnostics MUST distinguish observed results from missing or uncertain outcomes. This scope defines no hosted availability objective or accounting.
- **FR-007**: Missing diagnostics MUST NOT be represented as successful execution. No observation history or reporting service is required by this specification.
- **FR-008**: Isolate failures to the capabilities that actually depend on them. Retained viewable data MUST remain usable without claiming a failed refresh succeeded. Keep ordinary loading, empty, failure and retry outcomes understandable. Internal distinctions do not require separate screens: F06 follows and F07 reads reconcile discreetly, F10 support permits explicit retry after an ordinary send error without a receipt lookup, and F03 PDF opening uses an ordinary error. Preserve domain-specific safeguards for purchases, erasure and moderation. Bound waits and attempts; never invent success or block navigation indefinitely.

- **FR-010**: Application state and diagnostics MUST identify the useful scope, observation time, outcome, latest success and available partial-success counts. Never run, awaiting eligibility, paused, in progress, completed and failed states remain distinguishable. An unconfirmed outcome MUST NOT be presented as success. F05 cleanup diagnostics do not guarantee an exhaustive record across process interruption.
- **FR-011**: Manual business recovery MUST require the applicable domain permission and current resource conditions. An operator action or a cleared technical error does not by itself prove a recovered business outcome.

- **FR-013**: Diagnostics MUST link application failure stages and scope through technical references, times, outcome categories and safe actionable reasons. Exclude secrets, tokens, personal content, attachments and raw provider documents. Only the original account/provider references and minimum verification evidence needed for F05 manual cleanup may appear in private logs under F11 FR-027. No personal target reference appears in metric labels, public artifacts or user errors. User-facing errors offer a usable next step without internal details. This specification sets no hosted log-retention period.
- **FR-014**: The operator MUST have concise usable instructions for implemented business actions: understanding diagnostic outcomes, manually resolving important incomplete actions and rebuilding derived data. State permissions and prerequisites and verify the actual result; no generic recovery platform or exhaustive hypothetical-failure runbook is required.

#### Security and recovery of effects

- **FR-015**: Every private read or protected action MUST check applicable rights for the account, action and resource independently of the interface. An absent permission or unusable evidence MUST NOT grant implicit rights; still-valid evidence and bounded tolerances remain defined by F05/F09. Operational and administrative permissions belong to F12 and each domain. F12 FR-015–FR-018 requires a complete check at every cold start, with explicit retry after failure and no persisted access-configuration fallback. Foreground/authentication returns, partial administrative reads and changes do not establish a new successful startup check; current-process restrictions and revisions remain protected. A refusal MUST leave data unchanged.
- **FR-016**: Sign-out or an account change MUST prevent private data, rights and deferred responses from the previous session from being exposed or applied to the next. Retrying after sign-in MUST use the current authorized context; no local retention replaces F05 identity and session rules.
- **FR-017**: Secrets, private data and authorized recovery actions MUST remain accessible only to authorized people or operations, and sensitive exchanges MUST be protected. An unauthorized or abusive request MUST NOT bypass rights or trigger unbounded work. Application protection limits must satisfy their actual domain need without imposing a hosting provider or expanding business roles.
- **FR-018**: Qualification MUST include refusals without effects for a visitor, an account without permission, another owner, an invalidated session and a response from an old session. Evidence MUST distinguish effective operation protection from merely hiding the interface. F05 owns the accepted identity-provider session and account-return limits; this qualification adds neither stronger revocation guarantees nor a separate session-management requirement. Revocation, deletion, Pro tolerance and identity rules remain those of F05/F09/F11; their specific validation cannot be replaced by a generic sign-in check.
- **FR-019**: Ordinary repeated submissions, authorized retries and reconstruction MUST preserve business identity, current permissions and accepted data without unintended duplicate effects or stale overwrite. Domain-specific delivery limits apply, including F07 system-delivery limits and F10 exceptional duplicate issues resolved manually. A lost response is not proof of cancellation. Ordinary explicit retries may repeat an attempt; existing expected revisions, unique constraints, desired-state commands and domain confirmation protect actual effects. Specialized irreversible provider operations retain their supported evidence/manual handling. No universal operation receipt, retry ledger or automatic recovery protocol is required for every command. F10 explicitly permits a new attempt without mobile receipt lookup, accepting rare duplicate issues for manual closure. F05 specifically accepts ordinary provider errors/manual handling for simultaneous cleanup and return; original-business-UUID targeting and private-data isolation remain required.
- **FR-020**: Rebuilding derived data MUST use authoritative data and preserve usable prior results until complete replacement is established. A partial or failed rebuild MUST NOT publish an empty or incomplete result as complete. Without usable prior state, unavailability MUST be explicit. In every case, current visibility and entitlement restrictions MUST apply; no indexing or storage mechanism is chosen.
- **FR-021**: Partial work MUST retain accepted successes and expose useful failures or missing results without claiming completion. The next ordinary collection, a standard rerun or authorized manual intervention may complete the relevant work; fine-grained crash resumption and a generic recovery engine are not required. F01/F02 eligibility, authority and current restrictions still apply. Rebuilding derivatives MUST NOT alter authoritative data to fit an expected result.

#### Retained data and current restrictions

- **FR-024**: Accepted image bytes and their owning association MUST remain consistent under the owning feature rules. A stored URL alone does not establish usable bytes. F10 private support cases and native attachments remain provider-owned under F10; F14 owns preservation of V1 club logos before any reset. This requirement selects no backup mechanism or retention policy.

- **FR-027**: Before retained or reconstructed data becomes usable, apply current erasure, identity, rights and privacy obligations under F05/F09/F11 and F07 expiry/announcement rules. Keep affected reads or sends unavailable when those obligations cannot be established. This requirement does not prescribe infrastructure restoration, automatic reconstruction of interrupted operations or an effect journal.
- **FR-028**: Application code MUST be compatible with the schema and data it uses. Replacing code MUST NOT be presented as reversing data changes or permit incompatible access. V1 continuity and transition conditions remain owned by F14.

#### Platforms, accessibility and bounded continuity

- **FR-029**: The shared foundation MUST cover supported iOS and Android phones and Blockout content in French. Official names and source content remain faithful to their source; date, time and day semantics belong to F02/F03. No web application, dedicated tablet experience or additional translation is required. The actual matrix of qualified versions and devices MUST be declared. The supported application minimums MUST be iOS 16.4 and Android 10 (API 29). Test these application minimums and the latest stable runtime available in the supported simulator toolchain. If an application minimum cannot run in the current simulator toolchain, test the oldest compatible simulator runtime available instead and identify the actual version; this substitution does not establish qualification of the untested application floor or block initial control adoption. F12 governs minimum versions and update journeys.
- **FR-030**: Essential actions and information MUST be usable with system text enlargement and reduced motion on iOS and Android. Controls MUST present an understandable name, role and state, a touch target of at least 44 logical points, coherent content order and usable ordinary form keyboard/focus behavior; essential text MUST NOT be truncated in a way that prevents action. User-facing text MUST stay concise: omit redundant wording and context already supplied by the same section or active selection; separators combine complementary facts while preserving identity, result uncertainty and actionable validation or error information. An error, selection or essential information MUST NOT depend solely on color, an unexplained gesture or animation. Contrast and states must support these accessible interactions; a specification does not establish certification.

  Dedicated VoiceOver and TalkBack qualification campaigns are outside the verification scope: reader-enabled journeys, reader-specific gestures, reader cursor/focus navigation and spoken-announcement checks are not required. This applies to current and future tasks, including after launch; only an explicit human request may reintroduce these campaigns. Full compatibility with these screen readers is not guaranteed. This exclusion authorizes no removal of existing accessibility labels, roles, states, hints, announcements, native settings/adaptations or useful automated control tests. Ordinary form keyboard/focus, readability, enlarged text, reduced motion, touch targets and reachable actions remain required; ordinary field focus is distinct from screen-reader cursor navigation.

- **FR-031**: Journeys MUST retain consistent loading, absence, retained-data, error, refusal, action-in-progress and retry states with usable navigation. Retained information remains subject to current rights. Disconnection MUST NOT become an empty list or successful modification. Ordinary mobile business reads MAY use supported library retries and stale-query refresh. Writes MUST NOT retry automatically or queue for reconnection; recovery is an explicit new action. Owner-approved navigation/foreground refreshes and standard identity-token renewal remain distinct and do not authorize replay of an uncertain mutation. This mobile-request rule does not disable required F01/F07 backend processing retries or F09 provider-owned entitlement/cache behavior. No complete offline mode, offline action queue or continuous result stream is required; F03–F10 own their specific journeys.
- **FR-032**: F14 MUST have evidence of retained club-logo associations and usable files before any reset affecting them, explicitly distinguishing reapplied associations, associations awaiting a match and missing files. F02 identity rules prohibit automatic matching solely by name. No application test or storage URL alone authorizes deliberate loss of protected information.
- **FR-033**: In-app image selection, preview, replacement and removal MUST use one shared control across profile photos, sporting logos and support attachments. Labels, single/multiple selection, validation rules and removal versus automatic-logo restoration remain owned by the relevant feature. Absent images use the same neutral image glyph in their existing media slots throughout Blockout, including sporting logos, account avatars and image fields. Do not substitute initials, a crossed-out image or an empty image slot. Keep loaded artwork, media skeletons and owner-controlled read/error recovery. Profile, Team, Pool and Club identity cards MUST share the same media rail, sharp image slot, content spacing and rounded surface. Their decorative background uses the same authorized current photo or logo, centered, cropped to fill and blurred within the rail. The sharp foreground and decorative background have independent native loading/error presentation: a decorative failure leaves a usable foreground visible, and an unavailable foreground uses the shared fallback without hiding known identity.
