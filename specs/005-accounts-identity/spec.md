## User Scenarios & Testing

The actors are the visitor, the authenticated person, the account holder and authorized personnel handling a support request or failed external cleanup. Possession of an email address or a claimed identifier does not prove account ownership. A moderation permission grants neither access to another person’s profile nor manual Pro benefits. F12 assigns permissions; each personal action remains limited to its owner.

The scenarios describe a future implementation, not tests already executed. A, FR and SC references are local to F05. Qualification accounts and evidence remain private, without personal identifiers in public deliverables.

### User Story 1 — Access the product and recover my account (Priority: P1)

As a visitor or account holder, I want to browse public content and recover my account using my usual method, without a mandatory profile form or unexpected linking.

**Why this priority**: Identity determines personal actions and entitlement continuity; a sign-in failure must not close public content or support.

**Independent Test**: Exercise first access, both selected providers, an already linked identity, two principals sharing an address and missing provider information, with and without an existing business profile.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** an installation with no signed-in account, **when** the person chooses guest access or skips onboarding, **then** public content, legal documents and support remain accessible; no personal action or Pro benefit from the previous account is granted. An account-only action offers sign-in without being implicitly executed. F12 global gates suppress in-app legal, support and sign-in entries, including direct routes. Maintenance administration requires an already signed-in usable operator; a disconnected or expired operator cannot reconnect from that gate. (FR-001, FR-002)
2. **A02** — **Given** onboarding is presented, **when** the person completes it, skips it or refuses notifications, **then** they can continue. V2 completion is retained on the installation and is not reset by sign-out or business-profile recreation. F14 presents this common discovery once on first V2 use, including installations that completed V1 onboarding, without a separate migration welcome. (FR-002)
3. **A03** — **Given** a first Google or Apple sign-in and a usable provider address, **when** the identity is authenticated, **then** a single usable profile is created automatically, with a neutral valid username and the default avatar, without retrieving a provider photo; no mandatory additional form is requested. Two concurrent retries for the same identity find the same account. (FR-003, FR-004, FR-012, FR-013)
4. **A04** — **Given** an Auth0 principal with Google and Apple identities already linked, even with different addresses, **when** the person signs in with each, before or after business profile recreation, **then** they find the same account and its applicable entitlements under the F09/F14 restoration and continuity rules. A secondary identity or historical purchase identifier does not constitute a new business account. On transition to V2, explicit F14 reauthentication resets neither this continuity nor account age. (FR-005, FR-010, FR-023)
5. **A05** — **Given** two distinct, unlinked Auth0 principals supplying the same usable email, **when** each explicitly establishes its profile, **then** two distinct business accounts are allowed, including case differences in the email. Neither profile is merged or disclosed to the other. Concurrent creation for one principal still produces only one account. (FR-004, FR-006, FR-007)
6. **A06** — **Given** two unlinked identities using distinct addresses, including an Apple private address, **when** they are used, **then** no personal equivalence is inferred; each eligible account remains distinct. Two populated accounts are neither merged nor emptied by a support request or purchase restoration. (FR-004, FR-006, FR-007, FR-036)
7. **A07** — **Given** first creation without a usable address or with failed provider retrieval, **when** sign-in reaches profile creation, **then** creation remains suspended, without a fabricated address or an additional verification form. Retry, support and guest access are offered. No incomplete business profile is announced as ready. (FR-008, FR-017, FR-018)
8. **A08** — **Given** a recognized identity with an existing profile, **when** provider email is temporarily missing, **then** the reliable stored value remains. A usable updated email may also belong to another principal: it is synchronized without merging accounts. Loading failure remains an unavailable state. (FR-007–FR-009, FR-018)

### User Story 2 — Use and customize a minimal profile (Priority: P2)

As an account holder, I want a useful profile without unnecessarily copying my personal information, and to change my username or photo without loss on failure.

**Why this priority**: Optional profile customization follows usable account access. Minimal automatic profile creation and privacy remain prerequisites under User Story 1.

**Independent Test**: Create a profile, edit its fields separately, trigger a username conflict and a photo failure, then sign in again with the identity.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A09** — **Given** a provider also returning last name, first name and phone number, **when** the business profile is created or synchronized, **then** only username, email and photo constitute its personal presentation and contact information. References required for identity, entitlements and lifecycle remain separate; no deletion of the Google/Apple profile is inferred from this minimization. (FR-011, FR-012)
2. **A10** — **Given** username candidates that are too short, too long, contain forbidden characters or are already used, **when** a username is generated, **then** the result follows the same rules as manual input. When two people simultaneously request the same username regardless of letter case, at most one obtains it; the other keeps their old username and can correct it, or receives another valid username during automatic creation. (FR-012, FR-013)
3. **A11** — **Given** a customized profile, **when** the holder changes only their username and then signs in again, **then** the photo and customized username are preserved except for the requested change. An invalid or conflicting username is rejected without changing the profile. An attempt to edit, privately read or delete another person’s account is refused. (FR-004, FR-013, FR-014, FR-016)
4. **A12** — **Given** an old photo, **when** replacement fails or the file is rejected, **then** the previous photo remains available. Selection includes square crop and preview; cancelling preserves the previous choice, and a source requiring adaptation is checked against the upload rules after preparation. Successful explicit deletion removes the photo; a missing photo does not prevent account use. If the outcome of saving is uncertain, a reread establishes the state before announcing success or repeating the operation. (FR-014, FR-015)
5. **A33** — **Given** a usable signed-in account, **when** its holder opens the Profile tab, **then** “Mon profil” presents the accepted identity and grouped entries without editable fields or a save action. Its edit action opens the separate “Modifier mon profil” screen. An accepted save returns to the overview with the confirmed identity; cancellation/back returns without saving and applies the existing dirty-draft discard confirmation. Neither cancellation nor back signs out. (FR-014, FR-020, FR-040)
6. **A34** — **Given** guest access, an unavailable account read, uncertain Pro state or a session change during profile navigation, **when** the overview or editor is displayed, **then** the initial account and existing-photo reads use one whole-page skeleton until the useful content is ready. An initial failure or unusable session replaces the whole body with one owner-controlled recovery state; public consultation remains reachable through ordinary navigation outside F12 blocking. After admission, technical refresh failures retain useful content and the editor draft with scoped feedback; a refused current account removes the overview while an already admitted editor keeps its locked draft. No old identity, draft or command enters the new session. Sign-out is explicitly named and uses the existing session cleanup; unavailable account data cannot open editing or grant administration. (FR-016–FR-023, FR-039, FR-040)

### User Story 3 — Control my session without mixing accounts (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to recover after an outage, sign out and switch accounts without exposing my data or assigning my entitlements to someone else.

**Why this priority**: The sign-in outcome, business data loading and entitlement establishment are distinct; confusing them exposes information or unnecessarily blocks access.

**Independent Test**: Vary authentication, profile and entitlement loading, then switch accounts with delayed responses and a failed provider session closure.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A13** — **Given** a cancelled, failed or successful hosted login, **when** the person returns, **then** only actual success establishes authentication. The same “Se connecter” entry opens Google/Apple in production and email/password with native signup in preproduction. The database connection cannot authenticate the production application; preproduction cannot delete a social or cross-connection principal. Guest access and support remain available outside global gates. (FR-003, FR-017, FR-038, FR-039)
2. **A14** — **Given** an authenticated identity but an unavailable Blockout profile, **when** loading fails, **then** the state indicates what is missing and allows retry without requiring new authentication that is still valid. Public content remains accessible, personal actions are suspended and data from a previous account is not used. (FR-017, FR-018, FR-021)
3. **A15** — **Given** an established session, **when** a network outage occurs, **then** it is not treated as revocation and no personal write is announced as accepted without confirmation. **When** nonrenewable expiry or revocation is confirmed, **then** protected actions require reauthentication without preventing public consultation. (FR-019, FR-039)
4. **A16** — **Given** a signed-in account, **when** the person signs out and browser session closure fails, **then** the local session and its personal data are removed despite that failure. The application allows free guest access; it does not claim to sign out the global Google/Apple account or other devices. (FR-020, FR-021, FR-023)
5. **A17** — **Given** account A with profile, follow, notification or entitlement requests in progress, **when** the person switches to account B or guest mode and A’s responses then arrive, **then** none populates the new session. A personal operation not yet accepted does not execute under B. (FR-021, FR-023)
6. **A18** — **Given** a device associated with A, **when** sign-out disassociation fails, **then** local sign-out still completes without a persistent retry. Old-account system pushes may continue until the association is corrected, but cannot expose A’s private data in guest/B context; an old removal cannot remove B’s association. Erasure removes A’s destinations server-side. (FR-022)
7. **A19** — **Given** a signed-in account without usable entitlement evidence, **when** verification fails, **then** all public sporting consultation, including pool maps and full club calendars, remains accessible without advertising or an invitation to repurchase. Retry is possible and waiting never establishes a free or active Pro status. Account switching preserves isolation under FR-023. (FR-023)

### User Story 4 — Preserve account age faithful to my account (Priority: P1)

As an existing account holder, I want business profile recreation not to lose my account age for actions that depend on it.

**Why this priority**: Account age governs contribution eligibility under F08; replacing business storage must not reset that evidence or bypass the accepted age requirement.

**Independent Test**: Compare account-age evidence for a single identity, already linked identities and an account recreated after voluntary deletion, with the provider available and then unavailable.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A20** — **Given** an Auth0 principal existing for more than seven days and a business profile created today, **when** account age is requested, **then** the evidence corresponds to the retained principal, regardless of which already linked method is used. F08 applies its threshold and other conditions; no person’s age is inferred. (FR-024)
2. **A21** — **Given** a known date reliably attributed to the principal, **when** the provider is temporarily unavailable, **then** this evidence remains usable. Without a reliable date, no business date or arbitrary value replaces it; publication subject to account age remains suspended with retry, while other authorized functions remain accessible. The moderation exception depends on an established permission, not an invented date. (FR-024, FR-025)
3. **A22** — **Given** deletion followed by explicit registration, **when** account age is assessed, **then** it uses the actual current Auth0 principal creation date. Successful identity deletion normally gives a new date; failed identity deletion can preserve the old date and seven-day eligibility. No old business data, personal permission or gift returns. A reinstall preserves the existing principal date. (FR-024, FR-035)

### User Story 5 — Delete my account and return without recovering my old data (Priority: P1)

As an account holder, I want to delete my account, return directly to guest access and later recover a valid purchase without reactivating my old data.

**Why this priority**: Blockout must control removal of its business data while keeping external cleanup and operator handling proportionate.

**Independent Test**: Delete with and without a subscription; simulate a local failure, a lost response and provider failures; verify guest return, manual diagnostics, fresh business registration and native purchase restoration on each store.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A23** — **Given** an authenticated holder, possibly subscribed, **when** they open deletion, **then** an ordinary confirmation screen opened from the profile briefly explains irreversible personal-data removal and that any Pro subscription must be managed in its App Store or Google Play. There is no subscription-management action in this screen; a checkbox and explicit deletion action confirm intent. Cancelling preserves the account. Confirmation precedes deletion; sign-out must not prevent the authenticated operation. (FR-026, FR-027)
2. **A24** — **Given** a confirmed deletion, **when** the local transaction definitively fails, **then** no external deletion starts and the account and session remain usable. A lost response requires reading the original account before any explicit retry; if that read also fails, local sign-out remains possible with an ordinary error and no claim of confirmed deletion. (FR-027, FR-031)
3. **A25** — **Given** committed business deletion, **when** an external cleanup fails, **then** local deletion remains effective, other known providers are still attempted and the user returns directly to guest home. A private diagnostic and native operational alert support manual repair; no user-facing progress screen or success toast is added. A process interruption before logging may leave external cleanup unreported. (FR-027–FR-029, FR-031, FR-032)
4. **A26** — **Given** a logged external failure, **when** an authorized operator handles it, **then** they verify the original targets and current situation with existing tools before a destructive action. No business rollback, scheduled retry, deletion ledger or automatic replay is performed. A repeated request for the absent original business account has no provider effects. (FR-028, FR-031, FR-038)
5. **A27** — **Given** a deleted account that published a visible link and a hidden link, **when** its data is processed, **then** useful links may remain without association to the account; the hidden link remains hidden. A recreated account does not recover their ownership. Personal data in the content itself falls under an applicable F11 request, without a promise of total anonymization. (FR-030, FR-035, FR-038)
6. **A28** — **Given** business deletion and a successful provider acknowledgment, **when** the person signs in again explicitly, **then** a new business account may be created without polling for physical provider erasure. External cleanup failure also creates no pending-account block. Concurrent return during an in-flight provider deletion has no special coordination; ordinary sign-in errors and manual assistance apply. (FR-032, FR-033)
7. **A29** — **Given** a new business account, **when** a request still names the deleted original business account, **then** it cannot target the replacement account. Manual external cleanup verifies its original references and current use; restoring a backup reapplies current restrictions before affected data becomes accessible. No broader guarantee for concurrent provider deletion and return is inferred. (FR-031, FR-033–FR-035)
8. **A30** — **Given** a deleted business account and a still-valid store subscription, **when** the person creates a new account then restores the purchase with the store account that paid, **then** paid entitlements can be restored without the old profile, follows, contribution attribution or manual benefit. The Google/Apple identity used to sign in to Blockout need not be the one used by the store. Apple and Google journeys are qualified separately. (FR-029, FR-035, FR-036)
9. **A31** — **Given** a purchase associated with another Blockout account, **when** native restoration is explicitly requested, **then** the standard RevenueCat/store transfer scope applies, potentially to the full receipt, without merging Blockout profiles. Qualification establishes actual effects on both accounts. Cross-store impossibility leads to verified F09 assistance, never assignment by email. (FR-036)
10. **A32** — **Given** a person without the application installed or no longer able to sign in, **when** they follow the public deletion link, **then** they find a procedure and contact for requesting erasure without reinstalling the application. Proportionate ownership verification precedes action on the account; the reply address alone does not authorize deletion. Processing provides the status or result under F11. (FR-037, FR-038)

### Edge Cases

- Email never identifies or reserves an account. A missing business profile during migration does not invalidate established identity associations. (A04–A08)
- The same identity and username may be submitted repeatedly or concurrently without duplicate creation. Deletion targets the original business account; absence never redirects cleanup to its replacement. (A03, A10, A24–A26)
- Successful authentication, a ready profile and established Pro entitlements are three distinct outcomes; their responses may arrive after sign-out. (A14–A19)
- A missing deletion response may follow committed business removal; a definite local failure and an unknown outcome are not equivalent. (A24)
- A documented provider acknowledgment is sufficient even when external processing is asynchronous. No polling or pending-state gate is added; failures use private diagnostics and manual handling, with an accepted interruption-before-log gap. (A25–A29)
- A surviving Auth0 principal keeps its actual age; it restores neither business data, contribution ownership nor manual benefits. (A22, A27, A29–A30)

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

Advertising age declaration and correction belong to [F11](../004-advertising-privacy-legal/spec.md#entitlements-and-advertising-choices). They do not add a profile field, account age gate or identity check. Guest and signed-in users use the same installation-local advertising controls outside F12 blocking gates.

#### Access and identity resolution

- **FR-001**: The system MUST allow guest access to public content, legal documents and support before sign-in or after failure. Personal actions require a usable identity and account; their sign-in prompt MUST NOT automatically execute a purchase, publication, deletion or other mutation. F12 global maintenance, unavailable-access and mandatory-update gates take precedence over these ordinary journeys: no in-app legal/support/sign-in entry or direct-route bypass is available. Maintenance administration is limited to an already signed-in usable operator with current permissions; the gate offers no authentication entry. Ordinary sign-in failures outside global blocking retain support and legal access.
- **FR-002**: Onboarding MUST be completable or skippable without mandatory account creation or notification permission. Its V2 completion is installation-specific and retained on sign-out and business-profile recreation. Its content presents only capabilities retained by the specs, including F06 follows and actual F01 coverage, without promising unsupported club following. F14 owns first V2 initialization and the common discovery presented on every installation, including a completed V1 installation; no separate transition welcome or V1-origin detection is added.
- **FR-003**: Production sign-in MUST use Google and Apple through Auth0. Both variants use one “Se connecter” entry opening hosted Universal Login in the platform authentication browser; Blockout provides no in-app credential or social-login form. Preproduction offers only email/password with native public signup for anyone able to use that journey; no manual provisioning, separate test-account button or Blockout password form is required. Its dedicated database connection MUST be disabled in production, and social methods disabled for its application. Retain the existing tenant and distinct application registrations; their shared directory is not tenant isolation. Preproduction deletion MUST NOT target a production social principal or a principal linked to another connection. Cancellation, failure and success remain distinct. The owner qualifies Google/Apple using their own accounts in the production variant; preproduction success does not qualify these native journeys.
- **FR-004**: The system MUST resolve the account from the authenticated identity and its established associations, never from a merely declared email or identifier. Concurrent retries and creations for the same identity MUST lead to a single business account. Actions on an account MUST verify its holder or a permission dedicated to their purpose.
- **FR-005**: Already established Google/Apple associations MUST be preserved, even if their addresses differ. Linked methods find the same business account and its applicable entitlements, independently of a secondary identity or historical payment reference.
- **FR-006**: The system MUST NOT create any new Google/Apple association, even with two authenticated identities or the same address. It MUST neither merge nor delete data from two populated accounts to resolve a conflict. Support follows this rule; recovery of a paid entitlement remains a separate operation.
- **FR-007**: Distinct, unlinked Auth0 principals MAY use the same email, including the same address with different letter case. Each principal resolves its own business account. No email uniqueness reservation, conflict refusal, automatic linking or merge is permitted; email alone never proves ownership.
- **FR-008**: First creation MUST have a usable address from the authenticated identity provider; a missing, unusable or unretrieved address suspends creation. The system offers retry, support and guest access without fabricating an address or adding an input/verification form. Creation occurs only through explicit bootstrap after authentication; an ordinary profile read MUST NOT recreate a missing account or contact Auth0. That address alone is not proof of control over another account.
- **FR-009**: Synchronization of a recognized identity MUST NOT erase known information when it is temporarily missing. A usable new provider address may be shared with another principal and is synchronized without merging accounts. The holder retains access to their recognized account, subject to other restrictions; a loading failure follows FR-018. Provider information is retrieved at first creation and explicit sign-in, with targeted retrieval for a missing necessary value; no periodic, every-startup or every-business-request synchronization is required. An email may remain unchanged between explicit logins.
- **FR-010**: Migration business recreation MUST preserve Auth0 principals, existing associations and account age, without an additional profile form. Each V2 account receives a new business identity used for its RevenueCat association. Valid V1 paid entitlements are recovered through explicit native restoration, with verified manual assistance for exceptions, including unavailable original-store access; no migration engine is required. Necessary historical references remain discoverable for continuity and scoped erasure under F14, without exporting full profiles or merging by email. Gifts remain distinct from store-restorable purchases. F14 owns transition evidence and explicit first V2 reauthentication.

#### Profile and authorizations

- **FR-011**: Personal presentation and contact information in the business profile MUST be limited to username, email and optional photo. First name, last name and phone number MUST NOT be copied into this profile. Identity references, account-age evidence and information needed for entitlements, permissions and deletion requests remain possible according to their F11 purpose; this minimization does not erase the Google/Apple profile.
- **FR-012**: The profile MUST be created automatically with an available, valid, neutrally generated username, without an additional form or using email or provider personal presentation as its source. Generation MUST obey the same format and case-insensitive uniqueness rules as manual input. Creation MUST use the approved default avatar without retrieving a provider photo; the holder may customize the profile later.
- **FR-013**: Every username, generated or entered, MUST contain 3 to 32 characters from unaccented letters, digits, dot, hyphen and underscore. Surrounding whitespace is removed before validation; internal spaces are forbidden. Uniqueness ignores letter case and remains guaranteed under concurrency. A refused change preserves the previous profile and indicates how to correct it.
- **FR-014**: The authorized holder MUST be able to change their username and replace or delete their photo. Email is not freely edited in this journey. The single save applies the requested field changes together or preserves the previous profile on refusal. An unchanged field is retained, including a concurrent change to an omitted field; signing in again does not replace customization with the provider value. Success is announced only after the outcome is established; an uncertain response allows a reread without a blind modification.
- **FR-015**: The photo MUST remain optional. The holder selects one image, chooses a square crop and previews it before saving. Local preparation may convert and reduce the chosen image while preserving the library original; it produces a square image at most 1024 pixels per side without enlargement. The uploaded result MUST be PNG or JPEG and at most 5 MiB; the source photo need not already meet that upload bound. Cancellation, invalid output or preparation/upload failure preserves the previous photo. Photo deletion requires explicit intent; omission never deletes it. Do not retain unrelated original metadata. Replaced or removed Blockout files follow applicable erasure.
- **FR-016**: Private reading, editing and deletion of the account MUST be limited to the holder or the support process explicitly authorized for the relevant purpose. A session or moderation role does not grant access to all accounts or allow self-granting a permission. Explicit registration may create a new business account after deletion, without reactivating the deleted one. The current-account read also returns current F12 permissions and remains available for operator identification during maintenance; it neither creates an account nor opens blocked profile navigation or other account commands.

#### Sessions and isolation

- **FR-017**: Guest, sign-in in progress, authenticated identity with unavailable profile, usable account, expired/revoked session and absent account MUST have distinct outcomes. Provider success alone does not announce a ready profile; waiting or error states allow exit or retry instead of endless loading. Explicit recovery after failed authentication restoration may restart guest navigation and discard its unsaved input; ordinary sign-in/sign-out preserves public navigation and input.
- **FR-018**: If the identity is authenticated but the profile unavailable, the system MUST preserve the ability to retry without unnecessary reauthentication while the evidence remains valid. Public content remains accessible; personal actions requiring the profile remain suspended. A synchronization outage justifies neither creating a duplicate nor using the previous profile of another identity.
- **FR-019**: A transient network or provider outage MUST NOT be presented as revocation. An invalid or expired access token cannot authorize protected actions; a confirmed inability to renew requires sign-in when valid access is no longer available. Retain the existing session settings and qualify their current values rather than infer them from historical observations or provider defaults; V2 API token scopes may be revised. The system MUST follow standard Auth0 token validity, without promising that ending a provider session or removing refresh tokens retroactively invalidates every signed access token. Current business-account status and permissions still apply. No write is considered accepted without an established outcome; no offline personal-mutation queue is introduced. The same-subject return limitation is explicit in FR-035.
- **FR-020**: Sign-out MUST invoke native credential clearing and remove local personal information even if browser session closure fails. Native SDK defects in concurrent renewal or credential storage are accepted limitations; no application-specific compensation mechanism is required. The owner separately accepted the reproduced Android image-picker crop-file retention defect on 2026-10-25, as retained in the [native-photo qualification](quickstart.md#native-photo-and-shared-control),, pending an official correction without a manual dependency patch. Its private temporary file may survive local sign-out; it MUST NOT populate the next session’s profile or draft. This narrow filesystem exception does not relax removal of application-owned personal state. Sign-out allows continuing as a guest without claiming to sign out the global Google/Apple account or other devices. Voluntary deletion, in contrast, blocks the targeted business account on all devices. Neither case promises global Google/Apple logout or nonstandard retroactive access-token invalidation; FR-035 defines the accepted return limit.
- **FR-021**: Account switching or entering guest mode MUST isolate profiles, follows, notifications, permissions and entitlements; late responses and not-yet-accepted actions from the old session MUST NOT populate or modify the new one. A mutation already accepted for A must not be reassigned to B. Push destination reassociation has the explicit in-flight registration limit in FR-022; it never grants access to the former inbox. This mobile/context isolation is distinct from the standard Auth0 access-token limit for same-subject re-registration in FR-035; it does not require a persistent business session registry.
- **FR-022**: Ordinary sign-out or account switching MUST attempt disassociation of the current old notification destination once, without preventing local sign-out on failure. No persisted retry, disassociation secret or offline queue is required. A failed attempt may leave old-account operating-system pushes deliverable until reassociation, provider invalidation or manual correction; it is not reported as success. An already transmitted registration from the old session may also arrive last and replace the new destination association, even after successful disassociation. The next successful ordinary registration for the current account repairs it; until then, its push delivery may be missed. Discard obsolete callbacks and cancel still-controllable old requests, without a persistent ordering or retry mechanism. Such pushes expose no private old-account data through the application, and a stale removal cannot delete the new account association. Other devices remain associated after local sign-out. Business account deletion removes all affected destinations in the same local transaction. Local sign-out, account switching and confirmed erasure clear private application state and reset the app-controlled badge, independently of network cleanup. Already displayed system notifications may remain; no notification-center cleanup or recall is required. Their presence grants no access to the former inbox, does not mark it read and never blocks local sign-out.
- **FR-023**: Entitlements MUST belong to the current account and come from RevenueCat SDK state under F09. No available state means no advertising or purchase invitation; an inactive SDK state may allow advertising under F11 consent rules. Public sport remains accessible in every state. No custom refresh/tolerance clock or backend rights projection is added. Guests and new account sessions retain no previous account entitlement.

#### Account-age evidence

- **FR-024**: Account-age evidence MUST come from creation of the current Auth0 principal, shared by its already linked identities, not from business profile creation. Migration or reinstall does not reset this reference. After voluntary deletion, the actual principal returned by Auth0 determines age: a newly created principal has a new date; a principal surviving failed external cleanup retains its original date and may already meet the seven-day threshold. No artificial age reset is required. F08 owns the threshold and moderation exception. Account age does not identify a person’s age.
- **FR-025**: A known date reliably attributed to the principal MUST remain usable during provider unavailability. Without reliable evidence, the system MUST NOT substitute any arbitrary or business date and MUST allow retry; only actions requiring this evidence are suspended. A moderation exception requires the applicable permission to be established.

#### Deletion and return

- **FR-026**: The holder MUST be able to delete the account through an ordinary confirmation screen opened from the profile, with concise copy, a confirmation checkbox and explicit deletion action. Explain irreversible personal-data removal and that any Pro subscription must be managed in its App Store or Google Play; account deletion does not cancel it. No subscription-management action is added to this screen. A subscription does not require waiting for expiry. Cancellation preserves the account. Valid current owner authentication and confirmation are sufficient; no fixed authentication-age threshold applies. Reauthenticate only if ownership is no longer established.
- **FR-027**: Business deletion MUST complete synchronously in one local transaction before external cleanup or local sign-out. It removes the original account and its personal associations and detaches retained contributions. A definite transaction failure preserves the account and session and starts no external deletion. After commit the original account is unavailable on all devices; external failure cannot roll back business deletion. After the bounded cleanup attempts, clear local credentials and private state even if provider or browser logout fails, then return directly to guest home. A lost response requires an ordinary read of the original account before any explicit retry; if that read is unavailable, allow local sign-out with an ordinary error without claiming a confirmed outcome.
- **FR-028**: After local commit, attempt cleanup once for each known original external target using ordinary bounded calls; one failure MUST NOT prevent the other attempts. Do not require a successful live provider lookup to delete the business account. Missing target evidence, failed calls and uncertain external outcomes use the private diagnostics and native alert route in FR-031, followed by manual handling. No pending-account record, outbox, worker, polling, automatic retry, rollback or dedicated recovery screen is required. A process interruption between local commit and diagnostic emission may leave external cleanup unreported; no durable mechanism is added to close that gap.
- **FR-029**: Deletion MUST cover the business profile, the Auth0 profile and its associations, applicable revocations including Sign in with Apple, the RevenueCat customer record and personal data held by Blockout according to the matrix below. It does not delete the person’s Google/Apple accounts or automatically cancel their store subscriptions. A complete record MUST NOT be retained to facilitate future purchase restoration.
- **FR-030**: Useful live contributions MUST be retainable without association to the deleted account and without changing their moderation state. Their ownership does not automatically return to a recreated account. Personal information contained in the link itself remains subject to applicable F11 requests; removing the association is not a promise of total anonymization. F08 removes the reporter association and free-text reason during local account deletion while retaining counted report facts and moderation decisions.
- **FR-031**: Deletion MUST target the original business account. A repeated request for an absent original account has no external effects and MUST NOT target a replacement account. External failures and uncertainties MUST be logged privately with the original business identifier, relevant Auth0/RevenueCat references and minimum evidence needed to verify original targets, plus operation, environment, provider, time and safe outcome/reason. No email, profile, photo, credential, token, secret or raw provider payload is logged. Native Grafana alerting sends no personal target reference to Discord; alert closure is not proof of repair. Logs follow the existing 14-day retention. For an unresolved incident exceeding that period, the operator manually retains only still-needed references in the existing private support process until resolution or another applicable obligation. Before manual destructive action, verify original targets and current use; never resolve a target by email or guess a replacement. No dedicated recovery system is added.
- **FR-032**: The interface MUST return directly to guest home after business deletion and bounded external attempts, without a deletion receipt, progress view or extra success toast. Provider failure after local commit is handled operationally rather than shown as a failed business deletion. Trust each provider’s documented acknowledgment without polling for physical completion; external asynchronous acceptance alone does not require manual intervention. Neither business success nor provider acceptance claims instant erasure of every external copy or cancellation of store billing. Support remains available without an account and supplies applicable F11 outcomes.
- **FR-033**: Explicit authentication after business deletion MAY establish a new business account without waiting for a stored cleanup state or provider-erasure polling. No special gate or coordination handles return during an in-flight external deletion; ordinary provider errors and manual assistance cover that case. External failures do not restore the deleted business account. Environment and identity-scope safeguards still apply before any destructive provider action.
- **FR-034**: A restored backup or reconstruction MUST respect current deletion, rights and privacy obligations before affected data is made available. Standard provider checks and authorized manual review/correction are permitted under F13; unresolved affected resources remain unavailable. No generic automatic recovery engine or synchronous external evidence store is imposed. Backup retention is not active-profile retention.
- **FR-035**: Registration after deletion MUST create a new business identifier without restoring old profile data, follows, notifications, personal permissions, contribution attribution or manual Pro benefits. The actual Auth0 principal determines account age under FR-024, including when external deletion failed. Ordinary baseline permissions follow F12; administrative and moderation grants never return automatically. V2 relies on standard Auth0 token validity: if the same subject survives or is reused, a still-valid earlier token may authorize the new account under its current permissions until expiration. No re-registration delay, custom lifecycle claim or business session registry is added. This limitation restores no old business data and does not redirect an old business-account deletion to the new identifier.
- **FR-036**: After authentication on a usable account, explicit native restoration MUST use the standard RevenueCat/store transfer scope, including any receipt-wide scope, without a Blockout profile merge or revival of deleted personal data or gifts. Blockout does not guarantee transfer purchase by purchase or preservation of other paid purchases outside the native transfer scope. The sign-in identity and paying store account remain distinct. F09 owns actual provider qualification, uncertainty and verified assistance when native restoration is impossible. Email matching never replaces ownership evidence.
- **FR-037**: A prominent deletion section of the existing public privacy page, reachable by a direct link, MUST identify Blockout and provide the procedure and existing F11 contact email for requesting erasure without reinstalling the application or mandatory sign-in. This complements in-app deletion and satisfies the external request-resource requirement of Google Play. No additional account portal, dedicated web form or separate deletion page is selected. Proportionate ownership verification precedes acceptance; a reply address alone does not authorize deletion. Processing follows the same erasure lifecycle and F11 outcome obligations. F14 qualifies the store-published link.
- **FR-038**: Profile data, ownership evidence, private deletion diagnostics and associated records MUST remain accessible only to recipients authorized for their purpose. Individual requests and support documents follow [F11 FR-021–FR-037](../004-advertising-privacy-legal/spec.md), without adding periodic purging of GitHub issues or retaining personal data indefinitely by default. No secret, payment evidence or personal identifier may appear in public diagnostics or error messages.
- **FR-039**: F05 journeys MUST apply [F13](../003-shared-quality/spec.md) for quality objectives, mobile accessibility, language, understandable errors, security and degraded states. They distinguish loading, absence, refusal, failure and an uncertain outcome; an unavailable state allows retry or a usable public journey. F13 objectives do not promise the same turnaround for all asynchronous external processing.
- **FR-040**: The Profile tab MUST open the approved “Mon profil” overview rather than the editing form. It presents the current authorized identity, a named edit action and coherent grouped navigation entries. “Blockout Pro” precedes “Confidentialité” in “Mon compte” under [F09 FR-017](../006-pro-subscriptions/spec.md#functional-requirements); legal/support destinations, privacy choices and permission-aware administration retain their existing owners. Editing MUST use a separate ordinary screen with “Enregistrer” and “Annuler”. “Enregistrer” remains unavailable while the draft is unchanged; this UI availability does not invalidate an explicitly supplied current username under FR-014. Successful atomic saving returns to the overview, while cancellation/back preserves the accepted profile and uses the shared discard decision for dirty input. “Se déconnecter” names the existing session departure explicitly and MUST NOT substitute for cancel, back or save. Initial restoration and the first account/existing-photo reads MUST withhold the whole overview or editor body until it is ready, using a page skeleton without active content or guest-departure controls. Initial account/photo failures and unusable sessions MUST replace the whole body with one recovery state; a refused current account removes the overview. A known guest has the ordinary invitation, while Pro verification remains independent and cannot delay profile readiness or imply free membership. After admission, refresh failures MUST preserve the editor draft and use scoped feedback without inserting an error card among fields. Administration, legal and account navigation MUST use the same shared row/group anatomy. Profile MUST open “Collectes”, “Maintenance” and “Versions minimales” directly under their respective current grants, without an intermediate technical index; sporting permissions alone expose none of these destinations. Planned but undelivered Mapping, video moderation, account deletion and legal destinations remain visible and disabled where their account/capability conditions apply, without a fake route or operation. Guest and unavailable-account states preserve their existing sign-in/retry/public-navigation outcomes through the appropriate complete state and ordinary navigation. Read retention, current-session isolation, photo preparation and pending/uncertain-command safeguards remain binding across both destinations; moving the form introduces no new account operation or entitlement decision.

- **SC-001**: All A01–A08 journeys lead to the expected account, an explicit refusal or a usable retry; no new association, merge or duplicate business creation is allowed. Eligible first access requires no additional profile form. (FR-001–FR-010, FR-012)
