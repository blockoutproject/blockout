# Functional specification: sporting consultation and calendars

**Planning issue**: [#23](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/23), on `develop` under the planning-only initialization exception

**Created on**: 2026-09-19

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline; consultation lifecycle amendments authorized for the F03 dossier

**Requested scope**: [#263](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout-legacy/issues/263), F03 in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications). Define club, team, pool and match pages, their calendars, standings, maps, stream links and official documents. [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md) owns data semantics and retention; F03 owns consultation. No new paid benefit or sporting editor is introduced. The derived technical dossier is owned by #23.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-09

- Calendar lists keep their loaded rows, groups and progress on focus and foreground return. No automatic reread or local category reshuffle is required.
- Pull-to-refresh reads only the active tab endpoint. Calendar replacement and return to the beginning happen on success; failure retains still-authorized data. Common page information is read on a new page opening, not a simple back navigation.
- After a device timezone change, known instants may be reformatted locally while retained day groups keep their loading context. Subsequent pages keep that grouping context until manual refresh reconstructs the calendar. No special timezone screen or automatic reset is introduced.
- Match details retain focus, foreground and manual rereads. A freshly read detail need not rewrite a retained calendar. Known access/privacy restrictions still suppress affected retained data immediately.
- Pagination is a sequence of current reads, not an immutable historical snapshot. UUID deduplication prevents duplicate cards; concurrent date/result changes may require a refresh to rediscover a moved match.
- Official PDFs open directly through platform facilities. A targeted backend relay submits the FFVB information-sheet form; files used by the mobile reader are temporary, without a document archive.

## User scenarios and validation _(mandatory)_

The actors are the visitor, the signed-in person, the Pro beneficiary and the person holding an editing permission already defined by the relevant domain. Public consultation requires neither an account nor Pro entitlement; F09 governs only the advertising exemption. An editing permission does not follow from merely being able to view a page.

The scenarios define expected V2 outcomes. They prove neither a V1 code correction nor completed mobile or provider qualification. FR, A and SC identifiers are local to F03.

### User story 1 — Find information and navigate between resources (Priority: P1)

As a visitor, I want to view a club, its teams and their competitions, then open associated matches, even when some details are missing.

**Priority rationale**: Pages and their relationships form the main consultation journey; missing secondary information must not make the entire resource unusable.

**Independent validation**: Browse all four page types, their links and seasons with complete, partial or hidden information.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a publicly visible club with available contact details, **when** a guest opens its page, **then** they find its known name, logo, contact details, location, teams and calendar entries. Phone, email, website and map use effective F02 values and open only usable, authorized destinations. Corrected values prevail; deliberately absent or restricted fields receive no fallback to the source or an old value. Club location and full calendars are free independently of Pro. (FR-001, FR-002, FR-032)
2. **A02** — **Given** a viewable team and pool, **when** their pages are opened, **then** they present their known classifications, seasons and relationships; the team provides access to its club and participations, the pool to its calendar, standings and map according to rights. A phase without standings receives no invented standings. (FR-003, FR-004, FR-020)
3. **A03** — **Given** a viewable dated match, **when** its page is opened, **then** participants, available time, applicable result, usable detail, pool, venue, referees, streaming, access to the referenced official FFVB calendar and available documents remain distinct. Missing venue, referee, logo or set detail does not block the rest; it is not replaced by data from another match. (FR-005, FR-006)
4. **A04** — **Given** several available seasons for a club, including an upcoming season already published, **when** the page is opened without a current selection, **then** the most recent is selected and identified. Choosing another season controls club teams and calendars together; a delayed response from the previous one does not replace the current choice. A retrieval error does not become “No teams”. (FR-007, FR-018, FR-029)
5. **A05** — **Given** a retained page or relationship whose target is hidden, **when** an old link is followed, **then** unavailability is explained without exposing the hidden resource or redirecting to another assumed identity. A network error is distinguished from this unavailability. Returning to the list preserves the still-applicable context. (FR-008, FR-018, FR-031)
6. **A06** — **Given** existing follow, report and edit entries, **when** they are offered from a page, **then** they retain their destination and applicable follow/count information, and apply F05/F06/F08/F10/F12 and F02 rules according to the action. Missing permission prevents mutation, even from old access; F03 adds no score or match editor. (FR-001, FR-035)

### User story 2 — Understand times in my time zone (Priority: P1)

As a user in France or abroad, I want to read a consistent time and day without changing the meaning of the sporting schedule.

**Priority rationale**: Mixing the source time zone, the phone time zone and relative days can place the same match on the wrong day.

**Independent validation**: Compare Paris and New York for the same instant, then change time zones, cross a daylight-saving change and view matches without a time or date.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A07** — **Given** a match scheduled for September 20, 2026 at 00:30 in Paris according to an established source time, **when** two people view it in Paris and New York, **then** they see September 20 at 00:30 and September 19 at 18:30 respectively. Each person’s daily grouping and relative labels use their time zone, on the page and in lists. The match retains the same instant and identity. (FR-009)
2. **A08** — **Given** a retained calendar and a device time-zone change, **when** the app resumes, **then** known times may be converted with their date-specific offset without a network refresh or regrouping. Day groups and continuation retain the loading zone until manual refresh rebuilds them in the current zone. Match details use the current zone; no fixed Paris–New York offset or guessed source instant is introduced. (FR-009, FR-018)
3. **A09** — **Given** a dated match without a time, including an FFVB 00:00 value not confirmed as midnight under F02, **when** its page or calendar is viewed, **then** the known sporting date remains displayed as is with “Time to be confirmed”, without conversion to another day or a false 00:00 time. (FR-010)
4. **A10** — **Given** an identifiable match without a usable date, **when** lists, calendars or an old link are viewed, **then** no public match page is exposed. When a usable date is accepted, the same identity becomes viewable again if no other restriction remains. If an earlier date is explicitly withdrawn, hiding applies; a mere acquisition failure does not erase the known date. (FR-006, FR-011, FR-031)

### User story 3 — Find matches and distinguish missing results (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to find matches in “Upcoming” or “Completed”, without silent disappearance or false result confirmation.

**Priority rationale**: Elapsed time helps organize the calendar, but proves neither a result, cancellation nor a match in progress.

**Independent validation**: Cross time boundaries, publish or withdraw results, reschedule a match and paginate lists in several time zones.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A11** — **Given** a match without a definitive result scheduled for 20:00 in Paris, **when** a current read occurs after six hours have elapsed, **then** it belongs to “Completed” with “Result unavailable”. Just before the boundary it still belongs to “Upcoming”. Paris and New York apply the same transition instant, even if their displayed days differ; six hours means an elapsed duration, including during a daylight-saving change. The displayed day remains the match day, not the transition day. (FR-009, FR-012, FR-013)
2. **A12** — **Given** a dated match without a time or definitive result, **when** a current read occurs after the day following that date begins in Paris, **then** it belongs to “Completed” with “Result unavailable” for all users. Before this shared boundary, it remains in “Upcoming” with “Time to be confirmed”, without assigning it a sporting instant. (FR-010, FR-012, FR-013)
3. **A13** — **Given** an official definitive result accepted before the time boundary, **when** the list is newly loaded or refreshed, **then** the match appears in “Completed” with that result without waiting six hours. A provisional score does not trigger this early classification; after the boundary, it remains identified as provisional with “Definitive result unavailable”. No winner or zero score is inferred from waiting. (FR-012, FR-013, FR-019)
4. **A14** — **Given** a match classified in “Completed” without a result, **when** a new read after an official postponement establishes its scheduled time in the future, **then** it may return to “Upcoming” under the same identity. A late result replaces the unavailability indication. A withdrawn result removes the score and its detail, then reapplies boundaries to the current match date, without inventing cancellation. (FR-014, FR-019)
5. **A15** — **Given** a verified match withdrawal under F02 or a hidden parent, **when** calendars, the personal feed and an old link are viewed, **then** the match remains hidden even if a result is retained internally, it is followed through a team or pool, or the six-hour boundary is crossed. Reappearance authorized by F02 may lift the withdrawal without waiting for two collections, subject to a usable date and every other restriction; a collection outage proves no withdrawal. (FR-011, FR-015)
6. **A16** — **Given** several days, pools, equal times and unknown times, **when** lists are browsed, **then** “Upcoming” orders days ascending and “Completed” descending in the calendar loading time zone, retaining that grouping after travel until refresh. Matches are grouped by pool; known times follow the list direction, followed by unknown times, with stable ordering on ties. (FR-016)
7. **A17** — **Given** a paginated calendar, **when** another page fails, **then** loaded days remain usable with retry. Appended matches are deduplicated by identity; empty dates do not falsely end progression. Concurrent date/result changes may leave a moved match outside the traversed portion until refresh. A successful refresh replaces the first page, resets progression and reconstructs current grouping; failure preserves the prior list. (FR-017, FR-018, FR-029)

### User story 4 — Read standings and locate participants (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to read official information without invented calculations and, if my rights allow it, locate the clubs participating in a pool.

**Priority rationale**: Standings may be partial or absent without making the calendar or map unusable; a map position is not a guaranteed match venue.

**Independent validation**: View standings without complete team matching and a map with zero, one or several locations, including shared locations.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A18** — **Given** official standings with special positions and a row without a viewable Blockout team, **when** displayed, **then** official positions, order and statistics are respected. The unassociated row keeps its source label without a fictional link; its rank is not recalculated from its display position. A missing value does not become zero. (FR-020)
2. **A19** — **Given** temporarily unavailable standings or a phase without published standings, **when** the page is viewed, **then** the last usable standings remain presented with their known freshness if they exist; otherwise their unavailability is indicated. The calendar and independent information remain usable. A successful cache read does not become a new official observation. (FR-006, FR-021)
3. **A20** — **Given** a pool with known participations, some without a standings row, **when** a guest or signed-in person opens its map, **then** publicly visible teams with reliable coordinates are represented even without published standings. An unattributable standings row creates neither an assumed participant nor location. (FR-022, FR-032)
4. **A21** — **Given** a locality that is absent, ambiguous or no longer reliable after a change, **when** the map is viewed, **then** no forbidden old point or fictional point is presented. Zero usable points, a partial map and basemap failure are distinguished; independent pages and lists remain usable. The displayed location is at municipality level, without suggesting a precise gymnasium. (FR-023)
5. **A22** — **Given** several teams at the same location, **when** they are sought on the map, **then** each remains identifiable and accessible without artificially shifting its location. A missing logo does not make the team disappear. The visual selection form belongs to R02. (FR-006, FR-024)

### User story 5 — Open a stream or official document (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to find available links without their presence promising an active video or an actually retrievable document.

**Priority rationale**: External references and the sporting result are distinct information; their failures must not block match consultation.

**Independent validation**: Cross link presence, definitive/provisional/absent result and opening success/failure on iOS and Android.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A23** — **Given** an available stream link for a viewable match, **when** it is presented before the scheduled time, after six hours without a result or after a definitive result, **then** the label remains “Stream link”. Neither a red live indicator nor a replay promise is inferred from these conditions. Professional links and those authorized by F08 retain their applicable journey. (FR-025)
2. **A24** — **Given** a usable information-sheet reference, **when** the match is viewable before or after its scheduled time, with or without a result, **then** access to it remains available. Known venue/time information on the page remains usable even if the document does not open. (FR-005, FR-026, FR-028)
3. **A25** — **Given** a scoresheet reference, **when** an official definitive result is available, **then** access is offered. Without that result, including after six hours or with only a provisional score, it is not. Withdrawing the result reapplies this condition without inferring erasure of the source reference; the information sheet remains independent. (FR-014, FR-026, FR-027)
4. **A26** — **Given** an external or document opening, **when** the link is absent, expired, unavailable or fails, **then** these outcomes are not presented as a loaded document or a missing score. The person can return, retry and access the applicable reporting journey; the sporting page remains usable. iOS/Android journeys may differ without removing the ability to read. (FR-025, FR-028, FR-035)

### User story 6 — Consult during an outage without bypassing restrictions (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to understand what is missing, resume consultation and retain still-usable data without displaying forbidden or misleading information.

**Priority rationale**: An error, an empty result and a hidden resource require different responses; Pro and privacy rules must remain consistent on every surface.

**Independent validation**: Trigger initial and partial errors, change navigation context and entitlements, then resume consultation.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A27** — **Given** a page without initial data or an already loaded page, **when** loading or a section fails, **then** waiting, error, actual absence and partial result are distinguished. Previous still-authorized sections remain accessible with an indication of failure; no successful empty state is falsely announced. (FR-006, FR-029, FR-031)
2. **A28** — **Given** an open match page, **when** it gains focus, returns to the foreground or is manually refreshed, **then** data and presentation boundaries are reassessed. Refresh of other consultations respects F13 objectives after Blockout acceptance without claiming to trigger provider publication or promising a continuous score stream. (FR-012, FR-030)
3. **A29** — **Given** navigation to another resource, season or account, **when** an older response arrives, **then** it cannot replace the current context. Returning to a retained list preserves selection, groups and progress without rereading. A changed timezone updates formatting without moving loaded rows until refresh. Known hidden data remains suppressed and a timezone change never changes sporting season. (FR-007, FR-009, FR-018, FR-031)
4. **A30** — **Given** a guest or a person with confirmed-free, paid Pro, gifted, unknown or expired entitlements, **when** they open a pool map or either full club calendar, **then** content remains accessible without a Pro block, wait for entitlement verification or purchase invitation. Unknown entitlements for a signed-in account suppress advertising under F09/F11, whenever the SDK supplies no current-account state. Personal and administrative permissions and F02 visibility remain unchanged. (FR-001, FR-031–FR-032)
5. **A31** — **Given** a known privacy or visibility restriction, **when** an old page, cache or source reappearance is used, **then** that restriction prevails. Authorized contact details or referees are presented only for their sporting purpose; no personal diagnostic is published in errors. (FR-031, FR-033)
6. **A32** — **Given** partial states and “Result unavailable”, **when** read with supported accessibility tools, **then** their meaning does not depend solely on color or an icon. Retry and return actions remain identifiable, without permanent blocking by loading. (FR-013, FR-028, FR-034)
7. **A33** — **Given** an accepted correction and retained consultable state, **when** details/calendar are consulted or refreshed and affected projections are processed, **then** they reflect the accepted correction with honest freshness. Current restrictions override any retained representation; no global propagation stopwatch or continuous score stream is required. (FR-029–FR-031; F13 FR-004–FR-005)
8. **A34** — **Given** F03 qualification, **when** journeys and discrepancies are assessed, **then** every requirement and state is linked to evidence with known scope, covering relevant iOS/Android, time-zone, entitlement and error cases. A local V1 test or observed Figma screen does not validate V2 behavior; R02 covers states before technical design. (FR-036)

### Edge cases

- The display date in New York may be earlier than in Paris without changing the shared threshold or season. The six hours are not calculated by naively subtracting local times. (A07–A12)
- A match without a time may remain in “Upcoming” when the displayed sporting date has already passed in the phone time zone: its boundary is shared, at midnight in Paris. No fictional time explains this classification. (A09, A12)
- A match awaiting its result remains findable after several days; aging alone causes neither disappearance nor a zero score. Source/parent restrictions still apply. (A11, A15)
- A match may be classified in “Completed” without a result, with a provisional score or with a definitive result; these cases do not provide the same scoresheet access. (A13, A25)
- A withdrawn definitive result, an explicitly withdrawn date and failed source retrieval have different effects. (A10, A14, A27)
- A pool without standings may have participants and a map; an unassociated official row supplies no assumed identity or location. (A18–A22)
- Loaded day groups remain in their loading context after timezone or source changes; refresh reconstructs them. Newly formatted hours may temporarily cross a retained group date after travel. No automatic list reconstruction is required. (A08, A17, A29)

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional requirements

#### Consultation, relationships and seasons

- **FR-001**: Public sporting resources MUST remain free for guests and signed-in people under F05, including pool maps and full club calendars. Consultation, following, contribution and editing remain distinct actions with their own account and permission conditions. Pro MUST NOT restrict consultation.
- **FR-002**: The club page MUST present the applicable name and logo, available contact details, authorized city/address and location, and entries to its teams and calendars. Phone, email, website and map opening MUST use only a usable destination, without fabricating a geographic search from missing information or equating the contact address with a confirmed gymnasium. Contact details and location MUST apply effective F02 FR-049–FR-052 values: deliberate absence or an F11 restriction cannot be bypassed by displaying the source value, an old contact or an old point.
- **FR-003**: The team page MUST present its F02 identity and presentation, known club, division, format, gender and season, its viewable participations, associated calendars and standings, and available follow information and count under F06. Multiple participations remain distinguishable without merging phases: the existing horizontally scrollable team tab bar has one standings tab per visible pool, and upcoming/finished tabs aggregate its matches across visible participations with their pool context. Keep one team-follow action for the team identity. Use published names and available phase context to distinguish similar pool labels, without introducing qualification or progression controls; links must not choose an assumed namesake team.
- **FR-004**: The pool page MUST present known identity, league/competition, classification, format and season, calendars, standings, map access and applicable follow/count information under F06. Missing information or phases without standings MUST NOT be completed by inference from another pool.
- **FR-005**: The match page MUST present authorized participants and links, available date/time, result and usable detail, pool/competition, known venue and referees, and streaming, navigation to the official FFVB calendar when a usable reference exists, and applicable documents. Missing information must not be replaced by information from another match; the venue may remain “Venue to be confirmed”.
- **FR-006**: Absent, invalid, withdrawn and temporarily unavailable values MUST follow F02 distinctions. Missing detail, contact, logo or document must not block independent sections. An unavailable logo does not make the identity unreadable; a missing score does not become zero. Failed acquisition is insufficient to erase a usable previous value.
- **FR-007**: Club seasons MUST come from available viewable teams, be ordered newest to oldest and initially select the most recent, including an upcoming season already published. Selection MUST be identifiable and shared by club teams and calendars. Preserve the choice while it remains available; when a successful response establishes its disappearance, select the most recent remaining season, or display no season if none remain. An outage does not justify a silent switch. A closed season remains viewable under F02; postponement does not change its season by inference from the calendar year.
- **FR-008**: Relationships between resources MUST open their exact identity only if viewable. An unavailable or hidden target MUST have an understandable outcome, distinct from a retrieval error, without exposure through an old link or redirection to an assumed replacement. F02 governs parent and entity restrictions.

#### Time, visibility and calendar classification

- **FR-009**: Sporting instants established under F02 MUST be displayed in the phone time zone with the offset applicable on the match date. A newly loaded or refreshed calendar derives day groups and relative labels in its loading time zone. After a device time-zone change, retained rows may display newly converted times while their day groups keep the original loading context until refresh; no automatic fetch, regrouping or scroll reset is required. Match detail uses the current device zone. No new time-zone setting or resolution of source ambiguity is introduced.
- **FR-010**: A known sporting date without a time MUST remain displayed as is with “Time to be confirmed”, without assigning an instant or converting the date alone. The F02 rule for FFVB 00:00 remains applicable; midnight is a usable time only when explicitly confirmed. A date alone may share a displayed day with converted instants without claiming the same precision.
- **FR-011**: A match without a usable date MUST be hidden from all public surfaces, including lists, calendars and its page through an old link. Its identity and data remain retained under F02, without a new operator action added by F03. A usable date restores consultation only if no other hiding/withdrawal reason prevents it. Explicit date withdrawal reapplies hiding; a mere collection outage does not trigger it.
- **FR-012**: Calendars MUST retain “Upcoming” and “Completed”. Each current read classifies an official definitive result as “Completed” immediately. Otherwise a match with a reliable instant is “Upcoming” before six elapsed hours after kickoff and “Completed” at and after that boundary. For a date without time, the boundary is the following day at 00:00 Europe/Paris. These rules use the same evaluation instant everywhere, independently of display time zone, without redefining F02 sporting status. Previously loaded calendars retain their classification until refreshed; focus, foreground return and elapsed time do not trigger a calendar reread or local reclassification. Match details retain FR-030 rereads.
- **FR-013**: A match classified in “Completed” without a definitive result MUST bear “Result unavailable”, or “Definitive result unavailable” if a provisional score is displayed. This distinction MUST be explicit and accessible without depending solely on color. Both before and after the boundary, a score, winner, cancellation, postponement or “in progress” state MUST NOT be inferred solely from elapsed time. No new tab is required; R02 sets the visual form of the indication.
- **FR-014**: A current read after an official date change or a late, corrected or withdrawn result MUST reassess presentation under the same match identity. Postponement may return a match to “Upcoming”; result withdrawal removes its score/detail under F02 and reapplies the boundary. A retained calendar is rebuilt on refresh rather than automatically reshuffled by detail reads or source changes. The grouping date is the sporting date, never result receipt or threshold-crossing date. Result withdrawal differs from whole-match withdrawal under FR-015; known restrictions still take precedence.
- **FR-015**: F02 and FR-011 restrictions MUST prevail over time classification. A match withdrawn under F02 MUST be hidden everywhere, including its public results and page through an old link, even if its identity and result remain retained internally. A follow, acquired result, time threshold or time-zone change MUST NOT republish it. Authorized F02 reappearance may lift this withdrawal without waiting for two new collections; a hidden parent, inactive division and missing usable date remain applicable. A source outage alone does not constitute withdrawal.
- **FR-016**: Displayed days MUST be ascending in “Upcoming” and descending in “Completed”. Within a day, matches remain grouped by pool with stable group ordering for the same set; within a group, known times follow the list direction, followed by unknown times. Equal times MUST retain stable ordering for the same data, without inventing sporting priority. Unavailable results stay on the match day in “Completed”, without eviction solely due to age.
- **FR-017**: Pagination MUST allow browsing all eligible complete days in the loading time zone, without a fixed match count per page or total calendar cap. Empty eligible dates do not produce groups or terminate progression. Failed additional reads preserve loaded days and offer retry. Appending pages MUST deduplicate match identities. Reads are not one immutable snapshot: concurrent result/date changes can temporarily move a match outside the traversed portion; refresh restarts from the beginning to reconstruct current grouping and removes persistent omissions caused by retaining old cursors.
- **FR-018**: Navigation and changes of season, resource, entitlements or account MUST keep responses in their originating context. A late response cannot replace newer intent. Back navigation, focus and foreground return preserve loaded calendar rows, groups and progress while the page remains retained, without automatic reread. Time-zone changes follow FR-009; continuation retains its original grouping context until refresh. Pull-to-refresh reads only the active tab endpoint, replaces a calendar with its new first page and returns to the beginning only on success; it does not reread common page information or sibling tabs. New page openings read common information and the initial tab. Failed refresh retains authorized previous data; old pagination responses cannot append after a replacement. No process-restart persistence or fine scroll restoration is required.

#### Results, standings and maps

- **FR-019**: Overall result, set detail, provisional/definitive nature and sporting notations MUST follow F02 FR-028–FR-031. A usable overall result remains visible without valid detail; contradictory detail is hidden rather than reconstructed. No zero, winner, universal number of sets or administrative result may be invented; presentation classification in “Completed” does not turn a provisional score into a definitive result.
- **FR-020**: Standings MUST respect official order, positions and statistics under F02, without calculating rank from the display index or reconstructing from matches. A row without a matching viewable team keeps its source label without a fictional link. Missing values and special ratios are not replaced by zero. Team highlighting must not alter standings or infer a winner from an absent or ambiguous result.
- **FR-021**: Unavailable standings MUST remain distinct from usable standings or a phase without published standings. The last still-authorized usable state remains viewable with its known freshness; if none exists, unavailability is explained. Calendar retrieval does not announce freshness of unrefreshed standings; a cache read is not a recent provider observation.
- **FR-022**: The pool map MUST represent known, viewable participating teams with a reliable location, independently of standings availability. An unassociated official row creates neither a team nor a map point. Loss of a visible F02 participation removes its representation in that pool without deleting its other participations.
- **FR-023**: Points MUST represent clubs’ reliable municipality-level location under F02, without claiming to locate a match gymnasium. Absent, ambiguous or invalidated coordinates yield no assumed point. Complete absence of points, partial coverage and basemap failure MUST be distinguished; independent information remains viewable. An empty map must not suggest that the pool contains no teams.
- **FR-024**: When several teams share a location, each MUST remain identifiable and accessible without artificial geographic displacement. A missing image does not remove representation; links follow FR-008. Selection form, visual grouping and framing belong to R02, without a new need for phone location.

#### Streaming and documents

- **FR-025**: An available, authorized stream link MUST be presented as “Stream link”, before or after the match, without a red live indicator or replay promise inferred from time, result or link existence. Preserve applicable professional media and F08 link journeys; no permanent video-platform monitoring or new contribution authorization is added. An absent link offers no fictional opening; a failed opening leaves applicable return, retry and reporting available.
- **FR-026**: The information sheet MUST be accessible as soon as a usable reference is available and throughout the viewable match lifecycle, independently of the score. It remains distinct from the scoresheet; its unavailability does not hide venue/time information already known on the page.
- **FR-027**: The scoresheet MUST be offered only when the viewable match has a published official definitive result and a usable document reference. A provisional score or mere classification in “Completed” after the boundary is insufficient. Result withdrawal reapplies this condition without implicitly erasing the source reference.
- **FR-028**: Document opening MUST remain usable on iOS and Android with a platform-appropriate journey, without an imposed reader provider. Missing document, loading, expired link and opening error MUST remain distinguishable when their cause is established; an unknown cause is presented as opening failure without an invented diagnosis. Return, retry and access to applicable reporting must remain possible. Successful loading or offline availability is not promised solely by a URL’s presence.

#### Recovery, restrictions and quality

- **FR-029**: Initial loading, actual absence, partial response, initial error, refresh failure and hidden resource MUST have distinct outcomes. An unavailable section does not block those that remain usable. Lists must not translate an error into “no matches” or “no teams”; waiting allows return or retry without infinite repetition.
- **FR-030**: Match details MUST reread on focus, foreground return and manual refresh. Calendars MUST support explicit refresh and apply current accepted sporting changes on new reads, while retained lists follow FR-009/012/017/018 rather than automatic focus, foreground, reconnect or invalidation refetching. Independent section reads do not announce that the entire page was refreshed. No numerical propagation target or continuous score stream is introduced. Current restrictions apply to access decisions; consultation, supplier observation and integration times remain distinct.
- **FR-031**: Refresh failure MUST preserve still-usable previous data with an honest state, without declaring it newly acquired. Known visibility, privacy, identity or action-permission restrictions MUST prevail over obsolete data or caches; retaining a list does not authorize publication of content already known to be restricted. F02/F05/F11 own current restrictions and F13 owns recovery. F09 entitlement status governs only the advertising exemption and MUST NOT hide sporting data.
- **FR-032**: Pool maps and full calendars of clubs’ upcoming and completed matches MUST be freely accessible in every entitlement state, including guest, confirmed free, paid, gifted, unknown and expired. No Pro check, verification wait or purchase invitation may block these journeys. F09 determines only the advertising exemption; unknown entitlements for a signed-in account suppress advertising and repurchase invitations under F09/F11. F02 visibility, F05 account isolation and action-specific permissions remain applicable.
- **FR-033**: Club contact details, referees and links MUST respect F11/F02 purposes, corrections and restrictions; source reappearance does not republish information already forbidden. Public errors and diagnostics must expose neither private data, secrets nor unnecessary technical details. F03 adds no collection of personal location.
- **FR-034**: Journeys MUST apply F13 for language, readability, accessibility and recovery. Sporting state and unavailability MUST be understandable without depending exclusively on colors, logos or icons. Visual form belongs to R02; this requirement does not validate a mockup.
- **FR-035**: Existing following, reporting, contribution and authorized editing capabilities MUST remain reachable from their applicable contexts without inventing permissions or outcomes.
