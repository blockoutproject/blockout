# Feature Specification: Sporting consultation

**Feature Branch**: `develop`

**Created**: 2026-09-19

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline; technical planning is documented in [plan.md](plan.md), with implementation qualification pending.

**Input**: F03 in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications). Define club, team, pool and match pages, their calendars, standings, maps, stream links and official documents. [F02](../002-sporting-data/spec.md) owns data semantics and retention; F03 owns consultation. No new paid benefit or sporting editor is introduced.

## User Scenarios & Testing

Design input: [match](https://www.figma.com/design/rKu4xc8eJsx0f0Vu4E6U03?node-id=976-293), [standings](https://www.figma.com/design/rKu4xc8eJsx0f0Vu4E6U03/?node-id=1010-15711) and [map annotations](https://www.figma.com/design/rKu4xc8eJsx0f0Vu4E6U03/?node-id=1035-9522). Existing approval and the map evidence limits do not establish native/provider behavior.

The actors are the visitor, the signed-in person, the Pro beneficiary and the person holding an editing permission already defined by the relevant domain. Public consultation requires neither an account nor Pro entitlement; F09 governs only the advertising exemption. An editing permission does not follow from merely being able to view a page.

The scenarios define expected V2 outcomes. They prove neither a V1 code correction nor completed mobile or provider qualification. FR, A and SC identifiers are local to F03.

### User Story 1 — Find information and navigate between resources (Priority: P1)

As a visitor, I want to view a club, its teams and their competitions, then open associated matches, even when some details are missing.

**Why this priority**: Pages and their relationships form the main consultation journey; missing secondary information must not make the entire resource unusable.

**Independent Test**: Browse all four page types, their links and seasons with complete, partial or hidden information.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a publicly visible club with available contact details, **when** a guest opens its page, **then** they find its known name, logo, contact details, location, teams and calendar entries. Phone, email, website and map use effective F02 values and open only usable, authorized destinations. Corrected values prevail; deliberately absent or restricted fields receive no fallback to the source or an old value. Club location and full calendars are free independently of Pro. (FR-001, FR-002, FR-032)
2. **A02** — **Given** a viewable team and pool, **when** their pages are opened, **then** they present their known classifications, seasons and relationships; the team provides access to its associated club when present and its participations, the pool to its calendar, standings and map according to rights. A phase without standings receives no invented standings. (FR-003, FR-004, FR-020) A professional team without a club keeps its page and follow action, has no club link or invented map point, and restores its automatic logo according to F02 FR-046. (FR-023)
3. **A03** — **Given** a viewable dated match, **when** its page is opened, **then** participants, available time, applicable result, usable detail, pool, venue, referees, streaming, access to the referenced official FFVB calendar and available documents remain distinct. Missing venue, referee, logo or set detail does not block the rest; it is not replaced by data from another match. Scrolling past the summary leaves the ordinary Match title, return and support actions available; seeing the ordered teams, score and status again requires returning to their content in the body. (FR-005, FR-006)
4. **A04** — **Given** several available seasons for a club, including an upcoming season already published, **when** the page is opened without a current selection, **then** the most recent is selected and identified. Choosing another season controls club teams and calendars together; a delayed response from the previous one does not replace the current choice. A retrieval error does not become “No teams”. (FR-007, FR-018, FR-029)
5. **A05** — **Given** a retained page or relationship whose target is hidden, **when** an old link is followed, **then** unavailability is explained without exposing the hidden resource or redirecting to another assumed identity. A network error is distinguished from this unavailability. Returning to the list preserves the still-applicable context. (FR-008, FR-018, FR-031)
6. **A06** — **Given** existing follow, report and edit entries, **when** they are offered from a page, **then** they retain their destination and applicable follow/count information, and apply F05/F06/F08/F10/F12 and F02 rules according to the action. Missing permission prevents mutation, even from old access; F03 adds no score or match editor. (FR-001, FR-035)

### User Story 2 — Understand times in my time zone (Priority: P1)

As a user in France or abroad, I want to read a consistent time and day without changing the meaning of the sporting schedule.

**Why this priority**: Mixing the source time zone, the phone time zone and relative days can place the same match on the wrong day.

**Independent Test**: Compare Paris and New York for the same instant, then change time zones, cross a daylight-saving change and view matches without a time or date.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A07** — **Given** a match scheduled for September 20, 2026 at 00:30 in Paris according to an established source time, **when** two people view it in Paris and New York, **then** they see September 20 at 00:30 and September 19 at 18:30 respectively. Each person’s daily grouping and relative labels use their time zone, on the page and in lists. The match retains the same instant and identity. (FR-009)
2. **A08** — **Given** a retained calendar and a device time-zone change, **when** the app resumes, **then** known times may be converted with their date-specific offset without a network refresh or regrouping. Day groups and continuation retain the loading zone until manual refresh rebuilds them in the current zone. Match details use the current zone; no fixed Paris–New York offset or guessed source instant is introduced. (FR-009, FR-018)
3. **A09** — **Given** a dated match without a time, including an FFVB 00:00 value not confirmed as midnight under F02, **when** its page or calendar is viewed, **then** the known sporting date remains displayed as is with “Time to be confirmed”, without conversion to another day or a false 00:00 time. (FR-010)
4. **A10** — **Given** an identifiable match without a usable date, **when** lists, calendars or an old link are viewed, **then** no public match page is exposed. When a usable date is accepted, the same identity becomes viewable again if no other restriction remains. If an earlier date is explicitly withdrawn, hiding applies; a mere acquisition failure does not erase the known date. (FR-006, FR-011, FR-031)

### User Story 3 — Find matches and distinguish missing results (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to find matches in “Upcoming” or “Completed”, without silent disappearance or false result confirmation.

**Why this priority**: Elapsed time helps organize the calendar, but proves neither a result, cancellation nor a match in progress.

**Independent Test**: Cross time boundaries, publish or withdraw results, reschedule a match and paginate lists in several time zones.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A11** — **Given** a match without a definitive result scheduled for 20:00 in Paris, **when** a current read occurs after six hours have elapsed, **then** it belongs to “Completed” with “Result unavailable”. Just before the boundary it still belongs to “Upcoming”. Paris and New York apply the same transition instant, even if their displayed days differ; six hours means an elapsed duration, including during a daylight-saving change. The displayed day remains the match day, not the transition day. (FR-009, FR-012, FR-013)
2. **A12** — **Given** a dated match without a time or definitive result, **when** a current read occurs after the day following that date begins in Paris, **then** it belongs to “Completed” with “Result unavailable” for all users. Before this shared boundary, it remains in “Upcoming” with “Time to be confirmed”, without assigning it a sporting instant. (FR-010, FR-012, FR-013)
3. **A13** — **Given** an official definitive result accepted before the time boundary, **when** the list is newly loaded or refreshed, **then** the match appears in “Completed” with that result without waiting six hours. A provisional score does not trigger this early classification; after the boundary, it remains identified as provisional with “Definitive result unavailable”. No winner or zero score is inferred from waiting. (FR-012, FR-013, FR-019)
4. **A14** — **Given** a match classified in “Completed” without a result, **when** a new read after an official postponement establishes its scheduled time in the future, **then** it may return to “Upcoming” under the same identity. A late result replaces the unavailability indication. A withdrawn result removes the score and its detail, then reapplies boundaries to the current match date, without inventing cancellation. (FR-014, FR-019)
5. **A15** — **Given** a verified match withdrawal under F02 or a hidden parent, **when** calendars, the personal feed and an old link are viewed, **then** current server reads keep the match hidden even if a result is retained internally, it is followed through a team or pool, or the six-hour boundary is crossed. Reappearance authorized by F02 may lift the withdrawal without waiting for two collections, subject to a usable date and every other restriction; a collection outage proves no withdrawal. (FR-011, FR-015)
6. **A16** — **Given** several days, pools, equal times and unknown times, **when** lists are browsed, **then** “Upcoming” orders days ascending and “Completed” descending in the calendar loading time zone, retaining that grouping after travel until refresh. Matches are grouped by pool; known times follow the list direction, followed by unknown times, with stable ordering on ties. (FR-016)
7. **A17** — **Given** a paginated calendar, **when** another page fails, **then** loaded days remain usable with retry. Appended matches are deduplicated by identity; empty dates do not falsely end progression. Concurrent date/result changes may leave a moved match outside the traversed portion until refresh. Native refresh rereads the retained traversal using current server cursors; ordinary same-zone failure preserves usable prior rows. Selecting a new loading zone may instead show its own loading/error state. (FR-017, FR-018, FR-029)

### User Story 4 — Read standings and locate participants (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to read official information without invented calculations and, if my rights allow it, locate the clubs participating in a pool.

**Why this priority**: Standings may be partial or absent without making the calendar or map unusable; a map position is not a guaranteed match venue.

**Independent Test**: View standings without complete team matching and a map with zero, one or several locations, including shared locations.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A18** — **Given** official standings with special positions and a row without a viewable Blockout team, **when** displayed, **then** official positions, order and statistics are respected. The unassociated row keeps its source label without a fictional link; its rank is not recalculated from its display position. A missing value does not become zero. (FR-020)
2. **A19** — **Given** temporarily unavailable standings or a phase without published standings, **when** the page is viewed, **then** the last authorized usable standings remain visible if they exist; otherwise a contextual state card explains the absence. Observation dates and acquisition/cache diagnostics are not shown in the sporting interface. The calendar and independent information remain usable. A successful cache read does not become a new official observation. (FR-006, FR-021)
3. **A20** — **Given** a pool with known participations, some without a standings row, **when** a guest or signed-in person opens its map, **then** publicly visible teams with reliable coordinates are represented even without published standings. An unattributable standings row creates neither an assumed participant nor location. (FR-022, FR-032)
4. **A21** — **Given** a locality that is absent, ambiguous or no longer reliable after a change, **when** the map is viewed, **then** no forbidden old point or fictional point is presented. Zero usable points and partial location coverage are distinguished. Basemap loading follows the native provider; independent pages and lists remain usable even when tiles cannot load, without guaranteeing detection of every tile failure. The displayed location is at municipality level, without suggesting a precise gymnasium. (FR-023)
5. **A22** — **Given** several teams at the same location, **when** they are sought on the map, **then** each remains identifiable and accessible without artificially shifting its location. A missing logo does not make the team disappear. The bounded map follows the scene's identity without repeating a title, description or team/location count inside the map; the Pool or Club page already supplies its context. The floating selection preserves co-located choices and a clickable selected-team identity row with a trailing chevron, opening that exact team at ordinary and enlarged text sizes; no separate “Voir l’équipe” button repeats that action. When the official record is available for that exact team, the selection also presents played/won/lost counts and a proportional win/loss bar. Missing statistics never remove the participant or invent zero; no last-five-results strip is required. (FR-006, FR-024)

### User Story 5 — Open a stream or official document (Priority: P2)

As a user, I want to find available links without their presence promising an active video or an actually retrievable document.

**Why this priority**: External streams and documents enrich the core match, result and standings journeys; their failure must not block sporting consultation.

**Independent Test**: Cross link presence, definitive/provisional/absent result and opening success/failure on iOS and Android.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A23** — **Given** an available stream link for a viewable match, **when** it is presented before the scheduled time, after six hours without a result or after a definitive result, **then** the label remains “Stream link”. Neither a red live indicator nor a replay promise is inferred from these conditions. Professional links and those authorized by F08 retain their applicable journey. (FR-025)
2. **A24** — **Given** a usable information-sheet reference, **when** the match is viewable before or after its scheduled time, with or without a result, **then** access to it remains available. Known venue/time information on the page remains usable even if the document does not open. (FR-005, FR-026, FR-028)
3. **A25** — **Given** a scoresheet reference or a professional statistics PDF reference, **when** an official definitive result is available, **then** access to each available document is offered distinctly. Without that result, including after six hours or with only a provisional score, it is not. Withdrawing the result reapplies this condition without inferring erasure of the source reference; the information sheet remains independent. (FR-014, FR-026, FR-027)
4. **A26** — **Given** an external or document opening, **when** the link is absent or opening fails, **then** these outcomes are not presented as a loaded document or a missing score. The person can return, retry and access the applicable reporting journey; the sporting page remains usable. Both platforms provide direct in-app PDF reading without requiring another reader application. Returning restores the match context; temporary reading does not promise offline retention. (FR-025, FR-028, FR-035)

### User Story 6 — Consult during an outage without bypassing restrictions (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want to understand what is missing, resume consultation and retain still-usable data without displaying forbidden or misleading information.

**Why this priority**: An error, an empty result and a hidden resource require different responses; Pro and privacy rules must remain consistent on every surface.

**Independent Test**: Trigger initial and partial errors, change navigation context and entitlements, then resume consultation.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **A27** — **Given** a page without initial data or an already loaded page, **when** loading or a section fails, **then** waiting, error, actual absence and partial result are distinguished. Previous still-authorized sections remain accessible with an indication of failure; no successful empty state is falsely announced. (FR-006, FR-029, FR-031)
2. **A28** — **Given** an open match page, **when** it gains focus, returns to the foreground or is manually refreshed, **then** data and presentation boundaries are reassessed. Refresh of other consultations respects F13 objectives after Blockout acceptance without claiming to trigger provider publication or promising a continuous score stream. (FR-012, FR-030)
3. **A29** — **Given** navigation to another resource, season or account, **when** an older response arrives, **then** it cannot replace the current context. Returning to a retained list preserves its selection while native query freshness may reread its retained pages. A changed timezone updates formatting without moving loaded rows until refresh. Known hidden data remains suppressed and a timezone change never changes sporting season. (FR-007, FR-009, FR-018, FR-031)
4. **A30** — **Given** a guest or a person with confirmed-free, paid Pro, gifted, unknown or expired entitlements, **when** they open a pool map or either full club calendar, **then** content remains accessible without a Pro block, wait for entitlement verification or purchase invitation. Unknown entitlements for a signed-in account suppress advertising under F09/F11, whenever the SDK supplies no current-account state. Personal and administrative permissions and F02 visibility remain unchanged. (FR-001, FR-031–FR-032)
5. **A31** — **Given** a known privacy or visibility restriction, **when** an old page, cache or source reappearance is used, **then** that restriction prevails. Authorized contact details or referees are presented only for their sporting purpose; no personal diagnostic is published in errors. (FR-031, FR-033)
6. **A32** — **Given** partial states and “Result unavailable”, **when** read with supported accessibility tools, **then** their meaning does not depend solely on color or an icon. Retry and return actions remain identifiable, without permanent blocking by loading. (FR-013, FR-028, FR-034)
7. **A33** — **Given** an accepted correction and retained consultable state, **when** details/calendar are consulted or refreshed and affected projections are processed, **then** they reflect the accepted correction with honest freshness. Current restrictions override any retained representation; no global propagation stopwatch or continuous score stream is required. (FR-029–FR-031; F13 FR-004–FR-005)
8. **A34** — **Given** F03 qualification, **when** journeys and discrepancies are assessed, **then** every requirement and state is linked to evidence with known scope, covering relevant iOS/Android, time-zone, entitlement and error cases. A local V1 test or observed Figma screen does not validate V2 behavior; design coverage is assessed under the constitution. (FR-036)

### Edge Cases

- The display date in New York may be earlier than in Paris without changing the shared threshold or season. The six hours are not calculated by naively subtracting local times. (A07–A12)
- A match without a time may remain in “Upcoming” when the displayed sporting date has already passed in the phone time zone: its boundary is shared, at midnight in Paris. No fictional time explains this classification. (A09, A12)
- A match awaiting its result remains findable after several days; aging alone causes neither disappearance nor a zero score. Source/parent restrictions still apply. (A11, A15)
- A match may be classified in “Completed” without a result, with a provisional score or with a definitive result; these cases do not provide the same scoresheet access. (A13, A25)
- A withdrawn definitive result, an explicitly withdrawn date and failed source retrieval have different effects. (A10, A14, A27)
- A pool without standings may have participants and a map; an unassociated official row supplies no assumed identity or location. (A18–A22)
- Loaded day groups remain in their loading context after timezone or source changes; refresh reconstructs them. Newly formatted hours may temporarily cross a retained group date after travel. No automatic list reconstruction is required. (A08, A17, A29)

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

#### Consultation, relationships and seasons

- **FR-001**: Public sporting resources MUST remain free for guests and signed-in people under F05, including pool maps and full club calendars. Consultation, following, contribution and editing remain distinct actions with their own account and permission conditions. Pro MUST NOT restrict consultation.
- **FR-002**: The club page MUST present the applicable name and logo, available contact details, authorized city/address and location, and entries to its teams and calendars. Phone, email, website and map opening MUST use only a usable destination, without fabricating a geographic search from missing information or equating the contact address with a confirmed gymnasium. Contact details and location MUST apply effective F02 FR-049–FR-052 values: deliberate absence or an F11 restriction cannot be bypassed by displaying the source value, an old contact or an old point.
- **FR-003**: The team page MUST present its F02 identity and presentation, known club, division, format, gender and season, its viewable participations, associated calendars and standings, and available follow information and count under F06. Multiple participations remain distinguishable without merging phases: the existing horizontally scrollable team tab bar has one standings tab per visible pool, and upcoming/finished tabs aggregate its matches across visible participations with their pool context. Keep one shared team-follow action and count above the fixed opaque tabs while identity and content scroll within each scene. Use published names and available phase context to distinguish similar pool labels, without introducing qualification or progression controls; links must not choose an assumed namesake team. A professional team without a club MUST retain its page, matches, standings and follow action; club navigation appears only when a club is associated. Effective artwork and automatic-logo restoration follow F02 FR-046.
- **FR-004**: The pool page MUST present known identity, league/competition, classification, format and season, calendars, standings, map access and applicable follow/count information under F06. One shared pool-follow action and count remain above the fixed opaque tabs; each scene scrolls its identity and content independently. Missing information or phases without standings MUST NOT be completed by inference from another pool.
- **FR-005**: The match page MUST present authorized participants and links, available date/time, result and usable detail, pool/competition, known venue and referees, and streaming, navigation to the official FFVB calendar when a usable reference exists, and applicable documents. Missing scalar information and unknown numeric scores use ASCII `-`, without guessing a value or borrowing another match’s facts. Meaningful result-precision and schedule statuses remain explicit. Ordered team identities, the applicable score and status MUST remain in the scrollable page body. Fixed navigation retains the ordinary Match title, return and contextual support actions while scrolling, without revealing a duplicate teams/score recap. Viewing the score again after scrolling past it requires returning to that content. [F13's evidence scope](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#evidence-scope) owns the approved shared presentation and distinguishes prototype approval from Figma approval and native qualification.
- **FR-006**: Absent, invalid, withdrawn and temporarily unavailable values MUST follow F02 distinctions. Missing detail, contact, logo or document must not block independent sections. An unavailable logo does not make the identity unreadable; a missing score does not become zero. Failed acquisition is insufficient to erase a usable previous value.
- **FR-007**: Club seasons MUST come from available viewable teams, be ordered newest to oldest and initially select the most recent, including an upcoming season already published. Selection MUST be identifiable and shared by club teams and calendars. Preserve the choice while it remains available; when a successful response establishes its disappearance, select the most recent remaining season, or display no season if none remain. An outage does not justify a silent switch. A closed season remains viewable under F02; postponement does not change its season by inference from the calendar year.
- **FR-008**: Relationships between resources MUST open their exact identity only if viewable. An unavailable or hidden target MUST have an understandable outcome, distinct from a retrieval error, without exposure through an old link or redirection to an assumed replacement. F02 governs parent and entity restrictions.

#### Time, visibility and calendar classification

- **FR-009**: Sporting instants established under F02 MUST be displayed in the phone time zone with the offset applicable on the match date. A newly loaded or refreshed calendar derives day groups and relative labels in its loading time zone. After a device time-zone change, retained rows may display newly converted times while their day groups keep the original loading context until refresh; no automatic fetch, regrouping or scroll reset is required. Match detail uses the current device zone. No new time-zone setting or resolution of source ambiguity is introduced.
- **FR-010**: A known sporting date without a time MUST remain displayed as is with “Time to be confirmed”, without assigning an instant or converting the date alone. The F02 rule for FFVB 00:00 remains applicable; midnight is a usable time only when explicitly confirmed. A date alone may share a displayed day with converted instants without claiming the same precision.
- **FR-011**: A match without a usable date MUST be hidden from all public surfaces, including lists, calendars and its page through an old link. Its identity and data remain retained under F02, without a new operator action added by F03. A usable date restores consultation only if no other hiding/withdrawal reason prevents it. Explicit date withdrawal reapplies hiding; a mere collection outage does not trigger it.
- **FR-012**: Calendars MUST retain “Upcoming” and “Completed”. Each current read classifies an official definitive result as “Completed” immediately. Otherwise a match with a reliable instant is “Upcoming” before six elapsed hours after kickoff and “Completed” at and after that boundary. For a date without time, the boundary is the following day at 00:00 Europe/Paris. These rules use the same evaluation instant everywhere, independently of display time zone, without redefining F02 sporting status. Previously loaded rows are not locally reclassified by elapsed time or detail reads; current server reads follow the shared native Query policy. Match details retain FR-030 rereads.
- **FR-013**: A match classified in “Completed” without a definitive result MUST bear “Result unavailable”, or “Definitive result unavailable” if a provisional score is displayed. This distinction MUST be explicit and accessible without depending solely on color. Both before and after the boundary, a score, winner, cancellation, postponement or “in progress” state MUST NOT be inferred solely from elapsed time. No new tab is required; the approved design sets the visual form of the indication.
- **FR-014**: A current read after an official date change or a late, corrected or withdrawn result MUST reassess presentation under the same match identity. Postponement may return a match to “Upcoming”; result withdrawal removes its score/detail under F02 and reapplies the boundary. A retained calendar is rebuilt on refresh rather than automatically reshuffled by detail reads or source changes. The grouping date is the sporting date, never result receipt or threshold-crossing date. Result withdrawal differs from whole-match withdrawal under FR-015; known restrictions still take precedence.
- **FR-015**: F02 and FR-011 restrictions MUST prevail over current server time classification. Current representations of a withdrawn match MUST exclude it, and its refused detail MUST not republish retained sensitive facts. Previously fetched benign summaries follow FR-031's ordinary refresh boundary. A follow, acquired result, time threshold or time-zone change grants no new server visibility. Authorized F02 reappearance may lift withdrawal without two collections; hidden parents, inactive divisions and missing dates still apply. A source outage alone proves no withdrawal.
- **FR-016**: Displayed days MUST be ascending in “Upcoming” and descending in “Completed”. Within a day, matches remain grouped by pool with stable group ordering for the same set; within a group, known times follow the list direction, followed by unknown times. Equal times MUST retain stable ordering for the same data, without inventing sporting priority. Unavailable results stay on the match day in “Completed”, without eviction solely due to age.
- **FR-017**: Pagination MUST allow browsing all eligible complete days in the loading time zone, without a fixed match count per page or total calendar cap. Empty eligible dates do not produce groups or terminate progression. Failed additional reads preserve loaded days and offer retry. Appending pages MUST deduplicate match identities. Reads are not one immutable snapshot: concurrent result/date changes can temporarily move a match outside the traversed portion; native refresh reconstructs retained pages from the beginning using current server continuation boundaries.
- **FR-018**: Resource, season, category and account changes MUST keep responses in their originating query/client context. Calendar retention, retries and rereads follow the ordinary shared Query policy. Explicit refresh affects the selected tab; native infinite refetch may reread all retained pages. Changing the loading zone selects its new query immediately and may show loading/error rather than keep old-zone groups visible. Same-zone failure preserves still-usable data honestly. No first-page-only replacement, transactional zone adoption, process-restart persistence or fine scroll-restoration guarantee is required.

#### Results, standings and maps

- **FR-019**: Overall result, set detail, provisional/definitive nature and sporting notations MUST follow F02 FR-028–FR-031. A usable overall result remains visible without valid detail; contradictory detail is hidden rather than reconstructed. No zero, winner, universal number of sets or administrative result may be invented; presentation classification in “Completed” does not turn a provisional score into a definitive result.
- **FR-020**: Standings MUST respect official order, positions and statistics under F02, without calculating rank from the display index or reconstructing from matches. A row without a matching viewable team keeps its source label without a fictional link. Missing values and special ratios are not replaced by zero. The default list preserves row order. The approved card presentation may project the unique explicitly published positions 2–1–3 as a podium; all remaining rows keep source order. If any podium position is absent or ambiguous, ordinary cards preserve the complete source order without invented places. Team highlighting is limited to the list and must not alter standings or infer a winner from an absent or ambiguous result.
- **FR-021**: Unavailable standings MUST remain distinct from usable standings or a phase without published standings. The last still-authorized usable state remains viewable, retaining its original freshness metadata internally. The sporting interface shows no observation date, acquisition diagnostic or retained-data explanation. Qualified non-publication and a published empty table use contextual empty cards; no usable table uses the shared unavailable-read card with explicit retry. A failed explicit refresh preserves authorized content and uses the existing transient action feedback. Calendar retrieval and cache reads create no new provider observation.
- **FR-022**: The pool map MUST represent known, viewable participating teams with a reliable location, independently of standings availability. An unassociated official row creates neither a team nor a map point. Loss of a visible F02 participation removes its representation in that pool without deleting its other participations.
- **FR-023**: Points MUST represent clubs’ reliable municipality-level location under F02, without claiming to locate a match gymnasium. Absent, ambiguous or invalidated coordinates yield no assumed point. Complete absence of points and partial location coverage MUST be distinguished. Basemap loading and its failure presentation follow the native provider, without a custom guarantee to detect every tile failure; independent information remains viewable. An empty map must not suggest that the pool contains no teams. A professional team without an associated club supplies no municipality point; its other information remains usable.
- **FR-024**: When several teams share a location, each MUST remain identifiable and accessible without artificial geographic displacement. A missing image does not remove representation; links follow FR-008. The map occupies a bounded viewport after the scene's identity, with the selected-team card floating over the basemap. Pool and Club maps MUST NOT repeat a header, description or team/location count inside that viewport; their enclosing page supplies the context. Map controls and attribution MUST remain usable without a title gradient. Selection MUST keep co-located choices and the selected team's full identity reachable at every text size. The identity row, including its logo and name, MUST open that exact team and display a trailing chevron; do not duplicate it with a separate “Voir l’équipe” button. Optional statistics follow this row. Where the exact team has published played/won/lost statistics, show those values and a proportional win/loss bar without recomputing sporting facts. Missing, nonnumeric or special values remain unknown or retain their published text; they never become invented zero or a fabricated proportion. Standings unavailability MUST NOT block map identity or navigation. Rank and points retain their team/standings journey; a last-five-results strip is outside the current scope. Visual grouping and framing follow the [approved shared design scope](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#evidence-scope), without a new need for phone location.

#### Streaming and documents

- **FR-025**: An available, authorized stream link MUST be presented as “Stream link”, before or after the match, without a red live indicator or replay promise inferred from time, result or link existence. Preserve applicable professional media and F08 link journeys; no permanent video-platform monitoring or new contribution authorization is added. An absent link offers no fictional opening; a failed opening leaves applicable return, retry and reporting available.
- **FR-026**: The information sheet MUST be accessible as soon as a usable reference is available and throughout the viewable match lifecycle, independently of the score. This includes professional matches with a qualified document association under F01/F02. ### Key Entities

- **Viewable resource**: Club, team, pool or match retained by F02 whose restrictions currently allow consultation; a match requires a usable date.
- **Participation**: Relationship of a team to a pool/season, distinct from an official standings row.
- **Sporting time**: Instant established from the F02 source context, or a date alone without an instant when the time is missing.
- **Displayed day**: Group derived from the loading phone time zone for instants. A retained group is not rebuilt after travel until refresh; dates alone remain unchanged.
- **Calendar category**: “Upcoming” or “Completed” under FR-012, distinct from the result or confirmed sporting state.
- **Result / standings**: Official F02 information, with known completeness, provisional/definitive nature and freshness, without substitute sporting calculations.
- **Municipality-level location**: Reliable club point used to locate participants, distinct from the exact match venue.
- **External reference**: Stream link, information sheet, scoresheet or professional statistics PDF; its presence and successful opening are two distinct facts.
