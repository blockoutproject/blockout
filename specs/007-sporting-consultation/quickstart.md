# Validation guide: Sporting consultation

Native accessibility verification follows [F13 FR-030](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#platforms-accessibility-and-bounded-continuity).

## Prerequisites and setup

Follow the [local development setup](../../README.md#local-development) and run the [repository verification commands](../../README.md#verification). Use the scenarios below to qualify sporting consultation; shared checks alone do not establish the feature behavior or native/provider results required here.

Qualify sporting consultation with PostgreSQL fixtures, generated Java/TypeScript consumers and compatible native Expo development clients. Reuse existing iOS/Android simulator/emulator clients when their native dependencies and configuration match; rebuild only when that native boundary changes. Use fictional accounts, sporting data and controlled provider responses. Real source reads require their applicable authorization; never commit raw PDFs or personal source records. Android provider checks require authorized restricted map credentials and remain pending while the owner hold is in force; this guide authorizes no provider or billing operation.

[F13](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#evidence-scope) owns shared design references. Validate strongly blurred translucent navigation/tabs, one collapsible identity card with its applicable follow action, scene-local controls, shallow alignment and independent deep positions. Keep Match facts in the body and reveal its compact result/status center through scrolling. For the headerless map and clickable team row, retain the [scoped advance authorization](plan.md#constitution-check). Native motion, physical/provider behavior and live text-size changes require their own platform checks.

## Validation scope

Apply [F13's proportionate qualification model](../003-shared-quality/plan.md#proportionate-qualification): deterministic tests own exhaustive domain/time, cancellation and current-context boundaries; representative native compositions own actual formatting, layout, gestures and reachable actions. Across selected changed compositions cover both themes and a live appearance change, narrow/enlarged French text, reduced motion, ordinary focus/back and accessible names/roles/states without repeating every case for every appearance, font size and device. Reuse shared session/Query and unchanged native evidence only within its recorded source, dependency, runtime and configuration scope. Record actual fixture results, generated-consumer compilation, selected package/build versions and passed, failed or unavailable native outcomes in normal delivery evidence. Verify the library combination before adopting it; an incompatibility reopens the affected choice rather than authorizing a fork or silent platform upgrade. Physical/provider checks retain their feature owners; exact production signing, upgrade identity and store-test evidence remain with F13/F14. Prototype/Figma approval, source inspection and documentation checks do not qualify those boundaries.

### Native time presentation

Keep Paris/New York conversion, both zones' DST transitions, date-only values, unknown FFVB 00:00 versus confirmed midnight and absolute French dates in deterministic sporting-time, presentation and backend classification/calendar tests. A07's instant remains September 20, 2026 00:30 in Paris and September 19 18:30 in New York. Use a short formatting/foreground observation per native engine for the actual integration, reusing applicable existing evidence instead of replaying every boundary across calendars, cards and notifications. The accepted native travel qualification combines observed Android behavior with a scoped waiver for the unexecuted iOS travel/DST/retained-navigation/refresh cases; those iOS cases are not an observed pass. When the native formatting/resume integration changes, observe its changed boundary on the affected engine.

For that observation, verify a concise full date/time such as “7 novembre 2026 à 20h40” and one device-zone/foreground change: formatting updates while retained calendar groups and their captured zone/cursor stay unchanged until explicit refresh. Visual calendar rails retain compact day/weekday/abbreviated month and complete accessible absolute dates. This does not change sporting instants, results or date-only semantics.
