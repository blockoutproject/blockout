# Validation guide: Sporting consultation

## Prerequisites and setup

Follow the [local development setup](../../README.md#local-development) and run the [repository verification commands](../../README.md#verification). Use the scenarios below to qualify sporting consultation; shared checks alone do not establish the feature behavior or native/provider results required here.

Qualify sporting consultation with PostgreSQL fixtures, generated Java/TypeScript consumers, native Expo development builds and iOS/Android devices. Use fictional accounts, sporting data and controlled provider responses. Real source reads require their applicable authorization; never commit raw PDFs or personal source records. Configure restricted Android map credentials in the intended environment before native map checks.

[F13](../003-shared-quality/spec.md#evidence-scope) owns shared design references. Validate strongly blurred translucent navigation/tabs, one collapsible identity card with its applicable follow action, scene-local controls, shallow alignment and independent deep positions. Keep Match facts in the body and reveal its compact result/status center through scrolling. For the headerless map and clickable team row, retain the [scoped advance authorization](plan.md#constitution-check). Native motion, physical/provider behavior, live text-size changes and VoiceOver on iOS require their own platform checks.

## Validation scope

Record actual fixture results, generated-consumer compilation, selected package/build versions and native device outcomes in the delivery evidence. Verify the library combination before adopting it; an incompatibility reopens the affected choice rather than authorizing a fork or silent platform upgrade. Prototype/Figma approval proves the scoped experience only. Source inspection, contract lint and documentation checks do not prove integrated native behavior.

### Native time presentation

Verify A07's instant: September 20, 2026 00:30 in Paris is September 19 18:30 in New York. Include date-specific DST offsets, unchanged date-only values, unknown FFVB 00:00 versus confirmed midnight and French relative labels. Change the display zone and return from the background: formatting updates without regrouping a retained calendar or changing its captured zone/cursor.

### Complete-day calendar qualification

Prepare Paris/New York/Tokyo zones, known/date-only/absent times, both DST changes and Paris midnight. Cross all three selectors, multiple participations, 150 matches on one day, more than seven gapped days and equal/unknown-time ordering. Expect complete-day pages, exclusive continuation, one identity per page and a coherent response; a between-page change may require refresh.

On iOS/Android, select Upcoming/Completed, page horizontally, append, open the exact match and return. Fail append and explicit refresh independently: preserve authorized rows with the appropriate retry. Refuse a resource, then fail a later read: refused facts must not return. Change the grouping zone and verify that old-zone responses cannot enter the new selection. Keep independent common/sibling reads usable.

Compare [Pool calendar](https://www.figma.com/design/rKu4xc8eJsx0f0Vu4E6U03?node-id=4363-11492), [date rail](https://www.figma.com/design/rKu4xc8eJsx0f0Vu4E6U03?node-id=461-3693), [Completed row](https://www.figma.com/design/rKu4xc8eJsx0f0Vu4E6U03?node-id=4359-11985) and [large-text row](https://www.figma.com/design/rKu4xc8eJsx0f0Vu4E6U03?node-id=397-1375). Pool group headings remain informational; match cards open their exact detail. Phone-zone travel, physical devices and VoiceOver on iOS require native execution.

### Official standings and participant reads

Prepare published empty, not-published and unavailable tables, absent/special values, unmatched rows and participants independent of standings. Verify source order, exact identities, no inferred rank/zero and retained authorized content after a failed refresh. Lists/cards display only Pts and J/G/P while the backend retains all published statistics; compare the compact participation link and list/card/podium compositions in [the approved design](plan.md#design-and-validation).

### Participant map checks

Use the native map cases above. Refresh address-derived points from singleton to coincident and back: preserve every participant, expose the choice rail only for a shared resulting point and keep exact selected-team navigation. Check selection, Close, camera controls, the complete team identity, optional published statistics and attribution before/after header collapse and at enlarged text. Framing waits for native readiness and nonzero layout in either callback order. A readiness event does not prove tile availability. Verify physical-device, Android credential/provider and VoiceOver behavior on iOS separately from controlled map checks.

### Match recap and missing facts

Check top, partial scroll, fully revealed and return-to-top behavior against [FR-005](spec.md#consultation-relationships-and-seasons): the full body summary remains; the compact center reveals ordered logos with accepted final totals/exact notation or the current nonfinal status. Return and authorized actions remain visible. Include final zero, unknown totals, missing referee/venue `-`, no pool icon, one-line ellipsis and full accessible names.

### Sporting recovery and match rereads

Fail initial, common and independent section reads on Club/Team/Pool/Match; distinguish genuine absence from failure. Preserve authorized facts after ordinary failure, withdraw current refused contacts/images/resources and reject obsolete resource/season/account completions. Verify distinct ordinary/bypass image admission and the shared error/Retry feedback.

Correct a result/date/link, then exercise Match focus return, foreground and manual refresh. Only the addressed Match read changes; retained calendar rows/category remain under their own selection. Explicit refresh targets the active Club tab. Current refusal removes facts before a later technical failure; no provider fallback or alternate recovery owner is introduced.
