# Functional specification: advertising, privacy and legal documents

**Feature branch**: `feature/260-advertising-privacy-legal`

**Created on**: 2026-09-18

**Status**: Accepted functional baseline under [planning authority #9](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/9); technical-dossier acceptance and implementation evidence remain separate.

**Requested scope**: Approved functional rules for [#260](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout-legacy/issues/260), F11 in the [specification navigation](../../README.md#specifications). Define nonpersonalized advertising, its frequency and relationship with Pro, public consultation and versioned publication of legal documents, and shared privacy obligations. Detailed reporting journeys belong to F10; their shared constraints and decisions needed to write them are explicit here.

## User scenarios and validation _(mandatory)_

Visitors and signed-in users view content and documents, exercise their choices and may contact support. The authorized operator maintains versioned legal files; authorized personnel handle reports and data-related requests. Repository legal-content publication grants no access to support records. F12 defines assignment of shared permissions.

These scenarios describe expected outcomes of a future implementation. They prove neither V1 behavior, provider production settings nor certified legal compliance. Identifiers are local to this specification.

### User story 1 — View content with predictable advertising frequency (Priority: P1)

As a free user, I want to access sporting content and live streams without advertising or its errors persistently interrupting my journey.

**Priority rationale**: Advertising revenue must remain compatible with usable navigation, including when no ad can be displayed.

**Independent validation**: Run a sequence mixing sporting navigation and live streams, with ads available then unavailable, and compare accepted actions, actual displays and opened destinations.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A01** — **Given** a free user and a zero count, **when** nine eligible navigations then one live-stream opening are accepted, **then** the first nine proceed without an interstitial; the tenth triggers a presentation attempt if an ad is authorized and ready. After actual display, the count returns to zero; the live stream opens after dismissal. Any other combination of ten eligible actions follows the same rule. (FR-001–FR-003)
2. **A02** — **Given** the threshold reached, **when** the ad is unavailable on the tenth action, fails on the eleventh and displays on the twelfth, **then** every action may continue, the first two attempts reset nothing and only the third resets the count to zero. Multiple loading attempts do not count as user actions. (FR-003–FR-005)
3. **A03** — **Given** a transition in progress, **when** presentation fails, its confirmation does not arrive or the ad closes, **then** the accepted action is released exactly once. An ad loaded after the destination opens does not suddenly appear there. Concurrent requests produce neither overlapping ads nor double execution of the same action. (FR-004, FR-005)
4. **A04** — **Given** nine eligible actions, **when** the user views legal documents, opens support, signs in, refreshes a page, changes a filter or goes back, **then** those operations do not increment the count or trigger an ad. A mere foreground return triggers nothing; a new application session starts from zero, without synchronization with another device. (FR-001, FR-002, FR-006)

### User story 2 — Receive advertising consistent with my entitlements and choices (Priority: P1)

As a user, I want my Pro entitlements and privacy choices respected, even during an outage or account change.

**Priority rationale**: An unknown entitlement does not prove absence of a subscription; lack of personalization does not mean a guaranteed absence of advertising.

**Independent validation**: Cross entitlement states with applicable choices and advertising preparation outages on both selected mobile platforms.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A05** — **Given** a valid paid or gifted Pro entitlement, **when** a sporting action is performed or an external video opened, **then** Blockout serves no advertising without promising its removal from the external service. Guests and people with confirmed free status perform the same public sporting actions under the shared F11 count; guests do not wait for account entitlement consultation. No map or calendar requires Pro. (FR-007)
2. **A06** — **Given** a signed-in account, **when** the SDK supplies no state, **then** no ads or purchase invitation appear. An inactive SDK state may restore consent-based ad eligibility, including after native cache expiry, without debt or a Blockout outage clock. Public sport stays accessible. (FR-006–FR-008)
3. **A07** — **Given** account A and a pending entitlement response, **when** the user signs out or switches to account B, **then** A’s entitlements, including that late response, do not apply to the new session. Eligibility is reassessed before any new presentation, even if an ad was prepared. (FR-008)
4. **A08** — **Given** a fresh start, **when** Pro entitlements are not yet known, **then** privacy-choice preparation may proceed in parallel without alone authorizing display. An already known valid Pro entitlement avoids an unnecessary new advertising prompt; still-applicable choices are reused. (FR-009)
5. **A09** — **Given** an audience including adolescents aged 13–14 and adults, **when** each uses the application, **then** no age or birth date is requested or inferred from account age, and no personalized advertising is served. The same protective treatment applies to everyone, without a parental journey to activate personalization. (FR-010, FR-011)
6. **A10** — **Given** two free users performing the same sequence with different choices, **when** the threshold is reached, **then** opportunity frequency is the same. Authorized contextual or limited ads remain usable; no refused authorization is bypassed. Without an authorized mode or available ad, the journey continues without promising an identical number of impressions. (FR-012, FR-013)
7. **A11** — **Given** existing choices, **when** viewing or changing them fails, **then** failure is understandable and retry is possible without false confirmation. After a successful change, no incompatible prepared ad may be presented. Without an applicable decision authorizing processing, an outage does not constitute consent. (FR-013, FR-014)

### User story 3 — Read and maintain reliable legal documents (Priority: P1)

As a visitor, I want to read applicable documents without an account. As an authorized operator, I want to publish a consistent title, version and content.

**Priority rationale**: Information must be accessible before the actions it concerns; a publication error must not erase the last available document.

**Independent validation**: Read each of the three documents as a guest, then compare reviewed file publication, rejected invalid content and failed release against the still-published state.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A12** — **Given** a visitor without a session, **when** they open legal notices, the privacy policy or terms of use, **then** they can read the requested document without advertising. Loading, a missing document and failure are distinguished with possible retry; none of these states constitutes acceptance. (FR-015, FR-016)
2. **A13** — **Given** an authorized legal-file update, **when** reviewed files are released with the backend, **then** public reading returns the published title, version and content together. There is no mobile editor or write API. (FR-017–FR-019)
3. **A14** — **Given** empty or invalid legal-file content or a failed backend release, **when** publication is evaluated, **then** invalid content is rejected by documentary validation and the last deployed document remains readable. An uncertain deployment is checked through public reading, without a fictional success. (FR-017, FR-018)
4. **A15** — **Given** a document update, **when** a user views it or continues sign-in, **then** neither version history nor an acceptance register is created by that action alone. Privacy information is not treated as blanket consent to optional processing. (FR-020)

### User story 4 — Use sporting information without re-exposing restricted data (Priority: P1)

As a data subject, I want personal information useful for sport limited to that use and applicable corrections or restrictions to take effect.

**Priority rationale**: A publicly accessible source does not permit every use; preserving a sporting identity does not require republishing every historical personal datum.

**Independent validation**: Follow a club contact and a referee name from acquisition to consultation, then apply a correction or restriction and replay collection and restoration.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A16** — **Given** a club contact published by an authorized source or a referee associated with a match, **when** the information is used, **then** it serves the intended sporting context, not advertising profiling. Referees do not acquire standalone personal profiles and replaced old contact details do not form a viewable archive. (FR-021–FR-023)
2. **A17** — **Given** an applicable request targeting a personal contact detail, **when** it is handled, **then** the field is corrected or removed from relevant active views and copies without indiscriminately deleting the club, teams or matches. Later collection, even from the priority source, an administrative correction or an explicit return to the source does not re-expose the forbidden field. (FR-023, FR-024)
3. **A18** — **Given** a backup predating a restriction, erasure or applicable notification-retention boundary, **when** it is restored, **then** current obligations apply before any access. F07 notifications due for purge do not reappear and available non-personal match-level announcement facts prevent ordinary repeats. If those facts were lost, an exceptional repeat is accepted under F07 FR-045; personal erasure and expiry still apply, and uncertain compliance keeps affected data unavailable. Backup retention does not set active-data or log retention. (FR-025, FR-026; F07 A51)
4. **A19** — **Given** a technical diagnostic or secondary support notification, **when** produced, **then** no secret or personal content is exposed in a public artifact, user error or diagnostic log. Personal information needed for the support record remains in its authorized process, not in a log copy or public notification. (FR-021, FR-027, FR-031)

### User story 5 — Be contacted after a report even without sign-in (Priority: P1)

As a visitor or user encountering an error, I want to send a usable report and reply address without needing to succeed at the failing action.

**Priority rationale**: A “match inaccessible” report must retain the attempted target; sign-in difficulty must not close support access.

**Independent validation**: Establish expected data for a guest report, an inaccessible page and failed sign-in, then examine their destination and submission outcomes. F10 details the journey and its categories.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A20** — **Given** a guest, a signed-in user or failed sign-in, **when** a report is prepared, **then** an email address is mandatory, prefilled if available and editable before sending. An absent or invalidly formatted value prevents submission with an explanation; valid format proves neither deliverability nor possession. F12 access restrictions preserve legal information and the support journey, subject to its complete-receipt rules. (FR-028, FR-029)
2. **A21** — **Given** a route targeting a match known by identifier whose loading fails, **when** a report is sent, **then** the target type and identifier come from the attempted navigation. Unknown names remain absent; failure is not replaced by an assumed target. For failed sign-in, only the method, step and safe reason or useful reference are sent with minimal technical context. (FR-030)
3. **A22** — **Given** a report and its screenshots, **when** processed, **then** only authorized personnel access personal data, including linked files. The operator uses GitHub and may reply to the supplied address; no integrated dialogue or resolution tracking in the application is assumed. A declared address or identifier does not permit account recovery. (FR-029, FR-031, FR-032)
4. **A23** — **Given** a support submission, **when** its outcome is presented or retried, **then** only transmission of the content and every selected image, attached to the same authorized record, allows complete acceptance. Intermediate creation with failed attachment remains to be reconciled without duplication or an incomplete record treated as finalized. A lost response remains uncertain until the outcome is established; no isolated file becomes public. If only the secondary alert fails after complete receipt, the request remains accepted without resubmission. (FR-031, FR-033)
5. **A24** — **Given** a closed support issue, **when** time passes, **then** no new periodic purge or automatic deadline is imposed. If an individual erasure request applies, it is handled in the relevant ticket and attachments, even if they reside in different storage locations. (FR-026, FR-034)

### User story 6 — Exercise my rights without depending on an account (Priority: P1)

As a data subject, I want to know the contact and process for requesting access, rectification, erasure or objection applicable to my data.

**Priority rationale**: A request may concern a guest, sporting contact or person who can no longer sign in.

**Independent validation**: Prepare requests concerning an account, sporting contact and report, then identify the recipient, proportionate check and copies to process.

**Acceptance scenarios**:

1. **A25** — **Given** a person without a session, **when** they read the privacy policy, **then** they find an email contact and request process. Handling is manual; information requested to verify identity is proportionate to the request, without systematic identity documents. (FR-035)
2. **A26** — **Given** a received request, **when** examined, **then** affected data and recipients are identified, the response or reason for a limitation is communicated within the applicable legal deadline, and follow-up is distinguished from account deletion or a refund. A recipient outage does not allow declaring complete erasure. (FR-036)
3. **A27** — **Given** release preparation, **when** actual processing, providers and distribution countries are reconciled with user information, **then** their purposes, recipients, retention conditions and rights procedures are explained without fictional text, blanket consent or certification claims. Selected advertising settings are compatible with the 13+ audience without collecting age. (FR-011, FR-021, FR-037)

### Edge cases

- The threshold may be reached during advertising unavailability; missed opportunities do not form an ad debt to catch up in a burst. (A02)
- A ready ad may become forbidden after an entitlement or privacy change. (A07, A11)
- A foreground return is neither sporting navigation nor a new session by itself. (A04)
- An unreviewed file or failed backend release must not replace the currently published legal document. (A14)
- The same version string may be retained during a valid change; it is neither a timestamp nor evidence of acceptance. (A13, A15)
- An unavailable sporting source preserves still-authorized data under F02 without neutralizing an already applicable personal restriction. (A17)
- A guest may report an unloaded target; their entered address may belong to a third party and therefore proves no identity. (A20–A22)
- Deletion from business data does not automatically erase tickets, files or copies held elsewhere; every outcome remains distinct. (A24, A26)

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional requirements

#### Advertising actions and frequency

- **FR-001**: The system MUST offer only interstitials at eligible sporting transitions: voluntary opening of a club, team, pool or match page from sporting journeys, and voluntary opening of a live-stream link. An action corresponds to an accepted transition, counted once; internal attempts or repetitions of the same still-pending action create no additional actions. Filters, scrolling, refreshes, back navigation, starts, exits and foreground returns MUST NOT trigger advertising. No in-content, rewarded or launch placement is added.
- **FR-002**: Sporting navigation and live-stream openings MUST share a single count with a threshold of ten. Account, authentication, payment, privacy, legal-document and support journeys MUST remain outside this count and without interstitials. Opening a live stream MUST NOT trigger an ad independently of the shared threshold.
- **FR-003**: The first nine eligible actions from zero MUST continue without an ad. From the tenth onward, an authorized, ready ad MUST be subject to a presentation attempt. Without display, the count MUST remain at the threshold to retry on the next eligible action. Only actual presentation MUST reset it to zero; loading, a presentation call or an error is insufficient. One display resets all missed opportunities, without multiple catch-up displays.
- **FR-004**: An absent, unready or forbidden ad MUST NOT prevent the requested action. Advertising preparation MUST NOT require waiting for loading before that action. If an initiated presentation is not confirmed, the action MUST have a recovery path without indefinite waiting; technical timeouts will be defined and verified during the technical phase, without implicitly reusing V1 timeouts.
- **FR-005**: An accepted transition MUST execute exactly once after dismissal, failure or abandonment of the advertising wait. The system MUST neither overlap ads nor present a late ad after the destination opens. Returning to the application or a delayed response MUST NOT repeat an already released action. Failure of destination opening itself remains distinct from advertising failure.
- **FR-006**: The count MUST be local to the application runtime session, initialized to zero on a new start and retained on a mere background transition. It MUST NOT constitute persistent tracking between devices. Changes in advertising availability or choices MUST NOT reset the threshold without display. Actions performed with a valid Pro entitlement or unknown signed-in entitlements accumulate no advertising debt; presentation eligibility remains checked under FR-007–FR-008.

#### Entitlements and advertising choices

- **FR-007**: RevenueCat SDK active paid or gifted state suppresses Blockout advertising, the sole Pro benefit. External video-service ads are excluded. Without SDK state for a signed-in account, show no advertising or purchase invitation. SDK-inactive state, including native cached inactive state, permits advertising only under existing consent, availability and frequency rules. F09 owns SDK integration; no separate five-minute/72-hour guarantee or advertising debt. Public sporting access remains free.
- **FR-008**: Advertising eligibility MUST be reassessed before presentation from current-session entitlements. Sign-out, account switching and delayed responses MUST NOT transfer an exemption between accounts. Entitlement verification MUST be retryable without blocking any public sporting consultation, including maps and full club calendars. F05 and F09 own this lifecycle; personal and administrative permissions remain unchanged.
- **FR-009**: Privacy-choice preparation MUST be able to start in parallel with Pro entitlement retrieval, without automatically authorizing display. Still-applicable decisions MUST be reused without a new prompt at each launch. An already known valid Pro entitlement MUST avoid an unnecessary new advertising prompt; privacy information and controls remain accessible.
- **FR-010**: The application targets an audience aged 13 and above, including 13–14-year-olds, without collecting age or birth date for advertising. It MUST apply advertising treatment suited to that minor audience to everyone, without personalization. Account age MUST NOT be used to infer the person’s age. No parental journey is added to enable personalization.
- **FR-011**: Advertising qualification MUST account for current distribution countries and protections applicable to the selected audience, including incompatible ad categories. “Without personalization” MUST NOT be presented as proof that all local storage, identifiers or processing are exempt from authorization. Without a mode compatible with applicable protections and authorizations, no ad may be requested or displayed. This specification adds no country or arbitrary distribution restriction.
- **FR-012**: Refusing optional advertising processing or tracking authorization MUST NOT grant Pro status or intentionally reduce opportunity frequency. An authorized contextual or limited mode MUST remain usable when available. The same frequency rules apply to all free users, without guaranteeing an identical fill rate, revenue or impression count.
- **FR-013**: Advertising processing MUST respect applicable choices, permissions and conditions; refused, unknown or failed authorization MUST NOT be replaced by assumed agreement. A system permission MUST NOT be treated as blanket consent. Without tracking across applications or companies covered by ATT, no ATT request may be presented for this advertising. Absence of an authorized mode must allow continued use without advertising.
- **FR-014**: Applicable privacy information and choices MUST remain accessible without sign-in and independently of Pro. A consultation or modification error MUST be visible and recoverable, without fictional confirmation. A successful change MUST invalidate incompatible prepared ads before any new presentation; an outage preserves only still-applicable previous decisions without extending their authorizations.

#### Legal documents

- **FR-015**: Legal notices (`imprint`), the privacy policy (`privacy`) and terms of use (`terms`) MUST be viewable without sign-in, including before an action presented as acceptance of terms. Consultation MUST depend neither on Pro nor a favorable advertising choice. The sporting-document reader remains a distinct capability.
- **FR-016**: Reading MUST distinguish loading, a missing document and recoverable failure. Missing content MUST be replaced neither by fictional text nor implicit acceptance. The user MUST be able to leave reading and retry; no complete offline mode is added.
- **FR-017**: Legal notices, privacy and terms MUST be versioned repository files published with the backend. Remove the mobile editor and legal-write operations/permissions. The operator uses the existing reviewed documentary delivery process; public reading and the direct privacy-page account-deletion section remain available.
- **FR-018**: Published legal files MUST contain nonempty title, free-form version and content, with a truthful modification date. Validate the complete document before backend publication. A failed release preserves the last deployed document; inspect the deployed read result before claiming publication. No mobile partial-edit command is provided.
- **FR-019**: Reading after confirmed publication MUST return the published title, version and content together. The version MUST remain a free-form label, distinct from the actual last-modification date. No lexical comparison, mandatory increment or new version value is required to authorize a valid change.
- **FR-020**: Updating a document MUST NOT create viewable history, an acceptance register or new mandatory acceptance on every change. The privacy policy MUST inform about processing without being presented as blanket consent to optional processing. Applicable specific consent remains distinct from reading documents and accepting terms.

#### Purposes, minimization and shared restrictions

- **FR-021**: Every category of personal data used MUST have an identified purpose, only the data needed for that purpose, authorized recipients and explainable retention and rights-exercise conditions. The category matrix below constitutes the shared scope; it does not require collecting every field found in V1. No advertising profiling from accounts, follows, reports, sporting contacts or contributions is authorized.
- **FR-022**: Available, authorized club contacts and referee names MUST remain usable for sporting information and contact intended by F02. Publication by a source does not authorize unlimited reuse. Referees remain associated with matches, without creating a standalone directory or personal profile.
- **FR-023**: Applicable corrections and removals of personal fields MUST propagate to affected active copies and views. Replaced contact details MUST NOT populate an archive of previous contacts. F02 sporting identity and history retention remains distinct from retention of these personal fields; a source outage preserves only still-authorized values. Administrative corrections and deliberately absent F02 contact details remain subject to these restrictions; returning to the source does not lift an F11 restriction.
- **FR-024**: An applicable erasure or objection request MUST prevent republication of targeted fields through later collection, reactivation or reconstruction, even if the source still publishes those values. Handling MUST be proportionate to the affected data and MUST NOT indiscriminately delete clubs, teams and matches. F01/F02 source priority does not bypass this restriction.
- **FR-025**: Restorations MUST apply current restrictions, erasures, domain-specific retention periods and rights before any access, under F13 FR-027. If compliance cannot be established, affected data remains unavailable. Backups MUST remain protected and never provide another means of viewing erased data. For notifications, F07 FR-045 requires removal of expired or known-purged entries and preservation of available non-personal match-level announcement facts. If those facts were lost, an exceptional repeat announcement is accepted; that limit never waives personal erasure or expiry obligations.
- **FR-026**: F13’s 30-day backup retention MUST NOT become the default retention period for active data, support requests or logs. Each domain’s retention remains tied to its purpose and applicable obligations. F07 explicitly owns daily notification purge between 29 and 30 elapsed days, non-personal match-level announcement facts and protected copies under FR-019/FR-043–FR-046; this establishes neither support-request purging nor a shared log duration. Applicable individual erasure requests remain possible in controlled destinations and are not delayed by notification retention.
- **FR-027**: Technical diagnostics MUST respect F13 FR-013: no secret, personal data or raw provider content in diagnostic logs, public artifacts and user errors. Technical tracking information may describe the outcome, scope and a safe reference without reproducing the personal record. No new analytics or log-retention duration inferred from tickets or backups is added.

#### Support privacy and agreement with F10

- **FR-028**: F10 MUST allow reports without an account, before sign-in and after authentication failure. An email address MUST be mandatory for every report, prefilled when available and editable before sending. This collection serves request handling and response, not marketing or advertising personalization. F12 maintenance and mandatory update do not remove this support access or access to legal information.
- **FR-029**: The reply address MUST be checked for format without guaranteeing deliverability or adding an ownership-verification journey. An address, name or identifier declared by the client MUST NOT constitute evidence of account ownership or authorize linking, recovery, erasure or entitlement transfer. Evidence rules belong to F05/F09 and FR-035.
- **FR-030**: The report MUST automatically include available reliable context: screen, problematic action, type and identifier of the targeted resource even if loading fails, application version, system and device model useful for diagnosis. Unknown names remain absent. For failed sign-in, method, step and safe reason or diagnostic reference may be included; password, token, payment evidence and raw provider content are excluded from automatic context.
- **FR-031**: Ticket personal data, chosen screenshots and linked files MUST be accessible only to personnel authorized to handle them. Submission must not require a screenshot or raw log; users must be able to inspect chosen attachments before submission. Neither publication in a public space nor automatic copying of personal content to a secondary notification is authorized. A file URL proves neither access control nor erasure; partial failure must preserve these protections.
- **FR-032**: GitHub MUST remain the handling tool and ticket history selected by the operator; replies use the supplied email address. No integrated dialogue or resolution tracking in the application is added. This rule selects neither storage mechanisms nor secondary notifications.
- **FR-033**: F10 MUST accept a request only when its content and every selected image are transmitted and attached to the same authorized record. Refused validation, confirmed failure, intermediate state and uncertain outcome MUST remain distinct from complete acceptance. Intermediate effects MUST be reconciled without intentional duplication or an incomplete record treated as finalized; no partial-delivery or post-confirmation editing journey is added. A lost response MUST allow establishing or retrying the same submission. Failure of only the secondary alert MUST NOT invalidate a fully received request or require resubmission. No schema or technical recovery mechanism is prescribed here.
- **FR-034**: GitHub issues MUST be allowed to remain working history, without adding periodic purging or an automatic post-closure deadline. GitHub hosting MUST NOT be presented as validation of unlimited personal-data retention. An applicable request MUST be handled in the relevant ticket and files, including when stored separately; their deletion is not inferred from business account deletion.

#### Rights and information

- **FR-035**: The privacy policy MUST publish an operational email contact and rights-exercise procedures, accessible without sign-in. Handling is manual. Identity verification MUST be proportionate to the data and request; systematic identity documents or treating a report’s entered address as sufficient evidence are excluded. No new request form is required.
- **FR-036**: Request handling MUST identify affected data, active copies and recipients, apply relevant rights and communicate the outcome or reason for a limitation within applicable legal deadlines. An authorized extension must be explained under applicable conditions; it is not a silent extension. Successful and still-pending operations remain distinct. F05 owns the account-deletion journey and provider lifecycle; F09 owns paid-entitlement evidence and decisions. A data request constitutes neither a refund nor identity transfer.
- **FR-037**: Before publication, supplied information MUST match actual processing: controller and contact, applicable purposes and bases, relevant recipients and transfers, retention durations or criteria, rights and optional authorizations. Actual distribution countries and protections for the audience without age collection MUST be reconciled with selected settings. Starter text, this document or a writing checklist MUST NOT be presented as an audit of published text or compliance certification.

### Key entities

- **Eligible action**: Accepted voluntary sporting transition; belongs to the shared count without creating individual cross-device history.
- **Advertising state**: Distinct combination of Pro entitlements, applicable authorizations, preparation and ad availability. A count at the threshold replaces none of these checks.
- **Privacy choice**: Decision applicable to processing and its conditions; distinct from reading a document, system permission and Pro entitlement.
- **Legal document**: Stable type, title, free-form version, current content and actual modification date. Contains no acceptance register.
- **Sporting personal data**: Field useful for sporting context, subject to applicable restrictions independently of sporting identity retention.
- **Support record**: Transmitted content, reliable context, reply address, chosen attachments and acceptance outcome. Its declarations are not an authenticated identity.
- **Data-related request**: Data subject’s request, scope, proportionate verification, outcome and necessary recipient follow-up. Does not create a request-management product.

## Success criteria _(mandatory)_

### Measurable outcomes

- **SC-001**: For every controlled A01–A04 sequence, no ad precedes ten eligible actions from zero; every display resets the count to zero and every failure without display preserves the threshold. Excluded journeys produce no opportunity. (FR-001–FR-006)
- **SC-002**: In all A02–A03 failure, dismissal and delayed-response cases, each accepted action is released exactly once; no late or overlapping display or block without a recovery path is allowed. (FR-004, FR-005)
- **SC-003**: Every A05–A08 state respects the entitlement matrix: zero advertising for valid Pro or unknown signed-in entitlements without usable evidence, zero entitlement transfer between accounts, free access maintained. (FR-007–FR-009)
- **SC-004**: A09–A11 require no age data and produce no personalized advertising, refused processing or fictional confirmation. For an identical action sequence, choices do not change the threshold, without requiring identical impressions. (FR-010–FR-014)
- **SC-005**: A12–A15 cover public reading of all three versioned document types and backend publication, with no mobile editor/write operation. Title, version and content remain consistent; a free-form version is not a computed date. (FR-015–FR-020)
- **SC-006**: For A16–A19, no restricted personal data reappears through collection, reactivation, reconstruction or restoration; no independent sporting identity is deleted solely for removal of a personal field. (FR-021–FR-027)
- **SC-007**: Every accepted A20–A24 report has a validly formatted reply address; all known targets remain identifiable despite loading failure. No secret or public access to personal attachments is allowed. No complete confirmation lacks a selected image; intermediate and uncertain states are resolved with rare exceptional duplicates handled manually under F10, and secondary-alert failure does not invalidate the complete record. No new periodic purge is required. (FR-028–FR-034)
- **SC-008**: A25–A27 cover accounts, sporting contacts and support records; every request has a contact accessible without an account and an outcome or reason within the applicable deadline, without claiming completion of still-pending operations. Every matrix category has an identified purpose, recipients and functional owner. (FR-035–FR-037)

## Assumptions

- The selected audience is 13+, without age collection or new identity verification for advertising. Current countries are retained; their list and effective provider settings are qualification evidence to obtain, not values invented in this specification.
- The count is local to the application session. Its lack of cross-device persistence is a simplicity choice, not a guarantee of global frequency per person. Entitlements and choices remain checked on every presentation.
- GitHub is the selected support process, without a new issue-purge schedule. This does not waive handling applicable requests or honestly documenting retention conditions; no claim of compliance for unlimited retention is made.
- Matrix categories delimit authorized processing; they select neither new fields, additional providers nor a technical schema. Operational durations and parameters not set by a domain are not inferred from backups or tickets.
- Content, errors and journeys follow F13 for French language, accessibility, platforms, session isolation and understanding of uncertain outcomes. Privacy forms and legal documents remain usable with voice reading, enlarged text and consistent focus; their layout belongs to the global Figma review.
- The rights contact and legal notices must be real before publication; no fictional address or legal identity is supplied here. Legal deadlines remain distinct from a technical on-call deadline or a response deadline promised for every ticket.
- [F10](../012-reports-feature-suggestions/spec.md) defines the shared problem/suggestion form, optional subject, images and complete submission with safe retry. F05/F09 retain deletion, evidence and subscription decisions.
- This delivery changes no production code, contract, schema, provider or legal text. Full corpus review then Figma review R01/R02 under #247 precede technical planning.

### External references and limitations

- Google distinguishes [ad-serving modes](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/ad-serving-modes): nonpersonalized mode may still use identifiers for some functions. [Limited ads](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/10105530) allow nonpersonalized ads under their conditions without guaranteeing availability. FR-011–FR-013 therefore equate neither refusal with Pro nor absence of personalization with absence of all processing.
- [Apple’s privacy documentation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/) governs tracking covered by ATT. System permission and information or consent obligations remain distinct.
- [Teen advertising protections](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/12171027?hl=en) do not alone prove knowledge of every Blockout user’s age. The selected uniform treatment and absence of age collection remain requirements to qualify in actual markets.
- CNIL recommendations on [reuse of published data](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/recommandations-reutilisateurs-donnees-internet) justify not inferring unlimited authorization from a public source. FR-022–FR-024 preserve authorized sporting uses and applicable rights.
- CNIL describes [responding to access requests](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/repondre-une-demande-de-droit-dacces), normally within one month with regulated extensions and proportionate identity checks. FR-035–FR-036 refer to applicable deadlines without promising continuous human availability.

These references underpin distinctions useful to the specification; they certify neither advertising settings nor production processing. Qualification must examine the rules and settings applicable at that time.
