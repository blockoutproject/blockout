# Blockout Product Vision

Blockout makes French volleyball competitions easy to follow by turning federation data into clear, reliable, and
personal mobile experiences for players, supporters, clubs, and competition staff.

## Product Principles

- Make clubs, teams, pools, rankings, matches, and live information understandable without requiring knowledge of
  provider systems.
- Prioritize the information and actions that matter in the user's current context.
- Preserve the meaning and provenance of competition data while presenting it in a consistent Blockout vocabulary.
- Make following, notifications, search, and navigation feel continuous across the competition model.
- Treat accessibility, platform conventions, error recovery, and trustworthy feedback as product requirements.

## Boundaries

This vision does not define a capability, screen, contract, or delivered behavior. Product intent is owned by the
accepted `specs/<feature>/spec.md`. Current contracts, source code, and tests remain the authority for delivered
behavior; GitHub issues only track design or execution work.

Changes to this vision require explicit human approval and the normal Blockout review and release path.
