# Identity and Pro continuity

## Purpose and evidence boundaries

This discovery assessment supports [#253](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/253) and the [V1 functional inventory](v1-functional-inventory.md). It records observed V1 behavior, owner-approved V2 constraints and unresolved specification questions. It is neither an accepted feature specification nor a migration implementation plan. The [constitution](../../.specify/memory/constitution.md) governs future specifications; all functional specifications must be accepted before technical planning and implementation.

- **Source evidence:** the local monorepo at `03ca1bfec969ef8b85686bea0ed974f20a7bf945`. The owner accepts the reorganized monorepo as the functional V1 reference. This does not independently establish installed store builds or deployed backend versions. Relative source links may evolve after this snapshot.
- **Provider observations:** authorized, read-only inspection of the owner's production Auth0 tenant and Blockout RevenueCat project on 2026-09-15. The sanitized findings below describe the settings actually viewed, not all settings or every store configuration.
- **Account samples:** individual Auth0/RevenueCat records were inspected to establish that linked identities and anonymous aliases exist. No complete subscriber reconciliation, production purchase, restoration, linking, deletion or cutover test was performed.
- **Provider documentation:** official guidance supplies provider semantics, not evidence that Blockout implements them correctly. The relevant sources are linked at each boundary.
- **Product decisions:** the V2 constraints below were approved by the owner during framing. Their incorporation into accepted specifications remains necessary. Historical code is not authority for reproducing a discrepancy.

No personal identifiers, customer exports, raw provider payloads, credentials or token-bearing dashboard URLs belong in this document. No archived or external repository was consulted. GitHub owns execution status and review evidence.

### Provider semantics relevant to the transition

- Auth0 recommends authenticating both accounts before linking. The primary profile ID remains; the secondary identity is attached to it, and secondary metadata is not automatically preserved. Correctly establishing the authenticated primary after linking also needs handling. These semantics explain why equal emails alone are insufficient and why linking can affect downstream identifiers. [Account linking][auth0-linking], [linking flow][auth0-link-flow].
- Auth0 `created_at` describes creation of the Auth0 profile, not creation of the underlying Google or Apple account. [Profile structure][auth0-profile].
- RevenueCat custom IDs are project-scoped and case-sensitive. The same ID can retrieve access across platforms. Switching between two identified IDs with `logIn()` does not merge them or transfer purchases; anonymous-to-identified aliasing has separate rules. `original_app_user_id` must not be assumed to equal the current Auth0 subject. [Customer identity][rc-identity].
- The inspected restore setting transfers receipt-associated access to the restoring account; it is not a general Auth0-account merge. Native restoration depends on the purchasing store account, and subscription management remains with the store of purchase. User-initiated restoration is distinct from logging into the same Blockout account on another platform. [Restore behavior][rc-transfer], [restoring purchases][rc-restore].
- A renewal cancellation or billing issue is not by itself proof that access has expired. Store grace periods can preserve entitlement access. A refund/revocation must be interpreted using the effective entitlement state, not an event name alone. [Customer information][rc-info], [billing and grace periods][rc-grace].
- Deleting a RevenueCat customer removes its customer/purchase history there but does not cancel Apple/Google subscriptions. Apple also requires account-deletion handling for Sign in with Apple token revocation, and permits immediate deletion while informing users about continuing subscription billing. Neither a database delete nor an Auth0 delete alone demonstrates this complete lifecycle. [RevenueCat deletion][rc-delete], [Apple account deletion][apple-delete].

## Owner-approved V2 constraints

These are functional constraints to incorporate into specifications, not authorization to choose a schema, service topology, API shape or provider integration strategy.

### Identity, linking and account age

1. **Migration recreation:** keep existing Auth0 accounts and linked identities; automatically recreate the business profile after authentication. Do not require users to recreate their Google/Apple identity or fill a new mandatory profile form solely because of migration. Historical business data need not be migrated. Liquibase replaces Flyway for the future schema; this document defines no migration schema.
2. **New linking:** allow Google/Apple attachment only when their emails match and both identities have been authenticated. Do not introduce new links for different emails, including Apple private-relay differences. Preserve existing links even if they would not qualify under this new-linking rule.
3. **No duplicate workaround:** failed, declined or cancelled verification must not create a same-email business duplicate or silently link accounts. Allow retry or use of the existing login method. Do not merge two already populated business accounts; guide conflicts to assistance without automatic data loss. Assistance does not override the prohibition on new different-email links.
4. **Stable outcome:** an already linked person must reach the same business account and applicable Pro rights through either login method. A change to the technical authentication subject must not silently strand a subscription. Failed or interrupted linking recovery must be specified before implementation.
5. **Age:** permanently use the preserved principal Auth0 profile's creation date for the seven-day live-publication threshold, retaining the moderator exception. A new business row must not restart this wait. Missing/unavailable timestamps require a specification decision, not an invented fallback to the business creation date or unrestricted publication.

### Paid access, recovery and manual grants

1. **Paid continuity:** existing valid subscriptions remain usable after migration without repurchase. Require authentication before purchase and restoration. The same Blockout account retrieves its rights across devices and platforms. After logout, the visitor has free access, without retained Pro benefits from the previous account.
2. **Benefits:** retain the three established Pro benefits: no advertisements, the pool map, and club-wide upcoming/finished match lists. No new paid benefit is introduced here.
3. **Restore transfer:** allow restoration onto another signed-in account to transfer the corresponding paid right. Do not merge business data or leave that same paid right active on both accounts. The old account can remain Pro if it has an independent valid subscription or manual grant. A platform change with the same account differs from native receipt restoration onto a different account.
4. **Assisted recovery:** when autonomous restoration is unavailable, assistance may recover a verified existing paid right onto the person's new account, without a new purchase, business-account merge or duplicate use of that right. This is not a discretionary gift or an authorization to accept an email match as proof. Accepted evidence, operator permissions and the recovery procedure remain to be specified.
5. **Manual Pro at launch:** allow temporary or non-expiring grants, both revocable. Use a dedicated permission, initially held by the owner and explicitly authorized operators; moderator status alone does not authorize grants. A grant does not change paid renewal dates or charges; revoking it must not revoke independently valid paid access. It belongs to the beneficiary account and does not transfer with store restoration. After deletion or on another account, a new grant is required.

### Entitlement states and recovery

| Condition                                                                       | Required observable outcome                                                                                                                                | Specification boundary                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Confirmed active paid right or valid manual grant                               | Pro benefits apply.                                                                                                                                        | Derive authority, freshness and enforcement across mobile/backend during later specifications and technical plans.                                  |
| Confirmed expired/revoked paid right, no other valid grant/right                | Free access; no continuing Pro based solely on old paid status.                                                                                            | Define effective revocation/expiry handling and update latency. Do not classify every cancellation/refund notification as immediate loss of access. |
| Confirmed free account                                                          | Normal free experience.                                                                                                                                    | Advertising remains governed by applicable consent and advertising rules.                                                                           |
| Connected account, rights unavailable and no usable proof                       | Free content remains accessible; Pro features show a pending state with retry. No advertisements and no prompt to buy again while rights are undetermined. | Define recovery messaging, proof freshness and the boundary between pending and confirmed free.                                                     |
| Previously known subscriber whose entitlement cannot be refreshed around expiry | Apply a bounded tolerance, not indefinite Pro or an invented duration.                                                                                     | The Pro specification must choose duration and exit conditions. Store billing grace and a Blockout outage tolerance are separate concepts.          |

### Voluntary deletion and V2 cutover

Voluntary deletion removes the business account, Auth0 profile and associated personal data according to an explicitly specified provider/data lifecycle. It does not delete the person's Google/Apple account. Users may delete without waiting for subscription expiry; inform them that store billing can continue and provide subscription-management access. If they return while their paid subscription remains valid, restoration to a recreated account must be possible without restoring old business data or manual gifts.

The exact erasure/retention boundary, any justified retained records, provider revocation and recovery after partial deletion remain open. Do not silently retain a full customer dossier for convenience or claim immediate complete erasure while provider work is unresolved. Restoration after RevenueCat deletion must be proven rather than inferred from Auth0 deletion or ordinary reinstall behavior.

V2 requires an application update; durable functional V1 coexistence is not the selected approach. Retiring V1 must account for its role-assignment endpoint, email-triggered linking, existing sessions and other shared-provider interactions. Displaying a force-update screen alone does not prove V1 can no longer mutate shared identity state. No launch date, downtime budget or rollback threshold is selected here.

## Preservation prerequisites and release gates

The permitted business reset is conditional on paid-access continuity. It must not discard the only evidence needed to reconnect a subscriber. Preserve or prove reliable reconstruction of the minimum relevant information below; this is an information requirement, not an instruction to copy the whole database or choose a storage design.

| Required information                                                                                   | Why it matters                                                                              | Evidence needed before reset                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Auth0 principal, linked provider identities and profile creation date                                  | Preserve login equivalence and age independently of new business IDs.                       | Account-resolution and age scenarios, including linked profiles and unavailable fields; no assumption that every sample represents all users.                                                                  |
| RevenueCat identified IDs, original IDs and relevant anonymous/historical aliases                      | Paid rights may be associated with a secondary or historical identity.                      | Reconciliation against the actual subscriber population, with unresolved associations identified and resolved before destructive reset.                                                                        |
| Relationship between a valid purchase/right, its RevenueCat customer and the authenticated beneficiary | An equal email or a new numeric business ID cannot establish entitlement ownership.         | Demonstrated recovery path for every affected identity category, including purchases under secondary identities and verified support recovery. Keep evidence private.                                          |
| Entitlement/product/store associations and effective provider settings                                 | Preserve which purchases grant Pro and how restore/transfer and notifications behave.       | Confirm production catalog and store configuration; exercise relevant iOS/Android paths. Test Store records do not substitute for production configuration evidence.                                           |
| Any indispensable mapping only present in V1 business data                                             | Auth0 and RevenueCat inspection may not recover every historical association by themselves. | Identify whether such a dependency exists before deletion; retain only what is required or demonstrate an alternative reconstruction. No claim is made that a separate mapping export is definitely necessary. |

Release remains blocked by unexplained subscriber associations, unproven required restoration/linking outcomes, unresolved deletion/revocation behavior, or retirement of a V1 dependency that prevents authentication. Google notification delivery and other entitlement freshness paths require validation against the eventual Pro freshness requirements. The overview/customer-list counts from discovery are not a signed-off subscriber inventory.
