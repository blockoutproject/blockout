# Identity and Pro continuity

## Purpose and evidence boundaries

This discovery assessment supports [#253](https://github.com/blockoutproject/blockout/issues/253) and the [V1 functional inventory](v1-functional-inventory.md). It records observed V1 behavior, owner-approved V2 constraints and specification ownership. It is neither an accepted feature specification nor a migration implementation plan. The [constitution](../../.specify/memory/constitution.md) governs future specifications; all functional specifications must be accepted before technical planning and implementation.

- **Source evidence:** the local monorepo at `03ca1bfec969ef8b85686bea0ed974f20a7bf945`. The owner accepts the reorganized monorepo as the functional V1 reference. This does not independently establish installed store builds or deployed backend versions. Relative source links may evolve after this snapshot.
- **Provider observations:** authorized, read-only inspection of the owner's production Auth0 tenant and Blockout RevenueCat project on 2026-09-15. The sanitized findings below describe the settings actually viewed, not all settings or every store configuration.
- **Account samples:** individual Auth0/RevenueCat records were inspected to establish that linked identities and anonymous aliases exist. No complete subscriber reconciliation, production purchase, restoration, linking, deletion or cutover test was performed.
- **Provider documentation:** official guidance supplies provider semantics, not evidence that Blockout implements them correctly. The relevant sources are linked at each boundary.
- **Product decisions:** the V2 constraints below were approved by the owner during framing. Their incorporation into accepted specifications remains necessary. Historical code is not authority for reproducing a discrepancy.

No personal identifiers, customer exports, raw provider payloads, credentials or token-bearing dashboard URLs belong in this document. No archived or external repository was consulted. GitHub owns execution status and review evidence.

### Provider semantics relevant to the transition

- Auth0 recommends authenticating both accounts before linking. The primary profile ID remains; the secondary identity is attached to it, and secondary metadata is not automatically preserved. Correctly establishing the authenticated primary after linking also needs handling. These semantics explain why equal emails alone are insufficient and why linking can affect downstream identifiers. [Account linking][auth0-linking], [linking flow][auth0-link-flow].
- Auth0 `created_at` describes creation of the Auth0 profile, not creation of the underlying Google or Apple account. [Profile structure][auth0-profile].
- RevenueCat custom IDs are project-scoped and case-sensitive. The same ID can retrieve access across platforms. Switching between two identified IDs with `logIn()` does not merge them or transfer purchases; anonymous-to-identified aliasing has separate rules. `original_app_user_id` must not be assumed to equal the current Auth0 subject. [Customer identity][rc-identity].
- The inspected restore setting transfers receipt-associated access to the restoring account; it is not a general Auth0-account merge. Native restoration depends on the purchasing store account, and subscription management remains with the store of purchase. User-initiated restoration is distinct from logging into the same Blockout account on another platform. [Restore behavior][rc-transfer], [restoring purchases][rc-restore].
- A renewal cancellation or billing issue is not by itself proof that access has expired. Store grace periods can preserve entitlement access. A refund/revocation must be interpreted using the effective entitlement state, not an event name alone. [Customer information][rc-info], [billing and grace periods][rc-grace].
- Deleting a RevenueCat customer removes its customer/purchase history there but does not cancel Apple/Google subscriptions. Apple also requires account-deletion handling for Sign in with Apple token revocation, and permits immediate deletion while informing users about continuing subscription billing. Neither a database delete nor an Auth0 delete alone demonstrates this complete lifecycle. [RevenueCat deletion][rc-delete], [Apple account deletion][apple-delete].

## Owner-approved V2 constraints

These are functional constraints to incorporate into specifications, not authorization to choose a schema, service topology, API shape or provider integration strategy.

### Identity, linking and account age

[Accounts and identity (F05)](../../specs/005-accounts-identity/spec.md) owns the normal account lifecycle. The constraints below remain distinct from V1 observations.

1. **Migration recreation:** keep existing Auth0 accounts and linked identities; automatically recreate the business profile after authentication without a new mandatory profile form. Preserve identity resolution and existing email ownership even when the business database is empty; the first returning login method must not take over an address belonging to an unlinked identity. Historical business data need not be migrated. Liquibase replaces Flyway for the future schema; this document defines no migration schema.
2. **Existing links only:** preserve already established Google/Apple associations, including different-email links. Do not create new associations, even with equal emails or proof of both identities. Either existing linked method reaches the same account and applicable rights.
3. **No duplicate or merge workaround:** refuse first business-profile creation when the email already belongs to an unlinked identity, comparing case-insensitively without provider-specific alias rewriting. Allow the usual login method or assistance, not a new link or a merge of populated accounts. Different emails, including Apple private relay, do not establish equivalent identities. Paid recovery remains separate.
4. **Profile and missing fields:** automatically create a profile with a valid unique pseudo, email and optional photo; do not copy first name, last name or phone. Missing usable email suspends first creation with retry, guest access and assistance. For a recognized account, absent/conflicting provider data do not erase known values, overwrite another account or trigger linking. Preserve manual pseudo/photo changes.
5. **Age:** use the preserved principal Auth0 creation date for F08's seven-day threshold and moderator exception. A recreated business row or reinstall must not restart the wait. Reliably attributed known evidence remains usable during provider failure; without it, suspend only actions needing that evidence and allow retry. Voluntary deletion followed by signup starts a new account lifecycle and does not restore its former age, even if an identifier is reused. See F05 FR-024–FR-025 and A20–A22.

### Paid access, recovery and manual grants

1. **Paid continuity:** existing valid subscriptions remain usable after migration without repurchase. Require authentication before purchase and restoration. The same Blockout account retrieves its rights across devices and platforms. After logout, the visitor has free access, without retained Pro benefits from the previous account.
2. **Benefits:** retain the three established Pro benefits: no advertisements, the pool map, and club-wide upcoming/finished match lists. No new paid benefit is introduced here.
3. **Restore transfer:** allow restoration onto another signed-in account to transfer the corresponding paid right. Do not merge business data or leave that same paid right active on both accounts. The old account can remain Pro if it has an independent valid subscription or manual grant. A platform change with the same account differs from native receipt restoration onto a different account.
4. **Assisted recovery:** when autonomous restoration is unavailable, assistance may recover a verified existing paid right onto the person's new account, without a new purchase, business-account merge or duplicate use of that right. This is not a discretionary gift or an authorization to accept an email match as proof. F09 FR-023–FR-026 require an authenticated usable destination, corroborated transaction and ownership evidence, owner-only intervention through RevenueCat, before/after scope verification and a private decision/result trace. Insufficient evidence leads to a reasoned refusal or request for additional evidence; incompatible provider effects block the operation rather than justify a broader transfer or automatic gift.
5. **Manual Pro at launch:** allow temporary or non-expiring grants, both revocable. The owner alone operates through RevenueCat at launch, with immediate effect and an explicit end date or no expiry; any later delegation requires an explicit decision. No Blockout grant administration screen is required. The RevenueCat Support role has broader powers and is not a grant-only permission; moderator status alone does not authorize grants. A grant does not change paid renewal dates or charges; revoking it must not revoke independently valid paid access. It belongs to the beneficiary account and does not transfer with store restoration. After deletion or on another account, a new grant is required.

The [F09 specification](../../specs/006-pro-subscriptions/spec.md) defines these rules in FR-001–FR-034 and A01–A29. Provider qualification remains necessary; these requirements do not establish implemented V1 behavior.

### Voluntary deletion and V2 cutover

Voluntary deletion removes the business profile, Auth0 identity and associations, applicable Apple credentials, RevenueCat customer dossier, and associated personal data under [F05 FR-026–FR-038](../../specs/005-accounts-identity/spec.md) and F11. It does not delete the person's Google/Apple account or automatically cancel store subscriptions. Users may delete before subscription expiry; explain continuing billing and provide subscription-management access. Returning users can restore valid paid access through the purchasing store account, without old business data or manual grants.

Before durable acceptance, a confirmed failure leaves the account usable and no external erasure has begun. After acceptance, deny account access on all devices and continue erasure independently of the app; partial failures require safe retries and operator treatment of persistent blocks, not account resurrection. Distinguish acceptance, pending work and established outcome. A local transaction cannot undo external erasure. While identity/payment work could affect a recreated account, show deletion in progress and allow guest access/assistance. Establish resolution from actual provider guarantees without requiring unavailable proof; delayed cleanup must never affect the new account or restored purchases.

Useful live contributions remain without account attribution, preserving moderation state and without automatic ownership recovery. This is not a guarantee of total anonymization of their contents. F11 governs applicable requests affecting support records, attachments and other personal data, without a new periodic GitHub purge. Keep only purpose-limited evidence needed for completion, non-resurrection or applicable obligations; do not retain a full customer dossier for convenience. Restoration after RevenueCat deletion must be qualified on both stores, rather than inferred from ordinary reinstall behavior. Provide a public outside-app deletion-request pathway under F05 FR-037, with proportionate ownership verification.

V2 requires an application update; durable functional V1 coexistence is not the selected approach. Retiring V1 must account for its role-assignment endpoint, email-triggered linking, existing sessions and other shared-provider interactions. Displaying a force-update screen alone does not prove V1 can no longer mutate shared identity state. [F14](../../specs/014-v1-v2-transition/spec.md) defines explicit reauthentication, a dismissible pre-login welcome on identified V1 upgrades, availability on both stores before ordinary V1 retirement, and repair/restoration of V2 after opening without a required functional return to V1. No launch date, arbitrary downtime budget or destructive timeout is selected.

## Preservation prerequisites and release gates

The permitted business reset is conditional on paid-access continuity. It must not discard the only evidence needed to reconnect a subscriber. The separate sporting-data constraint in [F02](../../specs/002-sporting-data/spec.md), FR-043/FR-046 and A39, also requires F14 to preserve manually associated club logos, their club references and corresponding files before reset; it does not extend this account-reconciliation scope. Preserve or prove reliable reconstruction of the minimum relevant information below; this is an information requirement, not an instruction to copy the whole database or choose a storage design.

| Required information                                                                                   | Why it matters                                                                              | Evidence needed before reset                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Auth0 principal, linked provider identities and profile creation date                                  | Preserve login equivalence and age independently of new business IDs.                       | Account-resolution and age scenarios, including linked profiles and unavailable fields; no assumption that every sample represents all users.                                                                  |
| RevenueCat identified IDs, original IDs and relevant anonymous/historical aliases                      | Paid rights may be associated with a secondary or historical identity.                      | Reconciliation against the actual subscriber population, with unresolved associations identified and resolved before destructive reset.                                                                        |

Keep evidence private.                                          |
| Entitlement/product/store associations and effective provider settings                                 | Preserve which purchases grant Pro and how restore/transfer and notifications behave.       | Confirm production catalog and store configuration; exercise relevant iOS/Android paths. Test Store records do not substitute for production configuration evidence.                                           |
| Any indispensable mapping only present in V1 business data                                             | Auth0 and RevenueCat inspection may not recover every historical association by themselves. | Identify whether such a dependency exists before deletion; retain only what is required or demonstrate an alternative reconstruction. No claim is made that a separate mapping export is definitely necessary. |

Release remains blocked by unexplained subscriber associations, unproven required restoration/existing-identity resolution outcomes, unresolved deletion/revocation behavior, or retirement of a V1 dependency that prevents authentication. Google notification delivery and other entitlement freshness paths require validation against F09 FR-007–FR-011 and FR-032. The overview/customer-list counts from discovery are not a signed-off subscriber inventory.
